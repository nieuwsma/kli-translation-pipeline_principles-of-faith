# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a solid system and structure are necessary. Without them, inefficiency and chaos can easily arise. This is why establishing a strong foundation in doctrine is extremely important for living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
For this reason, "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which speaks about the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining 300-plus pages, which are filled with teachings and activities related to the Church and our roles in it, along with understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an exceptional tool for teaching in the classroom and for preparing leaders with a strong foundation in doctrine, and will also prove extremely beneficial for individual readers.
