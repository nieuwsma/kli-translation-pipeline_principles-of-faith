# Chapter 1 - The Importance of Sound Doctrine

<!-- p1 -->
The simple meaning of doctrine is the foundational truths we believe and teach—truths entrusted by God’s faithful people, embedded in Scripture, and lived out in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus described His church as ‘teaching,’ He was commanding them to share His instruction.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus came to them and began to say to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, and baptize them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> and teach them to observe all the things which I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus is commanding all of us to learn His teachings and replicate them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), who fully embodies God’s truth, knowledge, and revelation. This is the Incarnation, the Word (logos), who was in the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’ orderly teachings are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), in keeping His commandments (obedience, guarding, preserving) (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***)।

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in all of history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all. He alone knows the perfect blueprint of a lasting, unshakable structure. His understanding beyond others— it is inherent within him. He is the source of all architectural knowledge, and the origin of every sturdy building comes from His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the complete and comprehensive expression of God’s truth, knowledge, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)।

<!-- p10 -->
To bring his design to life, the architect makes a **detailed blueprint**—a structured plan document that contains the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build a structure that will stand firm. Without this blueprint no one can build properly. This blueprint represents Jesus’ structured teaching, His doctrine (***didaskalia***)—the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>)। His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the very means by which His wisdom is communicated and preserved so that whoever follows Him can build their life on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
But the blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also gives **precise instructions**, explaining how each part of the structure is to be joined, which beam is to be placed where, and how every element aligns with his proven design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-the actionable instructions that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>)। His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps that ensure the building stands strong in accordance with the original blueprint. Neglecting or altering His commandments would compromise the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine that the architect gathers some **disciples/trainees**—those who want to learn from him, build under his guidance, and one day teach others to build structures that endure. These trainees carefully study his blueprint, listen attentively to his instructions, and faithfully follow his commandments in their work. They do not build according to their own understanding, but according to the master’s design. These trainees represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)-those who observe, believe, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, shaping their lifestyle according to His word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>)। The more they study under the architect, the more skilled they become, and eventually they teach and train others in his ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the rock of our faith! He is the master architect who has the proven design and blueprint. Only by studying the blueprint, following the architect’s specific instructions, and developing a strong working relationship with Him can we build a life that stands in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, they labor in vain who build it; unless the LORD guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
If we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith, we cannot expect our lives to remain firm in difficulties, challenges, tribulations, upheavals, or even in the ordinary circumstances of life.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone does not make a person healthy and fit. To get the full benefit of the membership you must remain in the discipline of the gym. In the same way, mere attendance at church or being formally called a Christian will not do; we all must be educated, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith—only then will we receive the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command extends beyond us. It is very good that we become whole, healthy, disciplined, and submit to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples of others in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and become disciplined? So that we can build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, it is more than just a benefit; it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, then we build our lives on the sand, and a strong storm will break our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs direct our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in his salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed Him, "If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by remaining in his word (his logos)! The result of remaining in his word is our knowledge of the truth, which makes us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, who have been taught discipline, can obey the Master’s commands. In the New Testament church every person is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become a disciple -\> bring yourself under the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to come under the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we don’t have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us his Holy Spirit who will lead us into all truth and guide us (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us his word written in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders to help build us up, mature us, keep us safe, and organize us for good works.

<!-- p36 -->
The Christian faith is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us experts who know from experience how to help us grow and mature, and over time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p38 -->
> **<u>Ephesians 4:11-16</u>** (NKJV)

> And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers,
>
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the building up of the body of Christ,
>
> until we all come to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a perfect man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;
>
> that we should no longer be children, tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, in the cunning craftiness of deceitful schemes,
>
> but, speaking the truth in love, may grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ—
>

<!-- p39 -->
</span>

<!-- p40 -->
**An important note:** The word 'some' indicates that not all of us are called with the gifts of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but we have all been given gifts of ministry, talents, and special roles (discussed further in the chapter on the Church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given these special gifts to the Church so that He might bring us to maturity, and that we:

<!-- p42 -->
- able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- united in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- mature

<!-- p46 -->
- not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we will be <u>children, who are tossed to and fro and carried away.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God's family, but children by nature are:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to distinguish between good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - engaged only in what is momentary and immediate.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - vulnerable to harm.

<!-- p53 -->
### Sound teaching provides protection against the wickedness of the world

<!-- p54 -->
In this life we do not have the option of remaining spiritually neutral. We are influenced either by the Kingdom of God, or by the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul commanded his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, because they have itching ears,

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p61 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days difficult times will come:
>
> For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,
>
> unloving, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, cruel, haters of good,
>
> traitors, headstrong, arrogant, lovers of pleasure more than God,
>
> having a form of godliness but denying its power. From such people turn away!
>
> For of this sort are those who creep into houses and make captives of gullible women loaded down with sins, led by various desires,
>
> always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
>
> Now as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so also do these oppose the truth; their minds are corrupt and, concerning the faith, they are disqualified;
>
> but they will progress no further, for their folly will be manifest to all, as theirs also was.
>

<!-- p62 -->
</span>

<!-- p63 -->
The essence of this message is that difficult times will come. People will be so self-absorbed that in spite of all their ‘education’ they will not be under sound doctrine, will deny the power of God, and will walk with a corrupt and base mind. Paul urges Timothy to be well prepared to preach sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples we study and place ourselves under the discipline of sound doctrine; this provides protection against the wickedness of the world; we become fully developed and mature men and women of God, able to defend ourselves against the snares of the enemy and to protect those around us who have not yet reached full maturity.

<!-- p65 -->
We must be careful and diligent in our study; the sound teaching of the Scriptures can be led astray by many distorting influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can be distorted by the traditions of men. We see this truth clearly from the need for the great Reformers of Protestantism and in the history of the Old Testament. God raised up people to restore those fundamental truths which had been distorted by the traditions of men.

<!-- p79 -->

What are His teachings, what will we study?

<!-- p88 -->
In this work we will primarily study the basic teachings of the New Testament church. These teachings are basic because the Scriptures refer to them as such, but even more because they provide the learner with a firm foundation on which to build their life.

<!-- p89 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p95 -->

<!-- p96 -->

<!-- p97 -->
5.  Laying on of hands

<!-- p98 -->
6.  Resurrection of the dead

<!-- p99 -->
7.  Eternal judgment

<!-- p100 -->
We will also study the fundamental elements of our Christian life:

<!-- p101 -->
- Sanctification by the Holy Spirit

<!-- p102 -->

<!-- p103 -->
- Making disciples

<!-- p104 -->
- Spiritual authority

We will study each of these matters in the context of the Church, and we will study them so that we may ‘move toward perfection (maturity)’. Our study will focus on the various perspectives and aspects of the Godhead as has been revealed to us: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit.
