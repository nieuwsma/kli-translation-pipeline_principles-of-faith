# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Lasting Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? How did it come to be so? How do we know? How can we be certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond a surface-level desire merely to be right; it reaches to a desire to know with depth and precision, and to clearly express the new understanding that has been gained.

<!-- p3 -->
Though communication is common, effective and precise communication is challenging. To communicate a message effectively, the author and proponent must have an idea. But a lone idea is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are vague, fluid, and easily displaced.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to transform the raw idea from a hazy shape into a precise one. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us distinguish and separate what the idea is and what it is not. Now our shaped idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether long-form text, like this book, or short-form text, like a social media post, or an oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium carries that now well-developed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Thus far, we have spoken only of the proponent and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must correctly receive, decode, and understand the message. The transmitted and received messages should closely match one another.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human achievement is a byproduct of effective communication. The labor of one person can yield only limited fruit. To truly build or accomplish something great, many humans (speaking generally of humankind here) must labor together with a shared purpose and a shared idea. Apart from naturally occurring disasters (such as a volcanic eruption), we also know that the greatest catastrophes that have occurred so far have often happened largely because communication was absent, broken, or distorted. Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build a person, a family, a community, a nation—or to destroy them with equal force. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will be, how we will exist. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our own lives but, to a degree, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must properly establish our beliefs and also learn to share them effectively. Jesus said the same to His disciples when He said:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> And Jesus came and said to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians desire a more resolute faith, but lack a clear foundation upon which to build it. Without solid doctrines, we do not have the strong foundation we need to stand firm; we risk being swayed by false teachings, discouraged in trials, or left unprepared for the work to which God has called us. This book is designed to equip you with the basic teachings of Scripture-truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a life filled with the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be “*rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith*” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow as mature, Spirit-led believers, we first need deep roots in God's truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is more than mere information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings of this book are to educate and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament Church. My hope is that these beliefs will assure and inspire you on your journey of faith to put down deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings have been formed and shaped by my experience in the Church, particularly in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The composition of the teachings in this book is based on my own personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, during most of which I have been an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children's ministry, evangelism and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture as they walk in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small-group leaders** - those who are preparing others to make disciples, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip the believer in both **personal** and **practical** ways, making it ideal for personal growth as well as group teaching. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your spiritual journey with God or to train others, the material is structured for easy application and Holy Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to use this book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence, beginning with foundational truths like **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and progressing to the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic is standalone but also builds on previous chapters, making it easy to move forward progressively or to focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides material for preparing lessons, leading discussions, and effectively making disciples of others. To deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience, it includes personal reflection questions, group discussion questions (for facilitation), group activities, and word studies.

<!-- p25 -->
## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source of sound doctrine: the teachings of the Holy Spirit, inspired and written in Scripture, and entrusted to us through the teachings and tradition of the Church. This book will take the essential teachings, present them in an easy-to-understand format, and conclude each chapter with personal reflection, questions for group facilitation, and optional group activities.

After Jesus’ ministry, many eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical matters within the early Christian communities. These writings, which were divinely inspired, were carefully written, collected, and preserved over the centuries, and together formed what is now known as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
Generally, we will use the NKJV translation, but where another translation makes a passage clearer, we may use that as well. *To aid reading, Scripture references will be in italics.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly suggest that, where possible, you use multiple translations, so that the concept becomes clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful, not for their strict textual precision, but because they help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures are communicating.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore some key Bible terms (**word studies**) so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic Bible resources such as E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources will also give you access to Strong's Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong's Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original language, so that we can hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated in this book as OT) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated in this book as NT) was written in Greek. Strong's numbers will be represented as follows: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are neither new nor unique. We should be skeptical of ‘new’—never before seen or heard—teachings related to our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a compilation and explanation of teachings that have existed from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparison of Christian denominations. Pick up any two books on theology written by different authors with different denominational backgrounds and you will see great harmony in many parts of their work, yet you will also find notable differences. I will not claim infallibility, but I will certainly say that this work is well-studied, peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgments section), and well-supported by Scripture and by the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

I urge you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to internalizing them.
