# Chapter 14 - Stay Awake

<!-- p1 -->
We have been given Christ’s spiritual authority for the establishment of His kingdom on earth, so we must work diligently and with readiness, because Jesus is going to return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the end times is called **Eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *most of them disagree with each other*! Specifically, they disagree over interpretations (allegorical, literal, symbolic), and over the order and timing of events in Scripture. I am reminded of the saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonnke—“I will split hairs in heaven after I have broken chains on earth!”

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, I hesitate to go very deep into this highly disputed topic, because this book is mainly focused on the basics of the faith. I will limit myself to the highest-level view of the events described in Scripture and focus on the most important thing—**the same** **gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally agreed points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus will return

<!-- p6 -->
2.  Resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will review what the concept is, look at the reference scriptures, consider why it is important, and what we should do in light of it.

<!-- p12 -->

<!-- p28 -->
</span>

<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p33 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p34 -->
- **Be prepared:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness toward God.

<!-- p35 -->
- **Share the gospel:** Help people be prepared for the resurrection of life by leading them to Christ.

### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers will appear before the **judgment seat of Christ** for reward, while unbelievers will face the **Great White Throne judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away. And no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and dreadful final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for believers.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The judgment seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers, our names are written in the **Lamb's *Book of Life*** *(see the book of *Revelation*)**.** Therefore we will stand* *before the **judgment seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for punishment, but for reward! Again**,** it is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. It reminds us that our lives matter-not only for today but for **eternity**.

>
> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive according to what he has done while in the body, whether good or bad.
>

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

In this judgment we will receive rewards for the good works we have done! Every person present at this judgment has lived and died as a believer, therefore their eternal salvation is assured. However, not everyone will receive further rewards. The New Testament mentions crowns as rewards that represent victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns highlight God's recognition of the faithfulness and service of believers in the kingdom of God.

**What should we do?**

<!-- p60 -->

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For completeness, if you are curious about the common fault lines in eschatology, here is a **very** brief summary of the most disputed points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture:** Pre-, mid-, or post-tribulation? There is disagreement about when the rapture will occur in relation to the tribulation. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of trial?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial reign:** Is it literal **(premillennial)**, symbolic **(amillennial)**, or spiritual **(postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion:** Occurs after the millennium (literal event or metaphor?).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is excellent, but as D. L. Browning said, "Always keep the main thing the main thing!"

## **Final thoughts**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the culmination of God’s plan and the very reason we live and labor with readiness and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to stay awake and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the rewards of crowns or through the hope of the resurrection, our motivating reason is clear-Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**On that glorious day, may we be found ready, faithful, and waiting in hope!**
