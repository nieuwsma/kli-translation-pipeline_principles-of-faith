# Epilogue

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, my hope and prayer for you, dear reader, is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you nearer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit-this is the framework on which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If I could leave you with one thing, it is this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths described in this book are not meant to be read once and set aside; they are given to be lived daily. The journey of following Christ is a journey of continual learning, refinement, and transformation. We move from faith to faith, from glory to glory, ever pressing forward to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
It may be that some of your questions remain unanswered. You may feel that you have only touched the surface of these deep and eternal truths. That is a good place. Let those questions lead you deeper into the Word of God, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter-when you seek Him, He will lead you into all truth.
