# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the core learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity should take about 15–20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. With questions for personal self-reflection and group discussion, these activities are best used in a group study format to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Scripture Matching

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce key biblical concepts about doctrine by studying scriptures that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** into **pairs or small groups** of 3–4 people each.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign scriptures** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the word and defend against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Have each group do the following:

<!-- p13 -->
- Read **their assigned scripture** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- In 1–2 sentences, state a **summary of the key teaching** about doctrine.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** of how this teaching applies to guarding against falsehood or helping others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Have groups take **2 minutes** each to present their scripture, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life situations** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend says to you, “It really doesn’t matter what we believe, as long as we are sincere.” How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who is hearing conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. Which Scripture verses could you share to help clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops attending church because they feel that as long as they believe in God, going to church is unnecessary. How would you encourage them from Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - Groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 Scripture verses** that address this issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - Groups take turns presenting their scenario and response to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Teach participants to **distinguish truth from error** on the basis of Scripture.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Prepare the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud one by one. Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate the discussion** - Give groups time to discuss disagreements or find verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Only faith saves us.* *(Correct - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *If we are sincere, all paths lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name That Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect God’s names and attributes to practical life applications.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - On each card write **one name or attribute of God** (e.g., Yahweh-Yireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** given in the chapter and the accompanying **scripture reference**.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply in a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Conclusion** - Have each participant or group **present their thoughts**, emphasizing how God’s character applies practically in real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants so that they can meditate on one of the names of God given in the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that relates to the current need (e.g., **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that name, write a short **prayer**, praise God, and ask Him to act according to His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share prayers in small groups or pairs (optional) to **encourage and build faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **End with group prayer** - Thank God that He reveals Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Experiencing the attributes of God (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants **reflect on and apply** the attributes of God through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on an attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Give each group a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God taken from the chapter (e.g., **faithful, merciful, sovereign, provider**).

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that best resonates with their current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story sharing (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares a story** (personal experience or observation) that connects to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Example prompts:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When have you experienced God as your provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer** reflecting that attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God's character in your circumstances.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Salvation

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
To illustrate how the **effects of sin ripple outward**, impacting **relationships, communities, and spiritual life**, while emphasizing God's **redemptive plan**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop **a stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to observe the **ripples spreading outward** and discuss:  
      *“How does this scene illustrate the impact of sin in our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each a **biblical example** of sin:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What “waves” of sin have you personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God's grace worked to stop those waves and bring healing?*

2.  **Group Task:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss **sin or judgment** in that passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or redemption** presented.

<!-- p117 -->

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Have each group share their insights, linking the passages to build a **timeline of God's plan** for redemption.

### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
To guide participants to **personally embrace forgiveness**-**both** **receiving it** and **extending it to others**।

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **quietly reflect** on the areas where they:

<!-- p126 -->
- Where they need to **confess sin** and ask for God's forgiveness।

<!-- p127 -->
- Where they need to **forgive someone else** who has wronged them।

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Guide participants in private prayer। The facilitator does not need to listen to the conversation between the person and God।

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sin before God।

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the strength to forgive others।

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage them to write down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation)。

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (optional) **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**।

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-reflection and group discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives, and replace them with Holy Spirit-inspired actions.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Give participants a **list of potential dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** in their lives where they see evidence of dead works.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of potential dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services out of habit without engaging from the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Attempting to attain righteousness through personal efforts rather than relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to obtain favor, ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful habits or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Harboring grudges or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Fruitless Habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions Motivated by Fear or Guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of a mere sense of duty rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering merely to fulfill a formality, rather than joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced Priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing more on material success than on spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so that they (if comfortable) share one area they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to replace dead works with Spirit-filled works.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key learnings or the steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer for God’s guidance for a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->

<!-- p245 -->
#### Instructions:
