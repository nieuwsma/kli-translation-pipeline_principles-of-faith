# Chapter 14 - Be Watchful

<!-- p1 -->
Having received the spiritual authority of Christ on Earth to establish His Kingdom, we must work diligently and urgently, because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the last days is called eschatology. Many books have been written on eschatology, and most of them disagree with one another! In particular, they differ regarding interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), as well as the order and timing of events in the Scriptures. It brings to mind a saying by the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonnke - “I’ll split hairs in heaven when I’m finished breaking chains on Earth!”

<!-- p3 -->
As the author, I hesitate to delve into this hotly debated topic, given that this book is focused primarily on the basics of the faith. I will mostly stick to the broadest view of the events described in Scripture and focus on what matters most—the mission of the Gospel!

<!-- p4 -->
## Commonly recognized points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus is returning

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A New Heaven and a New Earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will consider what it is, look at the relevant passages of Scripture, think about why it matters, and what we should do in light of it.

<!-- p12 -->

<!-- p13 -->
**What it is:** **The Second Coming of Christ**—the literal return of Jesus to the earth as King and Judge.

>

<!-- p16 -->
</span>

<!-- p17 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

</span>

<!-- p20 -->
**Why this matters:** The return of Christ fulfills God's promise to complete redemption and establish His kingdom. It reminds us to live with urgency, knowing that one day we will see Him face to face.

<!-- p21 -->

<!-- p22 -->
- **Be watchful:** Live in expectation of His return.

<!-- p23 -->
- **Remain faithful:** Fulfill the Great Commission (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>) with purpose and diligence.

<!-- p24 -->

<!-- p25 -->
**What it is:** **Resurrection** — is the raising of all people — both the righteous and the unrighteous — from the dead to stand for **judgment**.

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="28-29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> Do not marvel at this; for the hour is coming in which all who are in the graves will hear His voice
>
> and come forth — those who have done good, to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of condemnation.
>

<!-- p28 -->
</span>

<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p30 -->
> **<u>1 Thessalonians 4:16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first.
>

<!-- p31 -->
</span>

<!-- p32 -->
**Why this matters:** The resurrection guarantees justice and eternal life for believers, warning unbelievers of judgment. It points to God's sovereignty over life and death.

<!-- p33 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p34 -->
- **Be ready:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness to God.

<!-- p35 -->
- **Share the Gospel:** Help others prepare for the resurrection of life by bringing them to Christ.

<!-- p36 -->
### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people — believers and unbelievers — will stand before God for judgment. Believers will stand at the **Judgment Seat of Christ** to receive rewards, and unbelievers — at the **Great White Throne Judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face earth and heaven fled away; and there was found no place for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God; and books were opened; and another book was opened, which is the Book of Life; and the dead were judged according to what was written in the books, according to their works.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and terrible final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
Being believers, our names are written in the ** *Book of Life* of the Lamb** *(*See the book of *Revelation)**.** Thus we will appear* *before the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but for reward! Again**,** this is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. This reminds us that our life has significance-not only for today, but also for **eternity**.

<!-- p45 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p46 -->
> **<u>2 Corinthians 5:10</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive according to what he has done in the body, whether good or bad.
>

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p49 -->
> **<u>1 Corinthians 3:12-15</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,
>
> each one's work will become clear; for the Day will declare it, because it is revealed by fire; and the fire will test each one's work, of what sort it is.
>
> If anyone's work which he has built on it endures, he will receive a reward.
>
> If anyone's work is burned, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.
>

<!-- p50 -->
</span>

<!-- p51 -->
At this judgment we will receive rewards for the good deeds we have done! Everyone who is at this judgment lived and died as a believer, therefore his eternal salvation is guaranteed. However, not everyone will have an additional reward. In the New Testament crowns are mentioned as rewards, representing victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns emphasize God's recognition of believers' faithfulness and their service in His Kingdom.

<!-- p52 -->
Rewards:

<!-- p53 -->
1.  **The Crown of Righteousness** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:8</span>)* - For those who long for Christ's appearing and live godly lives.

<!-- p54 -->
2.  **The Imperishable Crown** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-25" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 9:24-25</span>)* - For believers who practice self-control and faithfulness.

<!-- p55 -->
3.  **The Crown of Life** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JAS" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">James 1:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Revelation 2:10</span>)* - For those who endure trials, persecutions, and temptations.

<!-- p56 -->
4.  **The Crown of Glory** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Peter 5:2-4</span>)* - For leaders and shepherds who faithfully shepherd God's people.

<!-- p57 -->
5.  **The Crown of Rejoicing** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Thessalonians 2:19-20</span>)* - For those who bring others to Christ and make disciples.

<!-- p58 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p59 -->
- **Live with purpose:** Work diligently, knowing that our efforts for the Kingdom have eternal significance.

<!-- p60 -->
- **Examine ourselves:** Make sure that we are living a life of faith and obedience.

<!-- p61 -->
### **4. Eternal Destiny**

<!-- p62 -->
**What it is:** All people will spend eternity in one of two places — eternal life with God or eternal separation from Him.

<!-- p63 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="46" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p64 -->
> **<u>Matthew 25:46</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And these will go away into everlasting punishment, but the righteous into eternal life."
>

<!-- p65 -->
</span>

<!-- p66 -->
**Why this is important:** Our eternal destiny is determined by whether we have received Christ or rejected Him. This underscores the urgency of preaching the Gospel and discipleship.

<!-- p67 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p68 -->
- **Proclaim salvation:** Boldly share the Gospel to bring others to eternal life.

<!-- p69 -->
- **Live with eternity in mind:** Focus on what has eternal significance — faith, obedience, and love.

<!-- p70 -->
### **5. The Defeated Enemy**

<!-- p71 -->
**What it is:** Satan, the deceiver, will be cast into the **lake of fire** — forever defeated by Christ.

<!-- p72 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p73 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:10</u>** (NKJV)
>
> The devil, who deceived them, was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet are, and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.
>

<!-- p74 -->
</span>

<!-- p75 -->
**Why this is important:** This final act demonstrates God’s sovereignty over evil and assures believers that Satan’s reign — of deception and destruction — has ended. It gives us confidence in God’s ultimate victory.

<!-- p76 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p77 -->
- **Stand firm:** Resist the enemy’s lies, knowing that he is already defeated.

<!-- p78 -->
- **Walk in authority:** Live with boldness and faith, knowing that God has already won the victory.
