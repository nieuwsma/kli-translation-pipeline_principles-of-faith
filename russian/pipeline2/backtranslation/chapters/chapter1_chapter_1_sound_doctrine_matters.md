# Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Matters

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine simply means the core truths we believe and teach—truths handed down by God’s faithful people, rooted in Scripture, and expressed in our everyday lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His church to “teach,” He commanded them to share His teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.” Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus calls all of us to learn His teaching and reproduce it in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the Way, the Truth, and the Life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), fully embodying God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation. This embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). The structured teaching of Jesus is His doctrine (***didaskalia***); by keeping (obeying, keeping, guarding) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in all of history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. Only he understands the perfect design of a solid, unshakable structure. His understanding is not borrowed from others—it is inherent to Himself. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sturdy building has its origin in His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the full and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring His design to life, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—a structured plan that contains the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to erect a building that will stand. Without this blueprint, no one could build correctly. This blueprint represents the structured teaching of Jesus, His doctrine (***didaskalia***)—the foundation through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also gives **precise instructions**, detailing how each element of the structure must be assembled, where each beam should be installed, and how everything must align with His perfect plan. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)- actionable directives that guide obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps for the building to stand firm, in accordance with the original blueprint. To ignore or alter His commandments is to jeopardize the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathers a group of **apprentices**—those who desire to learn from Him, build under His guidance, and one day teach others to erect buildings that will stand. These apprentices carefully study His blueprint, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully apply His directives on the job. They build not by their own ideas, but according to the master’s design. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)- those who observe, obey, and internalize the teaching of Jesus, conforming their lives to His Word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The longer they learn from the architect, the more skillful they become, and eventually they teach and train others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
The teaching of Jesus is the foundation of our faith! He is the chief architect who has the perfect design and blueprint. Only by studying the blueprints, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and building a strong working relationship will we be able to construct a life that will stand in the storms.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, They labor in vain who build it; Unless the Lord guards the city, The watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand up to hardships, challenges, tribulations, persecution, or simply everyday life if we have not built them on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone is not enough for a person to become healthy and physically fit. One must continue to train with discipline to receive all the benefits of membership. In the same way, it is not enough merely to attend church or to call ourselves Christians in word; all of us need to be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith—and only then will we experience all the benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus' commission extends beyond ourselves. It is good that we become whole, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must also teach, instruct, equip, lead, and make disciples, bringing others to faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and become disciplined? To build our lives on the Rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is more than just an advantage—it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the Rock, we are building on sand, and a strong storm will destroy our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. The Book of Proverbs teaches that as a person thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral—we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the Rock so that we can stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continuing to abide in His word (His logos)! The result of abiding in His word is our knowing the truth, which makes us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples trained in discipline can carry out the Teacher's commands. Every person in the New Testament church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be a disciple -\> subject yourself to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to submit to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the Rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don't worry, we don't have to go through this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit, who will lead us and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His written Word in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders to build us up, make us mature, keep us safe, and organize us for good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect Coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us specialists who, from their own experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and in time you too will be able to become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p41 -->
God gave the church these special gifts to bring us to maturity, so that we can and are:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to labor and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound doctrine we will be <u>children, tossed to and fro and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children are by nature:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to discern good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - concerned with the temporal and the present moment.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - vulnerable to harm.

### Sound doctrine provides protection from the ungodliness of the world

<!-- p54 -->
We do not have the luxury of being spiritually neutral in this life. We are influenced either by the Kingdom of God or by the Kingdom of Darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul instructed his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, having itching ears, they will heap up for themselves teachers;
>
> and they will turn their ears away from the truth and be turned aside to

> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days perilous times will come:
>
> For men will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,
>
> without love, irreconcilable, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, despisers of good,

> traitors, headstrong, haughty, lovers of pleasure rather than God,

The essence of this message is that dangerous times will come. People will be so absorbed in themselves that, despite all their ‘learning’, they will not submit to sound doctrine, will deny the power of God, and will walk with a corrupt and incorrigible mind. Paul urges Timothy to be well prepared to preach sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples, we study and submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine; this serves as a protection from the malice of the world; we become grown, mature men and women of God, able to defend ourselves from the snares of the enemy and to protect those around us who have not yet reached full maturity.

<!-- p65 -->
We must be careful and diligent in our training: the sound teaching of the Scriptures can be thrown off course by several perverted influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can be distorted by the traditions of men. We see this truth in the example of the necessity of the great reformers of Protestantism and in the history of the Old Testament. God raised up people to restore the foundational truth that had been perverted by the traditions of men.
