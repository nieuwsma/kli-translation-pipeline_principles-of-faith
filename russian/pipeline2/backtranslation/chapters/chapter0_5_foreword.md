# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For everything to function effectively, a solid system and structure are necessary. Without them, discord and chaos easily arise. Therefore, establishing a solid foundation in doctrine is extremely important in order to live fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. Beginning with the very first chapter, which explores the importance of sound doctrine, and continuing through more than 300 pages filled with instruction and exercises about the church and our roles in it, as well as about understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an exceptional tool in classes for the training and preparation of leaders with a solid foundation in doctrine, and it will also be extremely useful for individual readers.
