# End-of-Chapter Assignments

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter assignments are designed to reinforce key learning objectives. Each assignment takes approximately 15–20 minutes to complete; additional resources not included in this book may be required. Alongside questions for personal reflection and group discussion, these assignments are best carried out in a group study format to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these assignments as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and modify the assignments as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 — Sound Doctrine Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Assignment 1: Comparing Scripture Passages

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Strengthen key biblical understandings of doctrine by exploring Scripture passages that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or small groups** of 3–4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Scripture passages** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and established in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1–4</span>** - *Preaching the Word and protection against false teaching.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11–16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound doctrine.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Let each group:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read their assigned Scripture passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Formulate the main idea** about doctrine in 1–2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real example** of how this doctrine helps guard against lies or helps others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share findings** - Give groups **2 minutes** each to present their passage, a brief summary, and practical application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Assignment 2: Doctrine in Action

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life situations** and reinforce practical ways to respond.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Split into groups** - form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Distribute the scenarios** - give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you: “It’s not that important what we believe, as long as we’re sincere.” How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are discipling a new believer who is hearing conflicting teachings about salvation by faith and salvation by works. What passages of Scripture could you share to clarify the issue?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend has stopped attending church because they think it’s unnecessary since they believe in God. How would you encourage this person using Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - Groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the root of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 passages of Scripture** that address this issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would instruct the person in this scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share your answers** - Groups take turns presenting their scenarios and responses to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Task 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p33 -->
Teach participants to **distinguish truth from error**, using Scripture as the foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Prepare the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud one by one. Some statements are **biblical truths**, and others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Give the groups time to discuss disagreements or find verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Only faith saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All roads lead to God if we are sincere.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Task 1: Name This Attribute (Interactive group session)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God with practical application in life.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare cards** - On each card write **one name or attribute of God** (for example, Yahweh-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Task for the group:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** and the accompanying **Scripture reference** from the chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Wrap-up** - Have each participant or group **present their reflections**, highlighting practical ways God’s character applies to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Task 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants to incorporate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Organize time for reflection** - Provide participants with a **quiet space** so they can reflect on one of the names of God from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that corresponds to the current need (for example, **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Write a short **prayer** using this name, praising God and asking Him to act in accordance with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs to **encourage and strengthen faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Conclude with a group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Session 3: Encounter with the Attributes of God (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants **understand and apply** the attributes of God in their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on attributes:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God (for example, **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**) taken from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that they believe resonates most with their current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Sharing stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares a story** (a personal experience or observation) that is related to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Example prompts:

<!-- p79 -->
- *«When have you experienced God as your Provider?»*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God’s mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in everyday life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer**, reflecting this attribute, and pray together, focusing on applying God’s character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Assignment 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (discussion and application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Goal:**

<!-- p87 -->
To show how **the consequences of sin ripple outward**, affecting **relationships, communities, and spiritual life**, while emphasizing God’s **redemptive plan**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Introductory Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop **a stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if the materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples on the water**, moving outward, and discuss:  
      *“How does this image illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term consequences of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God’s grace and the possibility of restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What «echoes» of sin have you personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace acted to stop these «echoes» and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

#### Objective:

Trace God’s **redemptive plan** from **Genesis to Christ**, focusing on His **grace** amid **judgment**.

<!-- p107 -->

<!-- p109 -->

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

2.  **Group Task:**

<!-- p115 -->

<!-- p116 -->

<!-- p117 -->

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->

### Assignment 3: Living in Forgiveness (prayerful application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p122 -->
Help participants to **personally experience forgiveness**—how to **receive it** and **extend it** to others.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **quietly reflect** on areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God’s forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Guide participants in personal prayer. It isn’t necessary for the leader to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g., to forgive someone, to pursue reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (if they wish) how **forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Session 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Examination and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
To help participants identify and eliminate “dead works” in their lives, replacing them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a **list of potential dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** where they see evidence of dead works in their lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of potential dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending worship services out of habit, without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts for approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Relying on one’s own strength:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to achieve righteousness by one’s own efforts instead of relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to earn favor while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful patterns or behavior:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Harboring grudges or unforgiveness.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing passion for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending an excessive amount of time on distracting activities or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Works driven by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of a sense of duty rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Donating money or volunteering just to check a box, rather than out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success at the expense of spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so they can share (if they are comfortable) one area they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to **replace dead works** with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Final reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer for God’s guidance in living a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->
### Session 2: The Path of Repentance (interactive visualization and discussion)

<!-- p216 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->
Help participants understand the steps of repentance and how they lead to transformation.

<!-- p218 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p219 -->
1.  **Create a visual pathway:**
