# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest post by Pastor Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) supplements our study of the role of the Holy Spirit by offering practical guidance on how to hear the voice of God. The following guest material is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning I asked **God** the following question: “What is the most important thing I could teach Your people for the times that are coming upon us?” And He answered me very quickly, and I felt that it was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me such direction, I always take a notebook and a pencil and begin to write down the little that I know, or what I think I know, and the Scripture passages that confirm it. I love to do studies on topics in the Word that the Lord leads me to teach. I love the anointing for preaching, but most of all I love to teach the Word. It is an adventure—finding things that have always been there but have gone unnoticed.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to uncover God’s truths, for he prayed in Psalm 119:18: “Open my eyes, that I may see Wondrous things from Your law.”

<!-- p5 -->
This is what I want to see-Wondrous things from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
While I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on hearing and discerning the voice of God, He said the following to my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them out:  
Out of troubles that could destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Out of the trials they are in,  
Out, into a place of peace, love, and joy, in which only a few have been.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Out, into a place of renewal,  
into a place of Divine ecstasy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Oh yes, teach them to hear My voice son  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points will help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible sound, but a KNOWING in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
Once His Word came so clear and powerful that it could have been audible. I answered Him out loud—it was that real. However, there was no one there except the Lord and me, so I cannot say for certain whether He spoke to me the way we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was a time when Jesus was being baptized in the Jordan River, and the Father said: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some of those present said that it sounded like thunder, so I suppose He could have spoken to me with an audible voice, but for now I want you to understand: when I say that God spoke to me, it is more of a KNOWING in my heart than a sound in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>This KNOWING is NOT built by the logic of your mind; however, it is a complete thought or understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
Never has the Lord said anything to me that confused me. Never has He said anything to me that left me with the question ‘why.’ It is always a KNOWING with full UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us to fill in the gaps. In the twinkling of an eye God can fill your heart with enough information to last you a lifetime of preaching. One touch from God can fill you with a fire that will burn in you forever. He is the perfect communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He NEVER DRIVES. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Taskmasters drive, but Shepherds lead. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He NEVER CONVICTS; He CONVINCES.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who have been Born Again (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5) by their confession of faith in **Jesus’** shed blood and His work on Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who is constantly convicting us of our mistakes? I believe that most of **God’s** children are afraid to hear **God** speak to them because they have been told that **God** will convict them of this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who, as it says there, convicts us or condemns us? It is OUR SPIRIT, or heart, that condemns a child of **God**, but **God the Holy Spirit**, who knows everything, is GREATER than our heart. He is here to be our Comforter. Read the following Scriptures, and this will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and **God’s** love. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our convict-er, our Counselor, not our punish-er, our Advocate and Helper, not our hinder-er. Read also <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (**God’s** voice) helps, comforts, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>God’s voice can be tested so that you can KNOW it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> commands us to “test every spirit,” so that we can be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing God, you have the right and responsibility to ask that voice a question (This is the testing or proving of the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Has Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The one thing familiar spirits will never acknowledge is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully Man and fully God. No false spirit or religion will acknowledge that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed mankind from all their sins forever. Test the voice you hear. You should also know that God will never lead you beyond His written Word, the Bible. If you hear something that cannot be found in the Bible, you are not hearing God. Get rid of it! Test the voice you hear to make sure it is from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said that He will always speak only what He hears, and He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to glorify your ministry, your works, or church programs. All of that is good, and much of it is necessary for what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If the voice you hear glorifies you, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage and strengthen you whenever He speaks to you, even when He corrects (directs) you with the Word. But glory is when you believe that YOU YOURSELF are somebody. You are nothing without Jesus-HE IS THE ONE, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>God’s voice will always REVEAL something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. In the Amplified translation, when considering the word “reveal,” the words added are: declare, disclose, and transmit. **God** always discloses something to us after He declares it to us, and then, through your faith, He transmits it to us. Study also <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never troubles us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace comes because there is KNOWLEDGE that gives us confidence before God that overflows into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to troubles you, you are not hearing God-turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives, but as He gives us through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His constant abiding (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “the Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH and therefore will speak only TRUTH. People have said to me something like this: “I can’t believe that what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And perhaps it is too good to be true; that’s why you MUST test the spirit by comparing it with the Word of God to be sure it is Truth. Some simply can’t believe it because what they hear is always positive, and they were taught that God convicts us of our mistakes. God will speak only TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that most people hear from the voice they listen to only bad news and never expect to hear good, so when the good comes, they question it as possible un-truth. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** makes us free, so how can **truth** be anything but good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always LEADS US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deut. 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never brings you down! He will never humiliate you; He only edifies you. When God speaks to you, He speaks from the position that everything the Word says about you is already accomplished and ACCEPTED by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you **GREAT AND MIGHTY** things that you do not know.” Does that sound like putting you down, or like gloom and doom? There is NO gloom and doom in God, so He cannot say anything gloomy or dismal to you. For gloom and doom you’ll have to tune in to the world news agencies.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS good thoughts about us—of peace, the future, and hope—why would He speak otherwise? The answer is, HE WOULD NOT, and HE DOES NOT! He leads us only UP and FORWARD with great hope, to grant us the expected future.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this will prompt you to “call upon Him” and learn to **hear His voice**. He has great things He wants to tell you, but He needs your attentive ear before all His blessings can bring you any benefit.
