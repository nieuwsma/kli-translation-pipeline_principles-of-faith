# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Laying a Strong Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How are we certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond a surface-level desire to be right, but is about knowing deeply and precisely and being able to clearly articulate the new understanding gained.

<!-- p3 -->
Though communication is common, effective and accurate communication is a challenge. To convey a message effectively, the author and initiator must have an idea. However, an idea by itself is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are blurry, fluid, and easily shifted.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to transform a raw idea from vaguely outlined to precisely formed. Words set boundaries for our idea, helping to discern and separate what the idea is and what it is not. Now our shaped idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether a long form, like this book, a short form, like a social media post, or an oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium conveys the now well-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
So far we have spoken only of the initiator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must receive, decode, and properly understand the message. The message sent and the message received should match as closely as possible.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human endeavor is a byproduct of effective communication. The labor of one person can only achieve so much. To truly build or accomplish something great, many people (speaking here of humanity in general) must work together with a shared purpose and a shared idea. Excluding natural disasters (for example, a volcanic eruption), we also know that the greatest calamities that have ever occurred usually happened largely because of missing, broken, or distorted communication. Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—or just as readily to destroy them. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will be, how we will exist. Since belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in being. Our convictions shape not only our own lives but, to some extent, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must precisely establish our beliefs and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said the same when He spoke to His disciples:

> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today desire a stronger faith, but they lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without sound doctrine, we do not have the solid foundation needed to stand firm; we risk being swept away by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or found unprepared for the work to which God has called us. This book is intended to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—truths that will strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be "*rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith*" (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God's truth. That is why doctrine matters. It is more than information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are intended to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. I hope these convictions will persuade and inspire your walk of faith to put down deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are shaped and informed by my experience in the church, especially in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The body of teachings in this book draws on my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and my experience of nearly two decades of participation in ministry, a significant portion of that time as an ordained minister. My experience includes youth and children's ministry, evangelistic and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and administration in the church and ministry.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who seek to strengthen their faith or to instruct others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - Those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who seek to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture, walking the path of greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - Those who are preparing to mentor others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, making it ideal for personal growth as well as group learning. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to teach others, the content is structured for easy application and growth under the Spirit’s leading.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to use this book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence, beginning with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving to the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic is self-contained, but also builds on the previous chapters, making it easy to go through the material step by step or to focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals** this book serves as a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For **teachers** it provides material for lesson preparation, leading discussions, and effectively mentoring others. Included are questions for personal reflection, questions to facilitate group discussion, group activities, and word studies that will help deepen understanding and prompt practical steps of obedience.

## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given the primary source of sound doctrine—the instruction of the Holy Spirit, inspired and recorded in Scripture, handed down through the teaching and tradition of the church. This book will take the core teachings, present them in an understandable form, and conclude each chapter with personal reflection, questions for leading a group, and optional group assignments.

After the ministry of Jesus, numerous eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters that addressed doctrinal and practical issues in the early Christian communities. These writings, God-breathed, were carefully recorded, collected, and preserved over the centuries, forming what is now known as the Bible.

I strongly recommend using multiple translations when possible to ensure that the thought is clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find translations such as the Amplified Bible helpful, not because of their sheer textual precision, but because they help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures convey.

The teachings presented by me in this book are not new or original. We should be skeptical of ‘new,’ never-before-seen and unheard-of teachings concerning our Christian faith. On the contrary, this book — a collection and explanation of teachings that have been present from the very beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of Church history and does not present a comparison of Christian denominations. Take any two books on theology written by different authors with various denominational affiliations, and, although you will see great agreement in many things, you will also notice significant disagreements. I will not claim infallibility, but I state that this work is well researched, reviewed by faithful brothers and sisters (see the «Acknowledgments» section), and well supported by Scripture and the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

I urge you to read Scripture for yourself and strive to master it. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I hope that you will be restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
