# Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Is Important

<!-- p1 -->
Teaching is, simply put, the core truths we believe and teach - truths handed down by people faithful to God, rooted in Scripture, and lived out in our daily walk.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His own church to 「teach」, He was commanding them to share His own teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, 「All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.」Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus commands all of us to learn His own teaching and reproduce it in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>）。Jesus perfectly embodies God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation. This embodiment, the Word (Logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14）。Jesus’ structured teaching is His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and by keeping His commands (to obey, watch, keep)（***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***)。

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine this. There is the greatest architect in history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect design for a structure that will endure and never be shaken. His understanding is not derived from anyone else but is inherently His own. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sound building finds its origin in His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos - the complete and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>）。

<!-- p10 -->
To realize this design, the architect creates **detailed blueprints** - a structured plan. They contain the principles, proportions, and foundational truths needed for the building to keep standing firmly. Without these blueprints, no one can build correctly. These blueprints represent Jesus’ structured teaching—His doctrine (***didaskalia***), a framework by which God’s truth is understood and applied（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>）。His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge. It is the means by which His wisdom is transmitted, preserved, and passed on, enabling all who follow Him to build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, blueprints alone are not enough. The architect also provides **precise procedures** for how each part of the structure should be assembled, where each beam should be placed, and how every element should be aligned with His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commands **(Entolē**）, actionable directives that guide obedience（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>）。His commands are not arbitrary rules. They are the necessary steps that ensure the building stands strong and continues to align with the original blueprints. To ignore or alter His commands is to compromise the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathering a group of **apprentices**. They desire to learn from him, to build under his guidance, and eventually to teach others how to construct buildings that endure. The apprentices study the blueprints carefully, listen attentively to his instructions, and faithfully apply his commands to their work. They do not build according to their own ideas but according to the design of their master, the architect. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**）—those who observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teaching and align their way of life with His word（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>）。The more they learn under the architect, the more skilled they become, and eventually they go on to teach and train others in his ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we learn the teaching?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the bedrock of our faith！He is the skilled architect with the perfect design and blueprints. Only by studying the blueprints, following the architect’s specific instructions, and building a strong partnership can we build a life that endures the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, the labor of those who build it is in vain. Unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman’s watch is in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
When faced with difficulties, challenges, tribulation, persecution, or simply everyday life, if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith, we cannot expect to keep standing strong.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership card alone does not make a person healthy and fit. To receive the full benefits of being a member, one must continue training at the gym. In the same way, we must not merely attend church or casually claim to be Christians. All of us must be educated, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith. Only then will we truly experience the blessings of membership！

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ mission does not stop with us alone. It is wonderful for us to become complete, healthy, trained, and obedient to God, but at the same time, we must teach, train, equip, guide, and raise others as disciples to lead them to faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we learn doctrine and become trained? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is more than a mere benefit; it is also a warning to us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we will end up building on sand, and fierce storms will smash our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs govern our actions and destiny. Proverbs teaches that as a person thinks in his heart, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral—we are all building on something. Jesus is calling us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who believed Him, "If you continue in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you shall know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continuing to abide in His word (His logos)! As a result of continuing in His word, we come to know the truth, and that truth sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
A disciple who has been trained can carry out the Master’s commands. Everyone in the New Testament church is called to the following:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become a disciple -\> Submit yourself to training in sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> Teach others to submit to training in sound doctrine.

### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry; you don’t have to go through this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us the Holy Spirit, who leads us and guides us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His word recorded in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given spiritually mature and gifted leaders who help build us up, mature us, keep us safe, and equip us to engage in good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect Coach who knows exactly what we need. The Lord has given specialists who, through experience, know how to help us grow and mature. In time, you can become one of them too!

<!-- p49 -->

<!-- p50 -->
