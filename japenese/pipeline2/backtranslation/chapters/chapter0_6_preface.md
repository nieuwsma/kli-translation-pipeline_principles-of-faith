# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building an Enduring Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have long been captivated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How can we be certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This captivation goes beyond a desire to be superficially right; it moves toward knowing deeply and precisely, and being able to articulate newly gained understanding clearly.

<!-- p3 -->
Communication is common, but effective and accurate communication is difficult. To convey a message effectively, the originator and author must have an idea. Yet that idea by itself is like water on a hard surface—its contours and boundaries are indistinct, fluid, and easily stirred up.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and turns of phrase to transform raw ideas from a vague form into a precise one. Words give ideas boundaries and help distinguish and separate what the idea is and what it is not. Our shaped ideas must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether in long form like this book, in short form like a social media post, or in spoken form like a conversation or video, the medium conveys well-formed ideas to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Thus far, we have spoken only of the originator and author of an idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must accurately receive, decode, and understand the message. The message sent and the message received should closely align.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human achievement is a byproduct of effective communication. There is only so much that can be accomplished by the labor of a single person. To build and carry out something truly great, many people (here I mean humanity in general) must work together under a common purpose and common ideas. Except for spontaneous natural disasters (such as volcanic eruptions), we also know that many of the greatest calamities that have ever occurred arose in large part because communication was missing, broken, or distorted. Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build people, families, communities, and nations—and likewise the power to destroy them. What we believe is the foundation for action and the blueprint for what we do, who we become, and how we exist. Because faith is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our own lives but, to some extent, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must learn to establish our faith accurately and to share it effectively. Jesus spoke precisely to this when He said to His disciples,

> And Jesus came to them and said, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p12 -->
Today, many Christians desire a stronger faith, yet they lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without solid doctrine, we lose the firm footing needed to stand, we are shaken by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or left unprepared for the work God has entrusted to us. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—the truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be "*rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith*" (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first take deep root in God’s truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is more than mere information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why This Book, and Who Is It For?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are intended to educate and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament Church. I hope these beliefs will assure and inspire your walk of faith, root you more deeply in Christ, and lead you to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experiences in the church, particularly by charismatic and Pentecostal manifestations. The structure of the teachings in this book is based on my own study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and roughly two decades of involvement in ministry (much of that time as an ordained pastor). My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelistic outreach and revival work, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and the administration of churches and ministries.

<!-- p17 -->
This book will be useful to believers who want to strengthen their own faith or who desire to lead others in spiritual growth through discipleship. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - Those who need a clear foundation to begin their walk as Christians.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who are walking in greater obedience and faith and seeking to further deepen their understanding of God and of Scripture.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - People who are prepared to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, making it ideal for individual growth and group teaching. Whether you use this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, its content is accessible and structured for Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
Each chapter follows a logical progression that begins with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moves into deeper workings such as the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands on its own while also building on the previous chapters, making it easy to progress step by step as needed or focus on particular areas.

<!-- p24 -->
For the **individual**, this book serves as a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For the **teacher**, it provides material for lesson preparation, leading discussions, and discipling others effectively. To deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience, it includes personal reflection questions, discussion questions for group facilitation, group activities, and word studies.

We have been given the primary source of sound doctrine: the teaching inspired by the Holy Spirit and recorded in Scripture, handed down through the teaching and tradition of the Church. This book highlights essential teachings, presents them in an accessible format, and includes personal reflection, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities at the end of each chapter.

The teachings presented in this book are neither new nor original。 We should be skeptical of 「new」 teachings about our Christian faith that we have never seen or heard before。 Rather, this book is a compendium of teachings that have existed from the beginning and commentary on them。

<!-- p68 -->
This book is neither a study of church history nor a comparison of Christian denominations。 If you pick up two theology books by separate authors from different denominational backgrounds、 you will see great harmony in many parts、 while also noticing notable differences。 I do not claim infallibility、 but I do assert that this work has been thoroughly researched、 peer-reviewed by devout men and women（see the Acknowledgments section）、 and well supported by Scripture and the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition。
