# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a solid framework and structure are indispensable. Without them, dysfunction and confusion easily arise. That is why building a strong foundation in doctrine is extremely important in order to live fully as a sound Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of Faith*" will be a powerful help to believers. From the first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining chapters—over 300 pages—filled with teaching and activities concerning the church and our role within it, as well as understanding and recognizing the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It is not only an outstanding tool for teaching, training, and equipping leaders with a solid doctrinal foundation in the classroom, but also highly beneficial for individual readers.
