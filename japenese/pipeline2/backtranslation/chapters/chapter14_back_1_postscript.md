# Afterword

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, my hope and prayer for you, my dear reader, is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit - it is the framework on which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is only one thing I would leave with you, it is this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking by faith. The truths outlined in this book are not meant to be read once and set aside, but to be lived daily. The journey of following Christ is a walk of continual learning, refining, and transformation. We keep moving forward to know the Lord more and more, progressing from faith to faith and from glory to glory.

<!-- p3 -->
Unanswered questions may remain. You may feel that you have only skimmed the surface of these profound and eternal truths. That is fine. Let those questions lead you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter - as you seek the Lord, the Holy Spirit will lead you into all truth.
