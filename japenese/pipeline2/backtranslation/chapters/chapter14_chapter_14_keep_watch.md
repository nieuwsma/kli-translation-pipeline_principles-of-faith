# Chapter 14 - Keep Watch

<!-- p1 -->
Because we have been given Christ's spiritual authority to establish His Kingdom on earth, and because Jesus is coming back, we should work diligently and swiftly!

<!-- p2 -->
The Second Coming of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the Second Coming of Christ and the end times is called **Eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *many of them do not agree with one another*! Specifically, opinions differ regarding interpretation (figurative, literal, symbolic) and the order and timing of the events recorded in Scripture. I am reminded of the words of the great Pentecostal evangelist, Reinhard Bonnke - "When we've finished breaking chains on earth, we'll nitpick in heaven!"

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, given that this book focuses primarily on the basics of the faith, I hesitate to delve very deeply into this hotly debated subject. I will largely remain at the highest-level view of the events depicted in Scripture and focus on what matters most - **that** **gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Points of Universal Agreement:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus will return

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  Defeated enemies

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point, we will identify what the concept is, look at reference verses, consider why it is important, and then reflect on what we should do in light of it.

## Commonly debated points

<!-- p89 -->
For completeness, if you are interested in the common points of divergence in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most frequently debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture:** Before, during, or after the tribulation? Views differ on **when** the rapture occurs in relation to the **tribulation**. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment on Israel, or a symbolic period of suffering?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial reign:** Is it literal **(premillennialism)**, symbolic **(amillennialism)**, or spiritual **(postmillennialism)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion:** Does it occur after the thousand years (a literal event or a metaphor?).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is wonderful, but let us not forget, as D.L. Browning said, "Always keep the main thing the main thing!"

## **Closing words**

<!-- p96 -->
The second coming of Christ is the culmination of God’s plan and the reason we live and work with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the parable of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to stay awake and faithful, always prepared for His return. Whether it is the reward of the crown or the hope of the resurrection, our motivation is clear - Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**On that glorious day, may we be prepared, faithful, and expectant!**
