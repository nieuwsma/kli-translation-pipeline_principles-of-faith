# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest appendix by Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) complements our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit, offering practical insights on how to hear the voice of God. The following guest contribution is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning, I asked **God** the following question: “What is the most important thing I could teach Your people for the times that are coming upon us?” And He answered me very quickly, and I sensed it was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me such direction, I always take a pad and a pencil and begin to write the little that I know, or what I think I know, and the Scriptures that support it. I love doing studies of the Word on topics the Lord leads me to teach. I love the anointing to preach, but I really love teaching the Word. It is an adventure to find things that have always been there but have been overlooked.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to discover the Truths of God, for he prayed in verse 18 of Psalm 119: “Open my eyes, that I may see wonders from Your law.”

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-Wonders of His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on hearing and discerning the voice of God, He spoke the following to my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always set them apart:  
Away from troubles that would destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Away from the trials they are in,  
Away, to a place of peace, love, and joy where few have been.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Away, to a place of refreshing,  
to a place of Divine ecstasy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Oh yes, teach them to hear My voice, son  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible sound but a CERTAINTY in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was a time when His Word came so clear and strong that it could have been audible. I answered Him out loud; it was that real. However, there was no one there but the Lord and me, so I really can’t say for sure whether He spoke to me as we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was the occasion when Jesus was being baptized in the Jordan River and the Father spoke: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some who were there said it sounded like thunder; so I suppose He could have spoken to me audibly, but for now I want you to understand that, when I say God spoke to me, it is a CERTAINTY in my heart more than a sound in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>CERTAINTY is NOT built by logic in your mind; however, it is a complete thought or understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
I have never had the Lord tell me something that confused me. He has never told me anything that left me wondering ‘why.’ It is always a CERTAINTY with COMPLETE UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never lets us fill in the blanks. In the blink of an eye, God can fill your heart with enough information to keep you preaching for a lifetime. One touch from God can fill you with fire that will keep you burning forever. He is a complete communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He NEVER DRIVES. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Cattlemen drive, but Shepherds lead. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He NEVER CONDEMNS US; He CONVICTS US.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who have been Born Again (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5) through their confession of faith in the shed blood of **Jesus** and His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who always condemns us for what we do wrong? I believe that most of the children of **God** are afraid to hear **God** speak to them because they have been told that **God** is going to condemn them for this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who does it say convicts us or condemns us? It is OUR SPIRIT or heart that condemns the child of **God**, but it is **God the Holy Spirit** who knows all things and is GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the following Scriptures, and this will help you understand that the only ones whom the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our condemn-er, our Counselor, not our punisher, our Intercessor and Helper, not our hinderer. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) helps, comforts, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you KNOW it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test every spirit” so that we can be sure it is of God. When you believe you are hearing from God, you have the right and the responsibility to ask that voice a question (That is testing or examining the spirit.) The question you must ask is: “Did Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The only thing that familiar spirits will never admit is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully man and fully God. No false spirit or religion will admit that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed mankind from all their sins for eternity. Test the voice you hear. You should also know that God will never lead you outside His written Word, the Bible. If you are hearing something that cannot be supported by the Bible, you are not hearing God. Get rid of it! Test the voice you hear to see if it is of God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said that He will always speak only what He hears, and He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to bring glory to your ministry or to your works or to church programs. All those things are good, and most of them are necessary to do what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If you are being glorified by the voice you are hearing, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage and strengthen you every time He speaks to you, even when He is correcting (guiding) you with the word. But glory is when you believe that YOU are somebody. You are nobody without Jesus-HE IS THE SOMEONE, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God will always REVEAL something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
See <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified adds the words: declare, reveal, and transmit when considering the word “reveal.” **God** always makes something known to us after declaring it to us, and then, by your faith, He transmits it to us. Also study <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never troubles us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace exists because there is a KNOWING that gives us confidence before God, which translates into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to is disturbing you, you are not hearing from God-turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not like the world gives but like He gives us through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His constant abiding (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “The Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH and therefore will only speak TRUTH! I have heard people say things like: “I can’t believe what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And maybe it is too good to be true; that’s why you MUST test the spirit by lining it up with the Word of God to make sure it is Truth. Some people simply can’t believe it because what they are hearing is always positive, and they have been taught that God convicts us of error. God will only speak TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that most people only hear bad news from the voice they are listening to and never anticipate hearing good news, so when good news comes, they question it as a possible non-truth. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** makes us free, so how could the **truth** be anything other than good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always MOVES US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deut. 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never puts you down! He never despises you; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He is speaking from the frame of reference that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and ACCEPTED by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you GREAT AND MIGHTY things that you do not know.” Does that sound like putting you down or doom and gloom? There is NO doom or gloom in God; therefore, He cannot tell you anything bleak or gloomy. You’ll have to tune in to the world’s news agency for doom and darkness.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the LORD, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS good thoughts of peace, a future, and hope toward us, why would He speak any other way? The answer is, HE WOULD NOT, and HE DOES NOT! He only guides us UPWARD and FORWARD with great hope to provide us with an expected future.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to “cry out to Him” and learn to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs your attentive ear before all His good things can do you any good.
