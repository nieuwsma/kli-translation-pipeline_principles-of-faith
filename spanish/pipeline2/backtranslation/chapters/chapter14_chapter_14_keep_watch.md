# Chapter 14 - Stay alert

<!-- p1 -->
Having been given the spiritual authority of Christ on Earth to establish His kingdom, we must work diligently and with urgency, ¡because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end times is known as **eschatology**. Many books have been written about eschatology, and *most of them do not agree with each other!* Specifically, they disagree on the interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), the order and the timing of the events in the Scriptures. The saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke comes to mind: “I'll get down to splitting hairs in heaven when I'm done breaking chains on Earth”.

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, I am reluctant to delve too deeply into this much-debated topic since this book focuses primarily on the foundations of the faith. I will largely stick to the high-level view of the events described in Scripture and focus on what matters most: **the** **Gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally accepted points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus will return

<!-- p6 -->
2.  Resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  New Heaven and New Earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will review what the concept is, look at the reference Scriptures, consider why it matters, and what we should do in light of it.

<!-- p12 -->

<!-- p29 -->

<!-- p33 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p34 -->
- **Be prepared:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness to God.

<!-- p35 -->

### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people-believers and unbelievers-will face judgment before God. Believers appear before the **Judgment Seat of Christ** to receive rewards, while unbelievers face the **Great White Throne Judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away. And no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and the books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things that were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and terrible final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers, our names have been placed in the ***Book of Life* of the Lamb** *(See the book of *Revelation)**.** As such, we will be* *before the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but rather for reward! Again**,** it is not a time of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. It reminds us that our lives matter-not only for today but for **eternity**.

>

>

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

At this judgment we will receive rewards for the good works we have done! All who are at this judgment lived and died as believers, so their eternal salvation is guaranteed. These laurel crowns highlight God's recognition of the faithfulness and service of believers in His Kingdom.

**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p60 -->

<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="46" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p68 -->

<!-- p69 -->

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For greater completeness, if you're curious about what the common points of division are in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most frequently debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the Rapture:** Pre-, Mid-, or Post-tribulation? Opinions differ on **when** the rapture occurs in relation to the **tribulation**. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the Tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of struggle?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial Kingdom:** Is it literal **(Premillennial)**, symbolic **(Amillennial)** or spiritual **(Postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Final rebellion of Satan:** It occurs after the millennium (literal or figurative event?).

<!-- p94 -->
It's great to study eschatology, but let us remember, as D.L. Browning said: "Always keep the main thing the main thing!"

## **Final reflections**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the culmination of God's plan and the reason we live and work with urgency and purpose. The parables of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always prepared for His return. Whether through the rewards of crowns or the hope of resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found ready, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**
