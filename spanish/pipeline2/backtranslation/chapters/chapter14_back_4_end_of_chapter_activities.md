# Activities at the end of the chapter

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the fundamental learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity is intended to take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. Along with personal reflection questions and group discussion, these activities are best used in a group study format to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, promote participation, and modify the activities as you see fit.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound doctrine matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Matching Bible Passages

<!-- p4 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce key biblical concepts about doctrine by exploring Scripture passages that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or small groups** of 3-4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Bible passages** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the Word and guard against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Ask each group:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read the assigned Scripture passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** about doctrine in 1-2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Analyze a real-life example** of how this teaching applies to guard against falsehood or to help others grow in the faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share ideas** - Each group takes **2 minutes** to present their Scripture passage, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Action

<!-- p18 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life situations** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you, “It really doesn’t matter what we believe, as long as we are sincere.” How do you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who hears conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. What Scriptures can you share to help clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops attending church because they feel it is unnecessary as long as they believe in God. How do you encourage them with Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the core of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 Scripture passages** that address the topic.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1 minute summary** of how they would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - The groups take turns presenting their scenarios and responses to the group, followed by **brief comments or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p33 -->
Equip participants to **discern truth from error** using Scripture as their foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Prepare the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud, one at a time. Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Decide whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate the discussion** - Allow time for the groups to discuss disagreements or look up verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Only faith saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All roads lead to God if we are sincere.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelations to guide us.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->

## Chapter 2 - God - Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name that attribute (Interactive group activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God with practical applications for life.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  Prepare index cards - Write a name or attribute of God (e.g., Yahweh-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent) on each card.

<!-- p52 -->
2.  Distribute the cards - Give one card per participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  Group task:

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the name or attribute and the corresponding biblical reference from the chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a 1-minute summary to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  Closing - Ask each participant or group to present their reflections, emphasizing the practical ways God’s character applies to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The names of God in prayer (Reflection and application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach the participants how to incorporate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set up a time of reflection** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants to meditate on one of the names of God from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses a current need (e.g., **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Write a brief **prayer** using that name, praising God and asking Him to act according to His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs to **encourage and strengthen faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Close with group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering the attributes of God (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants **reflect on and apply** the attributes of God to their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on the attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God (e.g., **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**) drawn from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that they feel resonates most with their current stage of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Share stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant **briefly shares a story** (personal experience or observation) related to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Examples of prompts:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When did you experience God as your Provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God’s mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to depend on this aspect of God in daily life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Have the group **write a brief prayer** that reflects the attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God’s character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The ripple effect of sin (Discussion and application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Purpose:**

<!-- p87 -->
Illustrate how **the effects of sin expand outward**, affecting **relationships, communities, and spiritual lives**, while emphasizing God’s **redemptive plan**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Initial illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a **stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if no materials are available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask the participants to **observe the ripples** that expand outward and discuss:  
      *“How does this image illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2SA 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God’s grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some “repercussions” of sin that you have personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace been working to stop those repercussions and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->
### Activity 2: Timeline of sin and restoration (journey through Scripture)

<!-- p105 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p106 -->
Trace God’s **redemptive plan** from **Genesis to Christ**, focusing on **His grace** in the midst of **judgment**.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups** - Assign each group a **passage of Scripture** that highlights sin and restoration:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:21</span>** - God provides garments for Adam and Eve.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warns Cain before his sin.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat carries away sin.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group task:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Analyze the **sin or judgment** in the passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or redemption** offered.

<!-- p117 -->
- Summarize the **lesson learned** about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Have each group share their insights, linking the passages into a **timeline of God’s plan** for redemption.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Application in Prayer)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants to **personally process forgiveness**-both **receive it** and **extend it** to others.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **reflect in silence** on the areas where they may:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God's forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive another person** who has hurt them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Guide participants in private prayer. It is not necessary for the facilitator to listen to the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Final reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (optional) **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from dead works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying dead works (Self-reflection and group discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Purpose:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives, replacing them with actions guided by the Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a **list of possible dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** where they see evidence of dead works in their own lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of possible dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services out of habit but without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-sufficiency:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to attain righteousness through personal effort instead of relying on God's grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to gain favor, ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful patterns or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding on to grudges or to unforgiveness.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing passion for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Works driven by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of obligation rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering to check a box instead of out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misaligned priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success over spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly activities with no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups to share (if they feel comfortable) an area they identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to replace dead works with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Close with a prayer asking for God's guidance to live a fruitful life.
