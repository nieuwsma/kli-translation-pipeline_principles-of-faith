# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Foundation that Endures

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How are we sure? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond the desire to be right at a surface level; it is about knowing deeply and precisely and being able to clearly articulate the newly gained understanding.

<!-- p3 -->
While communication is common, effective and precise communication is a challenge. To effectively communicate a message, the author and originator must have an idea. However, the idea by itself is like water on a hard surface-its edges and boundaries are ill-defined, are fluid, and move easily.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to transform the raw idea from vaguely formed to precisely formed. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us to distinguish and separate between what the idea is and what it is not. Now our formed idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether in long-form text, like this book, or short-form text, like a social media post, or in oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium transmits the now well-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
So far, we have only spoken of the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must receive, decipher, and understand the message accurately. The messages transmitted and received must closely align.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human work is the byproduct of effective communication. The work of a single man can only accomplish so much. To truly build or accomplish something great, many men (speaking here in the generic sense of humankind) must work together in a shared purpose, with a shared idea. With the exception of disasters of natural origin (such as a volcanic eruption), we also know that the greatest calamities that ever occurred generally did so largely because of absent, broken, or distorted communication. Truly life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation, or it has the power to destroy them alike. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and is the blueprint of what we will do, who we will be, how we will exist. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our lives, but to some extent the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must establish our beliefs accurately and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said as much when He told His disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today desire a stronger faith but lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without sound doctrine, we lack the firm foundation we need to stand strong; we risk being swayed by false teachings, discouraged in trials, or unprepared for the work to which God has called us. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture-truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be “rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God's truth. That is why doctrine matters. It is more than information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings of this book are intended to educate and encourage you, reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. I hope these beliefs will convict and inspire your walk of faith to plant deep roots in Christ and come to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experience in the church, particularly in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The composition of the teachings in this book is based on my own personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and on my nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of that time as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children's ministry, evangelistic outreach and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who seek to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - Those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian walk.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who seek to deepen their understanding of God and of the Scriptures while walking in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, Mentors, and Small Group Leaders** - Those who are preparing to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, which makes it ideal for personal growth as well as for group teaching. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, the content is structured for straightforward application and Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, beginning with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving into the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands on its own, but it also builds on the previous chapters, making it easy to move step by step or to focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide for understanding and applying Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides material to prepare lessons, lead discussions, and disciple others effectively. Personal reflection questions, questions to facilitate group discussions, group activities, and word studies are included to help deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

## How Will We Study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound doctrine—the teachings of the Holy Spirit, inspired and recorded in Scripture, passed down through the teachings and tradition of the church. This book will take essential teachings, present them in an easy-to-understand format, and conclude each chapter with a personal reflection, questions for group facilitation, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, numerous eyewitnesses and apostles documented accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical matters within the early Christian communities. These writings, divinely inspired, were carefully recorded, compiled, and preserved over the centuries, forming what is known today as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
Generally, we will use the NKJV translation, but we can also use a different translation when it makes the passage clearer. *Bible references will be in italics to aid readability.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly recommend using multiple translations whenever possible, to ensure that the concept is clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find that translations like the Amplified Bible are beneficial, not for their pure textual accuracy, but to help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures are communicating.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore certain key words of the Bible (**word studies**) so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic biblical resources such as: E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or the Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources will also give you access to Strong's Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong's Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original language, so that we can hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated OT in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated NT in this book) was written in Greek. The Strong’s number will be indicated: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are not new or novel. We should be skeptical of ‘new’ teachings never before seen or heard regarding our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a compilation and explanation of teachings that have been present from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparison of Christian denominations. Take two books on theology written by different authors with divergent denominational backgrounds and, although you will see great harmony in much of their work, you will see a notable division. I will not claim inerrancy, but I will affirm that this work is well studied, peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgments section), and well supported by the Scriptures and by widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

<!-- p69 -->
I implore you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to mastering them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. As you walk through these teachings, I pray that you not only grow in understanding, but also experience the power of the Holy Spirit working in you and through you. My hope is that you are restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God's purpose in your life.
