# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Lasting Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How do we become certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond the mere desire to be superficially right; rather, it is the desire to know deeply and precisely, and to be able to articulate newly gained understanding clearly.

<!-- p3 -->
While communication is common, effective and precise communication is challenging. To communicate any message effectively, the author and originator must have a concept. Yet the concept by itself is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are indistinct, fluid, and easily shift about.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terminology, and phrases to shape a raw idea from a vague form into a precise one. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us distinguish and identify what the idea is and what it is not. Now our formed idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Long-form writing, such as this book, or short-form writing, such as social media posts, or oral forms, such as conversations or video recordings—any medium now conveys the well-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point we have only discussed the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be effective, the recipient must properly receive, analyze, and understand the message. There should be close alignment between the message sent and the message received.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great work of humanity is the byproduct of effective communication. With the labor of one person, only limited work is possible. To build or accomplish something truly great, many people (here referring to humanity in general) must labor together with a shared purpose and shared ideas. Apart from naturally occurring disasters (such as volcanic eruptions), we also know that many of history’s great catastrophes have significantly occurred due to absent, broken, or distorted communication. Indeed, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it contain the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—or equally to destroy them. Our beliefs are the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will become, how we will exist. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it determines what roles we occupy within existence. Our beliefs shape not only our own lives but, to a degree, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
Our beliefs must be properly established, and we must learn to share them effectively. Jesus said this to His disciples:

<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians desire a more steadfast faith, but they lack a clear foundation on which to stand. Without sound doctrine we do not have the solid foundation we need; we risk being swayed by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or unprepared for the work to which God has called us. The aim of this book is to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—the truths that will strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to “be rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God’s truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is not merely information; it is a framework that shapes how we live and respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings of this book are written to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. My hope is that these beliefs will inspire and strengthen your faith journey so that you put down deep roots in Christ and know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings have been shaped and formed by my experience in the church—particularly within Charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The arrangement of the instruction in this book stands upon my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and the experience gained from nearly two decades of involvement in ministry, much of it as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelism and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their own faith or disciple others in spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture as they walk in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - who are preparing to disciple others, one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter has been prepared to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, suitable for personal growth as well as for group teaching. Whether you use this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, the material is arranged in such a way that it is easy to apply and develops under the leadership of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence; they begin with foundational truths like **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and then move on to the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic is self-contained yet built upon previous chapters, making it easy to progress step by step or focus on specific topics as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understand and apply Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides tools to prepare lessons, facilitate discussions, and disciple others effectively. It includes personal self-reflection questions, group-facilitation questions, group activities, and word studies that deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

<!-- p25 -->
## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source of sound doctrine—the teaching of the Holy Spirit—recorded under inspiration in Scripture, and passed down through the church’s teaching and tradition across generations. This book will take the essential teachings, present them in an accessible format, and conclude each chapter with personal self-reflection, group-facilitation questions, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, many eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, in which doctrinal and practical matters of the early Christian communities were discussed. These writings, composed under divine inspiration, were carefully recorded, compiled, and preserved over the centuries, and it is this collection that we know today as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
Generally we will use the NKJV translation, but where another translation makes a passage clearer, we may use that as well. *For reading aid, references to Scripture will be in italics.*

<!-- p60 -->
Where possible, I strongly recommend using multiple translations, so that the concept becomes clearly understandable to you. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful—not for their sheer textual accuracy, but for helping to understand in plain English what Scripture is conveying.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore some important biblical terms (**word studies**), so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I suggest that you learn to use electronic Bible resources like E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). <!-- p62 -->

The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated as OT in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated as NT in this book) was written in Greek. Strong’s numbers will be indicated as follows: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are not new or novel. We should be skeptical of ‘new’—never before seen or heard—teachings about our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have existed from ancient times.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparative analysis of Christian denominations. Take two theology books by different authors with different denominational backgrounds, and you will see great similarities in much of their work, but also notable differences. I will not claim infallibility; however, I will claim that this work is well-researched, peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the acknowledgments section), and well-supported by Scripture and the widely recognized Pentecostal tradition.

<!-- p69 -->
I urge you to read the Holy Scriptures for yourself and commit to mastering them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. As you progress through these teachings, I pray that you will not only grow in understanding, but also experience the power of the Holy Spirit at work in you and through you. My hope is that you will be restored, strengthened, and enabled to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
