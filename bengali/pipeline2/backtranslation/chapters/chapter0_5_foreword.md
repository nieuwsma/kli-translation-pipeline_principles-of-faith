# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
For anything to work effectively, robust systems and structures are essential. Without these, inefficiency and chaos can easily arise. Therefore, establishing a firm foundation in doctrine is extremely important in order to live fully as sound Christians.

<!-- p2 -->
This is why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, where the importance of sound doctrine is discussed, to the sections spanning the remaining 300-plus pages—filled with teaching and activities on the church and our role in it, and on understanding and recognizing the voice of God—this book is invaluable. It will be an excellent tool for teaching and preparing leaders on a firm doctrinal foundation in the classroom, and it will also be extremely helpful for individual readers.
