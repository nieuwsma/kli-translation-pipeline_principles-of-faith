# Chapter 14 - Stay Alert

<!-- p1 -->
Since Christ's spiritual authority has been given to us to establish His kingdom on earth, we should work with perseverance and diligence, because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end times is called **Eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *most of them do not agree with each other*! In particular, they disagree over different interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), and over the sequence and timing of events mentioned in Scripture. I remember a quote from the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke - “When the breaking of chains on earth is finished, I will go to heaven and do hair-splitting!”

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, since this book primarily focuses on the fundamentals of faith, I am reluctant to go very deep into this intensely debated subject. I will mostly stay with the highest-level view of the events described in Scripture and focus on what is most important — **That** **Gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally agreed-upon matters:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus will return

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal consequences

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each topic we will look at what the concept is, the relevant scripture passages, consider why it is important, and see what we should do in light of it.

### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers will appear at the **Judgment Seat of Christ** for reward, while unbelievers will face the **Great White Throne Judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Revelation 20:11–12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it; from His face the earth and the heaven fled away, and no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead—small and great—standing before God; and the books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things that were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and dreadful final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for believers.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers our names are written **in the Lamb’s *Book of Life*** *(*Revelation* see the book)**.** Therefore we will stand* *before that **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but for reward! Again**,** it is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration**—of what God has accomplished through us. This reminds us that our lives are important-not only for today but for **eternity**.

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For completeness, if you're curious about common points of division in eschatology, here's a **very** brief overview of the most debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture:** Pre-, mid-, or post-tribulation? There is disagreement over when the rapture will occur in relation to the tribulation—some denominations do not believe in the rapture at all.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel, or a symbolic period of trial?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial reign:** Is it literal **(premillennial)**, symbolic **(amillennial)**, or spiritual **(postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan's final rebellion:** Occurs after the millennium (literal event or metaphor?).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is great, but as D.L. Browning said, "Keep the main thing the main thing!"

## **Final word**

<!-- p96 -->
Christ's return is the culmination of God's plan and the very reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the parable of the two in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether the reward is crowns or the hope of the resurrection—either way, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**On that glorious day may we be found prepared, faithful, and expectant!**
