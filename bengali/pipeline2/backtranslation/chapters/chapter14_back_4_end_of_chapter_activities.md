# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to further reinforce each chapter’s key learning objectives. Each activity will take approximately 15–20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. Alongside personal reflection and group discussion questions, these activities work best in a group study format to deepen understanding of and engagement with the book’s core teachings. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Match the Scriptures

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Strengthen key biblical concepts about doctrine by exploring passages that emphasize the importance of doctrine.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** into **pairs or small groups** of 3–4.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign passages** - Give each group one from the following:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the word and beware of false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Ask each group to:

<!-- p13 -->
- Read **their assigned passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- Summarize the **key teaching** about doctrine in 1–2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- Discuss **practical examples** of how this teaching helps guard against error or helps others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Each group will take **2 minutes** to present their passage, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life scenarios** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you, “What we believe isn’t really that important, as long as we are sincere.” How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are advising a new believer who is hearing conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. Which Bible verses could you cite to clarify the issue?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend has stopped going to church because they feel it is unnecessary as long as they believe in God. How would you encourage them using Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** that is at the center of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 Scripture verses** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would advise the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share answers** - The groups will take turns presenting their scenario and answers to the whole group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants **to distinguish truth from falsehood** using Scripture as the foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Prepare the activity** - Read the **statements** below aloud, one at a time. Some statements are **biblical truth**, and some are **false** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Team response** - Tell the participants:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Before confirming the correct answer, give the groups time to discuss disagreements or look up verses.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Only faith saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *If we are sincere, then all paths lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelation for our guidance.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Good works are the result of salvation, not the cause.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:10</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name the Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
To help participants connect God's names and attributes with the practical applications of life.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  Prepare index cards - On each card, write a name or attribute of God (e.g., Yahweh-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  Distribute the cards - Give one card to each participant or team.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  Team task:

<!-- p54 -->
- From the chapter, read the name or attribute and the corresponding Scripture reference.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a 1-minute summary to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  Conclusion - Ask each participant or team to present their thoughts, emphasizing how God's character applies practically to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: God's Names in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate God’s names and attributes into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants to meditate on one of God’s names from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer practice:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses the current need (e.g., **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that name, write a brief **prayer** that praises God and asks Him to act in accordance with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: To **encourage and build faith**, (optional) share the prayers in small groups or pairs.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Close with group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering God’s attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants **reflect on and apply** God’s attributes in their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3–4.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on attributes:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Give each group **a list of 3–4 attributes** of God taken from the chapter (e.g., **faithful, compassionate, sovereign, provider**).

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to choose **one attribute** that resonates most with their current stage of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story sharing (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares a story** connected to the chosen attribute (personal experience or observation).

<!-- p78 -->
- Example prompt:

<!-- p79 -->
- *"When have you experienced God as your provider?"*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a brief prayer** that reflects that attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God's character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Explain how the effects of sin **ripple outward**, affecting **relationships, community, and spiritual life**, while emphasizing God's **plan of redemption**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a **stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if materials are unavailable).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to observe the outward-spreading **ripples** and discuss:  
      *“How does this scene illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example** of sin:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation)।

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile)।

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and consequences)।

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and opportunities for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some “waves” of sin you have personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace worked to stop those waves and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->
### Activity 2: Timeline of Sin and Restoration (Scripture Walk)

<!-- p105 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p106 -->
Trace God’s **plan of redemption** from **Genesis to Christ**, emphasizing His **grace** even in the midst of **judgment**.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups** - Assign each group a **passage of Scripture** that highlights sin and restoration:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:21</span>** - God provides clothing for Adam and Eve.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warns Cain before his sin.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat bears the sin away.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group task:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the **sin or judgment** in the passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or redemption** provided.

<!-- p117 -->
- Briefly state the **lessons** learned about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Ask each group to share their insights, and link the parts together to create a **timeline of God’s plan for redemption**.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants in **personally processing forgiveness**-both **to receive it** and **to extend it** to others-lead them.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to engage in **silent reflection** on the areas where they:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God's forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in personal prayer. The conversation between the person and God does not need to be heard by the director/facilitator.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sin to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage them to write down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants (optional) to share **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Helping participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives and replace them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Give participants the possible **list of dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** in their own lives where they see evidence of dead works.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **Possible list of dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church worship out of habit without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Doing religious acts not out of love for God but to gain recognition.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to achieve righteousness through personal effort rather than relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing solely on good works to obtain grace while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful habits or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding onto resentment or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, jealousy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Settling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful Habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambition at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions Motivated by Fear or Guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of mere obligation rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Donating money or volunteering merely to fulfill a formality, not out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced Priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing more on material success than on spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that lack eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so that they can (if comfortable) share an area they have identified as a ‘dead work’.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to replace dead works with Spirit-filled works.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share the key lessons or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer for God’s guidance on the path to a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->

## Chapter 6 - Faith in God

<!-- p242 -->
### Activity 1: Hearing God's Word (Scripture Exploration and Meditation)

<!-- p243 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p244 -->
Help participants understand how faith comes from hearing and recognize the moments when God’s Word becomes personal and living.

<!-- p245 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p246 -->
1.  Scripture reading (5 minutes):
