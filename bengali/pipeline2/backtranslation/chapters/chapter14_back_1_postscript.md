# Appendix

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, dear reader, it is my hope and prayer that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and brought you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit—it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would leave you with, it is this: keep growing. Keep exploring. Keep walking in faith. The truths presented in this book are not meant to be read once and set aside; they are to be lived out in everyday life. The journey of following Christ is a journey of continual learning, refining, and transformation. We move from faith to faith, from glory to glory, pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
Some questions may remain unanswered in your mind. You may feel that you have only touched the surface of these profound and eternal truths. That is good. Let those questions lead you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter—as you seek Him, He will lead you into all truth.
