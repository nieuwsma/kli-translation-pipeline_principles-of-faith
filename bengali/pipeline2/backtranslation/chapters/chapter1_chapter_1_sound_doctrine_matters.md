# Chapter 1 - The Importance of Sound Doctrine

<!-- p1 -->
By doctrine we simply mean what we believe and teach—the core truths entrusted by God's faithful people, rooted in Scripture, and implemented in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His Church to “teach,” He told them to teach His doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke with them and said, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus is instructing all of us to learn His teachings and reproduce them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>); He is the full embodiment of God's truth, wisdom, and revelation. This embodiment, that is, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and took on flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’ orderly teaching is His doctrine (***didaskalia***); by obeying His commandments (obedience, keeping, guarding) (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a chief architect—the greatest builder in history—whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. Only he understands the perfect design for a lasting, unshakable structure. His understanding is not received from others—it is inherent in Him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and the origin of every sturdy building is His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the full and perfect embodiment of God's truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring His design into reality, the architect creates a detailed blueprint—an orderly plan containing the principles, proportions, and fundamental truths necessary to construct a strong building. Without this blueprint no one can build correctly. This blueprint is Jesus’ orderly teaching, His doctrine (***didaskalia***)—through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not mere theoretical knowledge; it is the means by which His wisdom reaches everyone and is preserved, so that those who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also gives specific instructions, detailing how each part of the structure is to be set, where each beam is to be placed, and how every component is to remain aligned with His perfect design. These specific instructions are His commandments **(Entolē**)-the actionable directives that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps that make the building stand firm and remain consistent with the original design. To ignore or alter His commandments is to endanger the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathers a group of apprentices—those who want to learn from Him, build under His supervision, and one day teach others to construct buildings that endure. These apprentices study His blueprint attentively, listen carefully to His instructions, and faithfully apply His commands in their work. They do not build as they please; they build according to the master architect’s design. These apprentices represent the disciples **(Mathētēs**)-who observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, aligning their lives with His word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The more they study under the architect, the more skilled they become, and eventually they teach and train others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the cornerstone of our faith! He is the master architect, who has the perfect design and blueprint. Only by studying the blueprint, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and building a strong working relationship can we construct a life that will stand through the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, they labor in vain who build it; unless the LORD guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
If we do not build our lives on the solid rock of faith, then we cannot expect that our lives will stand firm—even when faced with hardship, challenges, tribulation, persecution, or ordinary day-to-day living.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone is not enough to make a person healthy and physically fit. To gain the full benefits of membership one must persist in regular workouts at the gym. In the same way, merely attending church or casually calling ourselves Christians will not do; each of us must be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith; only then will we realize the full benefits of the membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command extends beyond our own boundaries. It’s wonderful that we become whole, healthy, disciplined, and surrendered to God—but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and disciple others to bring them to faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and become disciplined? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, it is not merely a benefit; it also stands as a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, then we build our lives on sand, and a mighty storm will tear down our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our faith directs our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock, so that we can stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who believed Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Becoming a disciple of Jesus is only possible when we abide in His word (His Logos)! The result of abiding in His word is our knowledge of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, having learned discipline, can obey the Lord’s commands. Every person in the New Testament church has been called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become a disciple -> bring yourself under the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -> teach others so that they come under the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we don’t have to go it alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead us and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His word recorded in Scripture.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders who will help build us up, mature us, keep us safe, and organize us to do good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us specialists who, through experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and in time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

**An important note:** The word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called with the gift of being apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, or teachers, but all of us have been given gifts of service, talents, and special roles (discussed later in the chapter on the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God gave these special gifts to the church to bring us to maturity, so that we can and are:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Full of the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound doctrine we are <u>children, tossed to and fro and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children are by nature:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to distinguish between good and evil people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - concerned only with temporary and present matters.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - easily harmed.

### Sound doctrine provides defense against the evil of the world

<!-- p54 -->
We do not have the luxury of remaining spiritually neutral in this life. We are influenced either by the kingdom of God or by the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul charged his spiritual son Timothy in the Lord with these words:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but according to their own lusts, because they have itching ears, they will heap up for themselves teachers;
>
> And they will turn their ears away from the truth, and be turned aside

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p61 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days perilous times will come:
>
> For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,
>
> unloving, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, haters of good,
>
> traitors, headstrong, haughty, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,
>
> having a form of godliness but denying its power. From such people turn away!
>
> For of this sort are those who creep into households and make captives of gullible women loaded down with sins, led away by various lusts,

>

>

<!-- p62 -->
</span>

<!-- p63 -->
The main point of this message is that perilous times will come. People will be so self-centered that, despite all their “learning,” they will not bow to sound doctrine, will deny the power of God, and will walk with a corrupted and reprobate mind. Paul urges Timothy to be well prepared to proclaim sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples we study and submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine; this provides a defense against the evil of the world; we become mature men and women of God, able to guard ourselves against the enemy’s snares and to protect those around us who have not yet come to full maturity.

<!-- p65 -->
We must be careful and diligent in our study; the sound teaching of Scripture can be led astray by several perverting influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can be distorted by human traditions. We see this truth clearly in the need for the great Reformers of Protestantism and in the history of the Old Testament. God raised up people to restore the fundamental truths that had been distorted by human tradition.

<!-- p67 -->

What are His teachings, what will we study?

<!-- p88 -->

<!-- p89 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">
