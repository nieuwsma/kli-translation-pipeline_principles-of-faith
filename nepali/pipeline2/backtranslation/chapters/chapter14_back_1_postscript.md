# Afterword

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, dear reader, my hopes and prayers for you are simply these: that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you nearer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit-it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I wish to leave you with, it is this: Keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths presented in this book are not for reading once and setting aside; they are for putting into practice in daily life. The journey of following Christ is a continual journey of learning, refinement, and transformation. We move from faith to faith, from glory to glory, always pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may still have some questions left unanswered. You may feel as though you have only touched the surface of these profound and eternal truths. That is a good place to be. May those questions pull you deeper into the Word of God, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter-as you seek Him, He will lead you into all truth.
