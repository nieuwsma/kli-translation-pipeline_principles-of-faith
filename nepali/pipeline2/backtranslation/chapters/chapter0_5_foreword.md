# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For anything to function effectively, strong systems and structures are indispensable. Without these, inefficiency and chaos can easily arise. Therefore, to live fully as healthy Christians, it is extremely important to establish a strong foundation in doctrine.

<!-- p2 -->
Therefore, "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining more than 300 pages filled with teachings and activities concerning the church and our roles within it, as well as topics on understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is invaluable. In the classroom, it will be an extraordinary tool to teach and prepare leaders with a solid foundation in doctrine, and it will be highly beneficial for individual readers as well.

He has a genuine passion for seeing the church operate as a healthy representation of Christ on earth. I am very proud of the work he has accomplished through this book, and based on having collaborated with him for a long time, I can confidently attest that he has invested remarkable time and thought into this process, just as he does in every aspect of his life.
