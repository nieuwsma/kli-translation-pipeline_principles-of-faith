# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a lasting foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions। Why is *something* true? Why did it happen that way? How do we know? How can we be certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination is not merely a superficial desire to be right, but a desire to know deeply and precisely, and to be able to clearly express the new understanding we have gained।

<!-- p3 -->
Though conversation is common, effective and precise communication is challenging। To communicate any message effectively, the writer and originator must have an idea। But an idea by itself is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are blurry, fluid, and easily shifted।

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terminology, and phrases to transform a raw idea from a vague shape into a precise one। Words give our thoughts boundaries; they help distinguish and separate what an idea is and is not। Now that our idea has been shaped, it must be transmitted and received through some medium। Whether long-form text like this book, short-form text like a social media post, or a spoken form such as a conversation or a video recording, the medium carries the now well-formed idea to the recipient।

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point, we have spoken only of the originator and author of the idea। For an idea to be useful, the recipient must properly receive, decode, and understand the message। The transmitted and received messages must closely match one another।

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human undertaking is a byproduct of effective communication। The labor of a single person can accomplish only a little। To truly build or accomplish something great, many people (speaking of humanity in general here) must labor together with a shared purpose and shared ideas। Aside from naturally occurring disasters (such as volcanic eruptions), we also know that many of the greatest catastrophes in history largely occurred because of communication that was lost, corrupted, or distorted। Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>)।

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build up—or likewise to destroy—an individual, a family, a community, a nation। Our beliefs are the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we do, who we become, and how we live। Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence। Our beliefs shape not only our own lives, but to some extent the lives of everyone around us।

<!-- p8 -->
We must establish our beliefs accurately and learn to share them effectively। Jesus said this to His disciples regarding the same:

>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen।

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians want strong faith but lack a clear foundation on which to build। Without sound doctrine, we will not have the firm base necessary to stand strong; we risk being shaken by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or unprepared for the work God has called us to do। This book is made to equip you with Scripture-based foundational teaching that will strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a life filled with the Holy Spirit।

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be “*rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith*” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>)। To become mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God’s truth। This is why doctrine matters। It is not merely information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God।

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are to educate and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church। My hope is that these beliefs will inspire your journey of faith to put down deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally।

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experience in the church, especially in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions। The structure of the teachings in this book comes from my personal study of theology, formal and informal education, and nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of which I was an ordained minister। My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelism and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration।

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their faith or lead others into discipleship toward spiritual growth। Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey।

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those walking in greater obedience and faith, seeking to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture।

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - those preparing to make disciples of others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers in both a **personal** and **practical** way, making it suitable for personal growth as well as group teaching. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your walk with God or to train others, the material is structured for easy application and for Holy Spirit–led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence, beginning with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving on to deeper aspects of **the Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. While each topic stands on its own, it also builds on the previous chapters, making it easy to work through step by step as needed or to focus on specific areas.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understand and apply Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides material to prepare lessons, facilitate discussions, and disciple others effectively. It includes personal reflection questions, discussion questions for group facilitation, group activities, and word studies, which help deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound doctrine: the teachings of the Holy Spirit, which were inspired and written in the Scriptures and have been handed down through the Church’s teaching and tradition. This book will take the essential teachings and present them in an easily understandable format, and each chapter will end with personal reflection, questions for group facilitation, and optional group activities.

After Jesus’ ministry, numerous eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, addressing doctrinal and practical issues within the early Christian communities. These writings, by divine inspiration, were carefully written, compiled, and preserved over centuries, forming what we now know as the Bible.

Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful, not only for textual accuracy, but also for helping me understand in simple English what the Scriptures are communicating.

The teachings I have presented in this book are not new or extraordinary। Regarding our Christian faith, we should be skeptical of ‘new’, that is, never-before-seen-or-heard teachings। Rather, this book is a collection and interpretation of teachings that have existed from the beginning।
