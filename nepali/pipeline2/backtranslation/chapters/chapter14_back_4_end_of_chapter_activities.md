# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce each chapter’s core learning objectives. Each activity is intended to take about 15–20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. Along with personal reflection and group discussion questions, using these activities in a group study format deepens understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as guidelines. Be creative, encourage participation, and modify the activities as you deem necessary.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Scripture Matching

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce key biblical concepts about doctrine by studying scriptures that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** into **pairs or small groups** of 3–4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign scriptures** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the word and guard against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group Discussion** - Each group will:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read the assigned scripture** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** about doctrine in 1–2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** that shows how this teaching applies to avoiding falsehood or helping others grow in the faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Each group should take **2 minutes** to present its scripture, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life scenarios** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4–5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you, “As long as we are sincere, it doesn’t really matter what we believe.” How do you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who has heard conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. Which Scriptures can you share to clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops going to church, feeling that as long as they believe in God, it isn’t necessary to attend. How would you encourage them from Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups will:

<!-- p27 -->
- Identify the **doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1–2 Scripture verses** that address the topic.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** on how you would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - Groups take turns presenting their scenarios and answers to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **discern truth from error** with Scripture as their foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Set up the activity** - Read the **statements** below aloud, one at a time. Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Decide whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Present a **Bible verse** that supports their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Before confirming the correct answers, give groups time to discuss disagreements or look up verses.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *If we are sincere, all paths lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name That Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
To help participants connect God's names and attributes with practical life applications.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - On each card write **one name or attribute** of God (e.g., Yahweh-Yireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- From the chapter, read the **name or attribute** and the related **Scripture reference**.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply in personal or biblical situations?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Closing** - Each participant or group should **present** their thoughts, emphasizing how God's character applies practically in real-life struggles or victorious situations.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: God's Names in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate God’s names and attributes into **personal prayer**।

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Arrange time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet place** so that participants may focus on one of God’s names from the chapter।

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses the current need (e.g., **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing)।

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that name, write a short **prayer**, praising God, and petition Him to act according to His character।

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs to give encouragement and build faith।

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Close with group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes।

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Experiencing God’s attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants reflect on God’s attributes and apply them in their lives through personal stories and group prayer।

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people।**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on an attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Give each group **a list of 3-4 attributes** of God taken from the chapter (e.g., **faithful, merciful, sovereign, provider**)।

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that most closely matches their current life situation।

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story sharing (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant **shares one story** that connects with the chosen attribute (personal experience or observation)।

<!-- p78 -->
- Sample questions:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When did you experience God as your Provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Have the group **write a short prayer** that reflects that attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God's character to your situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Salvation

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
To demonstrate how the **effects spread outward like ripples**, affecting **relationships, communities, and spiritual lives**, and to emphasize God's **plan of redemption**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Initial Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Place **a stone in a bowl of water** (or describe the image if materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to observe the ripples spreading outward and discuss:  
      *“How does this scene illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and give each a **biblical example** of sin:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some of the “ripples” of sin you have personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How did God's grace work to stop those ripples and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

<!-- p105 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p106 -->
Following God's plan of salvation from Genesis to Christ, focus on His grace even amidst judgment.

<!-- p107 -->

<!-- p109 -->

<!-- p110 -->

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat bears sin and carries it away.

- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group task:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the **sin or judgment** seen in that passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or salvation** given.

<!-- p117 -->
- Present a **summary of the lesson** learned about God's character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Let each group share their insights, and connect those passages to create a **timeline** of God's plan for salvation.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants toward **handling forgiveness personally**-both **accepting it** and **giving it to others**.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to quietly reflect on the following:

<!-- p126 -->
- Confessing sin and asking for God’s forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Forgiving those who have wronged you.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in private prayer. The facilitator does not need to listen to the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess your sins before God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray, asking for strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage them to write down any **commitments** (for example, to forgive someone, to seek reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants (optional) to share **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
To help participants identify and address “dead works” in their own lives, and replace them with actions led by the Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with the **List of Potential Dead Works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to quietly reflect and identify **one or two areas** in their lives where there is evidence of dead works.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of Potential Dead Works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services out of habit but not engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Doing religious works to earn approval instead of out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Seeking to attain righteousness through personal effort rather than relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good deeds to obtain grace while neglecting the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful patterns or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding on to anger and resentment or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, jealousy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Apathy:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Settling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions motivated by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of a sense of duty rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering merely to check a box, not out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success rather than spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups and (if they are comfortable) have them share one area they have identified as dead works.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to **replace dead works** with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Close with prayer for God’s guidance to live a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->
### Activity 2: The Path of Repentance (Interactive Visual and Discussion)

<!-- p216 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->
Guide participants to understand the stages of repentance and how they lead toward transformation.

<!-- p218 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p219 -->
1.  **Create a visual pathway:**
