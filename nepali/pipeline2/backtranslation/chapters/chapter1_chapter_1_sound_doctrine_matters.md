# Chapter 1 - The Importance of Pure Doctrine

<!-- p1 -->
The simple meaning of doctrine is the fundamental truths we believe and teach—truths that God’s faithful people have handed down, are rooted in Scripture, and are lived out in our daily conduct.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus instructed His church to ‘teach,’ He was telling them to share His own doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came to them and said, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus directs all of us to learn His teachings and to replicate them in others as well.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), He is the full embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation. This very embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’s structured teachings are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), when we keep His commandments (***entolē)*** (obedience, to guard, to protect) we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a great architect, the greatest builder in history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom are supreme. Only he fully understands the perfect design of a durable and immovable structure. What he understands has not come from others—it is inherent within him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sound construction originates from his design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the complete and comprehensive embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring his design to life, the architect prepares a **detailed blueprint**—a structured plan that includes the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build a structure that will stand firm. Without this blueprint, no one can build correctly. This blueprint represents Jesus’s structured teaching, His doctrine (***didaskalia***) — through which divine truth is understood and put into practice (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is what transmits and preserves His wisdom so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
But a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also gives **precise instructions**—how to connect each part of the structure, where to place which timber/beam, and how to align every element with his perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-action-oriented commands that direct obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps to keep the building aligned with the original blueprint and strong. To ignore or alter His commandments is to compromise the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now, imagine that the architect gathers some **apprentices**—who want to learn from him, build under his guidance, and one day teach others to build enduring structures. These apprentices carefully study his blueprint, listen attentively to his instructions, and faithfully apply his commandments in their work. They build not according to their own ideas, but according to the master’s design. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)-they observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’s teachings, aligning their lives with His word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The more they remain in the architect’s discipleship, the more proficient they become, and eventually they are able to teach and train others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
The teaching of Jesus is the cornerstone of our faith! He is the great architect with the perfect design and blueprint. Only by studying the blueprints, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and developing a strong working relationship with Him can we build a life that will stand in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, they labor in vain who build it; unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand strong when facing hardship, challenges, suffering, persecution, or the ordinary circumstances of life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone is not enough to make a person healthy and physically fit. To gain the full benefit of the membership, one must continually and with discipline engage in the gym. Likewise, merely attending church or superficially claiming to be Christian will not do; we must all be instructed, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith, and only then will we experience the full benefit of the membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command extends beyond us. It is best for us to be whole, healthy, disciplined, and under God’s authority, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples of others in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why study doctrine and why be disciplined? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, it is not only a benefit; it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we build our lives on the sand, and a fierce storm will destroy our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our conduct and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We do not get to remain neutral—we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we may stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible when we continue in His word (His logos)! The result of continuing in His word is our knowledge of the truth, which makes us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples who have been taught discipline can fulfill the Master’s command. Every person in the New Testament church is called to this:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be discipled -\> Devote yourself to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> Teach others to submit to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Do not worry, we do not have to do this work alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who leads and guides us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His word written in Scripture.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given spiritually mature and gifted leaders, who help us to be built up, made mature, kept safe, and organized to do good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given experts who, from experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and over time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p38 -->
> **<u>Ephesians 4:11-16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers,
>
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the building up of the body of Christ,
>
> until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;
>
> so that we may no longer be children, tossed here and there and carried about by every wind of teaching, by the trickery of men and by cunning schemes,
>
> but, speaking the truth in love, let us grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ—
>
> from whom the whole body, being joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, when each part does its work effectively, produces the growth of the body for the building up of itself in love.
>

<!-- p39 -->
</span>

<!-- p40 -->
**An important note:** The word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called to the gifts of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but all of us have been given gifts of ministry, talents, and special roles (discussed later in the chapter on the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given these special gifts to the church to bring us to maturity, so that we may be able to be and do the following:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we will be <u>children, tossed and carried about</u>.

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children are by nature:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to distinguish between good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - concerned only with the moment and the present.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - vulnerable to harm.

<!-- p53 -->
### Sound teaching provides protection against the evil of the world

<!-- p54 -->
We do not have the luxury of remaining spiritually neutral in this life. We are influenced either by the kingdom of God or by the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul gave this charge to his spiritual son Timothy in the Lord:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Therefore I charge you before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, because their ears itch, they will heap up for themselves teachers;
>
> And they will turn their ears away from the truth, and be turned aside to fables.
>

<!-- p58 -->
</span>

<!-- p59 -->
Timothy, an emerging leader in the church, was instructed to maintain a deep understanding of doctrine. Paul warns Timothy that a time will come when people will not endure sound teaching and will turn away! In the previous chapter of the letter Paul sent to Timothy, he clarifies the nature and character of those who will not endure sound teaching:

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p61 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days difficult times will come:
>
> For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,
>
> unloving, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, haters of good,
>
> traitors, headstrong, haughty, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,
>
> having a form of godliness but denying its power. From such people turn away!
>
> For of this sort are those who stealthily enter households and make captives of women who are weighed down with sins, driven by various passions, easily ensnared,
>
> always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth।
>
> Now as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the truth—men of corrupt minds, disapproved concerning the faith;
>
> but they will progress no further, for their folly will be evident to all, as theirs also was।
>

<!-- p62 -->
</span>

<!-- p63 -->
The essence of this message is that difficult times will come। People will become so self-centered that, despite all their ‘teaching,’ they will not live under sound teaching, they will deny the power of God, and they will walk with corrupt and rejected minds। Paul urges Timothy to be well prepared to preach sound teaching।

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples we study and devote ourselves to the discipline of sound teaching; this provides protection against the wickedness of the world। In this way we become mature men and women of God, able to guard ourselves from the snare of the enemy and able to protect those around us who have not yet reached full maturity।

<!-- p65 -->
In our study we must be careful and diligent; many perverting influences can divert the sound teaching of Scripture।

<!-- p66 -->
Sound teaching can be distorted by human traditions। This truth is clearly seen in the need for the great Protestant Reformers and from the history of the Old Testament। God raised up people so that the fundamental truths that had been distorted by human traditions might be restored।

What are His teachings, what will we study?

<!-- p89 -->
