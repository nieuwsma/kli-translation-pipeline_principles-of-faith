<td>Declares Jesus’s supreme authority over all earthly rulers and kingdoms.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Lord of Lords</strong></p>

<td>Supreme Authority</td>
<td>Jesus as the ultimate authority over all, affirming His divine sovereignty.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>The Good Shepherd</strong></p>

<td><p><strong>Redeemer</strong></p>

<td>Jesus as the one who paid the price to free humanity from sin and its penalties.</td>

<td>Jesus as the ultimate mediator who intercedes for humanity before God.</td>

<td>Jesus as the source of spiritual nourishment and eternal life.</td>

<td>Jesus as the source of truth, dispelling spiritual darkness and guiding believers.</td>
