# Chapter 14 - Keep Watch

<!-- p1 -->
Having been given the spiritual authority of Christ on the Earth to establish His kingdom we should work with diligence and haste, for Jesus will return!


<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end times is known as **eschatology**. Many books on eschatology have been written, and *most of them disagree with each other*! Specifically, they disagree on interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), the order and timings of the events in the scriptures. I'm reminded of the saying by the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke - “I will split hairs in heaven when I am done breaking chains on Earth!”


<!-- p3 -->
As the author, I’m hesitant to dive very deep into this hotly debated topic given that this book is focused primarily on fundamentals of the faith. I will stick largely to the highest-level view of the events depicted in scripture and focus on what matters most - **the** **mission of the Gospel**!


<!-- p4 -->
## Universally Agreed Upon Points:


<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus Is Returning


<!-- p6 -->
2.  Resurrection of the Dead


<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for All People


<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal Destiny


<!-- p9 -->
5.  A Defeated Enemy


<!-- p10 -->
6.  New Heaven and New Earth


<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will review what the concept is, look at reference scriptures, consider why it matters, and what we should do in light of it.


<!-- p12 -->
### **1. Jesus Is Returning**


<!-- p13 -->
**What It Is:** The **Second Coming of Christ** is the literal return of Jesus to the earth as King and Judge.



<!-- p14 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Acts" data-chapter="1" data-verses="11" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p15 -->
> **<u>Acts 1:11</u>** (NKJV) *Men of Galilee, why do you stand gazing up into heaven? This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will so come in like manner as you saw Him go into heaven.*


<!-- p16 -->
</span>




<!-- p17 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Revelation" data-chapter="22" data-verses="12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p18 -->
> **<u>Revelation 22:12</u>***, (NKJV) And behold, I am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, to give to every one according to his work.*


<!-- p19 -->
</span>



<!-- p20 -->
**Why It Matters:** Christ’s return fulfills God’s promise to complete redemption and establish His kingdom. It reminds us to live with urgency, knowing we will one day see Him face-to-face.


<!-- p21 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p22 -->
- **Be Watchful:** Live in expectation of His return.


<!-- p23 -->
- **Stay Faithful:** Carry out the Great Commission (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Matthew" data-chapter="28" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:19-20</span>) with purpose and diligence.


<!-- p24 -->
### **2. Resurrection of the Dead**


<!-- p25 -->
**What It Is:** The **resurrection** is the raising of all people-both righteous and unrighteous-from the dead to face **judgment**.



<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="John" data-chapter="5" data-verses="28-29" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p27 -->
> **<u>John 5:28-29</u>** *(NKJV) **28** Do not marvel at this; for the hour is coming in which all who are in the graves will hear His voice **29** and come forth-those who have done good, to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of condemnation.*


<!-- p28 -->
</span>




<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="1 Thessalonians" data-chapter="4" data-verses="16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p30 -->
> **<u>1 Thessalonians 4:16</u>** *(NKJV) *… the dead in Christ will rise first.*


<!-- p31 -->
</span>



<!-- p32 -->
**Why It Matters:** The resurrection guarantees justice and eternal life for believers while warning unbelievers of judgment. It points to God’s sovereignty over life and death.


<!-- p33 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p34 -->
- **Be Prepared:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness to God.


<!-- p35 -->
- **Share the Gospel:** Help others prepare for the resurrection of life by leading them to Christ.


<!-- p36 -->
### **3. Judgment for All People**


<!-- p37 -->
**What It Is:** All people-believers and unbelievers-will face judgment before God. Believers appear at the **Judgment Seat of Christ** for rewards, while unbelievers face the **Great White Throne Judgment**.


<!-- p38 -->
#### Great White Throne Judgement



<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="Revelation" data-chapter="20" data-verses="11-12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** *(NKJV) **11** Then I saw a great white throne … **12** … And the dead were judged according to their works*


<!-- p41 -->
</span>



<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and dreadful final judgement for all non-believers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgement is not for the believer.**


<!-- p43 -->
#### Judgement Seat of Christ


<!-- p44 -->
As believers our names have been put in the **Lamb's *Book of Life*** *(*See the book of *Revelation)**.** As such we will stand* *the **Judgement Seat of Christ***. This judgement is not for condemnation, but rather for reward! Again**,** it is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. It reminds us that our lives matter-not just for today but for **eternity**.



<!-- p45 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="2 Corinthians" data-chapter="5" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p46 -->
> **<u>2 Corinthians 5:10</u>** (NKJV) *For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive the things done in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad.*


<!-- p47 -->
</span>




<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="3" data-verses="12-15" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p49 -->
> **<u>1 Corinthians 3:12-15</u>** (NKJV)
>
> ***12** Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,*
>
> ***13** each one's work will become clear; for the Day will declare it, because it will be revealed by fire; and the fire will test each one's work, of what sort it is.*
>
> ***14** If anyone's work which he has built on it endures, he will receive a reward.*
>
> ***15** If anyone's work is burned, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.*


<!-- p50 -->
</span>



<!-- p51 -->
At this judgement we will receive rewards for the good works we have done! Everyone at this judgement lived and died as a believer, so their eternal salvation is guaranteed. However not everyone will have further reward. The New Testament mentions crowns as rewards that represent victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns highlight God’s recognition of believers’ faithfulness and service in His Kingdom.


<!-- p52 -->
The rewards:


<!-- p53 -->
1.  **The Crown of Righteousness** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="2 Timothy" data-chapter="4" data-verses="8" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">2 Timothy 4:8</span>)* - For those who long for Christ’s appearing and live godly lives.


<!-- p54 -->
2.  **The Incorruptible Crown** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="9" data-verses="24-25" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 9:24-25</span>)* - For believers who practice self-discipline and faithfulness.


<!-- p55 -->
3.  **The Crown of Life** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="James" data-chapter="1" data-verses="12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">James 1:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Revelation" data-chapter="2" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Revelation 2:10</span>)* - For those who endure trials, persecution, and testing.


<!-- p56 -->
4.  **The Crown of Glory** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Peter" data-chapter="5" data-verses="2-4" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Peter 5:2-4</span>)* - For leaders and shepherds who faithfully guide God’s people.


<!-- p57 -->
5.  **The Crown of Rejoicing** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Thessalonians" data-chapter="2" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Thessalonians 2:19-20</span>)* - For those who lead others to Christ and make disciples.


<!-- p58 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p59 -->
- **Live with Purpose:** Work diligently, knowing our efforts for the Kingdom matter eternally.


<!-- p60 -->
- **Examine Ourselves:** Ensure we live lives of faith and obedience.


<!-- p61 -->
### **4. Eternal Destiny**


<!-- p62 -->
**What It Is:** All people will spend eternity in one of two places-eternal life with God or eternal separation from Him.



<!-- p63 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Matthew" data-chapter="25" data-verses="46" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p64 -->
> **<u>Matthew 25:46</u>** (NKJV) And these will go away into everlasting punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.*


<!-- p65 -->
</span>



<!-- p66 -->
**Why It Matters:** Our eternal destiny is determined by whether we have accepted or rejected Christ. It emphasizes the urgency of the Gospel message and discipleship.


<!-- p67 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p68 -->
- **Proclaim Salvation:** Share the Gospel boldly to lead others to eternal life.


<!-- p69 -->
- **Live with Eternity in Mind:** Focus on what matters eternally-faith, obedience, and love.


<!-- p70 -->
### **5. A Defeated Enemy**


<!-- p71 -->
**What It Is:** Satan, the deceiver, will be cast into **the lake of fire**-defeated forever by Christ.



<!-- p72 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Revelation" data-chapter="20" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p73 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:10</u>**, (NKJV) *The devil, who deceived them, was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone where the beast and the false prophet are. And they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.*


<!-- p74 -->
</span>



<!-- p75 -->
**Why It Matters:** This final act demonstrates God’s sovereignty over evil and assures believers that Satan’s reign of deception and destruction is over. It gives us confidence in God’s ultimate victory.


<!-- p76 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p77 -->
- **Stand Firm:** Resist the enemy’s lies, knowing He is already defeated.


<!-- p78 -->
- **Walk in Authority:** Live with boldness and faith, knowing God has already won the victory.


<!-- p79 -->
### **6. New Heaven and New Earth**


<!-- p80 -->
**What It Is:** After the final judgment and defeat of the enemy, God will create a **new heaven and new earth**-a perfect eternal dwelling place for His people.



<!-- p81 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="Revelation" data-chapter="21" data-verses="1,3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p82 -->
> **<u>Revelation 21:1,3</u>** *(NKJV) **1** Now I saw a new heaven and a new earth… **3** …God Himself will be with them and be their God.*


<!-- p83 -->
</span>



<!-- p84 -->
**Why It Matters:** It gives us hope for restoration, where God will wipe away every tear and make all things new-free from pain, sin, and death.


<!-- p85 -->
**What Should We Do?**


<!-- p86 -->
- **Fix Our Eyes on Eternity:** Keep our focus on the eternal hope rather than temporary struggles.


<!-- p87 -->
- **Persevere in Faith:** Endure trials, knowing that eternal joy awaits.


<!-- p88 -->
## Points of Common Disagreement


<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, should you be curious about the common division points are in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most frequently debated points.


<!-- p90 -->
- **Rapture Timing:** Pre-, Mid-, or Post-Tribulation? Views differ on **when** the rapture occurs relative to the **tribulation**. Some denominations don’t believe in the rapture.


<!-- p91 -->
- **Tribulation Purpose:** Is it literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of struggle?


<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial Reign:** Is it literal **(Premillennial)**, symbolic **(Amillennial)**, or spiritual **(Postmillennial)**?


<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s Final Rebellion:** Happens after the millennium (literal event or figurative?).


<!-- p94 -->
It is great to study eschatology, but let's remember as D.L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing, the main thing!”

## **Final Thoughts**


<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the culmination of God’s plan and the reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parables of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Matthew" data-chapter="25" data-verses="1-13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Matthew" data-chapter="24" data-verses="40-42" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always prepared for His return. Whether through the rewards of crowns or the hope of resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming back!


<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found ready, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**
