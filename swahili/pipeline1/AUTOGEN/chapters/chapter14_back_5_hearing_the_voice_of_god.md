# Hearing The Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest appendix by Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) complements our exploration of the Holy Spirit’s role, offering practical insights on hearing God’s voice. The following guest contribution is used with permission.


<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning, I asked **God** the following question: “What is the most important thing I could teach your people for the times that are heading our way?” And He answered me very quickly, and I sensed it was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My voice.**”


<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me a direction like that, I always pick up a pad and pencil and begin to write what little I know, or what I think I know, and the Scriptures that support it. I love doing Word studies on topics the Lord is leading me to teach. I love the anointing for preaching, but I really love to teach the Word. It is an adventure finding things that have always been there but have been overlooked.


<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to uncover God’s Truths, for He prayed in verse 18 of Psalm 119: “Open my eyes that I may see Wondrous things from Your law.”


<!-- p5 -->
That’s what I want to see-Wondrous things from His Word.


<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on hearing and discerning God’s voice, He spoke to my heart the following:


<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*


<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, Teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them away:  
Away from troubles that would destroy them,*


<!-- p9 -->
*Away from the trials that they are in,  
Away to a place of peace, love, and joy that few have ever been in.*


<!-- p10 -->
*Away to a place of refreshing,  
to a place of Divine ecstasy.*


<!-- p11 -->
*Oh yes, teach them to hear My voice son  
and these people will forever be free*.”


<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.


<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:


<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing God’s voice is NOT an audible sound but a KNOWING in your heart.</u>


<!-- p15 -->
There was one time when His Word came so clear and bold that it could have been audible. I answered Him by speaking out, it was so real. However, there was no one there but the Lord and I, so I can’t really say for sure if He spoke to me like we speak to each other.


<!-- p16 -->
There was the time when Jesus was being baptized in the river Jordan and the Father spoke: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some of those there said it sounded like thunder, so I guess He could have spoken audibly to me, but for now, I want you to understand that when I say God spoke to me, it is a KNOWING in my heart more than a sound in my ear.


<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>The KNOWING is NOT built by logic within your mind; however, it is a complete thought or understanding.</u>


<!-- p18 -->
I have never had the Lord speak anything to me that confused me. I have never had Him speak anything to me that left me wondering ‘why.’ It is always a KNOWING with complete UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="14" data-verses="33" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).


<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in complete thoughts.</u>


<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us to fill in the blanks. In the twinkling of an eye, God can fill your heart with enough information to keep you preaching a lifetime. One touch from God can fill you with fire that will keep you burning eternally. He is a complete communicator.


<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He NEVER DRIVES. He leads.</u>


<!-- p22 -->
He is not a task master; He is a Shepherd. Herdsmen drive, but Shepherds lead. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.


<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He NEVER CONVICTS; He CONVINCES.</u>


<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who have been Born Again (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="3" data-verses="3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 3:3</span>, 5) by their confession of faith in **Jesus’** shed blood and His work on Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who always convicts us of our wrong? I believe most of the children of **God** are afraid to hear **God** speak to them because they have been told that **God** will convict them for this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 John" data-chapter="3" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who does it say convicts us or condemns us? It’s OUR SPIRIT or heart that condemns the child of **God**, but it is **God the Holy Spirit** that knows all things and is GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the following Scriptures, and it will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are the sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Romans" data-chapter="8" data-verses="33-34" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and **God’s** love. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our convict-or, our Counselor, not our chastiser, our Intercessor and Helper, not our hinderer. Read also <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Acts" data-chapter="9" data-verses="31" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (**God’s** voice) aids, comforts, and strengthens us.


<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so you can KNOW it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>


<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 John" data-chapter="4" data-verses="1-3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test every spirit” so we can be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing from God, you have the right and the responsibility to ask that voice a question (That is testing or proving the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Did Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The one thing that familiar spirits will never admit is that Jesus came to the earth in the flesh. He was all man and all God. No false spirit or religion will admit Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed mankind from all their sin for eternity. Test the voice you hear. You must also know that God will never lead you outside His written Word, the Bible. If you are hearing something that cannot be backed up in the Bible, you are not hearing from God. Get rid of it! Test the voice you hear to see if it is from God.


<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>


<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the one here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said He will always speak only what He hears, and He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13-14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13-14</span>). He isn’t here to bring your ministry glory or your works or the church’s programs. All those things are good, and most of them are necessary to do what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If you are being glorified by the voice you hear, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage and strengthen you every time He speaks to you, even when He is correcting (guiding) you with the word. But glory is when you believe YOU are somebody. You are nobody without Jesus-HE IS THE SOMEBODY, not us.


<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>God’s voice will always REVEAL something to us.</u>


<!-- p30 -->
Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified adds the words: declare, disclose, and transmit when considering the word “reveal.” **God** always discloses something to us after He declares it to us, and then by your faith, He transmits it to us. Study also <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="2" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.


<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>


<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="26" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Colossians" data-chapter="3" data-verses="14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never disturbs us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace is because there is a KNOWING which brings us confidence before God, which translates into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to disturbs you, you are not hearing from God-shut it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives but as He gives us through the Holy Spirit’s comforting words and constant abiding (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="27" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:27</span>).


<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>


<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="17" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. As a matter of fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “The Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH and therefore will only speak TRUTH. I have had people say things like: “I can’t believe what I am hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And maybe it is too good to be true; that’s why you MUST test the spirit by lining it up with the Word of God to be sure it is Truth. Some people just can’t believe it because what they are hearing is always positive, and they have been taught that God convicts us of error. God will only tell the TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is most people only hear bad news from the voice they are listening to and never anticipate hearing good news, so when good news comes, they question it as a possible non-truth. **Truth is always good news!** It’s the **truth that** sets us free, so how could **truth** be anything but good news?


<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always LEADS US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>


<!-- p36 -->
Read Deut. 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Isaiah" data-chapter="30" data-verses="21" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13</span>


<!-- p37 -->
God never takes you down! He will never run you down; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He is speaking from the frame of reference that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and ACCEPTED by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Jeremiah" data-chapter="33" data-verses="3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you **GREAT AND MIGHTY** things which you do not know.” Does that sound like running you down or doom and gloom? There is NO doom and gloom in God, therefore He cannot tell you anything doomy or gloomy. You’ll have to tune in to the world’s news agency for doom and gloom.



<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Jeremiah" data-chapter="29" data-verses="11" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">


<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV) *For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.*


<!-- p40 -->
</span>



<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS good thoughts of peace, a future, and hope toward us, why would He speak otherwise? The answer is, HE WOULDN’T, and HE DOESN’T! He only leads us UPWARD and ONWARD with great hope to provide us an expected future.


<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to “call upon Him” and learn how to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs your listening ear before all His good things can do you any good.
