<tr>
<td><p><strong>I AM THAT I AM</strong></p>
<p>Eh-YEH a-sher Eh-YEH</p></td>
<td><p>Self-Existent, Eternal</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 3:14</span></p></td>
<td>God’s foundational name revealed to Moses, affirming His eternal, unchanging, and self-sustaining nature. This name emphasizes that God exists by His own power and will always be.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Elohim</strong></p>
<p>Eh-loh-HEEM</p></td>
<td><p>God, Creator</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Zab 19:1</span></p></td>
<td>Emphasizes God’s power and majesty as Creator; a plural form denoting greatness or the Trinity.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh (Jehovah)</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh (jeh-HO-vah)</p></td>
<td><p>LORD, "I AM"</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 3:14</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="42" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV">Is 42:8</span></p></td>
<td>The covenant name of God, indicating His eternal, self-existent nature, and faithfulness to Israel.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Adonai</strong></p>
<p>Ah-doh-NIGH</p></td>
<td><p>Lord, Master</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 15:2</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV">Zab 16:2</span></p></td>
<td>Reflects God's authority and lordship, acknowledging Him as the rightful ruler and owner of creation.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Shaddai</strong></p>
<p>El shad-DYE</p></td>
<td><p>God Almighty, All-Sufficient One</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 17:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="91" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Zab 91:1</span></p></td>
<td>Signifies God’s power to nourish, bless, and provide abundantly; shows His sufficiency.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Jireh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh YI-rah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Will Provide</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 22:14</span></p></td>
<td>Symbolizes God’s provision; first used when God provided a ram as a substitute for Isaac.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Rapha</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-fah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Who Heals</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 15:26</span></p></td>
<td>Shows God’s healing nature; He restores physical, emotional, and spiritual health.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Nissi</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh NEE-see</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is My Banner</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 17:15</span></p></td>
<td>Represents God’s protection and victory; used by Moses after a victory over the Amalekites.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-M’kaddesh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Meh-KAH-desh</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Who Sanctifies</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV">LEV 20:8</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 31:13</span></p></td>
<td>Points to God as the One who sets His people apart as holy, through His commandments and grace.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Shalom</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh shah-LOME</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is Peace</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JDG" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV">JDG 6:24</span></p></td>
<td>Gideon used this name after God’s reassurance; highlights God as the source of peace and wholeness.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Sabaoth</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Sa-ba-OTE</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD of Hosts (Armies)</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="1SA" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV">1Sam 1:3</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="46" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV">Zab 46:7</span></p></td>
<td>Refers to God’s supreme power over all heavenly and earthly forces; emphasizes His sovereignty.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Raah</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-ah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is My Shepherd</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Zab 23:1</span></p></td>
<td>David’s use of this name to show God’s guidance, care, and provision, like a shepherd with His flock.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Tsidkenu</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Tsid-KAY-noo</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Our Righteousness</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV">Yer 23:6</span></p></td>
<td>Indicates God as the source of righteousness and justice, fulfilled in the Messiah.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Shammah</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh SHAH-mah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is There</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EZK" data-chapter="48" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="35" data-version="NKJV">EZK 48:35</span></p></td>
<td>Used to describe God’s abiding presence; symbolizes God’s presence with His people, even in exile.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Elyon</strong></p>
<p>El EL-yawn</p></td>
<td><p>God Most High</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 14:18-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="57" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV">Zab 57:2</span></p></td>
<td>Signifies God’s supreme authority and exalted position above all other gods and powers.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Olam</strong></p>
<p>El OH-lahm</p></td>
<td><p>Everlasting God</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="21" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 21:33</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="40" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28" data-version="NKJV">Is 40:28</span></p></td>
<td>Emphasizes God’s eternal nature; He exists beyond time and is unchanging.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Makkeh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh MAH-keh</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Who Strikes</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EZK" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV">EZK 7:9</span>, 22:31</p></td>
<td>Highlights God’s justice in discipline and correction; a reminder of His holy standards.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Yahweh-Gmolah<br/>
</strong>YAH-weh GIM-oh-lah</td>
<td><p>The God of Recompense</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="51" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="56" data-version="NKJV">Yer 51:56</span></p></td>
<td>God’s justice in repaying and rewarding according to one’s deeds; emphasizes divine justice.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Qanna</strong></p>
<p>KAH-nah</p></td>
<td><p>Jealous</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 34:14</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="DEU" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV">Kum 4:24</span></p></td>
<td>Reflects God’s zealous love for His people; shows His intolerance for idolatry and unfaithfulness.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Roi</strong></p>
<p>El ROY-ee</p></td>
<td><p>The God Who Sees Me</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV">Mwa 16:13</span></p></td>
<td>Used by Hagar; emphasizes God’s attentiveness and awareness of personal struggles.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Channun</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh khan-NOON</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Who is Gracious</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="86" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV">Zab 86:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV">Kuto 34:6</span></p></td>
<td>Highlights God’s mercy and His readiness to forgive and show compassion.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Elyon</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh EL-yawn</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Most High</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV">Zab 7:17</span>, 47:2</p></td>
<td>Affirms God’s ultimate authority and position above all else.</td>
</tr>
