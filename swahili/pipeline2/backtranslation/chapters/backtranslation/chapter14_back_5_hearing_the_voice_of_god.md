# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
An additional piece written by guest, Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) completes our study on the role of the Holy Spirit, offering practical insights about hearing the voice of God. This guest contribution is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning, I asked **God** the following question: “What is the most important thing I can teach Your people for the times that lie ahead of us?” And He responded to me very quickly, and I realized it was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me a direction like that, I take a notebook and a pencil and begin to write what little I know, or what I think I know, and the Scriptures that support it. I like to study the Word on the topics the Lord leads me to teach. I appreciate the anointing to preach, but I especially love to teach the Word. It is an exciting journey to discover things that have always been there but have been overlooked.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to uncover God’s Truths, for he prayed in verse 18 of Psalm 119: “Open my eyes that I may see wondrous things out of Your Law.”

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-wondrous things from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on hearing and recognizing the voice of God, He spoke the following in my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them away:  
Away from troubles that will destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Away from the temptations that are in it,  
Away to a place of peace, love, and joy where very few have ever been.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Away to a place of refreshing,  
to a place of Divine joy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice, My son,  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible voice to your ears but a KNOWING in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was one time His Word came with such clarity and boldness that it could have been an audible voice. I responded by speaking out loud, it was so real. However, there was no one there except the Lord and me, so I cannot say for certain whether He spoke to me as we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was a time when Jesus was being baptized in the Jordan River and the Father spoke: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some of those who were present said it sounded like thunder, so I suppose He could speak to me in an audible voice, but for now, I want you to understand that when I say God spoke to me, it is a KNOWING in my heart rather than a voice in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>THAT KNOWING IS NOT BUILT by intellectual arguments in your mind; rather, it is a thought or understanding that is complete.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
I have never had a time when the Lord spoke to me something that confused me. I have never heard Him say to me something that left me asking ‘why.’ It is always a KNOWING with COMPLETE UNDERSTANDING. God is not the God of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="14" data-verses="33" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in thoughts that are complete.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us to fill in the gaps. In the blink of an eye, God can fill your heart with enough information to keep you preaching your entire life. One touch from God can fill you with a fire that will keep on burning in you forever. He is a perfect Communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, HE NEVER PUSHES. HE LEADS.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Drovers push, but Shepherds lead. Jesus is the Chief Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, HE NEVER CONDEMNS; HE ASSURES.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who have been born again (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="3" data-verses="3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 3:3</span>, 5) by their confession of faith in the shed blood of **Jesus** and His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who always condemns us for our mistakes? I believe many children of **God** are afraid to hear **God** speaking to them because they have been told that **God** will judge them for this or that. See <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 John" data-chapter="3" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 John 3:19-20</span>. It says who is it that condemns or blames us? It is OUR SPIRIT or our heart that blames the child of **God**, but it is **God the Holy Spirit** who knows all things and is GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the following Scriptures, and they will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit condemns are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Romans" data-chapter="8" data-verses="33-34" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can separate us from the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our accuser; He is our Counselor, not our rebuker; He is our Intercessor and Helper, not our restrainer. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Acts" data-chapter="9" data-verses="31" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) helps us, comforts us, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you can KNOW that it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of a mere spirit.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 John" data-chapter="4" data-verses="1-3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test every spirit” so that we can be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing from God, you have the right and responsibility to ask that voice a question (That is testing or proving the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Did Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” One thing that ordinary spirits will never confess is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully man and fully God. No lying spirit nor religion will confess that Jesus came to earth in the flesh and redeemed humankind from all their sins forever. Test the voice you are hearing. You must also know that God will never lead you outside His written Word, that is, the Bible. If you hear something that cannot be confirmed in the Bible, you are not hearing from God. Throw it away! Test the voice you are hearing to see whether it is from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work together with us, and Jesus said He will always speak only what He hears, and will always bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13-14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to bring glory to your ministry or your deeds or church programs. All those things are good, and many of them are important to accomplish what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If you are being defended or glorified by the voice you are hearing, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and empowered. The Holy Spirit will always encourage you and empower you whenever He speaks to you, even when He corrects you (leads you) by a word. But glory is when you think YOU are the important one. You are nothing without Jesus—HE IS THE IMPORTANT ONE, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God will always REVEAL something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
See <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified version adds the words: declare, disclose, and transmit when dealing with the word “reveal.” **God** always discloses something to us after He has declared it to us, and then by your faith, He transmits it to us. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="2" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="26" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Colossians" data-chapter="3" data-verses="14" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His Voice never troubles us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace is because there is a KNOWING that brings us confidence before God, which translates into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to is troubling you, you are not hearing from God-turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives but as He gives us through the words of comfort of the Holy Spirit and His abiding presence (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="27" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="14" data-verses="17" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “the Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH and therefore He will speak only TRUTH. I have heard people say things like: “I can’t believe what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And perhaps it is too good to be true; that’s why you MUST test the spirit by comparing it to the Word of God to make sure it is Truth. Some people simply cannot believe because what they are hearing is always positive, and they have been taught that God judges us for our mistakes. God will speak only TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that many people hear only bad news from the voice they are listening to and do not expect to hear good news, so when good news comes, they question it as perhaps not true. **Truth is always good news!** It is **that truth** that sets us free, so what else can **truth** be but good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always LEADS US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deuteronomy 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Isaiah" data-chapter="30" data-verses="21" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="John" data-chapter="16" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never brings you down! He will never belittle you; He will only build you up. When God speaks to you, He speaks from the standpoint that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and YOU HAVE ACCEPTED IT. <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="Jeremiah" data-chapter="33" data-verses="3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and I will show you GREAT AND MIGHTY things which you do not know.” Does that sound like belittling or destruction and darkness? There is NO destruction and darkness in God, therefore He cannot tell you anything destructive or dark. You will need to turn to the world’s news agencies for destruction and darkness.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="Jeremiah" data-chapter="29" data-verses="11" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV) *For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.*

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS good thoughts of peace, a future, and hope concerning us, why would He say otherwise? The answer is, HE WOULDN'T, and HE DOESN'T! He only directs us UP and FORWARD to a great hope to give us the future we are expecting.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to “call on Him” and to learn how to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs your listening ear before all His good things can benefit you.
