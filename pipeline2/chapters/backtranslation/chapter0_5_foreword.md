# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function efficiently, a strong system and structure are essential. Without them, dysfunction and chaos can easily arise. That’s why laying a solid doctrinal foundation is important to living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That’s why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining pages among more than 300, filled with teachings and activities about the church and our roles within it, along with understanding and recognizing the voice of God, this book is of inestimable value. It will be an excellent tool in the classroom for teaching and equipping leaders with a solid doctrinal foundation, while also being of great benefit to individual readers.
