# Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine is Important

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine simply means the foundational truths we believe and teach—truths handed down by faithful people of God, rooted in the Scriptures, and lived out in our daily walk.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His Church to ‘teach’, He was instructing them to impart His doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p4 -->
&gt; **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV) ***18** And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.* ***19** Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,* ***20** teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age." Amen.*

<!-- p5 -->
</span>
<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus commands all of us to learn His doctrine and to teach it to others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the Way, the Truth, and the Life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV">John 14:6</span>), fully manifesting the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God. This manifestation, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">John 1:1</span>, 14). The structured teachings of Jesus are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and by keeping (obeying, guarding, preserving) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine the master architect, the greatest builder in all of history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass everyone else. He alone understands the perfect design of a building that endures, unshakable. His understanding does not come from others—it resides within Him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every solid building begins with His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—fully and perfectly manifesting the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring His design into reality, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**-a plan with structure that highlights the principles, measurements, and foundational truths needed to build a building that will stand firm. Without this blueprint, no one could build accurately. This blueprint represents the organized teachings of Jesus, His doctrine **(Didaskalia)-**the system through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His teachings are not merely theoretical knowledge; they are the means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **precise instructions**, specifying how each part of the design should be joined, where each beam should be placed, and how every element should align with His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-actionable directives that guide obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are essential steps to ensure the building stands firm, aligned with the original blueprint. To ignore or alter His commandments would jeopardize the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now, imagine that the architect gathers **apprentices**-those who desire to learn from Him, to build under His guidance, and one day to teach others how to construct enduring structures. These apprentices study His blueprint carefully, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully carry out His commandments in their work. They do not build according to their own ideas but according to the master’s design. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)-those who observe, obey, and internalize the teachings of Jesus, aligning their lives with His Word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV">John 8:31</span>). The more they learn under the architect, the more skilled they become, eventually training and equipping others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why Should We Learn Doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ doctrine is the rock of our faith! He is the master architect with the perfect design and blueprint. It is by studying the blueprint, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and cultivating a strong working relationship that we can build lives that will endure the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="Psalm" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p16 -->
&gt; **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV) …*Unless the Lord builds the house, They labor in vain who build it; Unless the Lord guards the city, The watchman stays awake in vain.*

<!-- p17 -->
</span>
<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand firm in the face of hardship, challenges, tribulation, persecution, or even ordinary life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
More than a gym membership card is required to make someone healthy and fit. You must continue in the discipline of gym training to receive the full benefits of membership. Likewise, we cannot merely attend church or casually claim that we are Christians; we all must be educated, taught, invested in, and disciplined in the faith, then we will know the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command goes beyond us. It is a good thing that we become complete, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must teach, train, empower, lead, and make others disciples in the faith as well!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study the teachings and be disciplined? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is more than just a benefit; it is also a warning to us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we will build our lives on the sand, and a fierce storm will demolish our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We have no option to remain neutral—we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in His Salvation. True promise and freedom are for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p25 -->
&gt; **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV) ***31** Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed Him, "If you continue in My Word, you are truly My disciples.* ***32** And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."*

<!-- p26 -->
</span>
<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continuing in His Word (His logos)! The result of continuing in His Word is our knowledge of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, after being trained in discipline, can carry out the Lord’s commands. Everyone in the New Covenant church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be a disciple -\&gt; let us submit to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\&gt; teach others to submit themselves to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How Can We Build Our Lives on the Rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we don’t have to do this on our own.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will go before us and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His words written in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders to help us be built up, to mature, to be protected, and to be organized to do good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have the perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us experts who, by experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and in time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p38 -->
&gt; **<u>Ephesians 4:11-16</u>** (NKJV)
&gt;
&gt;***11** And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers,*
&gt;
&gt;***12** for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ,*
&gt;
&gt;***13** till we all come to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a perfect man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;*
&gt;

&gt;

&gt;
&gt;***16** from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, according to the effective working by which every part does its share, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love.*

<!-- p39 -->
</span>
<!-- p40 -->
**Key message:** the word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called to the gift of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher but we have all been given ministry gifts, talents, and specific responsibilities (to be discussed later in the chapter about the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given the church these special gifts to bring us into maturity, so that we may and be able to:

<!-- p42 -->
- Have the ability to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- Unite in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Be filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Be mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not be like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we will be <u>children, being tossed to and fro and carried about.</u>
<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but by nature children are:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Inexperienced - unable to distinguish between good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Selfish - caring about what is short-term and immediate.

<!-- p52 -->
- Unable to protect themselves - vulnerable to being harmed.

### Sound Teaching Provides Protection Against the Evil of the World

<!-- p54 -->
We have no room to be spiritually neutral in this life. We are influenced either by the Kingdom of God, or the Kingdom of Darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul instructed his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p57 -->
&gt; **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (KJV) I *charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom; Preach the word; be ready in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort with all longsuffering and doctrine. <u>For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine</u>; but according to their own desires they will heap up for themselves teachers, having itching ears; And they will turn their ears away from the truth, and will be turned aside to fables. (emphasis added)*

<!-- p58 -->
</span>
<!-- p59 -->
Timothy, a budding leader in the church, was instructed to have a deep understanding of doctrine. Paul warns Timothy that a time is coming when people will not endure sound teaching and will fall! In the preceding chapter of his letter to Timothy, Paul identifies the nature and character of these people who will not endure sound doctrine:

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p61 -->
&gt; **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
&gt;
&gt;***1** But know this, that in the last days perilous times will come:*
&gt;
&gt;***2** For men will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,*
&gt;
&gt;***3** unloving, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, despisers of good,*
&gt;
&gt;***4** traitors, headstrong, haughty, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,*
&gt;
&gt;***5** having a form of godliness but denying its power. And from such people turn away!*
&gt;
&gt;***6** For of this sort are those who creep into households and make captives of gullible women, loaded down with sins, led away by various lusts,*
&gt;
&gt;***7** always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.*
&gt;
&gt;***8** Now as Jannes and Jambres resisted Moses, so do these also resist the truth; men of corrupt minds, disapproved concerning the faith;*
&gt;
&gt;***9** but they will progress no further, for their folly will be manifest to all, as theirs also was*.

<!-- p62 -->
</span>
<!-- p63 -->
The essence of this message is that perilous times will come. People will be so self-centered that, despite their ‘teachings,’ they will not submit to sound doctrine, they will deny the power of God, and they will walk with a depraved and rejected mind. Paul urges Timothy to be well prepared to preach sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples we learn and submit to the discipline of sound doctrine; this gives us protection against the evil of the world, we become mature men and women of God, being able to guard ourselves against the snares of the enemy and to protect those around us who have not yet reached full maturity.

<!-- p65 -->
We must be careful and diligent in our studies; the sound teaching of the Scriptures can be distorted by several corrupt influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can be distorted by human traditions. We see this truth plainly from the need for the great Reformers of Protestantism and in the history of the Old Testament. God raised up people to restore fundamental truths that had been distorted by human traditions.

<!-- p67 -->
The Scriptures warn us:

<!-- p68 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p69 -->
&gt; **<u>Colossians 2:8</u>** (NKJV) *Beware lest anyone deceive you through philosophy and empty deceit, according to the tradition of men, according to the basic principles of the world, and not according to Christ.*

<!-- p70 -->
</span>
<!-- p71 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="9,13" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p72 -->
&gt; **<u>Mark 7:9,13</u>** (NKJV) **9** *He said to them, "You reject the commandment of God, that you may keep your tradition…* ***13** making the word of God of no effect through your tradition which you have handed down. And many such things you do."*

<!-- p73 -->
</span>
<!-- p74 -->
People can distort sound doctrine through private interpretation. This, sadly, is clearly seen in the many deadly cults and false messiahs that have emerged in every generation. These evil and corrupt leaders all claim to be special representatives of God with special knowledge of God and refuse to submit to the authority of Jesus and to the boundaries set in the Scriptures. The Spirit warns us:

<!-- p75 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2PE" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="20-21" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p76 -->
&gt; **<u>2 Peter 1:20-21</u>** (NKJV) **20** *knowing this first, that no prophecy of Scripture is of any private interpretation,* ***21** for prophecy never came by the will of man, but holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Spirit.*

<!-- p77 -->
</span>
<!-- p78 -->
All truth and revelation come from the Spirit of Truth. The Spirit of God is not a source of confusion; therefore no teaching or revelation should violate the Scriptures we have been given, which were written by the inspiration and guidance of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p79 -->
In both cases, whether through the tradition of a group or private interpretation, we know that the spirit at work to deceive the church is the devil:

<!-- p80 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p81 -->
&gt; **<u>1 Timothy 4:1</u>** (NKJV) *Now the Spirit expressly says that in the latter times some will depart from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons,*

<!-- p82 -->
</span>
<!-- p83 -->
Just as a parent must protect their children, mature disciples protect those who are immature in the faith against the destruction of this world. The ultimate result of this maturity is our ability to bear much fruit!

<!-- p84 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV">
<!-- p85 -->
&gt; **<u>John 15:8</u>** (NKJV) * By this My Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit; and you will be My disciples.*

</span>

Which of His teachings are they, what will we study?
