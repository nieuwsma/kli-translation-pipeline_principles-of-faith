# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Laying a Lasting Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is something true? What makes it so? How do we know? How can we be certain? If something is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond a desire to be correct at a surface level; it is to know deeply and precisely, and to be able to express the newly gained understanding clearly.

<!-- p3 -->
Though communication is common, effective and precise communication is challenging. To communicate effectively, the author and originator must first have an idea. However, an idea by itself is like water on a hard surface—it has blurry edges and boundaries, it is fluid, and it is easily pushed around.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to turn raw ideas from vague shapes into precise forms. Words give our ideas boundaries, helping us distinguish and separate what the idea is and what it is not. Now that the idea has taken shape, it must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether a long-form text, like this book, a short text, like a social media post, or an oral form, like a conversation or a recorded video, the medium carries this now-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point, we have only talked about the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the receiver must accurately receive, decode, and understand the message. What is transmitted and what is received should be in high agreement.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human achievement is a byproduct of effective communication. What one person’s labor can accomplish is limited. To truly build or accomplish something great, many people (used generically here for humankind) must labor together under a shared goal and shared ideas. Apart from natural disasters (such as volcanic eruptions), we also know that history’s greatest catastrophes often largely stemmed from missing, broken, or distorted communication. Life and death are indeed in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it have the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—and likewise the ability to destroy them. Our beliefs are the foundation of our actions, the blueprint for what we will do, who we will become, and how we will exist. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines our role in existence. Our beliefs not only shape our own lives but also, to some degree, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must establish our beliefs accurately and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said this to His disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you; behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p12 -->
Today, many Christians long for stronger faith yet lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without solid doctrine, we lack the firm footing needed to stand; we risk being shaken by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or underprepared for the work God has called us to. This book aims to equip you with the Bible’s foundational teachings—truths that will strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urges believers to be "rooted and built up in Him, and established in the faith" (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first put down deep roots in God’s truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is not merely information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why write this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are intended to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament Church. My hope is that these beliefs will persuade and inspire your walk of faith, rooting you deeply in Christ and leading you to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experiences in the Church, especially within Charismatic or Pentecostal practice. The compilation of this book’s teachings is built on my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and nearly two decades of ministry experience, most of which I have served as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelistic outreach and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who are seeking to strengthen their own faith or to lead others as disciples and help them grow spiritually. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those walking in greater obedience and faith while seeking to deepen their knowledge of God and the Scriptures.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small-group leaders** - Those preparing to lead others in discipleship, whether one-on-one, in a group, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers in both the **personal** and the **practical**, making it suitable for personal growth as well as group teaching. Whether you use this book to strengthen your walk with God or to train others, its content is arranged for easy application and to promote growth under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, starting with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and then moving into deeper topics such as the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic can stand on its own while also building on the previous chapters, making it easy to study step by step or focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book can serve as a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides material to prepare lessons, lead discussions, and disciple others effectively. The book includes personal reflection questions, group discussion questions for facilitators, group activities, and word studies to help deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

We have the primary source of sound doctrine—the teaching of the Holy Spirit—which was revealed and recorded in the Scriptures and passed down through the church’s teaching and tradition. This book will distill the essentials in an accessible format and include personal reflections, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities at the end of each chapter.

Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible very helpful—not because of their sheer textual accuracy, but because they help me understand the meaning conveyed by the passages in plain English.

The teachings I present in this book are not novelties. We should be skeptical of “new,” never-before-seen or unheard-of teachings concerning our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a compilation and exposition of teachings that have existed since the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor a comparison of Christian denominations. Pick up two theology books written by authors from different denominational backgrounds and you will see great harmony across much of their content, but you will also see significant disagreements. I do not claim to be inerrant, but I can say that this work has been thoroughly researched, peer-reviewed by faithful brothers and sisters (see the Acknowledgments), and strongly supported by Scripture and the widely recognized Pentecostal tradition.

I earnestly urge you to read the Bible for yourself and determine to gain a deep mastery of Scripture. I invite you to read this book with faith and expectation. My hope is that you will be restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
