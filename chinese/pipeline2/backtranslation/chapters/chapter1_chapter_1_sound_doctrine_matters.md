# Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine, simply put, is the core truths we believe and teach - truths passed down by God's faithful people, rooted in Scripture, and lived out in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus instructed His Church to ‘teach’, He was commanding them to share His doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus came and spoke to them, saying: “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me。
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you； behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age。” Amen。
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus was commanding all of us to learn His teachings and reproduce them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>），He fully embodies God's truth, wisdom, and revelation。 This manifestation, the “Word” (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14）。 Jesus’ structured teaching is His doctrine（***didaskalia***），and in keeping (obeying, guarding, protecting) His commandments（***entolē)*** we become His disciples（***mathētēs***）。

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass everything。 He alone understands the perfect design of a structure that endures and cannot be shaken。 His understanding does not come from another —— rather, it is inherent in Himself。 He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every reliable building comes from His design。 This architect represents Jesus, the Word (Logos) - the complete and perfect embodiment of God's truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>）。

<!-- p10 -->
To bring His design to reality, this architect drew up a **detailed blueprint** —— a structured plan that contains the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build a sturdy structure。 Without this blueprint, no one can build correctly。 This blueprint represents Jesus’ structured teaching, that is, His doctrine（***didaskalia***） — through it, people can understand and apply the framework of divine truth (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>）。 His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the way His wisdom is communicated and preserved, so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation。

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough。 The architect also provides **precise instructions**, detailing how each part of the structure should be assembled, where each beam should be placed, and how each element should align with His perfect design。 These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)- executable directives that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>）。 His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps to ensure the building is solid and aligned with the original blueprint。 To ignore or change His commandments would damage the integrity of the entire structure。

<!-- p12 -->
Now, imagine this architect gathering a group of **apprentices** —— those who long to learn from Him, to build under His guidance, and one day to teach others how to construct buildings that endure forever。 These apprentices carefully study His blueprint, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully practice His commandments in their work。 They do not build according to their own ideas, but according to the master’s design。 These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)- those who keep, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, aligning their lives with His words (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>）。 The more they learn under the architect’s instruction, the more skillful they become, and eventually they will also teach and train others in His way。

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine？

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the cornerstone of our faith！ He is the master architect who has the perfect design and blueprint。 Only by studying the blueprint, following the architect’s specific instructions, and building a strong partnership can we construct a life that stands firm in the storms of life。

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents。 Of Solomon。 Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain； unless the LORD guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain。
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
If we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith, we cannot expect to remain standing in the face of difficulties, challenges, suffering, persecution, or even ordinary daily life。

<!-- p19 -->
Simply having a gym membership card does not make a person healthy or fit。 You must train consistently at the gym to receive the full benefits of the membership。 Likewise, we cannot just show up at church or casually claim to be Christians； we all must be educated, trained, equipped, and exercised in the faith, and only then will we truly enjoy all the benefits of membership！

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus' commission does not end with ourselves. It is good for us to become whole, healthy, disciplined, and obedient to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, and lead others, and make them disciples, bringing them into this faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why study doctrine and be disciplined? To build our lives upon the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is not only beneficial; it also warns us. If we do not build our lives upon the rock, we will build our lives upon sand, and severe storms will sweep away our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and destiny. Proverbs teaches us: As a person thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on some foundation. Jesus calls us to build our lives upon the rock so that we may stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who had believed Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
To be a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continually abiding in His word (His logos)! The result of continually abiding in His word is that we come to know the truth, and the truth sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
A disciple, having been trained under discipline, is able to obey the Master's commands. Everyone in the New Testament church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be a disciple -\> subject yourself to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to submit to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives upon the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don't worry, we don't have to go alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given His Holy Spirit, who will guide us into all truth (John chapter 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His recorded word, contained in the Bible.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders to help build us up, bring us to maturity, keep us safe, and organize us for every good work.

<!-- p36 -->
The Christian faith is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us various specialists who, by experience, know how to help us grow to maturity; and over time, you can become one of them as well!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p38 -->
> **<u>Ephesians 4:11-16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers,
>
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ,
>
> till we all come to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;
>
> that we should no longer be children, tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, in the craftiness of deceitful scheming,
>
> but, speaking the truth in love, may grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ—
>
> from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, according to the effective working by which every part does its share, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love.
>

<!-- p39 -->
</span>

<!-- p40 -->
**Important note:** The word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called to receive the gifts of apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, or teachers, but we have all been given gifts, talents, and special roles for ministry (to be discussed later in the chapter on the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God gave these special gifts to the church to lead us toward maturity, so that we would be able and become:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- No longer like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound doctrine, we will become <u>children, tossed to and fro and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s household, but children are by nature:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or multiply.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to discern good and evil.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - concerned only with the temporary and the immediate.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - easily harmed.

<!-- p53 -->
### Sound doctrine provides defense against the evils of the world

<!-- p54 -->
In this life, we do not have the luxury of remaining spiritually neutral. We are either influenced by God’s kingdom or by the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul charged Timothy, his spiritual son in the Lord, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I charge you before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when people will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires; because they have itching ears, they will heap up for themselves teachers;
>
> and they will turn their ears away from the truth, and be turned aside to myths.
>

<!-- p58 -->
</span>

<!-- p59 -->
Timothy, an aspiring leader in the church, was taught to have a deep understanding of doctrine. Paul warned Timothy that there would be a period when people would not endure sound doctrine, and would turn away! <!-- p60 -->

>
> unloving, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control

What is His doctrine? What will we learn?

<!-- p88 -->
In this work, we will mainly study the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. These doctrines are called foundational, on the one hand because Scripture calls them so, and more importantly because they provide learners with a solid foundation upon which to build their lives.

<!-- p89 -->

<!-- p96 -->

<!-- p97 -->
5.  Laying on of hands

<!-- p98 -->

<!-- p99 -->
7.  Eternal judgment

<!-- p100 -->
We will also study the core elements in our Christian journey of faith:

<!-- p101 -->
- Sanctification by the Holy Spirit

<!-- p102 -->
- The Church and our role within it

<!-- p103 -->
- Making disciples

<!-- p104 -->
- Spiritual authority

<!-- p105 -->
We will study these from the church’s context, and in such a way that we may ‘go on to perfection (maturity)’。 Our study will focus on the different perspectives and dimensions of the Godhead as revealed to us: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit。
