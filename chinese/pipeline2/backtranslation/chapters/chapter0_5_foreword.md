# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively， sound systems and structures are indispensable。 Without them， disorder and chaos easily arise。 This is why establishing a solid foundation in doctrine is crucial to fully living out a healthy Christian life。

<!-- p2 -->
This is why "*Principles of Faith*" will become a powerful resource for believers。 From the first chapter addressing the importance of sound doctrine， to the remaining 300-plus pages filled with teaching and activities about the church and our role in it， as well as understanding and discerning God’s voice， this book is exceedingly valuable。 It will be an excellent tool in the classroom for teaching and preparing leaders with a solid doctrinal foundation， and it will also greatly benefit individual readers。

<!-- p3 -->
Over the years， Andrew has been an indispensable member of my inner circle， and his abilities and the way he understands matters of the Kingdom have consistently impressed me。 He carries a genuine passion to ensure that the church functions on earth as a healthy expression of Christ。 I am deeply proud of the work he has accomplished in this book， and because of my long-term collaboration with him， I can confidently attest that he has invested a great deal of time and thought into this process， just as he does with everything in his life。
