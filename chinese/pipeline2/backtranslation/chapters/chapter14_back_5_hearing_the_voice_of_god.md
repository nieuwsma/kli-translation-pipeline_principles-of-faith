# Listening to God's Voice

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest appendix written by Pastor Larry Low（[<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)），supplements our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit，providing practical insights on how to listen to God's voice。The following guest contribution is used by permission。

<!-- p2 -->
One morning while praying, I asked **God** this question：“In the days that are coming upon us, what is the most important thing I can teach Your people？”He answered me quickly, and I felt it was very urgent, because He said：“**Teach them to hear My voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me such direction, I always pick up pen and paper and begin to write down the little I know, or think I know, and the Scriptures that support it。我 like to do word studies in Scripture on the subjects the Lord leads me to teach。I love the anointing to preach, but I love teaching God’s Word even more。Seeking those things that have always been there yet have been overlooked is an adventure。

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to uncover God’s truth, for he prayed in Psalm 119:18：“Open my eyes, that I may see wondrous things from Your law.”

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-the wonders in His Word。

<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on hearing and discerning God’s voice, He said this in my heart：

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice，  
teach them to honor and obey.*”

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice, and I will often lead them away:  
away from the troubles that will destroy them，*

<!-- p9 -->
*away from the trials they are in，  
to a place of peace、 love and joy，few have ever entered。*

<!-- p10 -->
*to a place of renewal，  
a place of holy joy。*

<!-- p11 -->
*Oh, yes, son, teach them to hear My voice  
and these people shall be free forever*。”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 AM

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you：

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing God’s voice is not an audible sound outside of you, but a certainty in your heart。</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was a time when His word came so clear and powerful, it was almost like an audible voice。I even answered Him out loud because it was so real。然而，there was no one there but the Lord and me, so I could not be sure whether He had spoken to me the way we speak to each other。

<!-- p16 -->
When Jesus was baptized in the Jordan, the Father said：“This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased”，yet among those present some even said the sound was like thunder。所以 I suppose He may also have spoken to me in an audible way；but at this moment, I want you to understand that when I say God spoke to me, it was less a voice my ears heard than a certainty in my heart。

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>This certainty is not constructed by logical reasoning in your mind；however, it is a complete thought or understanding。</u>

<!-- p18 -->
The Lord has never said anything to me that left me confused。He has also never said anything to me that left me with a ‘Why.’ question。It is always a certainty that comes with complete understanding。For God is not a God of confusion（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>）。

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, what He gives is a complete thought。</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never lets us fill in the gaps ourselves. In an instant, God can fill your heart with enough information for you to preach for a lifetime. One touch from God can fill you with fire so that you burn forever. He is the perfect Communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He never drives; He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a shepherd. Herdsmen drive; shepherds lead. Jesus is the great Shepherd of the flock, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He never condemns; He convicts.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who are born again through their confession of faith in the precious blood shed by **Jesus** and His work on Calvary（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5）. Who wants to listen to someone who is always finding fault with us? I believe most of **God’s** children are afraid to hear **God** speak to them, because someone has told them that **God** will condemn them for this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who does the Scripture say convicts us or condemns us? It is our spirit or heart that condemns these children of **God**, but the **Holy Spirit of God** knows all things and is greater than our heart. He is there as our Advocate. Please read the following scriptures, which will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teach us that nothing can separate us from the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teach us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our Condemner; our Counselor, not our Punisher; our Advocate and Helper, not our Hindrance. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 to see how the Holy Spirit (**God’s** voice) helps, comforts, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you may know it is God and not your own thoughts or the voice of a familiar spirit.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test the spirits” so that we can be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing God speak, you have the right and the responsibility to ask that voice a question (this is testing or verifying that spirit). The question to ask is: “Has Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” One thing a familiar spirit will never confess is that Jesus came to the earth in the flesh. He is fully man and fully God. No false spirit or religion will acknowledge that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed all the sins of mankind once and for all. Test the voices you hear. You must also know that God will never lead you away from His written Word—the Bible. If what you hear cannot be confirmed in the Bible, it is not from God. Reject it! Test the voices you hear to see whether they are from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always glorifies Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the voice from heaven. He works with us on earth, and Jesus said He would speak only what He hears, and He always glorifies Jesus（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>）. He did not come to glorify your ministry, your works, or all the programs of the church. Those things are fine and are mostly needed for what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and give glory to the person of Jesus. If you are being glorified because of the voice you hear, what you are hearing is not from God. Being glorified is different from being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will encourage and strengthen you every time He speaks to you, even when He is correcting (guiding) you with the Word. But “glory” is when you believe you are something special. Without Jesus you are nothing-He is the One, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God will always reveal things to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified adds to the word “reveal”: declare, disclose, transmit. **God** always declares to us and then discloses, and then through your faith, He transmits it to us. Also study <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Please read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never disturbs us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace comes from a certainty that gives us confidence before God, and that becomes perfect peace. If the voice you are hearing disturbs you, you are not hearing God-turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives, but given through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His abiding presence（<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>）.

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God always brings truth.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Please read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit “the Spirit of truth.” God cannot lie! He is truth, and therefore He will speak only truth. I have heard people say, “I can’t believe what I heard was from God. It’s too good to be true.” It may indeed seem too good to be true; that’s why you must test that spirit against the Word of God to determine that it is the truth. Some people just can’t believe because what they hear is always positive, and they have been taught that God will condemn us for our mistakes. God will only speak truth, and that is always good news. The sad fact is that most people hear only bad news from the voices they listen to and never expect to hear good news, so when good news comes, they question that it might not be real. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth** that makes us free, so how could **truth** not be good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always leads us forward and upward.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Please read Deuteronomy 28:1-2，<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>，and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never lets you fall! He will never belittle you; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He does so from this point of reference: that everything the Word has said about you has already been accomplished and has already been received by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you great and unsearchable things which you do not know.” Does that sound like belittling you, or like gloom and despair? There is no gloom or despair in God, so He cannot tell you anything gloomy or bleak. If you want to hear gloom and despair, you need to tune in to the news agencies of this world.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.</span>

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Since God has good plans for us to give us peace, a future, and hope，why would He ever say the opposite? The answer is: He never would, and He has not! He will only lead us upward、forward with great hope, to give us the future we hope for。

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this will encourage you to “call on Him”，and to learn how to **hear His voice**。He has many wonderful things to tell you，but before all this goodness can benefit you，He needs your ears that are willing to listen。
