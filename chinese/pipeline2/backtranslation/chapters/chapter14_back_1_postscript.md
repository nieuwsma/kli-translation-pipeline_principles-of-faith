# Afterword

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, dear reader, my hope and prayer for you is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you nearer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit-it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, how we love, and how we serve in God’s kingdom.

<!-- p2 -->
If I could leave only one charge, it would be this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths presented in this book are not to be shelved after a single reading; they are to be lived out daily. The journey of following Christ is a journey of continual learning, refining, and renewal unto transformation. We go from faith to faith, from glory to glory, continually pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may still have unanswered questions. You may feel you have only brushed the surface of these profound and eternal truths. That is a very good place to be. Let these questions drive you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, your comforter-when you seek Him, He will lead you into all truth.
