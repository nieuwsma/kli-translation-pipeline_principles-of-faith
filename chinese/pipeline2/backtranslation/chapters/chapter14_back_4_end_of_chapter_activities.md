# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce each chapter’s core learning objectives. Each activity is expected to take about 15-20 minutes to complete, and may require additional resources not included in this book. Combined with personal reflection and group discussion questions, these activities are most effective in a small-group format for deepening understanding of and engagement with the book’s key points. Use these activities as a reference guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Scripture Matching

<!-- p4 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p5 -->
By studying passages that emphasize the importance of doctrine, reinforce key biblical concepts about doctrine.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or groups**, 3-4 per group.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Scriptures** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach God’s word, and guard against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Attain spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Have each group:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read their assigned passage** out loud

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching**, in 1-2 sentences explain what it says about doctrine

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example**, showing how this teaching can be applied to guard against error, or to help others grow in the faith

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Each group takes **2 minutes** to present to the whole group their passage, summary, and application.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life scenarios** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Grouping** - Divide people into small groups of **4-5 participants** each.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Assign each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend says to you, "As long as we are sincere, it doesn't matter what we believe." How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are counseling a new believer who has heard conflicting teachings about being saved by faith versus saved by works. What Scriptures could you share to help clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend has stopped attending church because they think it isn't necessary as long as they believe in God. How would you encourage them with Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - Each group should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the core doctrinal issue** in the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 passages** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** explaining how they would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - Each group presents their scenario and answers to everyone in turn, then receives others' **brief feedback or additions**.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to be grounded in Scripture and **discern truth from error**.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Set up the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud one by one. Some statements are **biblical truth**, while others are **errors** or **misunderstandings**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Guide the discussion** - Before confirming the correct answers, allow time for groups to discuss differences or look up passages.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *We are saved by faith alone.* *(Correct - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *As long as we are sincere, all roads lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name That Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect God's names and attributes with **practical life application**.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Guidelines:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - On each card write **one name or attribute of God** (e.g., Jehovah Jireh, faithfulness, omnipresence).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or each group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read that **name or attribute** in this chapter along with the corresponding **Scripture reference**.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Summary** - Ask each participant or group to **present their reflections**, emphasizing how God's character can be applied in practical ways to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: Using God's Names in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate God’s names and attributes into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Guidelines:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Schedule reflection time** - Provide participants a **quiet space** to meditate on one name of God from this chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that fits the current need (e.g., **Jehovah Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Use that name to write a short **prayer**, praise God, and ask Him to act according to His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayer in a group or in pairs (optional) to **encourage one another and build faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **End with corporate prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Experiencing God’s Attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants **reflect on and apply** God’s attributes to their lives through **personal stories** and **corporate prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Guidelines:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Attribute focus:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a list of God’s **3-4 attributes** from this chapter (e.g., **faithfulness, mercy, sovereignty, provider**).

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** they feel best fits their current life season.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story sharing (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **share a story** (personal experience or observation) related to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Sample prompt:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When have you experienced God as your provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *"How has God's mercy shaped your life?"*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *"In daily life, how can we encourage one another to rely on this attribute of God?"*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer** to reflect this attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God's character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Purpose:**

<!-- p87 -->
Explain how the **effects of sin spread outward**, impacting **relationships, communities, and spiritual life**, while emphasizing God's **plan of redemption**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Guidelines:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a **stone into a bowl of water** (if materials are unavailable, describe the scene).

<!-- p91 -->
- Have participants **observe the outward-spreading ripples** and discuss:  
      *"How does this image illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?"*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into small groups and assign each group a **biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and its consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What are the immediate and long-term effects of the sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *In each story, how do we see God's grace and opportunities for restoration?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What “ripples” of sin have you personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God's grace worked to stop those ripples and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

2.  **Group task:**

<!-- p115 -->

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or redemption** provided.

<!-- p117 -->

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Have each group share their insights, connecting these passages into a **timeline of God's redemptive plan**.

<!-- p121 -->
#### Purpose：

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants to **personally practice forgiveness**-both **receive** it，and **extend** it to others。

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions：

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection（5 minutes）：**

<!-- p125 -->
- Invite participants to **quietly reflect** on the following areas：

<!-- p126 -->
- Where they need to **confess** and seek God's forgiveness。

<!-- p127 -->

<!-- p128 -->

<!-- p129 -->

<!-- p135 -->

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Purpose:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and deal with the “dead works” in their lives and replace them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a **List of Possible Dead Works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect quietly and identify **one or two areas** in their lives where they see evidence of dead works.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of Possible Dead Works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals lacking faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church gatherings out of habit without heartfelt engagement.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Attempting to attain righteousness by personal effort rather than relying on God's grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to gain favor while neglecting the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Patterns of sin or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Harboring resentment or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, jealousy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending too much time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Behaviors driven by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of obligation rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving money or participating in volunteer service merely as a formality rather than out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Placing material success above spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups to share (if they are willing) one aspect they have identified as dead works.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to **replace dead works** with actions filled with the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Summary Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with prayer, asking God to guide us to live a more fruitful life.
