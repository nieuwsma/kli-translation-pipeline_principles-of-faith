# Chapter 14 - Watchfulness

<!-- p1 -->
Since we have been given Christ’s spiritual authority on earth to establish His Kingdom, we ought to work diligently and swiftly, for Jesus is coming again!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important component of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the end times is called **eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, *most of which disagree with one another*! Specifically, they differ in their interpretation of Scripture (figurative, literal, symbolic), and in the order and timing of events. I am reminded of a saying by the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonnke - “When I’m done breaking chains on earth, I’ll go to heaven to nitpick!”

<!-- p3 -->
As the author, given that this book mainly focuses on the foundations of the faith, I am not inclined to delve deeply into this hotly debated topic. I will largely stick to a high-level overview of the events depicted in Scripture and focus on what is most important - **the** **Gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Points of general agreement:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus will come again

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->

<!-- p10 -->

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, if you are curious about common points of disagreement in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most frequently discussed points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture：** Before, during, or after the Great Tribulation? Regarding **when** the rapture occurs relative to the **Great Tribulation**, views vary. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the Great Tribulation：** Is it a literal judgment on Israel, or a symbolic period of struggle?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial reign：** Is it literal **(Premillennialism)**, symbolic **(Amillennialism)**, or spiritual **(Postmillennialism)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion：** Occurs after the Millennium (is it a literal event or metaphorical？).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is good, but let us remember, as D.L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing the main thing！”

## **Final Thoughts**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the climax of God’s plan and the reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the parable of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the reward of crowns or the hope of resurrection, our motivation is very clear-Christ is coming again！

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found prepared、faithful and full of hope on that glorious day！**
