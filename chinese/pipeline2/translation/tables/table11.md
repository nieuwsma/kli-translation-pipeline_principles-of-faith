<th colspan="2"><h2 id="table-11---attributes-of-god">表 11 - 神的属性</h2></th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>属性</strong></p>
<p>类别</p>
<p>参考经文</p></th>
<th><strong>描述</strong></th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>爱</strong></p>
<p>关系的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:8</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="37-39" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:37-39</span></p></th>
<th>渴望与祂的受造物建立关系，并以委身与自我牺牲来表达。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>信实</strong></p>
<p>关系的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 10:13</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 2:13</span></p></th>
<th>完全可信，祂的应许永不落空。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>怜悯</strong></p>
<p>关系的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">TIT 3:5</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 103:8-18</span></p></th>
<th>神圣的良善，表现为减轻祂受造物的苦难，并对罪人显出忍耐。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>慈悲</strong></p>
<p>关系的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="LAM" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22-23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">LAM 3:22-23</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="145" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 145:8-9</span></p></th>
<th>神对人类的苦难深有感触，并以同理与关怀回应。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>良善</strong></p>
<p>道德的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 25:8</span></p></th>
<th>完全良善，虽然恶的存在源于自由意志和自然律。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>公正</strong></p>
<p>道德的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="89" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 89:14</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="DEU" data-chapter="32" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Deuteronomy 32:4</span></p></th>
<th>神确保公义，纠正不义并奖赏正义。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>忌邪</strong></p>
<p>道德的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 34:14</span></p></th>
<th>一种保护性的爱，渴望祂子民的专一忠诚。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>公义</strong></p>
<p>道德的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 11:3-4</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="AMO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">AMO 3:2</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="61" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 61:10</span></p></th>
<th>符合完美的标准，定恶人的罪，并赦免悔改者。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>圣洁</strong></p>
<p>道德的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 6:3</span></p></th>
<th>完全纯洁，与罪分别，不能容忍它。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>至高无上</strong></p>
<p>基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="DAN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="35" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">DAN 4:35</span></p></th>
<th>有权按祂所喜意治理并处置祂的受造物。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>智慧</strong></p>
<p>基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:2-3</span></p></th>
<th>总是在正确的时间以正确的方式行事。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>全知</strong></p>
<p>基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="147" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 147:5</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 6:3</span></p></th>
<th>知识完全，无需学习便能即时明白万事。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>无所不在</strong></p>
<p>基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">PRO 15:3</span></p></th>
<th>无处不在，察看万事，但祂亲自居住在与祂有关系之处。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>全能</strong> 基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JOB" data-chapter="42" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">JOB 42:2</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 19:26</span></p></th>
<th>全能，且自由去行一切与祂本性相一致的事；不能行与祂本性相违背的事。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>不变的</strong> 基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="102" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 102:27</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Hebrews 13:8</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JAS" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">James 1:17</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">MAL 3:6</span></p></th>
<th>本性与品格不改变，永远不变。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>无限</strong></p>
<p>基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="1KI" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1KI 8:27</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="DEU" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Deuteronomy 33:27</span></p></th>
<th>不受空间或时间限制，存在于各处且永恒不息。</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th><p><strong>神是灵</strong> 基础的</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 4:24</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="37" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 5:37</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PHP" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Philippians 2:6</span></p></th>
<th>神是有位格的灵，具有思想、情感，并与祂的受造物相交。祂没有物质的身体，却是真实、无限的存在。</th>
</tr>
