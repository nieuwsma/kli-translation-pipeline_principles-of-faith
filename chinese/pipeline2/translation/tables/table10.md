<th colspan="3"><h2 id="table-10---old-testament-names-of-god">表 10 - 旧约中神的名称</h2></th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>名称</strong> 发音</td>
<td><p><strong>含义</strong></p>
<p>经文参考</p></td>
<td><strong>意义</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>我是自有永有</strong></p>
<p>Eh-YEH a-sher Eh-YEH</p></td>
<td><p>自有自存、永恒</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 3:14</span></p></td>
<td>神向摩西所启示的根本之名，肯定其永恒、不改变、并自我维系的本性。此名强调神凭自己的大能而存在，并且永远如此。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Elohim</strong></p>
<p>Eh-loh-HEEM</p></td>
<td><p>神、创造主</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 19:1</span></p></td>
<td>强调神作为创造主的权能与威严；为复数形式，表示伟大或三位一体。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh (Jehovah)</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh (jeh-HO-vah)</p></td>
<td><p>主, "I AM"</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 3:14</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="42" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 42:8</span></p></td>
<td>神的立约之名，表明其永恒、自有自存的本性，并对以色列的信实。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Adonai</strong></p>
<p>Ah-doh-NIGH</p></td>
<td><p>主、主宰</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 15:2</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 16:2</span></p></td>
<td>体现神的权柄与主权，承认他是受造界当之无愧的统治者与拥有者。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Shaddai</strong></p>
<p>El shad-DYE</p></td>
<td><p>全能的神、全足者</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 17:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="91" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 91:1</span></p></td>
<td>表明神有能力滋养、赐福并丰富供应；显明他的全备。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Jireh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh YI-rah</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华必预备</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 22:14</span></p></td>
<td>象征神的供应；首次用于神为以撒预备一只公羊作替代。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Rapha</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-fah</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华医治</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 15:26</span></p></td>
<td>彰显神医治的属性；他使人身心灵得着恢复。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Nissi</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh NEE-see</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华是我旌旗</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 17:15</span></p></td>
<td>代表神的保护与得胜；摩西在战胜亚玛力人后使用此名。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-M’kaddesh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Meh-KAH-desh</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华使人成圣</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">LEV 20:8</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 31:13</span></p></td>
<td>指出神是那位借着其诫命与恩典使他的百姓分别为圣的。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Shalom</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh shah-LOME</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华是平安</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JDG" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">JDG 6:24</span></p></td>
<td>基甸在神的安慰后使用此名；强调神是平安与完全的源头。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Sabaoth</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Sa-ba-OTE</p></td>
<td><p>万军之耶和华 (军队)</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="1SA" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Samuel 1:3</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="46" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 46:7</span></p></td>
<td>指神对一切天上与地上一切势力的至高权能；强调他的主权。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Raah</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-ah</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华是我的牧者</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 23:1</span></p></td>
<td>大卫用此名来表明神的引导、看顾与供应，如同牧人对其羊群。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Tsidkenu</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Tsid-KAY-noo</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华我们的义</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 23:6</span></p></td>
<td>表明神是义与公义的源头，并在弥赛亚里得以成全。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Shammah</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh SHAH-mah</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华在那里</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EZK" data-chapter="48" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="35" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">EZK 48:35</span></p></td>
<td>用于描述神永存的同在；象征神与他的子民同在，即使在被掳之中。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Elyon</strong></p>
<p>El EL-yawn</p></td>
<td><p>至高的神</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 14:18-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="57" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 57:2</span></p></td>
<td>表示神的至上权柄与被高举的位置，高过一切其他的神与权势。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Olam</strong></p>
<p>El OH-lahm</p></td>
<td><p>永在的神</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="21" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 21:33</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="40" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 40:28</span></p></td>
<td>强调神的永恒本性；他超越时间且不改变。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Makkeh</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh MAH-keh</p></td>
<td><p>耶和华施行击打</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EZK" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">EZK 7:9</span>, 22:31</p></td>
<td>强调神在管教与纠正中的公义；提醒其圣洁标准。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Yahweh-Gmolah<br/>
</strong>YAH-weh GIM-oh-lah</td>
<td><p>施行报应的神</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="51" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="56" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 51:56</span></p></td>
<td>神的公义按人的行为施行报偿与赏赐；强调神圣的公义。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Qanna</strong></p>
<p>KAH-nah</p></td>
<td><p>忌邪</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 34:14</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="DEU" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Deuteronomy 4:24</span></p></td>
<td>反映神对其子民热切的爱；显示他对偶像崇拜与不忠的不容忍。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>El Roi</strong></p>
<p>El ROY-ee</p></td>
<td><p>看顾我的神</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 16:13</span></p></td>
<td>出自主哈迦；强调神对个人挣扎的关注与察觉。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Channun</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh khan-NOON</p></td>
<td><p>施恩的耶和华</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="86" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 86:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 34:6</span></p></td>
<td>强调神的怜悯，以及他乐意赦免并施予怜悯。</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Elyon</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh EL-yawn</p></td>
<td><p>至高的耶和华</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 7:17</span>, 47:2</p></td>
<td>确认神至高无上的权柄与地位，高过一切。</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
