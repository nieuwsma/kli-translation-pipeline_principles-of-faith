# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the core learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity is intended to take about 15-20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. Alongside personal reflection questions and group discussion, these activities are best used in a group study format to deepen understanding and engage with the book's key lessons. Use these activities as guidelines. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as you see fit.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Matching Scripture Passages

<!-- p4 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce core biblical concepts about teaching by exploring texts that affirm its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or small groups** of 3-4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign scripture passages** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preaching the word and warning against false teaching.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Ask each group to:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read their assigned scripture passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** about doctrine in 1-2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** of how to apply this teaching to guard against error or help others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Each group takes **two minutes** to present its scripture passage, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Teaching in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound teaching to real-life situations and strengthen practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Identify the scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you: “It really doesn’t matter what we believe, as long as we are sincere.” How do you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are guiding a new believer who hears conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. What scripture verses can you share to help clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops attending church because he feels it is unnecessary as long as he believes in God. How would you encourage him using the Bible?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 scripture verses** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would guide the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - The groups take turns presenting their scenarios and answers to the group, followed by **brief comments or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Bible Verse?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **discern truth from error** using the Bible as their foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Activity setup** - Read the following **statements** aloud, one at a time. Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- decide whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- provide a **scripture verse** that supports their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate the discussion** - Allow time for the groups to discuss points of disagreement or to look up verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All roads lead to God if we are sincere.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Right teaching is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelations to guide us.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Good works are the result of salvation, not its cause.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:10</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - the Eternal Creator and the Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name That Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God with **practical life applications**.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - Write **one name or attribute of God** (e.g., Jehovah-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent) on each card.

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** and the **Scripture reference** accompanying it from the chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **one-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Closing** - Ask each participant or group to **present their reflections**, emphasizing the practical ways God’s character applies to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to integrate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet place** for the participants to reflect on one of God's names from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses a current need (for example، **Yahweh-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Write a short **prayer** using that name، glorifying God and asking Him to act in accordance with His attributes.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs for **encouragement and building faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Conclude with a group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering God's Attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants to **reflect and apply** God's attributes in their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on the attributes:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God (e.g.: **Faithful، Merciful، Lord over all، Provider**) taken from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** it feels is most aligned with its current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Sharing stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly shares a **story** (a personal experience or an observation) related to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Examples of prompts:

<!-- p79 -->
- *"When have you experienced God as your Provider؟"*

<!-- p80 -->
- *"How has God's mercy shaped your life?"*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *"How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life?"*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer** that reflects the attribute and to pray together, focusing on applying God's attributes in their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Purpose:**

<!-- p87 -->
To illustrate how **the effects of sin ripple outward**, impacting **relationships, communities, and spiritual life**, while emphasizing **God's redemptive plan**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop **a stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples** as they spread outward and discuss:  
      *"How does this scene illustrate the impact of sin in our lives and relationships?"*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small-group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each group **a biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What are the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Meditation (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some of the “ripples” of sin that you have personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace been at work to stop those ripples and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->
### Activity 2: Timeline of Sin and Restoration (A Tour Through the Bible)

<!-- p105 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p106 -->
Follow **God’s redemptive plan** from **Genesis to Christ**, focusing on **His grace** in the midst of **judgment**.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups** - Assign each group a **Scripture passage** that highlights sin and restoration:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:21</span>** - God provided coverings for Adam and Eve.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warned Cain before he sinned.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat carries sin away.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group task:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the **sin or judgment** in the passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the **grace or redemption** presented.

<!-- p117 -->
- Summarize the **lesson learned** about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Ask each group to share its insights, linking the passages together into a **timeline of God’s plan** of redemption.

### Activity 3: Practicing Forgiveness (My Prayer Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p122 -->
Guide participants to **process forgiveness personally**-whether **accepting it** or **granting it** to others.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Meditation (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **quietly reflect** on areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God's forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone else** who wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in a private prayer. It is not necessary for the facilitator to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confessing sins to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Praying for strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g.: forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (optionally) **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 4 - Jesus-the Messiah

<!-- p138 -->
### Activity 1: Names and Titles of Jesus (Meditation and Bible Study)

<!-- p139 -->
#### Purpose:
