# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Lasting Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always had a fascination with questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How can we be confident? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond merely wanting to be right at a superficial level, to knowing deeply and precisely, and to being able to clearly articulate the newly gained understanding.

<!-- p3 -->
Although communication is common, effective and accurate communication is difficult. And for content to be conveyed effectively, the writer and the creator must have an idea. Yet an idea by itself is like water on a hard surface-its edges and boundaries are not well defined, fluid, and easily moved.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to transform the raw idea from a vague form into a precise form. Words give our idea a boundary, and help us distinguish and separate between what the idea is and what it is not. Now our formulated idea must be transmitted and delivered through some medium. Whether in a long text, like this book, or a short text, like a social media post, or in a spoken form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium carries the well-formulated idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point, we have only discussed the originator of the idea and its author. For the idea to be useful, the recipient must receive the message, decode it, and understand it accurately. The sent message and the received message must match to a large degree.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human work is a byproduct of effective communication. The work of a single man can accomplish only a limited amount. And to build or perform something truly great, many must work together (I speak here generally of humankind) toward a common end, and with a common idea. With the exception of natural disasters (such as a volcanic eruption) we also know that the greatest catastrophes that have ever occurred happened in large part because of communication that was missing, broken, or distorted. Truly, death and life are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build a person, a family, a community, or a nation, or has the same power to destroy them. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will be, and how we will exist. And since belief is expressed through action and communication, it determines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs not only shape our lives, but to some extent the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must firmly establish our beliefs and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said this when He told His disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today desire a stronger faith but lack a clear foundation to build upon. Without sound doctrine, we lack the solid foundation we need to stand firm; and we risk being swayed by false teaching, being discouraged in trials, or being unprepared for the work God has called us to. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture-truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and enable you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be “rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To grow into Spirit-led, mature believers, we must first have deep roots in the truth of God. This is why doctrine matters. It is more than mere information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book aim to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the core doctrines of the New Testament Church. I hope these beliefs will persuade you and inspire your faith journey to put down deep roots in Christ and to come to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings were formed and shaped through my experience in the church, especially in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The arrangement of the teachings in this book is based on my personal study of Scripture, both formal and informal education, and my experience in nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of that time as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelism and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who seek to strengthen their faith or to disciple others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - who seek to deepen their understanding of God and of Scripture as they walk in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - who are preparing to disciple others, whether individually, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers on both the **personal** and **practical** levels, making it ideal for personal growth as well as group teaching. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your walk with God or to train others, the content is organized for ease of application and for Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence, beginning with foundational truths such as **God** and **sin** and **repentance** and **faith**, then moving into the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit** and **discipleship** and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands on its own, but it also builds on the previous chapters, making it easy to progress step by step or to focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book functions as a guide for understanding the Scriptures and applying them. And for **teachers**, it provides material for preparing lessons, leading discussions, and discipling others effectively. Each unit includes questions for personal reflection, questions for facilitating group discussion, group activities, and word studies to help deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound doctrine, namely the teachings of the Holy Spirit, inspired and recorded in the Scriptures, and handed down through the teaching and traditions of the Church. This book will take the foundational teachings, present them in an easy-to-understand format, and conclude each chapter with a personal reflection, questions to facilitate group discussion, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, many eyewitnesses and apostles documented accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical matters within the early Christian communities. These writings, divinely inspired, were recorded, collected, and carefully preserved over the centuries, forming what is now known as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
In general, we will use the New King James Version translation, but we may also use a different translation where that makes the passage clearer. *Bible references will be written in italics to facilitate reading.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly recommend using multiple translations whenever possible to ensure the concept is clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find translations such as the Amplified Bible helpful, not for their purely textual accuracy, but because they help me understand what the Scriptures convey in plain English.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore some key words in the Bible (**word studies**) so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic Bible resources such as: E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources will also give you access to Strong’s Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong’s Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original languages, so that we can hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated as OT in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated as NT in this book) was written in Greek. The Strong’s number will be indicated as follows: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are not new or novel. We should be skeptical of ‘new’ teachings that have not been seen or heard before concerning our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have existed from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparison between Christian denominations. Pick up any two books on theology written by different authors with varying denominational backgrounds, and while you will see great harmony in much of their work, you will also see notable division. I will not claim infallibility, but I will claim that this work is well-researched, peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgments section), and well supported by Scripture and by the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

I urge you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to mastering them.
