# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, there must be order and a solid structure. Without them, dysfunction can arise and chaos can easily spread. Therefore, laying a strong foundation in doctrine is crucial to living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
Therefore, "*Principles of Faith*" will be a powerful resource for believers. From the first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining 300-plus pages filled with teachings and activities about the church and our roles in it, as well as understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is priceless. It will be an exceptional tool in the classroom for teaching and preparing leaders on a solid doctrinal foundation, and it will also be extremely beneficial for individual readers.
