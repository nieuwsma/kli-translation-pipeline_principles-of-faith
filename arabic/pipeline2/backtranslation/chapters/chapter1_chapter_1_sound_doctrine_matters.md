# Chapter 1 - The Importance of Sound Doctrine

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine simply means the essential truths that we believe and teach-truths conveyed by people faithful to God، rooted in the Holy Bible، and lived in our daily walk.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His church to «teach»، He was instructing them to share His teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus came and spoke to them, saying: «All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,
>
> and teach them to keep all that I have commanded you؛ and behold, I am with you all the days to the end of the age». Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words، Jesus instructs each one of us to learn His teachings and to reproduce them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)، and He fully embodies the truth of God, His wisdom, and His revelation. This embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh and dwelt among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>، 14). Jesus’s organized teachings are His doctrine (***didaskalia***)، and by keeping (obedience, guarding, protection) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a skilled architect, the greatest builder in all of history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect design for a solid, unshakable structure. His understanding is not derived from another, but is inherent in him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sound building finds its origin in his design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos-the complete and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, His wisdom, and His revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>، <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
And to bring his design into reality, the architect sets out a **detailed blueprint**-an organized plan that includes the principles, proportions, and fundamental truths necessary for a building that will remain firm. Without this blueprint no one can build properly. This blueprint represents the organized teaching of Jesus, that is, his doctrine (***didaskalia***) — the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>، <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge؛ rather, it is the means by which his wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that everyone who follows him can build his life on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
But the blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **precise instructions**, detailing how each part of the structure should be assembled, where each beam is to be placed, and how every element should conform to his overall design. These specific instructions represent his commandments **(Entolē**)-the practical directives that regulate obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>، <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules؛ rather, they are the necessary steps to ensure the stability of the structure and its consistency with the original blueprint. Ignoring or distorting his commandments endangers the integrity of the whole structure.

<!-- p12 -->
And now, imagine the architect gathering a group of **apprentices**-those who desire to learn from him, to build under his guidance, and someday to teach others how to construct buildings that endure. These apprentices study his blueprint carefully, listen attentively to his instructions, and faithfully apply his commandments in their work. They do not build according to their own ideas, but according to the master’s design. These apprentices represent the disciples **(Mathētēs**)-those who observe, obey, and internalize the teachings of Jesus, aligning their lives with his word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>، <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The longer they learn under the architect, the more skilled they become, and in the end they teach and train others in his ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine؟

<!-- p14 -->
The teaching of Jesus is the bedrock of our faith! He is the teacher-architect with the perfect design and blueprint. And only by studying the blueprints، obeying the architect’s specific instructions، and developing a strong working relationship with him، can we build a life that stands in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain؛ unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman keeps watch in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to withstand hardships, challenges, trials, and persecutions، or even the details of daily life، unless we build our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
Gym membership alone is not enough for a person to become healthy and physically fit. Rather, one must continue in the discipline of the gym to obtain the full benefits of membership. Likewise، we cannot be content with attending church or the passing claim that we are Christians؛ rather, we must all learn, train, be equipped, and be disciplined in the faith, and only then will we receive the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’s commission goes beyond our boundaries. It is wonderful to become complete, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make other disciples in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and become disciplined? So that we build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
Even so, this is more than just a benefit; it also constitutes a warning to us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, then we are building our lives on sand, and a strong storm will shatter our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs dictate our actions and our destiny. The book of Proverbs teaches us that as a person thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is ours!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by abiding in His word (His Logos)! And the result of abiding in His word is our knowing the truth, which is what sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, after they have learned discipline, are able to carry out the Master’s commands. Everyone in the New Testament Church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  To be disciples -\> to submit ourselves to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  To make disciples -\> to teach others to submit to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we begin building our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we don’t have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead us and guide us into all the truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His words recorded in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us gifted, spiritually mature leaders to help build us up, make us mature, keep us safe, and organize us to do good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect Coach who knows exactly what we need. And He has given us specialists who, through experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and over time you too can be one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

**Important note:** The word 'some' indicates that not all of us are called with the gift of an apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but we have all been given gifts for service, talents, and special roles (this will be discussed later in the chapter on the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given the church these special gifts to bring us to maturity, so that we become:

<!-- p42 -->
- capable of working and bearing fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- united in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- mature

<!-- p46 -->
- not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we will be <u>children, tossed and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children by nature are:

<!-- p49 -->
- immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- naive - unable to distinguish between the good and the bad.

<!-- p51 -->
- self-centered - preoccupied with the transient and the present.

<!-- p52 -->
- unprotected - vulnerable to harm.

What are His teachings, what will we study?

<!-- p88 -->

<!-- p89 -->

<!-- p95 -->
3.  Faith toward God

<!-- p96 -->

<!-- p97 -->
5.  Laying on of hands

<!-- p98 -->
6.  Resurrection of the dead

<!-- p99 -->

<!-- p100 -->
We will also study essential elements in our Christian journey:

<!-- p101 -->
- Sanctification by the Holy Spirit

<!-- p102 -->

<!-- p103 -->
- Making disciples

<!-- p104 -->

We will study each of these within the context of the church, and we study them so that we may ‘advance to perfection (maturity)’. Our study will focus on the different perspectives and aspects of theology as revealed to us: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit.
