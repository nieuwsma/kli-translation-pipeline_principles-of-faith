# Postscript

<!-- p1 -->
As I close this book, my hope and prayer for you, dear reader, is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit—it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the Kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would like to leave you with, it is this: Keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths outlined in this book were not meant to be read once and set aside; they were meant to be lived daily. The journey of following Christ is one of continual learning, refinement, and transformation. We go from faith to faith, from glory to glory, always pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may have questions that remain unanswered. You may feel that you have only scratched the surface of these deep and eternal truths. That is a good place to be. May these questions lead you deeper into the Word of God, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter—He will lead you into all truth as you seek Him.
