# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a solid system and structure are essential. Without them, dysfunction and chaos can easily arise. That is why establishing a solid foundation in doctrine is crucial to living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining more than 300 pages filled with teachings and activities about the church and our roles in it, as well as about understanding and recognizing the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an exceptional classroom tool to teach and prepare leaders with a solid foundation in doctrine, in addition to being extremely beneficial for individual readers.

He has a genuine passion for ensuring that the church operates as a healthy representation of Christ on Earth. I feel great pride in the work he has done with this book and, having collaborated with him for quite some time, I can confidently attest that he invested considerable time and thought into this process, just as he does with everything in his life.
