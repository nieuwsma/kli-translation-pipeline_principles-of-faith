# Chapter 14 - Watch

<!-- p1 -->
Having been given the spiritual authority of Christ on Earth to establish His kingdom, we must work with diligence and promptness, for Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end times is known as **eschatology**. Many books on eschatology have been written, and *most of them disagree with each other*! Specifically, they disagree regarding the interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), the order and timing of the events in Scripture. I remember the saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke - "I'll argue over minutiae in heaven when I'm finished breaking chains on Earth!"

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, I am reluctant to dive too deeply into this hotly debated topic, since this book is focused mainly on the foundations of the faith. I will largely stick to the higher-level view of the events described in Scripture and focus on what matters most - **the** **Gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally Accepted Points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus Will Return

<!-- p6 -->
2.  Resurrection of the Dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for All

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal Destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A Defeated Enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  New Heaven and New Earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point, we'll look at what the concept is, examine the reference Scriptures, consider why it matters, and what we should do in light of it.

<!-- p28 -->
</span>

<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p33 -->

<!-- p34 -->

<!-- p35 -->

### **3. Judgment for all people**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers appear at the **Judgment Seat of Christ** for rewards, while unbelievers face the **Great White Throne Judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away. And no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and terrible final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers our names were placed in the **of the Lamb *Book of Life*** *(*See the book of *Revelation)**.** Thus we will be* *at the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but rather for reward! Once again**,** it is not a time of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God accomplished through us. This reminds us that our lives matter-not only for today but for **eternity**.

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

However not all will have additional reward. The New Testament mentions crowns as rewards that represent victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns highlight God’s recognition of the faithfulness and service of believers in His Kingdom.

**What should we do?**

<!-- p59 -->

<!-- p60 -->

<!-- p78 -->

## Common Points of Disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, if you are curious about what the common points of division in eschatology are, here is a **very** brief summary of the most frequently debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the Rapture:** Pre-, Mid-, or Post-Tribulation? Views differ on **when** the rapture occurs in relation to the **tribulation**. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the Tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of affliction?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial Kingdom:** Is it literal **(Premillennial)**, symbolic **(Amillennial)**, or spiritual **(Postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Final rebellion of Satan:** Does it happen after the millennium (literal or figurative event?).

<!-- p94 -->
It is great to study eschatology, but let’s remember, as D.L. Browning said: “Always keep the main thing the main thing!”

## **Final Considerations**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the culmination of God’s plan and the reason why we live and work with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always prepared for His return. Whether through crowns as rewards or the hope of the resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found ready, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**

<!-- p105 -->
