# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest appendix, from Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)), complements our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit by offering practical insights on how to hear the voice of God. The following guest contribution is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning, I asked **God** the following: “What is the most important thing I could teach Your people for the times that are coming?” And He answered me very quickly, and I felt it was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me such direction, I always take a pad and a pencil and begin to write the little I know, or what I think I know, and the Scriptures that support it. I love doing Word studies on topics the Lord is leading me to teach. I love the anointing to preach, but I really love teaching the Word. It is an adventure to find things that have always been there but were neglected.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to unveil God’s Truths, for he prayed in verse 18 of Psalm 119: “Open my eyes that I may see Wonderful Things from Your law.”

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-Wonderful Things from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
As I studied and prepared to teach the second message on hearing and discerning the voice of God, He spoke the following to my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them away:  
Away from the troubles that would destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Away from the trials they are in,  
Away, to a place of peace, love, and joy where few have ever been.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Away, to a place of refreshing,  
to a place of Divine Ecstasy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Oh yes, teach them to hear My voice, son  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible sound, but a CERTAINTY in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was a time when His Word came so clear and emphatic that it could have been audible. I answered Him speaking out loud, it was so real. However, there was no one there besides the Lord and me, so I can’t really say for sure whether He spoke to me as we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was the occasion when Jesus was being baptized in the Jordan River and the Father spoke: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some of those who were there said it sounded like thunder, so I suppose He could have spoken audibly to me, but for now, I want you to understand that when I say that God spoke to me, it is a CERTAINTY in my heart more than a sound in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>CERTAINTY is NOT built by logic in your mind; however, it is a thought or complete understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
It has never happened that the Lord told me something that confused me. It has never happened that He told me something that left me asking ‘why.’ It is always a CERTAINTY with complete UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never lets us fill in the gaps. In the blink of an eye, God can fill your heart with enough information to keep you preaching for a lifetime. One touch from God can fill you with fire that will keep you burning forever. He is a complete communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He NEVER DRIVES. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Cowboys drive, but Shepherds lead. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He NEVER CONDEMNS; He CONVICTS.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who have been Born Again (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5) by their confession of faith in the shed blood of **Jesus** and in His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who is always condemning us for our mistakes? I believe that most of **God’s** children are afraid to hear **God** speak to them because they have been told that **God** is going to condemn them for this or that. See <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who is it that says that accuses or condemns us? It is OUR SPIRIT, or heart, that condemns the child of **God**, but it is **God, the Holy Spirit**, who knows all things and is GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the Scriptures below, and this will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our accuser, our Counselor, not our punisher, our Intercessor and Helper, not our hindrance. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) aids, comforts, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you KNOW it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test every spirit” so that we can be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing from God, you have the right and the responsibility to ask that voice a question (This is testing or proving the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Did Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The one thing familiar spirits will never admit is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully man and fully God. No spirit or false religion will admit that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed humanity of all its sins for all eternity. Test the voice you hear. You also need to know that God will never lead you outside His written Word, the Bible. If you are hearing something that cannot be backed up in the Bible, you are not hearing from God. Get rid of it! Test the voice you hear to see if it is from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said that He will always speak only what He hears, and He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to bring glory to your ministry, your works, or church programs. All these things are good, and most of them are necessary for us to do what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If you are being glorified by the voice you hear, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage you and strengthen you every time He speaks to you, even when He is correcting (guiding) you with the Word. But glory is when you believe that YOU are somebody. You are nobody without Jesus—HE IS THE SOMEBODY, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God always REVEALS something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
See <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified adds the words: declare, disclose, and transmit when considering the word “reveal.” **God** always discloses something to us after declaring it to us and then, by your faith, He transmits it to us. Also study <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God always brings peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never disturbs us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace exists because there is a CERTAINTY that brings us confidence before God, which translates into perfect peace. If the voice you are hearing disturbs you, you are not hearing from God—turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives it, but as He gives it through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His constant indwelling (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “The Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is the TRUTH and, therefore, will only speak the TRUTH. I have heard people say something like: “I can’t believe that what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And maybe it is too good to be true; that is why you MUST test the spirit by confronting it with the Word of God to make sure it is the Truth. Some people simply cannot believe because what they are hearing is always positive, and they have been taught that God condemns us for wrongdoing. God will only speak the TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that most people only hear bad news from the voice they are listening to and never expect to hear good news; so, when the good news comes, they question it as possibly untrue. **The Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** sets us free, so how could the **truth** be anything other than good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always LEADS US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deut. 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never takes you down! He never puts you down; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He speaks from the assumption that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and ACCEPTED by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you GREAT AND MIGHTY things which you do not know.” Does that sound like putting you down or doom and gloom? There is NO doom and gloom in God; therefore, He cannot tell you anything dark or gloomy. You will have to tune in to the world’s news agency for doom and gloom.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the LORD, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS thoughts of peace, of a future, and of hope about us, why would He speak differently? The answer is: HE WOULD NOT, and HE DOES NOT! He only leads us UPWARD and FORWARD with great hope to provide us with a hoped-for future.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to “call upon Him” and to learn how to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs your attentive ear before all His good things can do you any good.
