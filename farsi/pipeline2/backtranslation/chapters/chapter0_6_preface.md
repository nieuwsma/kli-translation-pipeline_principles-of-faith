# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Foundation That Endures

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What has made it so? How do we know? What are we certain of? If *something* is true, what else is also true? What things are false? This fascination goes beyond merely being right at a superficial level; I want to know deeply and precisely and to be able to express the understanding gained clearly.

<!-- p3 -->
Though communication is common, effective and precise communication is challenging. To transmit a message effectively, the writer and creator must have an idea. However, the idea itself is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are faint, fluid, and easily shift.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to transform the raw idea from something vague into something precise. Words give our idea boundaries; they help us distinguish and separate what the idea is and what it is not. Now the formed idea must be transmitted and received through a medium. Whether in long-form text, like this book, or short-form text, like a social media post, or in oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium carries the now well-structured idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point we have only spoken of the creator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must correctly receive, decode, and understand the message. The transmitted message and the received message must align well with each other.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human work is a byproduct of effective communication. The work of a single person alone is limited. To build or do something truly great, many people (here I speak generally of humankind) must work together with a shared goal and a shared idea. Aside from natural disasters (such as a volcanic eruption), we know that the greatest catastrophes that have occurred have usually been, to a significant extent, due to missing, broken, or distorted communication. Truly, death and life are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we express those beliefs have the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—or just as much the power to destroy them. Our beliefs are a foundation for our actions and a blueprint for what we will do, what we will be, and the manner of our being. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines our role in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our own lives but, to a degree, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must build our beliefs carefully and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said the same when he told his disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today desire a stronger faith, but they lack a clear foundation to build upon. Without solid teaching, we will be deprived of the firm footing we need to stand; we risk being led astray by false doctrines, becoming discouraged in trials, or remaining unprepared for the work God has called us to. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to "*be rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith*" (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). In order to grow into mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God's truth. For this reason, doctrine matters. Doctrine is more than information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why This Book, and Who Is It For?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are arranged to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. My hope is that these beliefs will steady and inspire your steps of faith so that you put down deep roots in Christ and know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings have been shaped within the context of my experience in the church, particularly in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The composition of the teachings in this book is built on my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal training, and nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of it as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children's ministry, evangelistic efforts and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those who seek to deepen their knowledge of God and Scripture while also taking steps in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders - those who are being prepared to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both personally and practically, making it ideal for personal growth as well as group instruction. Whether you use this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, the content is structured to lead to easy application and Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to use this book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression; beginning with foundational truths such as God, sin, repentance, and faith, and moving to the deeper work of the Holy Spirit, discipleship, and spiritual authority. Each topic stands on its own but builds on previous chapters, making it easy to work step-by-step or focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For individuals, this book is a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For teachers, it provides the materials needed to prepare lessons, lead discussions, and effectively disciple others. Personal reflection questions, group discussion questions for facilitation, group activities, and word studies are included to help deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

<!-- p25 -->
## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
A primary source for sound teaching has been given to us: the teachings of the Holy Spirit that are inspired and recorded in Scripture and have been handed down to us through the teachings and tradition of the Church. This book takes the essential doctrines, presents them in an easy-to-understand format, and ends each chapter with personal reflection, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, many eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical issues within the early Christian communities. These writings, which were divinely inspired, were carefully recorded, compiled, and preserved over the centuries, and produced what is known today as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
In general, we will use the NKJV translation, but wherever another translation makes a phrase clearer we may use it. *Bible references will be italicized for ease of reading.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly recommend using multiple translations whenever possible so that the meaning becomes clear in your mind. I personally find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful; not because of the text’s mere accuracy, but because it helps me understand in plain English what the Bible is saying.

<!-- p61 -->
We take time to examine some key words of the Bible (**word studies**) to better understand what God is saying and how this word applies in our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic Bible resources such as: E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>)، or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources also provide you with access to Strong’s Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong’s Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original language, so that we may hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (which sometimes in this book is abbreviated OT) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (which sometimes in this book is abbreviated NT) was written in Greek. The Strong’s number is designated as follows: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are neither new nor novel. We should be suspicious of «new» teachings about our Christian faith that have never been seen or heard before. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have been present from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is neither a study in church history nor a comparison of Christian denominations. Take any two theological books written by different authors with various denominational backgrounds؛ while you will see a great deal of harmony in much of their work, you will also see significant differences. I do not claim to be inerrant، but I do claim that this work has been well studied, has been peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgments section)، and is well supported by the Bible and the accepted Pentecostal tradition.

I ask you to read the Bible for yourself and commit to mastering it. God’s Word promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. My hope is that you will be restored، strengthened، and empowered to accomplish God’s purpose in your life.
