# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a solid system and structure are necessary. Without them, disorder and chaos easily arise. For this reason, laying a solid foundation in doctrine for living fully as a healthy Christian is vital.

<!-- p2 -->
For this reason, "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound teaching, to the more than 300 pages that follow, brimming with teachings and activities about the church and our roles in it, as well as understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is priceless. In the classroom, this book will be an exceptional tool for teaching and preparing leaders with a firm foundation in doctrine, while also being very beneficial for individual readers.
