# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the main learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity takes about 15-20 minutes and may require additional resources not included in this book. Along with personal reflection questions and group discussion, these activities work best in a group study format to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as you see fit.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Matching Bible Verses

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce key biblical concepts about teaching by examining verses that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or small groups** of 3-4.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign the verses** - give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Proclaim the word and guard against false teaching.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - ask each group to:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read their assigned passage of Scripture** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** about teaching in 1-2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** of how this teaching applies to guarding against falsehood or helping others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - each group has **2 minutes** to present their Scripture passage, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Teaching in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound teaching to **real-life scenarios** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend tells you: “What we believe isn’t very important, as long as we’re sincere.” How do you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who is hearing conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. What verses from Scripture can you share to help bring clarity?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend no longer goes to church because they feel there’s no need as long as they believe in God. How would you encourage them using Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- Identify the **doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 verses from Scripture** that address this issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Sharing responses** - The groups take turns presenting their scenarios and responses to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Phrase or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **discern truth from error** using Scripture as their foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Activity setup** - Read the following **statements** aloud one by one. Some of the statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Decide whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Scripture verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Before confirming the correct answers, give the groups time to discuss disagreements or look up verses.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Only faith saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All paths, if we are sincere, lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name the Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God to **practical life applications**.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare the note cards** - On each card write **one name or attribute of God** (e.g., Jehovah-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give each participant or group one card.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** and the **Scripture reference** that accompanies it from the chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply in a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Wrap-up** - Ask each participant or group to present their reflections and emphasize the practical ways that God’s character applies in real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate the names and attributes of God into their **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants to reflect on one of the names of God from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses a current need (for example, **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that name, write a short **prayer**, praise God, and ask Him to act in harmony with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: To encourage and strengthen faith, (optionally) share the prayers in small groups or in pairs.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **End with a group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering God’s Attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants reflect on God’s attributes through personal stories and group prayer and apply them in their lives.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on an attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a list of 3-4 attributes of God (for example, **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**) taken from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to choose one attribute they feel resonates most with their current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story sharing (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly shares a story (a personal experience or an observation) related to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Sample questions:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When did you experience God as your Provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *"How has God's mercy shaped your life?"*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *"How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in everyday life?"*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer** that reflects that attribute and to pray together, focusing on applying God's character in their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Salvation

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Show how the **consequences of sin ripple outward** and affect **relationships, communities, and spiritual lives**, while emphasizing **God's redemptive plan**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- **Drop a stone into a bowl of water** (or, if materials aren't available, describe the image).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples** spreading outward and discuss:  
      *"How does this image illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?"*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small-Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term consequences of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some “waves” of sin you have personally experienced؟*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace been at work to halt those waves and bring healing؟*

<!-- p104 -->

<!-- p105 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p106 -->
Trace the arc of God’s redemptive plan from Genesis to Christ، focusing on his grace amid judgment.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups** - assign each group a **Scripture passage** that emphasizes sin and restoration:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:21</span>** - God provides covering for Adam and Eve.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warns Cain before his sin.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat carries sin away.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group work:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the sin or judgment present in the passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the grace or redemption offered.

<!-- p117 -->
- Summarize the lesson learned about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Ask each group to share their insights and connect the passages to form a timeline of God’s plan for redemption.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Stepping into Forgiveness (Application with Prayer)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Guide participants to **personally face forgiveness**-both **to receive it** and **to extend it to others**.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **reflect in silence** on areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God's forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone else** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in private prayer. The facilitator does not need to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the ability to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage them to write down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants (optional) to share **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from dead works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and eliminate “dead works” in their lives and replace them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a list of **possible dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect in silence and identify **one or two areas** where they see signs of dead works in their lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of possible dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services out of habit، but without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval instead of doing them out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to attain righteousness through personal effort، rather than relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to earn favor and ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful patterns or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding on to grudges or unforgiveness.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride، envy، or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency with the status quo:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer، study of the Bible، or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Slipping into spiritual apathy and losing passion for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful Habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending too much time on distractions or entertainments that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions Driven by Fear or Guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of obligation, not out of love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering merely to discharge a duty, not out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced Priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success instead of spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Spending time and energy on worldly matters that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so that (if comfortable) they can share one area they have identified as a «dead work».

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps for **replacing dead works** with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Summary and Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer for God’s guidance in living a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->
### Activity 2: The Path of Repentance (Interactive Demonstration and Discussion)

<!-- p216 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->
Guiding participants to understand the steps of repentance and how they lead to transformation.

<!-- p218 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p219 -->
1.  **Create a visual pathway:**

<!-- p220 -->
- Draw or display a path with the following steps: *Conviction → Godly sorrow → Confession → Turning away → Transformation*.

<!-- p221 -->
2.  **Interactive group exercise:**

<!-- p222 -->
- Assign groups to discuss each step in this path:

<!-- p223 -->
- *What does this step look like in practice?*

<!-- p224 -->
- *What are the obstacles to this step, and how can we overcome them?*

<!-- p225 -->
3.  **Reflection and sharing:**

<!-- p226 -->
- Ask each group to share its insights about one step of the path.

<!-- p227 -->
- Conclude by explaining how repentance leads to spiritual growth and alignment with God’s will.

<!-- p228 -->
### Activity 3: Learning from David (Bible Study and Personal Prayer)

<!-- p229 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p230 -->
Draw lessons from David’s repentance to encourage participants to embrace a life filled with humility and renewal.

<!-- p231 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p232 -->
1.  **Read Psalm 51 together (10 minutes):**

<!-- p233 -->
- Reflect on David’s heartfelt plea for forgiveness and focus on verses such as <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="51" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 51:10-12</span>.

<!-- p234 -->
2.  **Group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p235 -->
- Ask: *What stands out to you in David’s repentance?*

<!-- p236 -->
- Discuss: *How can we model this level of transparency and humility in our own lives?*

<!-- p237 -->
3.  **Personal application:**

<!-- p238 -->
- Encourage participants to write a personal prayer of repentance inspired by David’s words.

<!-- p239 -->
4.  **Conclusion:**

<!-- p240 -->
- Conclude with prayer and ask God to help the participants cultivate within themselves a steadfast spirit and a heart fully surrendered to Him.

### Activity 1: Hearing the Word of God (Exploration and Reflection in Scripture)

<!-- p243 -->
#### Goal:

<!-- p244 -->
Help participants understand how faith comes by hearing and identify moments when God’s Word becomes personal and alive.

<!-- p245 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p246 -->
