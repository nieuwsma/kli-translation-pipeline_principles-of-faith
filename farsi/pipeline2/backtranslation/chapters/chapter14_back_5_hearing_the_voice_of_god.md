# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest addendum from Pastor Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) completes our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit and provides practical insights on hearing the voice of God. This guest piece is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
One morning during prayer, I asked **God** this question: "What is the most important thing I can teach Your people for the times that are coming toward us?" And He answered me very quickly, and when He said, "**Teach them to hear My voice.**" I felt it was very urgent.

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me such direction, I always pick up a piece of paper and a pencil and start writing the little that I know, or what I think I know, and the Scriptures that support it. I love doing word studies on subjects the Lord leads me to teach. I love the anointing for preaching, but I truly love teaching the Word. Discovering things that have always been there but have gone unnoticed is an adventure.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have been eager to discover God's truths, for in verse 18 of Psalm 119 he prayed: "Open my eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of Your law."

<!-- p5 -->
That's what I want to see - wonders from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
While I was studying and preparing to teach the second message about hearing and discerning the voice of God, He said these things to my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them away:  
away from the troubles that destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*away from the trials they are in,  
away to a place of peace, love, and joy that few have ever been.*

<!-- p10 -->
*away to a place of renewal,  
to a place of divine ecstasy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Ah yes, My son, teach them to hear My voice  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 AM

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing God's voice is not an audible voice but a knowing in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
Once His word came so clear and emphatic that it could have been audible. I answered Him out loud, it was that real. However, there was no one there but the Lord and me, so I really can't say for certain that He spoke to me the way we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was a time when Jesus was being baptized in the Jordan and the Father said, "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased," but even some of those who were there said His voice sounded like thunder; therefore I suppose He may have spoken to me audibly, but for now I want you to understand that when I say God spoke to me, it is more a knowing in my heart than a voice in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>This knowing is not constructed by the logic of your mind; nevertheless, it is a complete thought or understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
It has never happened that the Lord told me something that confused me. It has never happened that He told me something that left me with the question "Why." It is always a knowing with complete understanding. God is not the source of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in the form of complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us to fill in the blanks on our own. In the blink of an eye, God can fill your heart with so much information that you could preach for the rest of your life. One touch from God can fill you with a fire that will keep you ablaze forever. He is a perfect communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He never drives. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a hard taskmaster؛ He is a shepherd. Herdsmen drive, but shepherds lead. Jesus is the great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He never condemns؛ He convinces.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who are “born again” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>، 5) by confessing their faith in the shed blood of **Jesus** and His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who is always condemning us for our faults? I believe most of **God’s** children are afraid of hearing the voice of **God**, because they have been told that **God** will condemn them for this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. It says who is it that convicts or condemns us? It is our own spirit or heart that condemns a child of **God**, but it is **God’s Holy Spirit** who knows all things and is greater than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read these verses؛ they will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that nothing can separate us from the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>، 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our condemner؛ our Counselor, not our disciplinarian؛ our Advocate and Helper, not our restrainer. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 to see examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) helps us, comforts us, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you know it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> commands us to “test every spirit” to be sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing from God, you have the right and responsibility to ask that voice a question (this is the testing or proving of the spirit.) The question you must ask is: “Did Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The one thing familiar spirits will never confess is that Jesus came in the flesh. He was fully man and fully God. No false spirit or religion will confess that Jesus came in the flesh to this earth and delivered mankind forever from all their sins. Test the voice you are hearing. You must also know that God will never lead you outside His written Word, the Bible. If you hear something that cannot be supported by Scripture, you are not hearing from God. Cast it aside! Test the voice you hear to see whether it is from God or not.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always gives glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the voice of heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said He will always speak only what He hears, and He will always glorify Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to glorify your ministry or your works or church programs. All those things are good, and most of them are necessary for what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring glory to the person of Jesus. If the voice you hear glorifies you, you are not hearing from God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will encourage and strengthen you every time He speaks to you, even when He is correcting (guiding) you with the Word. But glory is when you believe that you yourself are «somebody». You are nobody without Jesus—He is the «somebody», not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God always reveals something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and discloses it to us. The Amplified translation, when considering the word “reveal,” adds the words “declare,” “disclose,” and “transmit.” After **God** declares something to us, He will always disclose it to us and then, by your faith, transmit it to us. Also study <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> و 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God always brings peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> و 27، و <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> و 15. His voice never upsets us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. The inner peace is because there is a certainty that gives us confidence with God, and that translates into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to troubles you, you are not hearing from God-turn it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives, but as He gives to us through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His continual presence (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God always brings truth.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>، 26؛ <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">16:13</span>. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit “the Spirit of truth.” God cannot lie! He is truth, and therefore He will speak only truth. People have said to me: “I can’t believe what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And maybe it really is too good to be true؛ that’s why you must test the spirit by aligning it with the Word of God to make sure it is truth. Some just cannot believe, because what they hear is always positive, and they have been taught that God condemns us for our faults. God will only speak truth, and it is always good news. The sad truth is that most people only hear bad news from the voice they’re listening to and never expect to hear good news, so when the good news comes, they question it as a possibility for being untrue. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** sets us free, so how can **truth** be anything but good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always leads us forward and upward.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deuteronomy 28:1-2، <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>، and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God will never pull you down! He will never belittle you؛ He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He speaks from the framework that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and has been received by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you great and mighty things, which you do not know.” Does that sound like belittling you or doom and gloom? There is no doom and gloom in God, therefore He cannot say anything doom-filled or dark to you. For doom and gloom you must go to the world’s news agencies.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God is thinking good thoughts of peace, future, and hope about us, why would He speak contrary to that? The answer is: He never will, and He does not! He only leads us upward and forward with a great hope to provide us with a hoped-for future.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to «call on Him» and learn how to **hear His voice**. He has great things to say to you, but before all His goodness can benefit you, He needs your listening ear.
