# Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Matters

<!-- p1 -->
Teaching simply means the core truths we believe and teach—truths that have been passed on by God’s faithful people, are rooted in Scripture, and are lived out in our daily way of life.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded his church to “teach,” he instructed them to share his teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came near and spoke to them and said, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations and baptize them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> and teach them to observe everything I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus calls all of us to learn his teachings and reproduce them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the Way, the Truth, and the Life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), who perfectly embodies the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God. This embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’s structured teachings are his teaching (***didaskalia***), and by keeping (obedience, guarding, protection) his commandments (***entolē)*** we become his disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in all of history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect plan for constructing an enduring and unshakable structure. His understanding is not derived from others—it is intrinsic to who he is. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sturdy edifice finds its origin in his design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the complete and perfect embodiment of the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring his plan to life, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—a structured design that contains the principles, proportions, and foundational truths required to build a steadfast structure. Without this blueprint, no one can build correctly. This blueprint represents the structured teaching of Jesus, his teaching (***didaskalia***)—the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His teaching is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the means by which his wisdom is communicated and preserved so that all his followers can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
But having the blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **detailed instructions** that explain how each part of the structure must be assembled, where each beam should be placed, and how every component aligns with his complete plan. These specific instructions represent his commandments **(Entolē**)—the executive commands that guide obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps for the structure to stand firm and be aligned with the master plan. Ignoring or altering his commandments endangers the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathers a group of **apprentices**—those who want to learn from him, build under his supervision, and one day teach others how to construct enduring buildings. These apprentices study his blueprint carefully, listen closely to his instructions, and faithfully apply his commands in their work. They do not build according to their own ideas, but according to the master’s plan. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)—those who observe, obey, and internalize the teachings of Jesus, aligning their lives with his word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The more they learn under the architect, the more skilled they become, and eventually they also train others in his ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study the teachings?

<!-- p14 -->
The teaching of Jesus is the cornerstone of our faith! He is the master architect who has a perfect plan and blueprint. Only by studying the plans, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and cultivating a strong working relationship can we build a life that stands firm through the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain; unless the LORD guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand firm against hardships, challenges, calamities, persecutions, or even ordinary life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
More than a gym membership is required for a person to be healthy and physically fit. You must be consistent in the discipline of the gym to enjoy all the benefits of membership. Likewise, we cannot merely attend church or casually claim to be Christians; we all must be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith; only then will we understand all the benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ charge goes beyond us. It is great to become complete, sound, disciplined, and surrendered to God, but we must also teach others, train, equip, lead, and make disciples so that they too may enter into the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study the teachings and become disciplined? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is not only a benefit, but also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we build our lives on sand, and a powerful storm will shatter our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral—all of us build on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is ours!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who had believed Him: "If you remain in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continuing in His word (His Logos)! The result of continuing in His word is the knowledge of the truth that sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, who have learned discipline, can carry out the Master’s commands. Every person in the New Testament church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be disciples -\> commit ourselves to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to commit themselves to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Do not worry; we do not have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit to guide us and to lead us into all the truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His written words in the Holy Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us mature, spiritually gifted leaders to build us up, bring us to maturity, protect us, and organize us for doing good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us specialists who, through experience, know how to help our growth and maturity, and over time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

**An important note:** The word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called as apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, or teachers, but all of us have been given ministry gifts, abilities, and special roles (which will be discussed later in the chapter on the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given these particular gifts to the church to bring us to maturity, so that we can and will be:

<!-- p42 -->
- able to do the work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- united in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- full of the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- mature

<!-- p46 -->
- not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we will be <u>children who are tossed about and carried away.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God's family, but children are inherently:

<!-- p49 -->
- immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- naive - unable to discern between good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- self-centered - preoccupied with passing things and the present.

<!-- p52 -->
- defenseless - exposed to harm.

What are His teachings، what will we study؟

<!-- p88 -->

<!-- p97 -->

<!-- p99 -->

<!-- p101 -->
