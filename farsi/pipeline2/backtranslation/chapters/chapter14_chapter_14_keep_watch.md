# Chapter 14 - Be Awake

<!-- p1 -->
Since the spiritual authority of Christ to establish His kingdom on earth has been given to us, we must act with earnestness and urgency, because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end times is called Eschatology. Many books have been written about eschatology, and most of them disagree with one another! Specifically, they disagree about interpretations (allegorical, literal, symbolic), and the sequence and timing of the events found in Scripture. This saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist, Reinhard Bonnke, comes to mind - "When I’m done breaking chains on earth, I’ll split hairs in heaven!"

<!-- p3 -->
As the author, I hesitate to go too deep into this highly debated subject, because this book primarily focuses on the foundational principles of the faith. For the most part I will settle for a high-level overview of the events depicted in Scripture and focus on what matters most - **that** **gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Points of general agreement:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus returns

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  The defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will examine what it means, look at the reference verses, consider why it matters, and, in light of it, what we should do.

### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What is it:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers will appear before the **judgment seat of Christ** to receive reward, while unbelievers will face the **great white throne judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it; from His presence the earth and the heaven fled away, and no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter concerning sin, the final judgment will be great and terrifying for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! This judgment is not for the believer.

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers, our names are in the **Lamb’s *Book of Life*** *(*see the book of *Revelation*)**.** Therefore we will stand* *at the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but for reward! Again**,** this is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but **a celebration** of what God has done through us. It reminds us that our lives matter—not just for today but for **eternity**.

At this judgment, we will receive rewards for the good works we have done! All who are present at this judgment have lived and died as believers, so their eternal salvation is guaranteed. These laurel crowns highlight God’s commendation of the faithfulness and service of believers in His kingdom.

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For completeness, if you’re curious what the common lines of division in eschatology are, here is a very brief overview of the most debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture:** Pre-tribulation, mid-tribulation, or post-tribulation? Views differ on when the rapture occurs relative to the **Tribulation**. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the tribulation:** Is it an actual judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of suffering?

<!-- p92 -->
- **The millennial reign:** Is it literal **(premillennial)**, symbolic **(amillennial)**, or spiritual **(postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion:** Happens after the millennium (a literal event or metaphorical?).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is great, but let’s remember, as D. L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing the main thing!”

## **Final words**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the climax of God’s plan and the reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the reward of crowns or the hope of resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is returning!

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found ready, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**
