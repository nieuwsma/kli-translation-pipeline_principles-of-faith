# Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine simply means the fundamental truths that we believe and teach—truths passed down by God’s faithful people, rooted in Scripture, and lived out in our daily walk.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus asked His Church to ‘teach’, He was asking them to share His doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus asks all of us to learn His teachings and reproduce them in others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the Way, the Truth, and the Life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), fully embodying the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God. This embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). The structured teachings of Jesus are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and by observing (obeying, keeping, guarding) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in all history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect plan for a durable, unshakable structure. His understanding does not come from others—it is inherent in Him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every solid building finds its origin in His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the full and complete embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To bring His design to life, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—a structured plan that contains the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build an edifice that will stand firm. Without this plan, no one could build properly. This blueprint represents the structured teaching of Jesus, His doctrine (***didaskalia***) — the framework by which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not mere theoretical knowledge; it is the means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **precise instructions**, detailing how each part of the structure must be assembled, where each beam must be placed, and how every element must align with His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-the concrete directives that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps to ensure the building remains strong, aligned with the original plan. Ignoring or altering His commandments would amount to compromising the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathering a group of **apprentices**—those who desire to learn from Him, build under His guidance, and one day teach others how to erect buildings that last. These apprentices carefully study His blueprint, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully apply His commandments in their work. They do not build according to their own ideas but according to the Master’s design. These apprentices represent the disciples **(Mathētēs**)-those who observe, obey, and integrate the teachings of Jesus, aligning their lives with His Word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The more they study with the architect, the more skilled they become, eventually teaching and training others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the foundation of our faith! He is the master architect who has a perfect design and plan. Only by studying the blueprints, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and developing a strong working relationship can we build a life that will stand in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain; unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman keeps watch in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to remain strong in the face of hardships, challenges, tribulations, persecutions, or simply ordinary life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
It takes more than a gym membership to make a person healthy and physically fit. You must persevere in the discipline of the gym to gain all the benefits of the membership. Likewise, we cannot simply attend a church or casually claim to be Christians; we must all become educated, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith—only then will we fully realize the benefits of belonging!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ mission goes beyond us. It’s wonderful for us to become whole, healthy, disciplined, submitted to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples by bringing others to faith !

<!-- p21 -->
Why study doctrine and become disciplined ? To build our lives on the rock !

<!-- p22 -->
However, it is not only a benefit, it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we build them on sand, and a strong storm will destroy our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so he is ! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in his salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us !

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who had believed in Him: "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by continuing in His word (His logos) ! The result of continuing in His word is our knowledge of the truth, which sets us free !

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, having been taught discipline, can carry out the master’s commands. Every person in the New Testament Church is called to :

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be a disciple -\> submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to submit to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we go about building our lives on the rock ?

<!-- p32 -->
Do not worry, we do not have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His words recorded in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us gifted and spiritually mature leaders to build us up, make us mature, keep us safe, and organize us to do good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us specialists who know from experience how to help us grow and mature, and over time, you too can become one of them !

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ,
>

>

**An important note:** the word « some » indicates that we are not all called with a gift of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but that we have all received ministry gifts, talents, and particular roles (addressed later in the chapter on the Church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given the Church these particular gifts to bring us to maturity, so that we can and will be:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to carry out works and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound doctrine we will be <u>children, tossed and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of the family of God, but children are inherently:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Naive - unable to discern between good and bad actors.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - preoccupied with the temporal and the immediate.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - vulnerable to evil.

### Sound doctrine offers a defense against the wickedness of the world

<!-- p54 -->
We do not have the luxury of being spiritually neutral in this life. We are either influenced by the Kingdom of God or by the Kingdom of Darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul exhorted his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready at all times, in season or out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, because they have itching ears

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p61 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days perilous times will come:
>
> For men will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,
>
> unfeeling, implacable, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, despisers of good,
>
> traitors, headstrong, puffed up with pride, lovers of pleasures rather than of God,
>
> having a form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such people!
>
> For of this sort are those who creep into households and make captives of gullible women, loaded down with sins, led away by various passions,
>
> always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
>

>

>

<!-- p62 -->
</span>

<!-- p63 -->
The essence of this message is that perilous times will come. People will be so self-centered that, despite all their ‘teachings,’ they will not submit to sound doctrine, will deny the power of God, and will walk with a corrupt and reprobate mind. Paul exhorts Timothy to be well prepared to preach sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples, we study and submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine, this provides a defense against the wickedness of the world, we become men and women of God fully mature, able to defend ourselves against the snares of the enemy and able to protect those around us who have not yet reached full maturity.

<!-- p65 -->
We must be attentive and diligent in our study, the sound teaching of the Scriptures can be led astray by several perverse influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can become distorted by the traditions of men. We see this evident truth in the need of the great reformers of Protestantism and in the history of the Old Testament. God raised up men to restore the fundamental truth that had been perverted by the traditions of men.

What are His doctrines, what are we going to study?

<!-- p88 -->
In this work, we will mainly study the foundational doctrines of the New Testament Church. These doctrines are foundational in that the Scriptures refer to them as such, but even more because they provide a solid foundation upon which the learner can build their life.

<!-- p89 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p93 -->
1.  Principal doctrines of Christ (the Anointed One)

<!-- p94 -->

<!-- p95 -->
3.  Faith in God

<!-- p96 -->
4.  Doctrine of baptisms

<!-- p97 -->
5.  Laying on of hands

<!-- p98 -->
6.  Resurrection of the dead

<!-- p99 -->
7.  Eternal judgment

<!-- p100 -->
We will also study essential elements of our Christian walk :

<!-- p101 -->
- Sanctification by the Holy Spirit

<!-- p102 -->
- The Church and our role within it

<!-- p103 -->
- Making disciples

<!-- p104 -->
- Spiritual authority

<!-- p105 -->
We will study each of these topics in the context of the Church, and study them so that we may ‘go on to perfection (maturity)’. Our study will focus on the various perspectives and aspects of the Godhead as they are revealed to us : God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit.
