# Foreword

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a solid system and structure are essential. Without them, dysfunction and chaos can easily arise. That’s why establishing a solid foundation in doctrine is crucial to living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of the Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which addresses the importance of sound doctrine, through the remainder of the more than 300 pages filled with teachings and activities on the Church and our roles within it, as well as understanding and recognizing the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an exceptional classroom tool for teaching and preparing leaders on solid doctrinal foundations, while also being very beneficial for individual readers.
