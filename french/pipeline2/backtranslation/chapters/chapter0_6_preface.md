# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Foundation that Lasts

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How can we be sure? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This fascination goes beyond a mere desire to be right on the surface; it aims to know deeply and precisely, and to be able to clearly articulate the newly acquired understanding.

<!-- p3 -->
While communication is common, effective and precise communication is difficult. To communicate a message effectively, the author and the initiator must have an idea. However, the idea alone, like water on a hard surface-its contours and boundaries are ill-defined, fluid, and easily displaced.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and expressions to transform the raw idea from a vague form into a precise form. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us distinguish and separate what the idea is from what it is not. Now our shaped idea must be transmitted and received by some medium. Whether a long text, like this book, a short text, like a social media post, or an oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium conveys the now well-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Thus far, we have discussed only the initiator and the author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must accurately receive, decode, and understand the message. The messages transmitted and received should be closely aligned.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human work is the byproduct of effective communication. The work of a single man can accomplish only so many things. To truly build or accomplish something great, many men (speaking here of humanity generically) must work together with a shared purpose around a common idea. With the exception of natural disasters (such as a volcanic eruption), we also know that the greatest calamities that have ever occurred were largely because of communication that was absent, broken, or distorted. Life and death are truly in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it have the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—or have the power to destroy them just as much. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint of what we will do, who we will be, how we will exist. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our lives, but to some extent the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must establish our beliefs accurately and learn to share them effectively. Jesus said it well when He declared to His disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying: "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today desire a stronger faith but lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without sound doctrine, we lack the firm foundation we need to stand; we risk being carried away by false teachings, discouraged in trials, or ill-prepared for the work to which God has called us. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—truths that fortify your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to be “rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To become mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God’s truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is more than information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings in this book are intended to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament Church. I hope these beliefs will convict and inspire your walk of faith to plant deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experience in the Church, particularly within charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The composition of the teachings in this book draws on my own personal study of Scripture, formal and informal training, as well as nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of it as an ordained minister. My experience includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelism and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who seek to strengthen their faith or to make disciples by walking alongside others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - Those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who seek to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture while walking in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - Those preparing to make disciples, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, making it ideal for personal growth as well as for group teaching. Whether you use this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, the content is structured for easy application and Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to use this book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, beginning with foundational truths like **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, then addressing the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands on its own while building on the previous chapters, allowing you to move forward step by step or focus, as needed, on specific areas.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understand and apply the Scriptures. For **teachers**, it provides material to prepare lessons, facilitate discussions, and make disciples effectively. Personal reflection questions, group discussion questions for facilitation, group activities, and word studies are included to deepen understanding and encourage concrete steps of obedience.

<!-- p25 -->
## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound doctrine, the teachings of the Holy Spirit, inspired and recorded in Scripture, handed down through the teaching and tradition of the Church. This book will revisit essential teachings, present them in an easy-to-understand format, and conclude each chapter with personal reflection, questions for group facilitation, and optional group activities.

After Jesus’ ministry, many eyewitnesses and apostles recorded accounts of his life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical issues within the early Christian communities. These writings, divinely inspired, were carefully recorded, compiled, and preserved over the centuries, forming what is known today as the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
In general, we will use the NKJV translation but we may also use another translation when it makes the passage clearer. *Bible references will be in italics to make reading easier.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly recommend using multiple translations when possible, to ensure that the concept is clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible useful, not for their pure textual accuracy, but to help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures communicate.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore certain key words in the Bible (**word studies**) in order to better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic Bible resources such as: E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or the Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). <!-- p62 -->

The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated OT in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated NT in this book) was written in Greek. The Strong number will be indicated: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are neither new nor original. We should be skeptical of ‘new,’ never-before-seen-or-heard teachings regarding our Christian faith. This book is rather a compilation and an explanation of teachings present since the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is neither a study of Church history nor a comparison of Christian denominations. Take two works of theology written by different authors from divergent confessional backgrounds, and while you will see great harmony in much of their work, you will also see notable differences. I will not claim inerrancy, but I affirm that this work is seriously studied, reviewed by peers—faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgments section)—and solidly supported by the Scriptures and by a widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

I implore you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to mastering them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. As you go through these teachings, I pray that you grow not only in understanding, but that you also experience the power of the Holy Spirit at work in you and through you. My hope is that you are restored, strengthened, and enabled to accomplish God’s purpose in your life.
