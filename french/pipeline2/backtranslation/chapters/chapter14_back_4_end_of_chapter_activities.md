# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the main learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity is intended to take about 15 to 20 minutes and may require additional resources not included in this book. In addition to personal reflection and group discussion questions, these activities are ideally used in a group study setting to deepen understanding and commitment to the book’s key teachings. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and modify the activities as you see fit.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Bible Passage Matching

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Strengthen key biblical concepts regarding doctrine by exploring passages that highlight its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants into** **pairs or small groups** of 3 to 4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Bible passages** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the Word and guard against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Ask each group to:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read aloud** the passage assigned to them.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** on doctrine in 1 to 2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a concrete example** showing how this teaching applies to guarding against error or helping others grow in the faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share reflections** - Each group takes **2 minutes** to present to the whole group their passage, summary, and application.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Action

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life situations** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions :

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following :

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1** : *A friend tells you: “It doesn’t really matter what we believe, as long as we are sincere.” How do you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2** : *You are accompanying a new believer who hears conflicting teachings on salvation by faith versus salvation by works. What Scriptures can you share to help clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3** : *A Christian friend stops attending church because he feels it’s pointless as long as he believes in God. How do you encourage him using Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should :

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **List 1-2 Scripture passages** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **one-minute summary** of how they would counsel the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - The groups take turns presenting their scenarios and their answers to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3 : Affirmation or Scripture ?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective :

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **discern truth from falsehood** using Scripture as the foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions :

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Set up the activity** - Read aloud the following **affirmations**, one at a time. Some affirmations are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to :

<!-- p37 -->
- Decide if the affirmation is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Allow time for groups to discuss disagreements or look up verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All roads lead to God if we are sincere.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelations to guide us.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Good works are the result of salvation, not its cause.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:10</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1 : Name this attribute (interactive group activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God to practical applications in life.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare cards** - Write **a name or attribute of God** (e.g., Yahweh-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent) on each card.

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** and the **corresponding Bible reference** taken from the chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **one-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Conclusion** - Ask each participant or group to **present their reflections**, emphasizing the practical ways God’s character applies to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2 : The names of God in prayer (reflection and application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants to integrate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set up a time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants to meditate on one of the names of God from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** that addresses a current need (e.g., **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Write a short **prayer** using that name, praising God, and asking Him to act in alignment with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs to **encourage and strengthen faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Conclude with a group prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering the attributes of God (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants to **reflect on and apply** the attributes of God to their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3 to 4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on the attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group with a **list of 3 to 4 attributes** of God (e.g., **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**) drawn from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that, in their view, resonates most with their current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Sharing stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant **briefly shares a story** (personal experience or observation) that relates to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Sample prompts:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When have you experienced God as your Provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life ?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 Minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group : *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life ?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to **write a short prayer** reflecting this attribute and to pray together, focusing on applying God's character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (discussion and application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Illustrate how the effects of sin spread outward, affecting relationships, communities, and spiritual life, while emphasizing God's redemptive plan.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a **stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if the materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples** that spread outward and discuss :  
      *“How does this visual illustrate the impact of sin in our lives and relationships ?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 Minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example of sin** :

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss :

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin ?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each account ?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Gather the groups and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some “repercussions” of sin that you have experienced personally?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace acted to stop these repercussions and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

<!-- p117 -->

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3 : Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants to **personally integrate forgiveness**-both **to receive it** and **to grant it** to others.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **reflect in silence** on areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God’s forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone else** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Guide participants in a private prayer. The facilitator does not need to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess their sins to God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage noting any **commitment** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Final reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (optional) **how forgiveness** brings **freedom and restoration**.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identify dead works (personal reflection and group discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives while replacing them with actions guided by the Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a **list of potential dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect in silence and identify **one or two areas** where they see evidence of dead works in their own lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of potential dead works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending services out of habit, without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-sufficiency:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Seeking to attain righteousness by one's own efforts rather than depending on the grace of God.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to earn favor, while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful habits or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding grudges or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Settling into spiritual apathy, losing passion for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending too much time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Works motivated by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of obligation rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering to check a box rather than out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success at the expense of spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits with no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups to share (if they are comfortable) an area they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to **replace dead works** with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or actions they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer asking for God's guidance to live a fruitful life.

<!-- p243 -->
#### Objective :

<!-- p244 -->

<!-- p245 -->
#### Instructions :

<!-- p246 -->
