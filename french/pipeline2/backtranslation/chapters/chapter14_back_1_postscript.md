# Afterword

<!-- p1 -->
As I finish this book, my hope and prayer for you, dear reader, is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit—it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the Kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would leave you with, it is this: Keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths set forth in this book are not meant to be read once and then set aside; they are made to be lived out daily. Following Christ is a continual process of learning, refining, and transformation. We go from faith to faith, from glory to glory, continually pressing on to know him more.

<!-- p3 -->
It may be that some questions remain unanswered. You may feel as though you have only skimmed the surface of these deep and eternal truths. That is a good thing. Let these questions drive you to go deeper into the Word of God, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter—He will lead you into all truth as you seek him.
