# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
The following guest appendix from Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) complements our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit, by offering practical insights for hearing the voice of God. The following guest contribution is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
During prayer one morning, I asked **God** the following question : « What is the most important thing I could teach Your people for the times ahead? » And He answered me very quickly, and I felt it was very urgent when He said: « **Teach them to hear My voice.** »

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me a direction like that, I always take a notebook and a pencil and begin to write the little that I know, or what I think I know, as well as the Scriptures that support it. I like to do word studies on the subjects the Lord leads me to teach. I love the anointing to preach, but I truly love teaching the Word. It is an adventure to find things that have always been there but have been overlooked.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have been thirsty to discover the Truths of God, for he prayed in verse 18 of Psalm 119 : « Open my eyes, that I may see wondrous things from Your law. »

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-wonderful things drawn from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message on listening to and discerning the voice of God, He said the following to my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My voice,  
Teach them to honor and obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My voice and I will always lead them away:  
Far from the troubles that would destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Far from the trials in which they find themselves,  
Far to a place of peace, love and joy where few have ever gone.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Far to a place of refreshing,  
to a place of Divine ecstasy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Oh yes, teach them to hear My voice my son  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points should help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible sound but a CERTAINTY in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was a time when His Word came so clear and so bold that it could have been audible. I answered Him by speaking out loud, it was so real. However, it was only the Lord and me there, so I cannot really say with certainty whether He spoke to me as we speak to each other.

<!-- p16 -->
There was the moment when Jesus was baptized in the Jordan River and the Father said: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” but even some of those who were there said that it sounded like thunder, so I suppose He could have spoken to me audibly, but for now, I want you to understand that when I say that God spoke to me, it is a CERTAINTY in my heart more than a sound in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>CERTAINTY is NOT built by logic in your mind; however, it is a thought or a complete understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
I have never heard the Lord tell me anything that confused me. I have never heard Him tell me anything that left me asking ‘why’. It is always a CERTAINTY with a complete UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He expresses Himself in complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us to fill in the blanks. In the blink of an eye, God can fill your heart with enough information to keep you preaching for a lifetime. A single touch from God can fill you with a fire that will keep you burning forever. He is a perfect communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks to us, He NEVER PUSHES. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Herdsmen drive, but shepherds lead. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, He NEVER CONDEMNS; He CONVICTS.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who are Born again (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5) through their confession of faith in the shed blood of **Jesus** and His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who always condemns us for our wrongs? I believe that most children of **God** are afraid to hear **God** speak to them because they have been told that **God** will condemn them for this or that. Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Whom does this passage say condemns us? It is OUR SPIRIT or heart that condemns the child of **God**, but it is **God the Holy Spirit** who knows all things and is GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the following Scriptures, and this will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit convicts are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our condemner; our Counselor, not our chastiser; our Intercessor and our Helper, not the one who hinders us. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) helps us, comforts us, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you MAY KNOW that it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> instructs us to “test every spirit” so that we can be sure it is from God. When you think you hear God, you have the right and the responsibility to ask a question of that voice (That is testing or trying the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Has Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” The only thing familiar spirits will never admit is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully man and fully God. No false spirit or false religion will admit that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed humanity from all its sins for eternity. Test the voice you hear. You must also know that God will never lead you outside of His written Word, the Bible. If you hear something that cannot be attested by the Bible, you are not hearing God. Reject it! Test the voice you hear to see if it is from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God will always bring Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is the One who is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said that He will always say only what He hears, and that He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to bring glory to your ministry, your works, or church programs. All these things are good, and most are necessary to do what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and bring Glory to the Person of Jesus. If you are being glorified by the voice you hear, you are not hearing God. There is a difference between being glorified and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage and strengthen you whenever He speaks to you, even when He corrects you (guides you) through the Word. But glory is when you believe that YOU are somebody. You are nobody without Jesus-HE IS THE SOMEBODY, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God will always REVEAL something.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
Look at <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified version adds the words: declare, disclose, and transmit when considering the word “reveal.” **God** always discloses something to us after having declared it to us, then, by your faith, He transmits it to us. Study also <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God will always bring peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never troubles us. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. The inner peace is there because there is a KNOWLEDGE that brings us confidence before God, which results in perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to troubles you, you are not hearing God-cut it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives but as He gives it to us through the comforting words of the Holy Spirit and His constant indwelling (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. In fact, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “The Spirit of truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH and therefore will speak only TRUTH. I have heard people say things like: “I just can’t believe that what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” And maybe it is too good to be true; that is why you MUST test the spirit by lining it up with the Word of God to be sure it is the Truth. Some people just can’t believe it because what they hear is always positive, and they have been taught that God condemns us for our mistakes. God will tell only the TRUTH, and it will always be GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that most people hear only bad news from the voice they are listening to and never anticipate hearing good news; so, when good news comes, they question it as a possible untruth. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** makes us free, so how could the **truth** be anything but good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** ALWAYS LEADS US FORWARD AND UPWARD.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deuteronomy 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never puts you down! He never belittles you; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He speaks from the frame of reference that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and ACCEPTED by you. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you **GREAT AND MIGHTY** things which you do not know.” Does that sound like putting you down or doom and gloom? There is NO doom or darkness in God; therefore He cannot tell you anything gloomy or dark. You will have to tune in to the world’s news agency for doom and darkness.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the LORD, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS toward us good thoughts of peace, future, and hope, why would He speak otherwise? The answer is, HE WOULDN’T, and HE DOESN’T! He only leads us UPWARD and FORWARD with great hope to give us an expected future.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this will encourage you to “call on Him” and to learn to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs your attentive ear before all His good things can benefit you.
