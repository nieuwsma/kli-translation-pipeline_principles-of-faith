# Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Is Important

<!-- p1 -->
By education we simply mean the basic facts that we believe and that we teach—the facts that God’s faithful people have passed on, that are rooted in the Holy Scriptures, and that are put into practice in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His church to ‘teach,’ He was commanding them to share His teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came to them and began to say, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
>
> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> and teach them to observe all the things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus is instructing all of us to learn His teachings and pass them on to others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), the full expression of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation. This embodied expression, that is, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’ systematic teachings are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and in following His commandments (to obey, to keep, to guard) (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect, the greatest builder in history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect design of a durable, unshakable structure. His understanding is not derived from others—it is intrinsic to Him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and the foundation of every strong building is found in His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the complete and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To put His design into practice, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—an organized plan consisting of the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build a structure that will stand firm. Without this blueprint no one can build correctly. This blueprint represents Jesus’ systematic teaching, His doctrine (***didaskalia***) — the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the very means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, the blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **precise instructions**, which state how to join each part of the structure, where to place each beam, and how to harmonize every element according to His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-the practical directives that regulate obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps that ensure the building stands firm and conforms to the original blueprint. To ignore or alter His commandments is to jeopardize the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine that the architect gathers a group of **disciples**—those who want to learn from Him, build under His supervision, and one day teach others to build structures that endure. These disciples study His blueprint carefully, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully carry out His commandments in their work. They build not by their own opinions but according to the Master’s design. These disciples **(Mathētēs**)-are those who observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, shaping their lives according to His Word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). The longer they remain under the Master’s training, the more skilled they become, until they too teach and train others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the rock of our faith! He is the Master Builder who has the perfect design and blueprint. Only by studying the blueprints, following the Master’s specific instructions, and establishing a strong practical fellowship can we build a life that will stand in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain; unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand strong in the face of hardships, challenges, troubles, persecutions, or simply everyday life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone is not enough to make a person healthy and physically fit. To receive the full benefits of membership you have to be consistent in gym practice. Similarly, we cannot get by merely attending church or superficially calling ourselves Christians; we all must be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith—only then will we receive the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ responsibility extends beyond us. It is very good that we become perfect, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, guide, and make others disciples in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and why should we be disciplined? So that we build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is not only a benefit, it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, then we build our lives on the sand, and a strong storm will break our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on some foundation. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we may stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.
>
> And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by abiding in His word (His logos)! The result of abiding in His word is our knowledge of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, once they have learned discipline, can carry out the Master's commands. In the New Testament church, every person is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become disciples -\> subject yourselves to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to be subject to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don't worry, we won't have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead us into all truth and guide us (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His written word in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, capable leaders who help to strengthen us, establish us, keep us safe, and organize us for good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows well what we need. He has given us experts who, through experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and over time you too can become one of them!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

**An important note:** The word ‘some’ indicates that we are not all called with the gift of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but we have all been given gifts of service, abilities, and specific roles (which will be discussed further in the chapter on the Church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given these special gifts to the Church in order to bring us to maturity, so that we can be and become:

<!-- p42 -->
- Able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- United in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Full of the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound doctrine we will be <u>children, tossed here and there and carried away.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but by nature children are:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - not able to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Simple-minded - unable to discern between good and evil people.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - concerned only with temporary and present things.

<!-- p52 -->
- Defenseless - vulnerable to harm.

What are His teachings; what will we study?

<!-- p88 -->

<!-- p96 -->

<!-- p97 -->

<!-- p98 -->

<!-- p99 -->

<!-- p101 -->

<!-- p102 -->

<!-- p103 -->

<!-- p104 -->
