# Chapter 1 - The Importance of Sound Teaching

<!-- p1 -->
By teaching we simply mean those fundamental truths in which we believe and which we teach—the truths that have been passed down through God’s faithful people, are rooted in Holy Scripture, and are practically lived out in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus instructed His church to “teach,” He was directing them to share His teaching.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-verses="18-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV) ***18** And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.* ***19** Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,* ***20** teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.” Amen.*

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus is directing all of us to learn His teachings and pass them on to others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-verses="6" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:6</span>), who fully embodies God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation. This embodiment, that is, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-verses="1" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 1:1</span>, 14). Jesus’ organized teachings are His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and by keeping His commandments (to obey, guard, keep) (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect—the greatest builder in history—whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone understands the perfect design for a durable, unshakable structure. His understanding is not derived from others—it is inherent to Him. He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and the origin of every sturdy building is found in His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the full and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, wisdom, and revelation (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-verses="1" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-verses="6" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To put His design into practice, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—an organized plan that includes the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to construct a building that will stand firm. Without this blueprint, no one can build correctly. This blueprint is Jesus’ organized teaching, His doctrine **(Didaskalia)-**the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-verses="1" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His teaching is not mere theoretical knowledge; it is the very means by which His wisdom is conveyed and preserved so that anyone who follows Him can build their life on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, the blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides **precise instructions**, detailing how each part of the structure is to be joined, where each beam is to be placed, and how every component is to harmonize with His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)—practical directives that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-verses="15" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-verses="20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the essential steps that ensure the building stands firm, according to the original blueprint. To ignore or alter His commandments is to compromise the integrity of the whole structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine that the architect gathers a group of **apprentices**—those who want to learn from Him, build under His guidance, and one day teach others how to construct enduring buildings. These apprentices study His blueprint carefully, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully implement His commandments in their work. They build not according to their own opinions but according to the Master’s design. These apprentices represent disciples **(Mathētēs**)—those who observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, shaping their lives according to His word (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-verses="31" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 8:31</span>). The more they study under the architect’s training, the more skilled they become, until they eventually teach and train others in His ways.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the rock of our faith! He is the master architect who has the perfect design and blueprint. Only by studying the plans, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and establishing a strong practical relationship can we build a life that will stand in the storms of life.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-verses="1" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV) …*Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain; unless the LORD guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain.*

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand against hardships, challenges, troubles, persecutions, praises, or ordinary life if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
Just having a gym membership is not enough to make a person healthy and physically fit. To obtain the full benefits of the membership, you must adhere to the gym’s discipline. In the same way, we cannot get by merely attending church or casually claiming to be Christian; we all must be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith; only then will we receive the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command goes beyond just ourselves. It is very good for us to become perfect, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples of others in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and be disciplined? So that we build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is not merely a benefit; it is also a warning for us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we will build our lives on sand, and a severe storm will destroy our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-verses="24-27" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-verses="7" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral-we are all building on some foundation. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we may stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-verses="31-32" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV) ***31** Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are truly My disciples.* ***32** And you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free."*

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by abiding in His word (His Logos)! The result of abiding in His word is the knowledge of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, after being taught in discipline, can carry out the Master’s commands. In the New Testament church, every person is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become a disciple -\> subject yourself to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to subject themselves to the discipline of sound teaching.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we go about building our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we won’t have to do this alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead us into all truth and guide us (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His written word in the Holy Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders who will strengthen us, mature us, keep us safe, and equip us for good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows what we need. He has given us experts who, through experience, know how to help our growth and maturity, and over time you can become one of them as well!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-verses="11-16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

God has given these special gifts to the Church to bring us to maturity, so that we can do and be:

<!-- p42 -->
- able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- united in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- full of the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- mature

<!-- p46 -->
- not like children

Without sound teaching we will be <u>children, tossed to and fro and carried away.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children naturally have these traits:

<!-- p49 -->
- immature - not able to bear fruit or reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- naive - not able to discern between good and bad people.

<!-- p51 -->
- self-centered - concerned with the temporary and the present.

<!-- p52 -->
- defenseless - vulnerable to harm.

### Sound teaching provides defense against the evil of the world

<!-- p54 -->
In this life we do not have the luxury of remaining spiritually neutral. We are influenced either by the Kingdom of God, or by the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul gave this instruction to his spiritual son Timothy in the Lord:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-verses="1-4" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p57 -->

What are His teachings; what will we study?
