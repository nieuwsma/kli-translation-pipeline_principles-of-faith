# Afterword

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, my hope and prayer for you, dear reader, is that these foundational truths strengthen your faith, deepen your understanding, and draw you nearer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit—it is the framework upon which we build our lives, determining how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would like to leave you with, it is this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths presented in this book are not meant to be read once and set aside; rather, they are meant to be lived out in daily life. The journey of following Christ is one of continual learning, refining, and transformation. We grow from faith to faith, from glory to glory, ever pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may have questions that remain unanswered. You may feel that you have only touched the surface of these deep and eternal truths. That is a good thing. Let those very questions lead you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter—He will lead you into all truth as you seek Him.
