# Postscript

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, my hope and prayer is that, dear reader, these foundational truths will strengthen your faith, deepen your understanding, and draw you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual pursuit—it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping how we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would like to leave you with, it is this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths laid out in this book are not meant to be read once and set aside; they are meant to be embraced in daily, practical living. The journey of following Christ is a continual journey of learning, refining, and transformation. We grow from faith to faith, from glory to glory, always pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may still have some questions unanswered. You may feel that you have only touched the surface of these deep and eternal truths. That is a good place to be. Let those questions draw you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter—He will lead you into all truth as you seek Him.

<!-- p4 -->
Paul wrote
