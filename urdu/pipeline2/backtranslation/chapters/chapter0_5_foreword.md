# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
For things to function effectively, a strong system and structure are essential. Without them, disorder and chaos can easily arise. Therefore, to live fully as a healthy Christian, it is extremely important to establish a firm foundation in doctrine.

<!-- p2 -->
Therefore, "*Principles of Faith*" will prove to be a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which discusses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining 300-plus pages that are filled with teachings and activities about the Church and our role in it, as well as material related to understanding and recognizing the voice of God, this book is immensely valuable. It will prove to be an exceptional resource for teaching in the classroom and for preparing leaders with a strong foundation in doctrine, and it will be very useful for individual readers as well.

He has a genuine passion for seeing the Church function as a healthy representation of Christ on earth. I am very proud of the work he has accomplished with this book, and since I have collaborated with him for a long time, I can confidently testify that he has invested considerable time and thought into this process, just as he does with everything else in his life.
