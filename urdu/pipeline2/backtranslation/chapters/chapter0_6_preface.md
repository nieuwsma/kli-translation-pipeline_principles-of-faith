# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Lasting Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? What made it so? How do we know? How are we certain? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This interest goes beyond a mere desire to be superficially right; it is to know with depth and precision, and to have the ability to clearly express the new understanding gained.

<!-- p3 -->
Although communication is common, effective and precise communication is challenging. To convey a message effectively, the author and originator must have an idea. However, an idea alone—like water on a hard surface—has vague edges and boundaries, is fluid, and is easily moved about.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to shape the raw idea from a vague form into a precise one. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us distinguish and differentiate what the idea is, and what it is not. Now our formed idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether in long-form text, like this book, or in short-form text, like a social media post, or in spoken form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium conveys this now formally shaped idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point, we have only spoken of the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must accurately receive, decode, and understand the message. The sent and received messages must be closely aligned with each other.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human achievement is a byproduct of effective communication. One person’s labor can only go so far. To truly build or accomplish anything great, many people (in the broad sense of humanity) work together with a shared purpose and a common idea. Setting aside natural disasters (e.g., volcanic eruptions), we also know that the greatest catastrophes that have occurred were often largely due to lost, broken, or distorted communications. Indeed, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-verses="21" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe—and how we express it—has the power to build a person, a family, a community, a nation, or to destroy them just as easily. Our belief is the foundation of our actions, and it is the blueprint for what we will do, what we will become, how we will exist. Since faith is manifested through action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our lives, but to some extent the lives of everyone around us as well.

<!-- p8 -->
We must learn to establish our beliefs correctly and share them effectively. Jesus said the same thing to His disciples in this way:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-verses="18-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV) ***18** … "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.* ***19** Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, **20** teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.*

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians want strong faith but lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without solid doctrines, we do not have the firm foundation on which to stand securely; we remain at risk of being swept away by false teaching, becoming disheartened in trials, or being unprepared for the work to which God has called us. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture—truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your fellowship with Christ, and enable you to live a life filled with the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul exhorted believers to “be rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith” (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-verses="7" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Colossians 2:7</span>). To become mature believers led by the Holy Spirit, we first need deep roots in God’s truth. That is why doctrine matters. It is not merely information; it is the framework that determines how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and for whom?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings of this book are intended to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Covenant Church. My hope is that these beliefs will persuade and inspire you in your walk of faith, so that you put down deep roots in Christ and know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are shaped and formed by my experience in the church, particularly in charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The arrangement of the teachings in this book is based on my personal study of the Word, formal and informal education, and nearly twenty years of ministry experience, during most of which I have been an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelistic outreach and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. In particular:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - Those who want a clear foundation for beginning their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture while walking in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - those who are preparing to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is structured to equip believers personally and practically, making it suitable for personal growth as well as group instruction. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to train others, the material is arranged for easy application and growth under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, beginning with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving on to the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands complete on its own yet also builds on previous chapters, making it easy to proceed step by step or focus on particular aspects as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book provides guidance for understanding and applying the Scriptures. For **teachers**, it supplies material to prepare lessons, lead discussions, and disciple others effectively. To deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience, it includes personal reflection questions, facilitation questions for group discussion, group activities, and word studies.

## How Will We Study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a foundational source for sound teaching: the instruction of the Holy Spirit, which was inscribed in Scripture by inspiration and has been transmitted through the teaching and tradition of the Church. This book will take the foundational doctrines and present them in an accessible way, concluding each chapter with personal reflection, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, countless eyewitnesses and apostles recorded the events of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, called epistles, which addressed doctrinal and practical matters within the early Christian communities. These writings, which were divinely inspired, were carefully recorded, collected, and preserved over the centuries, and thus the collection was formed which is now called the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
Generally, we will use the NKJV translation, but where a passage becomes clearer with another translation, we may use that as well. For ease of study, Scripture references will be written in italics.

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly advise that, where possible, you use multiple translations, so that the concept becomes thoroughly clear in your mind. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful, not merely for their pure textual accuracy, but because they help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures are saying.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to review some key biblical words (**word studies**) so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I recommend learning to use electronic Bible resources such as E-Sword (<https://www.e-sword.net/>), or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources will also provide you access to Strong’s Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong’s Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original language, so that we can hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated as OT in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated as NT in this book) was written in Greek. A Strong’s number will appear like this: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I am presenting in this book are not new or novel. We should be skeptical of ‘new,’ never-before-seen-or-heard teachings concerning our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have been present from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of ecclesiastical history, nor a comparative survey of Christian denominations. Pick up any two books on theology written by different authors from different denominational backgrounds, and you will see great harmony in most of their work, but you will also see notable divisions. I will not claim to be infallible, but I will say that this work is well researched, has been peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see the Acknowledgements section), and is amply supported by the Scriptures and the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

<!-- p69 -->
I urge you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to mastering them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. As you go through these teachings, my prayer is that you not only grow in understanding but also experience the power of the Holy Spirit working in you and through you. My hope is that you will be restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
