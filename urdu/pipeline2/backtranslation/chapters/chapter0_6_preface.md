# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Foundation That Endures

<!-- p2 -->
I have always been fascinated by questions. Why is *something* true? How did it become so? How do we know? How are we certain? If *something* is true then what else is true? What is false? This interest goes beyond a superficial desire to be correct؛ the aim is to know with depth and accuracy, and to be able to articulate the new understanding gained clearly.

<!-- p3 -->
Although communication is common, effective and precise communication is difficult. To convey a message effectively, the writer and originator must have an idea. However, the mere idea, like water on a hard surface- its edges and boundaries are vaguely defined, fluid, and easily shift about.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to turn the raw idea from a vague form into a precise form. Words give boundaries to our idea؛ they help us distinguish and separate what the idea is, and what it is not. Now this molded idea must be sent and received through a medium. Whether in long written form, like this book, or in short written form, like a social media post, or in spoken form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium delivers this well-formed idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to now we have only spoken of the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, it is necessary that the recipient correctly receive the message, decode it, and understand it properly. The messages sent and received should be closely aligned.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human achievement is the result of effective communication. The labor of one person can accomplish only to a limited extent. To truly build or accomplish something great, many people (speaking here generally for humanity) labor together with a shared purpose and a shared idea. Setting aside natural disasters (such as volcanic eruptions) we also know that the greatest catastrophes in history often occurred largely because communication was absent, fractured, or corrupted. Truly life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>)۔

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we express it has the power to build up a person, a household, a community, a nation, or likewise to destroy them. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will be, how we will exist. Because belief is manifested through action and communication, it determines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our lives but, to a degree, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must learn to establish our beliefs correctly and share them effectively. Jesus likewise said to His disciples:

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying، "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth۔
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations، baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit،
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you؛ and behold، I am with you always، to the end of the age۔" Amen۔
>

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians desire a strong faith but lack a clear foundation to build upon. Without sound doctrine we do not have the solid foundation on which we can stand firm؛ we are at risk of being swept away by false teachings, discouraged in trials, or unprepared for the ministry to which God has called us. This book is designed to equip you with the foundational teachings of Scripture- truths that will strengthen your faith, deepen your fellowship with Christ, and empower you to live a Spirit-filled life-۔

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul urged believers to “*be rooted and built up in Him and be established in the faith*” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>)۔ To become mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God’s truth. That is why doctrine matters. It is not merely information؛ it is the framework that determines how we live and how we respond before God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The purpose of the teachings in this book is to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational doctrines of the New Testament church. My hope is that these doctrines persuade and inspire your walk of faith to put down deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings have been compiled and shaped from my experience in the church, especially within Charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The formation of this book is built upon my personal study of the Word, formal and informal education, and nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of which time I have been an ordained Christian minister. My background includes youth and children's ministry, evangelism and revival/renewal ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and the administrative affairs of church and ministry.

<!-- p17 -->
This book is for those believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. In particular:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation for the start of their Christian life۔

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture as they walk in greater obedience and faith۔

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small-group leaders** - those who are preparing to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is structured to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, making it ideal for individual growth as well as group instruction. Whether you use this book to strengthen your walk with God or to train others, the material is arranged for easy application and growth under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to use this book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, beginning with foundational truths like **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving toward the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic stands on its own yet also builds on previous chapters, making it easy either to work step by step or to focus on specific sections as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book provides guidance for understanding and applying the Scriptures. For **teachers**, it supplies material for preparing lessons, leading discussions, and discipling others effectively. Personal reflection questions, group discussion questions, group activities, and word studies are included to deepen understanding and encourage steps of practical obedience.

## How will we study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound teaching: the teachings of the Holy Spirit, which were inspired and recorded in the Scriptures and passed down through the Church’s teaching and tradition. This book will take core doctrines and present them in an accessible manner, and will conclude each chapter with personal reflection, group guiding questions, and optional group activities.

After the ministry of Jesus, countless eyewitnesses and apostles recorded the events of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, that is, epistles, which addressed the doctrinal and practical matters of the early Christian communities. These writings, which were inspired, were carefully written, compiled, and preserved over centuries, and thus the collection was formed that is now called the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
In general we will use the NKJV translation, but where it helps to make the wording clearer, another translation may also be used. *For ease of study, the reference verses will be italicized.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly advise that where possible you use multiple translations so that the meaning settles clearly in your mind. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful, not merely for their literal accuracy but because they explain to me in simple English what the Scriptures are saying.

<!-- p61 -->

The Old Testament (sometimes abbreviated in this book as OT) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (sometimes abbreviated in this book as NT) was written in Greek. The Strong’s number will appear like this: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I am presenting in this book are not new or novel. We should be skeptical of ‘new’ teachings concerning our Christian faith that have never before been seen or heard. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have existed from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparative survey of Christian denominations. Pick up any two books on theology written by different authors from various denominational backgrounds, and you will see deep harmony across large portions of their work, yet you will also notice notable differences. I will not claim to be inerrant, but I will claim that this work is well researched, has been reviewed by faithful men and women (see the acknowledgments section), and is well attested by Scripture and by the widely accepted Pentecostal tradition.

<!-- p69 -->
I urge you to read the Scriptures for yourself and resolve to become proficient in them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we will find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to read this book with faith and expectation. As you go through these teachings, my prayer is that you not only grow in understanding but also experience the power of the Holy Spirit working in you and through you. My hope is that you will be restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
