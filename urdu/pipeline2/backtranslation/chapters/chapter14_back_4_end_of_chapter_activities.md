# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
## These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce each chapter's core learning objectives. Each activity is intended to take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. With questions for personal reflection and group discussion, these activities are best used in a group study setting to deepen understanding of and engagement with the book's key lessons. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and adapt the activities as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Teaching Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Scripture Match

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
Reinforce foundational biblical concepts about teaching by reviewing verses that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** into **pairs or small groups**, each consisting of 3-4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign passages** - Give each group one of the following references:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-verses="7" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-verses="1-4" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the word and guard against false teaching.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-verses="11-16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Have each group do the following:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read their assigned passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the core teaching** about sound teaching in 1-2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** of how this teaching helps protect against false teaching or helps others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share observations** - Each group takes **2 minutes** to present their reference, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Teaching in Action

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound teaching to a **real-life scenario** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups consisting of **4-5 participants**۔

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend says to you، “It really doesn’t matter what we believe، as long as we are sincere.” How would you respond؟*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who hears conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works۔ What Bible verses could you share to help clarify؟*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend has stopped going to church because they feel that as long as they believe in God، it is unnecessary۔ How would you encourage them from Scripture؟*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - Groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** that is at the center of the scenario۔

<!-- p28 -->
- **1-2 Bible verses** list that address this issue۔

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would advise the person in the scenario۔

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - Groups should, in turn, present their scenario and responses before the group، followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others۔

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture؟

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants so that, on the basis of Scripture, they can **discern between truth and falsehood**۔

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Prepare the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud, one at a time،۔ Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**۔

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Decide whether the statement is **true** or **false**۔

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer۔

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Before confirming the correct answers, give groups time to discuss disagreements or to find verses۔

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-verses="8-9" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *If we are sincere, every path leads to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-verses="6" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Right doctrine is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-verses="16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelations for guidance.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-verses="16-17" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Good works are the result of salvation, not its cause.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Ephesians 2:10</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name the Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help the participants connect the names and attributes of God to **practical life applications**.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - Write **one name or attribute of God** on each card (e.g., Jehovah-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- From this chapter, read the **name or attribute** and the accompanying **Scripture reference**.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Conclusion** - Have each participant or group present their reflections, emphasizing how the practical applications of God’s character show up in real-life struggles or successes.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate God’s names and attributes into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide participants a **quiet place** to meditate on one of God’s names from this chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer practice:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Select a **name or attribute** that corresponds to the current need (e.g., for healing, **Yahweh-Rapha**).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that same name, write a brief **prayer**, praise God, and ask Him to act in accordance with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share prayers in small groups or pairs (optional) so that **encouragement happens and faith is strengthened**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **End with corporate prayer** - Thank God that He has revealed Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering God’s attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants to **reflect on and apply them in their lives** regarding God’s attributes through **personal stories** and **corporate prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on an attribute:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Give each group a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God taken from this chapter (e.g., **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**).

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **select one attribute** that aligns most with their current stage of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Sharing stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares one story** (personal experience or observation) related to the selected attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Sample questions:

<!-- p79 -->
- *"When did you experience God as your Provider?"*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped/formed your life؟”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to trust in this aspect of God in everyday life؟”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ask the group to write a short prayer reflecting this attribute and pray together، focusing on applying God's character to your situation۔

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Spreading Effects of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Explain how the effects of sin ripple outward like waves، and how they affect relationships، communities، and spiritual lives، while also emphasizing God's plan of redemption۔

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Example (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a stone into a bowl of water (or describe the scene if materials are not available)۔

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to observe the outward-spreading ripples and discuss:  
      *“How does this scene illustrate the effect of sin in our lives and relationships؟”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and give each one a biblical example of sin:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-verses="6-13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation)۔

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-verses="3-12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Envy، murder، and exile)۔

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-verses="2-27" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences)۔

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin؟*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see the opportunity for God's grace and restoration in each story؟*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Regather the groups and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *In what situations have you personally felt the “waves” of sin?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace worked to stop these waves and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->
### Activity 2: Timeline of Sin and Restoration (Scripture Review)

<!-- p105 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p106 -->
Trace God’s plan of salvation from Genesis to Christ, and focus on His grace amid judgment.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Divide into small groups** - give each group a Scripture reference that highlights sin and restoration:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-verses="21" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Genesis 3:21</span>** - God provides garments for Adam and Eve.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-verses="6-7" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warns Cain before he sins.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-verses="13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-verses="21-22" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Leviticus 16:21-22</span>** - The goat appointed for Azazel carries the sin away.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-verses="29" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">John 1:29</span>** - Jesus, the Lamb of God, takes away sin.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group Work:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the sin or judgment present in this passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the grace or salvation presented.

<!-- p117 -->
- Summarize the lessons learned about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Have each group share its insights, linking the references together to form a timeline of God’s plan of salvation.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Purpose:

<!-- p122 -->
Guide participants to **personally put forgiveness into practice** - both to **receive it** and to **extend it to others**.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **reflect silently** on the areas where they:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and ask God for forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive** someone who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-verses="9" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Guide participants in private prayer. It is not necessary for the facilitator to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins before God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-verses="12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to (optionally) share how forgiveness brings freedom and restoration.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
To help participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives, and replace them with works led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Provide participants with a **List of Possible Dead Works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** in their lives where they see evidence of dead works.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of Possible Dead Works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church worship out of habit without engaging from the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to gain approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to attain righteousness by personal effort instead of relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to obtain grace while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful attitudes or habits:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Harboring grudges or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual practices such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal and passion for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or pursuits that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish goals at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions done out of fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others merely out of duty rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering just to check a box, not out of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success over spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so that they (if appropriate) share one aspect they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to **replace dead works** with Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key points or the actions they will take to deal with dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer that God's guidance would help us live a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->

#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->
Guide participants to understand the stages of repentance and how they lead to transformation.

<!-- p218 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p219 -->

<!-- p220 -->

<!-- p221 -->
2.  **Interactive Group Exercise:**

<!-- p222 -->
- Assign groups to discuss each step of the pathway:

<!-- p223 -->
- *What does this step look like practically؟*

<!-- p224 -->

<!-- p225 -->
3.  **Reflection and Sharing:**

<!-- p226 -->
- Have each group share their insights on one step of the pathway۔

<!-- p227 -->
- Conclude with how repentance leads to spiritual growth and alignment with God’s will۔

<!-- p228 -->

#### Instructions:

<!-- p237 -->
3.  **Personal Application:**

<!-- p238 -->
- Encourage participants to write a personal prayer of repentance inspired by David’s words۔

<!-- p239 -->
4.  **Conclusion:**

<!-- p240 -->
- Conclude with a prayer in which God is implored to help the participants cultivate a steadfast spirit and a heart that is wholly surrendered to Him.

#### Objective:

<!-- p244 -->
Help participants understand that **Faith comes by hearing** and recognize those moments when God's Word becomes personal and living.

<!-- p245 -->

<!-- p252 -->
- Invite participants to share their reflections in small groups or as a whole congregation.

<!-- p253 -->
- Discuss: *“What steps can we take to prepare ourselves to hear God's voice more clearly?”*

<!-- p254 -->
### Activity 2: Faith Produces Obedience (Practical Application)

<!-- p255 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p256 -->
Clarify the mutual relationship between **faith and obedience** and encourage participants to take bold steps of faith.

<!-- p257 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p258 -->
1.  **Discussion (5 minutes):**

<!-- p259 -->
