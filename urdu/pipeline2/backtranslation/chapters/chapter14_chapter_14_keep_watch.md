# Chapter 14 - Keep Watch

<!-- p1 -->
Since we have been given Christ's spiritual authority to establish His kingdom on earth, we should work diligently and swiftly, because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the end times is called **Eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *most of them disagree with each other*! In particular, they differ in interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), the sequence of events, and the timing. I am reminded of the saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke — “When I’m done breaking chains on earth, I’ll split hairs in heaven!”

<!-- p3 -->
As the author, I am reluctant to go very deep into this overly debated subject because this book is primarily focused on the basics of faith. For the most part, I will limit myself to a high-level overview of the events described in Scripture and focus on what is most important — **the same** **gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally agreed points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus is coming back

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  The judgment of all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will see what the concept is, look at the reference verses, consider why it is important, and, in light of it, what we should do.

<!-- p12 -->
### **1. Jesus is coming back**

<!-- p13 -->
**What it is:** **The Second Coming of Christ** is that Jesus will actually return to the earth as King and Judge.

<!-- p14 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-verses="11" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p15 -->
> **<u>Acts 1:11</u>** (NKJV) *Men of Galilee, why do you stand gazing up into heaven? This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come back in the same way as you saw Him go into heaven.*

<!-- p16 -->
</span>

<!-- p17 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="REV" data-chapter="22" data-verses="12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p18 -->
> **<u>Revelation 22:12</u>***, (NKJV) *And behold, I am coming soon, and My reward is with Me, to repay each one according to his work.*

<!-- p19 -->
</span>

<!-- p20 -->
**Why this matters:** The return of Christ is the fulfillment of God's promise that He will complete redemption and establish His kingdom. It reminds us to live with urgency، knowing that one day we will see Him face-to-face.

<!-- p21 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p22 -->
- **Be watchful:** Live in expectation of His return.

<!-- p23 -->
- **Be faithful:** Carry out the Great Commission (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 28:19-20</span>) with purpose and devotion.

<!-- p24 -->
### **2. The Resurrection of the Dead**

<!-- p25 -->
**What is it:** **Resurrection** is that all people-both righteous and wicked-will be raised from the dead to face judgment.

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-verses="28-29" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p27 -->
> **<u>John 5:28-29</u>** *(NKJV) **28** Do not marvel at this؛ for the hour is coming in which all who are in the graves will hear His voice **29** and come forth-those who have done good، to the resurrection of life، and those who have done evil، to the resurrection of condemnation.*

<!-- p28 -->
</span>

<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="4" data-verses="16" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p30 -->
> **<u>1 Thessalonians 4:16</u>** *(NKJV) *… the dead in Christ will rise first.*

<!-- p31 -->
</span>

<!-- p32 -->
**Why this matters:** The resurrection guarantees justice and eternal life for believers while warning unbelievers of judgment. It points to God's sovereignty over life and death.

<!-- p33 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p34 -->
- **Be ready:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness to God.

<!-- p35 -->
- **Share the Gospel:** Help others prepare for the resurrection of life by bringing them to Christ.

<!-- p36 -->
### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What is it:** All people-believers and unbelievers-will face judgment before God. Believers will appear at the judgment seat of Christ for reward، while unbelievers will face the Great White Throne judgment.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-verses="11-12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** *(NKJV) **11** Then I saw a great white throne … **12** … and the dead were judged according to their works*

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and terrifying final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers, our names are written in the **Lamb’s *Book of Life*** *(see the book of *Revelation*)**.** Thus we will stand* *the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation but for reward! Again**,** this is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. It reminds us that our lives are important—not only for today but for **eternity**.

<!-- p45 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p46 -->
> **<u>2 Corinthians 5:10</u>** (NKJV) *For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive the things done in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad.*

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-verses="12-15" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p49 -->
> **<u>1 Corinthians 3:12-15</u>** (NKJV)
>
> ***12** Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,*
>
> ***13** each one’s work will become clear; for the Day will declare it, because it will be revealed by fire; and the fire will test each one’s work, of what sort it is.*
>
> ***14** If anyone’s work which he has built on it endures, he will receive a reward.*
>
> ***15** If anyone’s work is burned, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.*

<!-- p50 -->
</span>

<!-- p51 -->
At this judgment we will receive rewards for the good works we have done! Everyone present at this judgment has lived and died as a believer; therefore their eternal salvation is secure. However, not everyone will receive further reward. In the New Testament, crowns are mentioned as rewards that represent victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns highlight God’s acknowledgment of believers’ faithfulness and service in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p52 -->
Rewards:

<!-- p53 -->
1.  **Crown of Righteousness** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-verses="8" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">2 Timothy 4:8</span>)* - For those who long for Christ’s appearing and live godly lives.

<!-- p54 -->
2.  **Incorruptible Crown** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="9" data-verses="24-25" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Corinthians 9:24-25</span>)* - For those believers who practice self-discipline and faithfulness.

<!-- p55 -->
3.  **Crown of Life** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="JAS" data-chapter="1" data-verses="12" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">James 1:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="REV" data-chapter="2" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Revelation 2:10</span>)* - For those who endure trials, persecution, and testing.

<!-- p56 -->
4.  **Crown of Glory** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="5" data-verses="2-4" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Peter 5:2-4</span>)* - For those leaders and shepherds who faithfully lead God’s people.

<!-- p57 -->
5.  **Crown of Joy** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="2" data-verses="19-20" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">1 Thessalonians 2:19-20</span>)* - For those who bring others to Christ and make disciples.

<!-- p58 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p59 -->
- **Live with purpose:** Work diligently, knowing that our efforts for the Kingdom carry eternal significance.

<!-- p60 -->
- **Examine yourselves:** Ensure that we live a life of faith and obedience.

<!-- p61 -->
### **4. Eternal Destiny**

<!-- p62 -->
**What it is:** All people will spend eternity in one of two places-eternal life with God or eternal separation from Him.

<!-- p63 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-verses="46" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p64 -->
> **<u>Matthew 25:46</u>** (NKJV) And these will go away into everlasting punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.*

<!-- p65 -->
</span>

<!-- p66 -->
**Why it matters:** Our eternal destiny depends on whether we have accepted Christ or rejected Him. This highlights the urgent importance of the gospel message and discipleship.

<!-- p67 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p68 -->
- **Proclaim salvation:** Boldly share the gospel to bring others toward eternal life.

<!-- p69 -->
- **Live with eternity in view:** Focus on the things that matter forever-faith, obedience, and love.

<!-- p70 -->
### **5. A Defeated Enemy**

<!-- p71 -->
**What it is:** Satan, the deceiver, will be thrown into the **Lake of Fire**-defeated forever by Christ.

<!-- p72 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="quote" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-verses="10" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p73 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:10</u>**, (NKJV) *The devil, who deceived them, was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone where the beast and the false prophet are. And they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.*

<!-- p74 -->
</span>

<!-- p75 -->
**Why it matters:** This final act reveals God's sovereignty over evil and assures believers that Satan's reign of deception and destruction has ended. It gives us confidence in God's ultimate victory.

<!-- p76 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p77 -->
- **Remain steadfast:** Resist the enemy's lies, knowing that he has already been defeated.

<!-- p78 -->
- **Walk in authority:** Live with boldness and faith, knowing that God has already won the victory.

<!-- p79 -->
### **6. A New Heaven and a New Earth**

<!-- p80 -->
**What this is:** After the final judgment and the defeat of the enemy, God will make for His people a **new heaven and a new earth**-a perfect, eternal dwelling.

<!-- p81 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-type="fragment" data-book="REV" data-chapter="21" data-verses="1,3" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">

<!-- p82 -->
> **<u>Revelation 21:1,3</u>** *(NKJV) **1** I saw a new heaven and a new earth… **3** …God Himself will be with them and be their God.*

<!-- p83 -->
</span>

<!-- p84 -->
**Why this matters:** It gives us the hope of restoration, where God will wipe away every tear and make all things new-free from pain, sin, and death.

<!-- p85 -->
**What should we do?**

<!-- p86 -->
- **Fix your eyes on eternity:** Set your focus on the eternal hope rather than on temporary hardships.

<!-- p87 -->
- **Remain steadfast in faith:** Endure trials, knowing that eternal joy awaits.

<!-- p88 -->
## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, if you are curious about common dividing points in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the Rapture:** Pre-tribulation, mid-tribulation, or post-tribulation? Views differ on when the Rapture will occur in relation to the **Tribulation**. Some denominations do not believe in the Rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the Tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of struggle?

<!-- p92 -->
- **Millennial Kingdom:** Is it literal **(Premillennial)**, symbolic **(Amillennial)**, or spiritual **(Postmillennial)**?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion:** This occurs after the millennial period (literal event or symbolic?).

<!-- p94 -->
The study of eschatology is very good, but remember, as D. L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing the main thing!”

## **Closing thoughts**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the fulfillment of God’s plan and the very reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parables of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-verses="1-13" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-type="ref" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-verses="40-42" data-lang="en" data-version="NKJV">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the rewards of crowns or the hope of the resurrection, our motivation is clear—Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**May God find us prepared, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**
