# Chapter 14 - Stay Awake

<!-- p1 -->
We have been given Christ’s spiritual authority to establish His kingdom on earth; therefore we must work with diligence and urgency, because Jesus will return!

<!-- p2 -->
The study of Christ’s return and the end times is an important part of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the end times is called **eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *most of them do not agree with each other*! They especially differ over interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), the sequence of events, and timing. I am reminded of the saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonnke - “I will split hairs in heaven when I’m done breaking chains on earth!”

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, I hesitate to go very deep into this highly controversial subject because this book is primarily focused on the foundations of faith. I will mostly limit myself to a high-level overview of the events described in the Scriptures and focus on what is most important - **the** **gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally agreed points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus is coming back

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for everyone

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destinies

<!-- p9 -->
5.  Defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will see what the concept is, look at reference verses, why it is important, and what we should do in light of it.

### **3. Judgment for All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What is it:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers will appear at the **Judgment Seat of Christ** for rewards, while unbelievers will face the **Great White Throne judgment**.

<!-- p38 -->
#### Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away. And no place was found for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and dreadful final judgment for unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers our names are in the **Lamb’s *Book of Life*** *(*see the book of *Revelation*)**.** Therefore we will stand* ***the Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation but for reward! Again**,** this is not a moment of **shame or fear** for believers, but a **celebration** of what God has accomplished through us. It reminds us that our lives matter—not only for today but for **eternity**.

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, if you're curious to know what the common points of division are in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most debated points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the Rapture:** Pre-, Mid-, or Post-Tribulation? Opinions vary on when the Rapture happens in relation to the tribulation. Some denominations do not believe in the Rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the Tribulation:** Is it a literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of struggle?

<!-- p92 -->
- **The Millennial Kingdom:** Is it literal (Premillennial), symbolic (Amillennial), or spiritual (Postmillennial)?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan's final rebellion:** This happens after the millennial period (literal event or allegorical?).

<!-- p94 -->
The study of eschatology is excellent, but let us remember, as D.L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing, the main thing!”

## **Final thoughts**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the fulfillment of God's plan and the very reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the parable of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to be watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the reward of crowns or the hope of the resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming back!

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found prepared, faithful, and expectant on that glorious day!**
