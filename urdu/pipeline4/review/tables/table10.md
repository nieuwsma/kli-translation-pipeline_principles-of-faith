<td>Symbolizes God’s provision; first used when God provided a ram as a substitute for Isaac.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Rapha</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-fah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Who Heals</p>

<td>Shows God’s healing nature; He restores physical, emotional, and spiritual health.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<p>YAH-weh NEE-see</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is My Banner</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 17:15</span></p></td>
<td>Represents God’s protection and victory; used by Moses after a victory over the Amalekites.</td>

<td>Points to God as the One who sets His people apart as holy, through His commandments and grace.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Gideon used this name after God’s reassurance; highlights God as the source of peace and wholeness.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Sabaoth</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Sa-ba-OTE</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD of Hosts (Armies)</p>

<td>Refers to God’s supreme power over all heavenly and earthly forces; emphasizes His sovereignty.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Raah</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh RAH-ah</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD is My Shepherd</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalms 23:1</span></p></td>
<td>David’s use of this name to show God’s guidance, care, and provision, like a shepherd with His flock.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Yahweh-Tsidkenu</strong></p>
<p>YAH-weh Tsid-KAY-noo</p></td>
<td><p>The LORD Our Righteousness</p>
<p><span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 23:6</span></p></td>
<td>Indicates God as the source of righteousness and justice, fulfilled in the Messiah.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Used to describe God’s abiding presence; symbolizes God’s presence with His people, even in exile.</td>

</strong>YAH-weh GIM-oh-lah</td>
