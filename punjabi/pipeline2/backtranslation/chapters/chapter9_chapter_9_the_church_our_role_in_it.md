# Chapter 9 - The Church &amp; Our Role in It

<!-- p1 -->
The Church is the way by which God—on the earth—through His people, doing His work—reveals Himself. We are called to be His body; we are His hands and feet to fulfill His work and purpose. Without our efforts, the harvest will remain in the field and will not be gathered.

<!-- p2 -->
## Who Is the Church?

<!-- p3 -->
The Church, ***Ekklesia** (eh-klee-SEE-ah - **G1577**),* is derived from two Greek words:

<!-- p4 -->
- **"Ek"**: "out of."

<!-- p5 -->
- **"Kaleo"**: "to call."

<!-- p6 -->
Taken together, **Ekklesia** literally means "the called-out ones" or "the assembly called out." In ancient Greek city-states, it referred to a gathering of citizens summoned for a specific purpose, such as legislative or judicial meetings. It was a secular term used for an assembly of people.

<!-- p7 -->
In the New Testament, it is used primarily for the community of those who believe in Jesus Christ. It can refer to:

<!-- p8 -->
- **The local church**: A specific gathering of believers in a particular place (for example, the church in Corinth, Philippi, etc.).

- **The universal church**: The collective body of all believers across time and place.

<!-- p16 -->
The Church is the people, not the building or place where they meet. The called-out ones: this idea indicates that Christians are called out of the world and set apart for God’s purposes (for example, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Peter 2:9</span>) for a specific mission and purpose (the Great Commission). Technically, wherever two or more believers gather together (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 18:20</span>) that is the church. Typically, the church is organized into regular meetings, perhaps with a set place and time, but wherever two or more people meet in Jesus’ name, there the church is!

<!-- p17 -->
The Church was established by Jesus, and it grew through the work of the disciples who followed Him in the first century AD. The church that we have and experience today in all its local congregations throughout the earth is the direct result of the original work of Christ and His apostles. As believers, we should be committed to a local church for fellowship, edification, equipping for service, and doing good works!

<!-- p18 -->
To help us understand what the church is, Scripture gives us powerful images that show what it is designed to be.
