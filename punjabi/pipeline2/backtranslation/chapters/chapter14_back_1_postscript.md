# Epilogue

<!-- p1 -->
As I bring this book to a close, my hope and prayer for you, dear reader, is that these foundational truths have strengthened your faith, deepened your understanding, and drawn you closer to God. Doctrine is not merely an intellectual endeavor-it is the framework upon which we build our lives, shaping the way we live, love, and serve in the kingdom of God.

<!-- p2 -->
If there is one thing I would like to leave you with, it is this: keep growing. Keep seeking. Keep walking in faith. The truths laid out in this book are not to be read once and set aside; they are to be lived every day. The journey of following Christ is one of continual learning, refining, and transformation. We move from faith to faith, from glory to glory, always pressing on to know Him more.

<!-- p3 -->
You may have some questions that still remain unanswered. You may feel that you have only touched the surface of these profound and eternal truths. That is a good place to be. Let those questions draw you deeper into God’s Word, into prayer, and into fellowship with other believers. The Holy Spirit is your guide, your teacher, and your comforter-when you seek Him, He will lead you into all truth.
