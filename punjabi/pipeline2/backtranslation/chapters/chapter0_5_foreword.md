# Preface

<!-- p1 -->
To run things effectively, a robust system and structure are absolutely essential. Without these, disorder and chaos can easily arise. That is why, to live fully as a healthy Christian, it is crucial to lay a strong foundation in doctrine.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which discusses the importance of sound doctrine, to the remaining 300-plus pages that include teachings and activities about the church and our roles in it, as well as understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an unparalleled tool for teaching in the classroom and preparing leaders with a strong foundation in doctrine, and it will also be very beneficial for individual readers.

Andrew has been an integral part of my inner circle for many years, and his talents and the way he understands matters of the Kingdom have always impressed me. He has a genuine passion to ensure that the church on earth functions as a healthy representation of Christ. I am very proud of the work he has done with this book, and having collaborated with him for a long time, I can say with full confidence that he has invested ample time and thought into this process, just as he does with every aspect of his life.
