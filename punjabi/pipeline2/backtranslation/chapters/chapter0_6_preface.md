# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building a Foundation That Lasts

<!-- p2 -->
I have always had a fascination with questions. Why is something true? How did it come to be so? How do we know? How are we certain? If something is true, what else is true? What is false? This interest goes beyond a surface-level desire to be right, but rather to know deeply and accurately, and to be able to articulate the newly gained understanding with clarity.

<!-- p3 -->
Although communication is common, effective and accurate communication is challenging. To communicate a message effectively, the writer and originator must have an idea. But an idea by itself is like water on a hard surface—its edges and boundaries are not properly defined, it is fluid and easily shifts around.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, terms, and phrases to turn a raw idea from a vague shape into a defined form. Words give our idea a boundary; they help us distinguish and separate what the idea is, and what it is not. Now our shaped idea must be transmitted and received through some medium. Whether in long-form text, like this book, or in short-form text, like a social media post, or in oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the medium now carries that well-crafted idea to the recipient.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to this point we have only spoken of the originator and writer of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must correctly receive, decode, and understand the message. The sent and received messages should closely match.

<!-- p6 -->
Every great human creation is the product of effective communication. The labor of one person can only do so much. To truly build or do something great, many people (speaking broadly of humankind here) must labor together with a shared purpose and a common idea. Setting aside natural disasters (such as a volcanic eruption) we also know that the greatest catastrophes that have occurred were often, in large part, due to lost, broken, or distorted communication. Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate it has the power to build up a person, a family, a community, a nation—or to destroy them just as thoroughly. What we believe is the foundation of our actions and the blueprint for what we will do, who we will become, and how we will live. Because belief is expressed through action and communication, it determines the role we play in existence. Our beliefs shape not only our own lives, but, to some extent, the lives of everyone around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must learn to properly establish our beliefs and to share them effectively. Jesus said the same when He told His disciples:

<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Amen.

</span>

<!-- p12 -->
Today many Christians desire a strong faith but lack a clear foundation to build upon. Without sound doctrine, we do not have the solid foundation we need to stand firm; we are at risk of being swayed by false teaching, discouraged in trials, or unprepared for the work God has called us to do. This book is designed to equip you with the basic teachings—truths of Scripture—that will strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a life filled with the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p13 -->
The apostle Paul encouraged believers to be "*rooted and built up in Him, and established in the faith*" (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). To become mature, Holy Spirit-led believers, we must first have deep roots in God’s truth. This is why doctrine matters. It is more than mere information; it is the framework that determines how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why this book, and who is it for?

<!-- p15 -->
The teachings of this book are intended to instruct and encourage you, the reader, in the foundational principles of the New Testament church. It is my hope that this faith will assure and inspire you to put down deep roots in Christ and to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
These teachings are framed and shaped by my experience in the church, particularly within charismatic or Pentecostal expressions. The body of teaching in this book arises from my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and nearly twenty years of involvement in ministry, much of which I spent as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children’s ministry, evangelistic outreach and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book serves believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. In particular:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New believers** - those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - those who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture as they walk in greater obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, mentors, and small group leaders** - those who are preparing to make disciples of others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers in both a personal and practical way, making it ideal for group instruction as well as personal growth. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your own walk with God or to equip others, the content is crafted for easy application and Spirit-led growth.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical sequence, beginning with foundational truths like **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and then moving on to the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual authority**. Each topic is complete in itself but also builds on previous chapters, making it easy to work through step by step or focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understanding and applying Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides material for preparing lessons, leading discussions, and discipling others effectively. To deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience, it includes personal reflection questions, group discussion questions for facilitation, group activities, and word studies.

## How Will We Study?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a primary source for sound teaching—the teachings of the Holy Spirit—inspired and written in Scripture, and handed down through the Church’s teaching and tradition. This book will take essential teachings, present them in an easy-to-understand format, and conclude each chapter with personal reflection, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities.

After Jesus’ ministry, many eyewitnesses and apostles recorded details of His life, ministry, death, and resurrection. They also wrote letters, or epistles, that addressed doctrinal and practical matters within the early Christian communities. These writings, divinely inspired, were carefully recorded, collected, and preserved over the centuries, resulting in what is now called the Bible.

<!-- p59 -->
Generally, we will use the NKJV translation, but where it makes a passage clearer, we may use a different translation as well. *Scripture references will be in italics to assist with reading.*

<!-- p60 -->
I strongly suggest that wherever possible, multiple translations be used so that the concept is clearly understood in your mind. Personally, I find translations like the Amplified Bible helpful, not for their sheer textual accuracy, but because they help me understand in plain English what the Scriptures are communicating.

<!-- p61 -->
We will take time to explore some key biblical words (**word study**) so that we can better understand what God is saying and how it applies to our lives. I suggest that you learn to use electronic Bible resources, such as: E-Sword (<https: www.e-sword.net=""></https:>), or Blue Letter Bible ([<u>https://www.blueletterbible.org/</u>](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)). Such resources will also give you access to Strong’s Concordance.

<!-- p62 -->
Strong’s Concordance helps us understand what the Bible meant in its original language, so that we can hear more clearly what God is saying. The Old Testament (abbreviated as OT several times in this book) was written in Hebrew. The New Testament (abbreviated as NT several times in this book) was written in Greek. A Strong’s number will be indicated as: **Hxxx** (Hebrew) or **Gyyy** (Greek).

The teachings I present in this book are neither new nor unique. We should be skeptical of ‘new’—never-before-seen-or-heard—teachings about our Christian faith. Rather, this book is a compilation and explanation of teachings that have existed from the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparison of Christian denominations. Pick up any two books on theology written by different authors with different denominational backgrounds—you will see great agreement in much of their work, but also notable differences. I will not claim infallibility, but I will claim that this work is well researched, peer-reviewed by faithful men and women (see Acknowledgements section), and well supported by the Scriptures and the mainstream Pentecostal tradition.

I urge you to read the Scriptures for yourself and commit to becoming proficient in them. The Word of God promises that when we seek Him, we find Him (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:13</span>). I invite you to approach this book with faith and expectation. As you go through these teachings, my prayer is that you not only increase in understanding, but also experience the power of the Holy Spirit working in you and through you. My hope is that you would be restored, strengthened, and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose in your life.
