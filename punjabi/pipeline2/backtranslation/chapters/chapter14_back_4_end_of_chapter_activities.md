# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the main learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity should take approximately 15–20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources that are not included in this book. Along with questions for personal reflection and group discussion, these activities are best used in the form of group study to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as guidelines. Be creative, encourage participation, and modify the activities as needed.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Matters

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Scripture Match-Up

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
To reinforce the biblical core concepts about doctrine by exploring passages that emphasize the importance of doctrine.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** into **pairs or small groups** of 3–4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Scripture** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preach the word and beware of false teaching.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group discussion** - Have each group do the following:

<!-- p13 -->
- Read **their assigned Scripture** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- Provide a **summary of the key teaching** about doctrine in 1–2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- Discuss a **real-life example** of how this teaching helps counter falsehood or helps others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share insights** - Give each group **2 minutes** to present their passage, summary, and application to the whole group.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Practice

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to **real-life scenarios** and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign scenarios** - Give each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend says to you, “As long as we are sincere, what we believe doesn’t really matter.” How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are mentoring a new believer who hears conflicting teaching about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. What Scripture verses could you share for clarity?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops going to church because he feels that as long as he believes in God, it isn’t necessary. How would you encourage him with Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** that is at the center of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- List **1-2 Scripture verses** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** of how they would counsel the person in this scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share responses** - Groups, in turn, present their scenarios and answers to the group, and then others provide **brief feedback or additional suggestions**.

<!-- p31 -->
### Activity 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **discern between truth and error** using Scripture as the foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Set up the activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud, one at a time. Some statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group response** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide a **Bible verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Facilitate discussion** - Before confirming the correct answers, give the groups time to discuss disagreements or to look up verses.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(Correct - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *If we are sincere, all roads lead to God.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Sound teaching is not as important as loving others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *We need the Bible, but we also need modern revelations for our guidance.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Good works are the result of salvation, not the cause of it.* *(Correct - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:10</span>)*

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name that Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God with their **practical life application**.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare index cards** - On each card, write **one name or attribute of God** (e.g., Jehovah-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent).

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the cards** - Give one card to each participant or group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group work:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** from this chapter and the accompanying **Scripture reference**.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal or biblical situation?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Conclusion** - Allow each participant or group to present their reflections, emphasizing how God's character applies in practical ways to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how to incorporate God’s names and attributes into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set aside time for reflection** - Provide a **quiet place** for the participants so that they can focus on one of God’s names from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided prayer practice:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** related to your current need (e.g., for healing **Jehovah-Rapha**).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Using that name, write a short **prayer**, praise God, and ask that he act in keeping with his character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: To encourage and strengthen faith, share prayers in small groups or pairs (optional).

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Conclude with a group prayer** - Thank God for revealing himself through his names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering God’s attributes (15-20 minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants bring **reflection and application** of God’s attributes into their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on attributes:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Give each group a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God selected from the chapter (e.g., **faithful, merciful, sovereign, provider**).

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that resonates most with their current life stage.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Sharing stories (10 minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares a story** (personal experience or observation) that connects with the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- **Example prompt questions:**

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When did you experience God as your provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“In daily life, how can we encourage one another to trust in this aspect of God?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Have the group **write a short prayer** that reflects that attribute and pray together, focusing on applying God's character in your situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Show that the **effects spread outward like ripples**, affecting **relationships, communities, and spiritual lives**, while also highlighting God's **plan of redemption**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Throw **a stone into a bowl of water** (or, if materials are not available, describe the image/scene).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples** spreading outward and discuss:  
      *“How does this scene illustrate the effects of sin in our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small-group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one **a biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see opportunities for God's grace and restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Regather the groups and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What “waves” of sin have you personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How is God's grace working to stop those waves and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

2.  **Group work:**

<!-- p115 -->

<!-- p116 -->

<!-- p117 -->

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->

### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Prayerful Application)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants to **personally process forgiveness**-both **receiving it** and **extending it to others**.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **quietly reflect** on areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God's forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided prayer time (10 minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>** Read

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in personal prayer. The facilitator does not need to hear the conversation between the individual and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins before God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for the strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g., forgiving someone, seeking reconciliation).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Final reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>** Read

<!-- p136 -->

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from dead works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Self-Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify “dead works” in their lives and replace them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Give participants a **List of Potential Dead Works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** where they see evidence of dead works in their lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of Potential Dead Works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious rituals without faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services as a habit but not engaging with the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious duties to gain approval instead of out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to attain righteousness through personal effort rather than relying on God’s grace.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to obtain grace, while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful tendencies or behaviors:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Holding on to grudges or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to dominate relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Negligence:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual practices such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Falling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Fruitless habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending excessive time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing selfish ambitions at the expense of serving others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions motivated by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others merely out of duty rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering just to go through the motions instead of joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success over spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly pursuits that have no eternal value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups so that (if comfortable) they share one area they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to replace dead works with Holy Spirit-filled actions.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Brief Reflection (5 minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or the steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer to God for guidance toward a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->

#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->

<!-- p218 -->

<!-- p220 -->

<!-- p222 -->

<!-- p223 -->

<!-- p226 -->

<!-- p227 -->
