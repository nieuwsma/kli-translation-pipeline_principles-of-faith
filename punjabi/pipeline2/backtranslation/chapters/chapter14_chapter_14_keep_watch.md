# Chapter 14 - Stay Awake

<!-- p1 -->
Having received Christ’s spiritual authority on earth to establish His kingdom, we should work with diligence and urgency, because Jesus will come again!

<!-- p2 -->
Christ’s return is an important part of our faith. The study of Christ’s return and the end times is called **eschatology**. Many books have been written on eschatology, and *many of them disagree with each other*! In particular, they disagree on the interpretations (figurative, literal, symbolic), and on the order and timing of events in the Scriptures. I am reminded of a saying by the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonnke - “When I have broken chains on earth, then I will split hairs in heaven!”

<!-- p3 -->
As an author, I hesitate to go very deep into this much-debated subject because this book primarily focuses on the fundamentals of the faith. I will mainly stay at the highest-level perspective of the events described in Scripture and focus on what matters most - **that** **Gospel mission**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Universally Agreed Points:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus is coming again

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The resurrection of the dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  Judgment for all people

<!-- p8 -->
4.  Eternal destiny

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A defeated enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  A new heaven and a new earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point, we will see what this concept is, look at the reference Scriptures, think about why it is important, and what we should do in light of it.

### **3. Judgment for all people**

<!-- p37 -->
**What it is:** All people—believers and unbelievers—will face judgment before God. Believers will appear before the judgment seat of Christ for rewards, while unbelievers will face the Great White Throne judgment.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The judgment of the Great White Throne

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it; from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away. And there was found no place for them.
>

>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As described in the chapter on sin, there will be a great and terrible final judgment for all unbelievers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers our names are written in **the Lamb’s *Book of Life*** *(see the book of *Revelation*)**.** Therefore we will stand* *before the **judgment seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but for reward! Then**,** for believers this is not a moment of **shame or fear**, but a **celebration** of what God has done through us. It reminds us that our lives matter-not only for today but for **eternity**.

## Common points of disagreement

<!-- p89 -->
For the sake of completeness, if you want to know about common points of division in eschatology, here is a **very** brief overview of the most discussed points.

<!-- p90 -->
- **Timing of the rapture:** Pre-, mid-, or post-tribulation? Views differ on when the rapture occurs in relation to the tribulation. Some denominations do not believe in the rapture.

<!-- p91 -->
- **Purpose of the tribulation:** Is it literal judgment for Israel or a symbolic period of struggle?

<!-- p92 -->
- **The millennial kingdom:** Is it literal (premillennial), symbolic (amillennial), or spiritual (postmillennial)?

<!-- p93 -->
- **Satan’s final rebellion:** Occurs after the millennial period (literal event or metaphorical?).

<!-- p94 -->
Studying eschatology is a good thing, but let’s remember, as D.L. Browning said, “Always keep the main thing the main thing!”

## **Final thoughts**

<!-- p96 -->
The return of Christ is the climax of God’s plan and the reason we live and labor with urgency and purpose. The parable of the ten virgins (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 25:1-13</span>) and the parable of the two men in the field (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="24" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 24:40-42</span>) remind us to remain watchful and faithful, always ready for His return. Whether through the rewards of crowns or through the hope of the resurrection, our motivation is clear-Christ is coming again!

<!-- p97 -->
**May we be found ready, faithful, and expectant on that glory-filled day!**
