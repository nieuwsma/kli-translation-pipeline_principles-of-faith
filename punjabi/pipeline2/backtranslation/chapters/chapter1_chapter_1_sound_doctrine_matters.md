# Chapter 1 - Right Teaching Matters

<!-- p1 -->
Teaching, in the simple sense, means the central truths we believe and teach—truths that have been transmitted by God’s faithful people, rooted in Scripture, and lived out in our daily conduct।

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His church to ‘teach’, He was commanding them to share His teaching।

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me।
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age. Amen।
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus is commanding all of us to learn His teachings and reproduce them in others।

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), the complete embodiment of God’s truth, knowledge, and light। This embodiment, the Word (logos), existed from the beginning and became flesh to dwell among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14)। Jesus’ structured teaching is His doctrine (***didaskalia***); by keeping (obeying, guarding, preserving) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***)।

<!-- p9 -->
Imagine a master architect—the greatest builder in history—whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom are supreme। Only he understands the perfect design for a durable, unshakable structure। His understanding is not derived from any other—it is inherent within him। He is the source of all architectural wisdom, and every sound construction finds its origin in his design। This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the complete and perfect embodiment of God’s truth, knowledge, and light (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)।

<!-- p10 -->
To bring his design into reality, the architect creates a **detailed blueprint**—a structured plan that includes the principles, proportions, and foundational truths necessary to build a structure that will stand firm। Without this blueprint, no one could build correctly। This blueprint represents Jesus’ structured teaching, His doctrine (***didaskalia***) — the framework through which divine truth is understood and applied (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>)। His teaching is not merely theoretical knowledge; it is the means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that whoever follows Him can build their life on the right foundation ‘ਤੇ।

<!-- p11 -->
Yet the blueprint alone is not enough। The architect also gives **clear commands**, detailing how each part of the structure should be joined, where each beam should be placed, and how every element should be aligned with his perfect design। These specific commands represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-the practical instructions that govern obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>)। His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps for the structure to stand strong and remain aligned with the original blueprint। Ignoring or altering His commands would compromise the integrity of the entire structure।

<!-- p12 -->
Now imagine the architect gathers a group of **disciples**—those who want to learn from him, build under his guidance, and one day teach others to build structures that endure। These disciples carefully study his blueprint, listen closely to his instructions, and faithfully apply his commands in their work। They do not build according to their own ideas but according to the master’s design। These disciples **(Mathētēs**)-are those who observe, obey, and internalize Jesus’ teachings, aligning their lives with His word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>)। The more they study under the architect, the more skilled they become, and eventually they teach and equip others in his ways।

<!-- p13 -->
## Why should we study doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
Jesus’ teaching is the rock of our faith! He is the master architect who has the perfect design and blueprint। Only by studying the blueprints, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and developing a strong working partnership can we build a life that will stand in the storms of life।

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon। Unless the Lord builds the house, they labor in vain who build it; unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain।
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to stand strong in the face of hardships, challenges, tribulations, sufferings, or simply everyday life, if we have not built our lives on the solid rock of faith।

<!-- p19 -->
A gym membership alone is not enough to make a person fit and physically strong। To receive the full benefits of the membership, you must be consistent in the discipline of the gym। Likewise, we cannot get by merely attending church or simply claiming to be Christians in name; we all must be instructed in the faith, trained, equipped, and disciplined—only then will we realize the full benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus’ command extends beyond us. It is good that we become whole, healthy, disciplined, and submitted to God, but we must also teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples of others in the faith!

<!-- p21 -->
Why should we study doctrine and become disciplined? To build our lives on the rock!

<!-- p22 -->
But it is more than just a benefit; it also stands as a warning to us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, then we build our lives on sand, and a fierce storm will bring our house down. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our beliefs determine our actions and our destiny. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We cannot remain neutral—we are all building on something. Jesus calls us to build on the rock so that we can stand firm and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is for us!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed Him, "If you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed.
>
> And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Being a disciple of Jesus is only possible by abiding in His word (His Logos)! Abiding in His word results in our recognition of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples who have been taught discipline can carry out the Master’s commands. Every person in the New Testament church is called to this:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Become a disciple -\> Subject yourself to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> Teach others to remain under the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don’t worry, we don’t have to walk this path alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His recorded word in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature, gifted leaders who help build us up, establish us, keep us safe, and organize us for good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect Coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us specialists who, through experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and in time you can be one of them too!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ,
>
> till we all come to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a perfect man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;
>
> that we should no longer be children, tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, in the cunning craftiness of deceitful plotting,
>
> but, speaking the truth in love, may grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ—
>
> from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, according to the effective working by which every part does its share, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love.
>

<!-- p39 -->
</span>

<!-- p40 -->
**An important note:** The word ‘some’ points to the fact that not all of us are called to the gift of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but all of us have been given gifts of service, talents, and specific roles (discussed further in the chapter on the Church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given the Church these special gifts so that He may bring us to maturity, so that we may be and do:

<!-- p42 -->
- able to work and bear fruit

<!-- p43 -->
- united in the faith

<!-- p44 -->
- filled with the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- mature

<!-- p46 -->
- not like children

<!-- p47 -->
Without sound teaching we are <u>children, tossed to and fro and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of God’s family, but children by nature are:

<!-- p49 -->
- immature - unable to bear or produce fruit.

<!-- p50 -->
- naive - unable to discern between good and evil people.

<!-- p51 -->
- self-centered - concerned with temporary and present things.

<!-- p52 -->
- unprotected - vulnerable to harm.

### Sound teaching provides protection against the corruption of the world

<!-- p54 -->
In this life we do not have the luxury of remaining spiritually neutral. We are either under the influence of the Kingdom of God, or of the kingdom of darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul commanded his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> Therefore I charge you before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at His appearing and His kingdom:
