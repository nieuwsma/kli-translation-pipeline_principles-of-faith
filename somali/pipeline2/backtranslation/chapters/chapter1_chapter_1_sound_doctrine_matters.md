# Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p1 -->
Doctrine simply means the basic truths that we believe and learn—truths delivered by God’s faithful people, tied to Scripture, and practiced in our daily lives.

<!-- p2 -->
When Jesus commanded His Church to “teach,” He commanded them to share His doctrine.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Matthew 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.
>
> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
>
> teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the world.” Amen.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
In other words, Jesus directs all of us to learn His teaching and pass it on to others.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 1](../tables/table1.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Jesus is the Way, the Truth, and the Life (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>), fully representing the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God. This expression, the Word (logos), existed in the beginning and became flesh and dwelt among us (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, 14). The structured teaching of Jesus is His doctrine (***didaskalia***), and by holding to (obedience, keeping, defense) His commandments (***entolē)*** we become His disciples (***mathētēs***).

<!-- p9 -->
Consider a master architect, the greatest builder in all history, whose vision, knowledge, and wisdom surpass all others. He alone knows the perfect design for a resilient, unshakable structure. His understanding doesn’t come from others—it is innate to Him. He is the source of all building wisdom, and every sound structure ultimately derives from His design. This architect represents Jesus, the Logos—the full and complete expression of the truth, wisdom, and revelation of God (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>).

<!-- p10 -->
To translate His design into life, the architect creates a detailed blueprint—a structured plan that carries the principles, measurements, and basic truths necessary to build a resilient structure. Without this blueprint, no one could build correctly. This blueprint represents the structured teaching of Jesus, His doctrine (***didaskalia***)—the framework for understanding and applying divine truth (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titus 2:1</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:13</span>). His doctrine is not limited to knowledge alone; it is the means by which His wisdom is transmitted and preserved so that all who follow Him can build their lives on the right foundation.

<!-- p11 -->
However, a blueprint alone is not enough. The architect also provides precise instructions, detailing how each part fits together, where each pillar or beam is placed, and how everything aligns with His perfect design. These specific instructions represent His commandments **(Entolē**)-the practical directives that guide obedience (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:20</span>). His commandments are not arbitrary rules; they are the necessary steps that ensure the structure is strong and conforms to the original design. Ignoring or altering His commandments would compromise the integrity of the entire structure.

<!-- p12 -->
Now, imagine the architect assembling a group of apprentice learners—those who want to learn from Him, build under Him, and eventually teach others how to build resilient structures. These apprentices study His design carefully, listen attentively to His instructions, and faithfully apply His commandments in their work. They do not build as they please; they follow the master’s blueprint. These apprentices represent the disciples **(Mathētēs**)-those who hold, obey, and internalize the teaching of Jesus, aligning their lives with His Word (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 8:31</span>). As they learn under the architect, they become increasingly skilled, and eventually they teach and train others in His way.

<!-- p13 -->
## Why Do We Need to Learn Doctrine?

<!-- p14 -->
The teaching of Jesus is the solid foundation of our faith! He is the master architect with a perfect design and plan. Only by studying the blueprints, obeying the architect’s specific instructions, and forming a strong working relationship can you build a life that stands when the storms of life come.

<!-- p15 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="127" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p16 -->
> **<u>Psalm 127:1</u>** (NKJV)
>
> A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, They labor in vain who build it; Unless the LORD guards the city, The watchman stays awake in vain.
>

<!-- p17 -->
</span>

<!-- p18 -->
We cannot expect our lives to endure when facing troubles, challenges, suffering, persecution, or even daily life, if we do not build our lives on the solid rock of faith.

<!-- p19 -->
Simply having a gym membership does not make someone healthy and strong. You must continue the discipline of exercise to gain all the benefits of membership. Likewise, it is not enough merely to attend church or loosely claim to be Christians; we all must be taught, trained, equipped, and disciplined in the faith—only then will we receive all the benefits of membership!

<!-- p20 -->
Jesus' command goes beyond just us. It is good that we become complete, healthy, disciplined, obedient to God, but we must teach, train, equip, lead, and make disciples of others so that they also come to faith!

<!-- p21 -->
What leads us to learn doctrine and become disciples? So that we build our lives on the Rock!

<!-- p22 -->
However, this is more than just a benefit; it is also a warning to us. If we do not build our lives on the rock, we are building on sand, and a strong storm will destroy our house. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 7:24-27</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Our faith guides our actions and our destinies. Proverbs teaches us that as a man thinks, so is he! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 23:7</span>). We can no longer remain neutral—we are all building something. Jesus has called us to build on the rock so that we may endure and walk in His salvation. The promise of truth and freedom is ours!

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="31-32" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>John 8:31-32</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then Jesus said to the Jews who believed in Him, "If you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed.
>
> And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."
>

<!-- p26 -->
</span>

<!-- p27 -->
To be a disciple of Jesus is only possible if you abide in His Word (His logos)! The result of abiding in His Word is our knowledge of the truth, which sets us free!

<!-- p28 -->
Disciples, since they have been disciplined, can carry out the Lord's commands. Everyone in the New Covenant church is called to:

<!-- p29 -->
1.  Be a disciple -\> submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p30 -->
2.  Make disciples -\> teach others to submit themselves to the discipline of sound doctrine.

<!-- p31 -->
### How do we build our lives on the Rock?

<!-- p32 -->
Don't worry, this is not something we have to do alone.

<!-- p33 -->
1.  God has given us His Holy Spirit who will lead us and guide us into all truth (John 14).

<!-- p34 -->
2.  God has given us His written Word in the Scriptures.

<!-- p35 -->
3.  God has given us spiritually mature and gifted leaders to help us build, to mature us, to protect us, and to equip us for doing good works.

<!-- p36 -->
Christianity is a team sport, and we have a perfect coach who knows exactly what we need. He has given us experts who, because of their experience, know how to help us grow and mature, and in time you can become one of them too!

<!-- p37 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p38 -->
> **<u>Ephesians 4:11-16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers,
>
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ,
>
> till we all come to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a perfect man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;
>
> that we should no longer be children, tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, and by the cunning craftiness of deceitful scheming,
>
> but, speaking the truth in love, may grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ—
>
> from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, according to the effective working by which each part does its share, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love.
>

<!-- p39 -->
</span>

<!-- p40 -->
**Important notice:** the word ‘some’ indicates that not all of us are called to the gift of apostle, prophet, evangelist, pastor, or teacher, but we have all been given gifts of service, giftings, and specific roles (this will be discussed later in the chapter about the church).

<!-- p41 -->
God has given the church these particular gifts to bring us to maturity, so that we may become:

<!-- p42 -->
- Skilled, diligent, and fruitful

<!-- p43 -->
- United in faith

<!-- p44 -->
- Full of the knowledge of God

<!-- p45 -->
- Mature

<!-- p46 -->
- Not like children

<!-- p47 -->
If we do not have sound doctrine we will become <u>children, tossed and carried about.</u>

<!-- p48 -->
Children are an important part of the family of God, but children by nature are:

<!-- p49 -->
- Immature - unable to bear fruit or to reproduce.

<!-- p50 -->
- Undiscerning - unable to distinguish the good from the evil.

<!-- p51 -->
- Self-centered - preoccupied with the temporary and the immediate.

<!-- p52 -->
- Unable to defend themselves - vulnerable to harm.

<!-- p53 -->
### Sound doctrine provides protection against the evil of the world

<!-- p54 -->
In this life we do not have the option of being spiritually neutral. We are either influenced by the Kingdom of God, or by the Kingdom of Darkness.

<!-- p55 -->
Paul instructed his spiritual son in the Lord, Timothy, saying:

<!-- p56 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p57 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 4:1-4</u>** (NKJV)
>
> I therefore charge you before God and our Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom:
>
> Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with much patience and teaching.
>
> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, because their ears are itching, they will heap up for themselves many teachers;
>
> and they will turn their ears away from the truth, and they will turn aside to fables.
>

<!-- p58 -->
</span>

<!-- p59 -->
Timothy, who was an emerging leader in the church, was instructed to have a deep understanding of doctrine. Paul warned Timothy that a time would come when people would not endure sound doctrine and would turn away! Paul, in the previous chapter of his letter to Timothy, makes clear the character and traits of these men who would not endure sound doctrine:

<!-- p60 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p61 -->
> **<u>2 Timothy 3:1-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> But know this, that in the last days difficult times will come:
>
> For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,
>
> without love, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, haters of good,
>
> traitors, headstrong, haughty, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,
>
> having a form of godliness but denying its power. And from such turn away!
>
> For of this sort are those who creep into houses and make captives of gullible women loaded down with sins, led away by various desires,
>
> always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
>
> Now as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so also these oppose the truth: men of corrupt minds, not approved in matters of the faith;
>
> but they will progress no further, for their folly will be evident to all, as theirs also was.
>

<!-- p62 -->
</span>

<!-- p63 -->
The essence of this message is that difficult times will come. People will become self-seeing/self-centered, and though they gain much ‘learning’, yet they will not submit to sound doctrine; they will deny the power of God and will walk in a corrupt and reprobate mind. Paul urges Timothy to prepare himself well so that he may preach sound doctrine.

<!-- p64 -->
As disciples we learn and submit ourselves to the discipline of sound doctrine; this gives us protection against the evil of the world, and we become men and women of God fully mature, able to defend ourselves against the snares of the enemy and to protect those around us who are not yet mature.

<!-- p65 -->
We need to be careful and diligent when we are learning; the correct teaching of Scripture can be distorted by many crooked influences.

<!-- p66 -->
Sound doctrine can distort the customs of people. The truth is seen in the need that the great Protestant reformers had and in the history of the Old Testament. God stirred up men to restore the fundamental truth that had been distorted by the traditions of men.

What are His doctrines, what will we learn?
