# Hearing the Voice of God

<!-- p1 -->
This guest contribution written by Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) complements our exploration of the role of the Holy Spirit, providing practical insights on hearing the voice of God. This guest article is used with permission.

<!-- p2 -->
While I was in prayer one morning, I asked **God** this question: “What is the most important thing I can teach Your people in the days ahead?” And He answered me quickly, and I sensed the matter was very urgent when He said: “**Teach them to hear My Voice.**”

<!-- p3 -->
When the Lord gives me direction like this, I always take paper and pencil and begin to write what little I know, or what I think I know, and the supporting Scripture. I like to do studies in the Word on the topics the Lord leads me to teach. I love the power of preaching, but I truly love to teach the Word. It is an adventure to discover things that have always been there but were overlooked.

<!-- p4 -->
David must have longed to obtain God’s Truths, for he prayed in Psalm 119:18: “Open my eyes that I may see wondrous things from Your law.”

<!-- p5 -->
That is what I want to see-Wondrous things from His Word.

<!-- p6 -->
As I was studying and preparing to teach the second message about hearing and discerning the voice of God, He said this in my heart:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Teach them to hear My Voice,  
Teach them to honor and to obey.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Yes, teach them to hear My Voice and I will always guide them:  
Away from troubles that would destroy them,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Away from the trials they are in,  
Toward a place of peace, love, and joy that very few have ever reached.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Toward a place of restoration,  
a place of divine joy.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Yes, yes, teach them to hear My Voice my son  
and these people will be free forever*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
The following eleven points will help you understand how the Lord will communicate with you:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Hearing the voice of God is NOT an audible voice; it is a KNOWING in your heart.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
There was a time His Word came so clear and bold that it could have seemed audible. I even answered Him out loud; it was that real. However, there was no one there but the Lord and me, so I can’t truly say that He spoke to me as we speak to one another.

<!-- p16 -->
There was a time when Jesus was baptized in the Jordan River and the Father spoke: “This is My Son, in whom I am well pleased,” yet even some who were there said it sounded like thunder; so it is possible that He spoke audibly to me; but for now, I want you to understand that when I say God spoke to me, it is a KNOWING in my heart more than a voice in my ear.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>This KNOWING is not based on logic within your mind; rather, it is a thought or a complete understanding.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
The Lord has never spoken to me in a way that left me confused. He has never said anything that left me asking ‘why?’ It is always a KNOWING with a COMPLETE UNDERSTANDING. God is not the author of confusion (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>When He speaks, He speaks in complete thoughts.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
He never leaves us without filling in the empty places. In a split second, God can fill your heart with enough information to make you preach all your life. One touch of God can ignite in you a fire that will keep on burning forever. He is a perfect communicator.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>When God speaks with us, HE NEVER DRIVES. He leads.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
He is not a hard taskmaster; He is a Shepherd. Those who drive the sheep drive them, but the shepherd leads. Jesus is the Great Shepherd of the sheep, and we are the sheep of His pasture.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>When He speaks to His children, HE NEVER CONDEMNS; HE CONVINCES.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
His children are those who are born again (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 3:3</span>, 5) through their confession of faith in the blood that **Jesus** shed and His work at Calvary. Who wants to listen to someone who is always condemning us for our mistakes? I believe that most of the children of **God** are afraid to hear **God** speaking, because they have been told that **God** will condemn them for this or that. See <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 3:19-20</span>. Who does it say condemns us or judges us? It is OUR SPIRIT or our heart that reproves the child of **God**, but it is **God the Holy Spirit** who knows all things and is FAR GREATER than our hearts. He is there to be our Comforter. Read the following verses, and they will help you understand that the only ones the Holy Spirit condemns are sinners. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 8:33-34</span> teaches us that NOTHING can come between us and the love of **God**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:16</span>, 26 teaches us that the Holy Spirit is our Comforter, not our condemner; our Counselor, not our punisher; our Mediator and our Helper, not our hinderer. Also read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Acts 9:31</span> and 15:28 as examples of how the Holy Spirit (the voice of **God**) helps us, comforts us, and strengthens us.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>The voice of God can be tested so that you KNOW it is God and not your thoughts or the voice of familiar spirits.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 4:1-3</span> commands us to “test every spirit” so we can make sure it is from God. When you think you are hearing from God, you have the right and responsibility to question that voice (That is testing or proving the spirit.) The question to ask is: “Has Jesus Christ come in the flesh?” One thing familiar spirits will never confess is that Jesus came to earth in the flesh. He was fully Man, and fully God. Neither a false spirit nor a false religion will confess that Jesus came to this earth in the flesh and redeemed people from all their sins forever. Test the voice you are hearing. You should also know that God will never lead you beyond His written Word, the Holy Bible. If you are hearing something that cannot be supported by the Holy Bible, you do not have God. Leave it! Test the voice you are hearing to see if it is from God.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>The voice of God always brings Glory to Jesus Christ.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
The Holy Spirit is the Voice of Heaven. He is here on earth to work with us, and Jesus said He will always speak only what He hears, and He will ALWAYS bring glory to Jesus (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13-14</span>). He is not here to bring glory to your ministry, or your works, or the church programs. All those things are good, and most of the time necessary to accomplish what we do, but the Holy Spirit will speak and will bring glory to the Person of Jesus. If the voice you are hearing is praising you, you have not heard God. There is a difference between being praised and being encouraged and strengthened. The Holy Spirit will always encourage you and strengthen you whenever He speaks with you, even when He corrects (guides) you with the word. But praise is when you believe that YOU are somebody. You are nothing without Jesus—HE IS THE ONE, not us.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>The voice of God will always REVEAL something to us.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
See <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:14</span>. He always takes what belongs to **Jesus** and reveals it to us. The Amplified adds the words: announce, disclose, and transmit regarding the word “reveal.” **God** will always disclose something to us after He announces it to us, and then He transmits it to us by your faith. Also study <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 2:10</span> and 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>The voice of God always brings peace.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:26</span> and 27, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 3:14</span> and 15. His voice never makes us anxious. It is a voice that brings perfect peace. Inner peace is due to there being a KNOWING that produces confidence before God, which turns into perfect peace. If the voice you are listening to confuses you, it is not from God—shut it off. Jesus promised us peace, not as the world gives, but as He gives us through the words of comfort of the Holy Spirit and His constant presence (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>The voice of God ALWAYS brings TRUTH.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Read <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. The truth is, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span> calls the Holy Spirit: “the Spirit of Truth.” God cannot lie! He is TRUTH, therefore He will speak only TRUTH. People have said to me something like this: “I can’t believe that what I’m hearing is from God. It’s too good to be true.” It may be too good to be true; therefore you should TEST the spirit by aligning it with the Word of God to make sure it is Truth. Some people simply cannot believe because what they are hearing is always positive, and they have been taught that God condemns us for our mistakes. God will only speak TRUTH, and it is always GOOD NEWS. The sad truth is that most people only ever hear bad news from the voice they are listening to and never expect to hear good news, so when good news comes, they question it as if it might not be true. **Truth is always good news!** It is the **truth that** sets us free, so how can **truth** be anything other than good news?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>The voice of **God** always LEADS US FORWARD AND UP.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Read Deuteronomy 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Isaiah 30:21</span>, and <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
God never leads you down! He never puts you down; He only builds you up. When God speaks to you, He speaks from a frame of reference that everything the Word says about you has already been accomplished and YOU HAVE ACCEPTED IT. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 33:3</span> says: “Call to Me, and I will answer you, and I will show you **GREAT AND MIGHTY** things which you do not know.” Does that sound like being put down or doom and gloom? In God there is NOTHING of doom and gloom, therefore He cannot tell you anything doomy or gloomy. You have to tune in to the world’s news agencies to get doom and gloom.

<!-- p38 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p39 -->
> **<u>Jeremiah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the LORD, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Now, if God THINKS good thoughts of peace, future, and hope toward us, why would He speak any differently? The answer is, HE WOULD NEVER SPEAK THAT WAY, AND HE DOESN'T! He only leads us UP and FORWARD with great hope in order to prepare us for a future we expect.

<!-- p42 -->
I hope this encourages you to “pray to Him” and learn how to **hear His voice**. He has great things to tell you, but He needs you to listen before all His good things can benefit you.
