# Chapter 14 - Be Alert

<!-- p1 -->
Since we have been given the spiritual authority of Christ on earth to build His kingdom, we must work diligently and with urgency, for Jesus is coming back!

<!-- p2 -->
The return of Christ is an important part of our faith. The study of the return of Christ and the end of the age is called **eschatology**. Many books about eschatology have been written, and they *often disagree*! In particular, they differ in interpretations (e.g., literal, symbolic), the order and timing of events in Scripture. It reminds me of the saying of the great Pentecostal evangelist Reinhard Bonke - “I will sort out the small things in heaven when I’m finished breaking chains on earth!”

<!-- p3 -->
As the author, I hesitate to go too far into this hotly debated subject since this book focuses on the foundations of the faith. For the most part I will stick to the high-level view of the events revealed in Scripture, and I will emphasize what is most important - **the work** **of the Gospel**!

<!-- p4 -->
## Points that are generally agreed upon:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  Jesus Is Coming Back

<!-- p6 -->
2.  The Resurrection of the Dead

<!-- p7 -->
3.  The Judgment of All People

<!-- p8 -->
4.  The Eternal Future

<!-- p9 -->
5.  A Defeated Enemy

<!-- p10 -->
6.  New Heaven and New Earth

<!-- p11 -->
For each point we will review what the concept is, we will look at the Scripture references, we will consider why it matters, and what we do in light of it.

<!-- p12 -->
### **1. Jesus Is Coming Back**

<!-- p13 -->
**What Is It:** **The Second Coming of Christ** is the literal return of Jesus to the earth as King and Judge.

<!-- p14 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p15 -->
> **<u>Acts 1:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> who also said, "Men of Galilee, why do you stand gazing up into heaven? This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will so come in like manner as you saw Him go into heaven."
>

<!-- p16 -->
</span>

<!-- p17 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p18 -->
> **<u>Revelation 22:12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> "And behold, I am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, to give to each one according to his work."
>

<!-- p19 -->
</span>

<!-- p20 -->
**Why It Matters:** The return of Christ fulfills God’s promise of the completion of redemption and the establishment of His kingdom. It reminds us to live with watchfulness and readiness, knowing that one day we will see Him face to face.

<!-- p21 -->
**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p22 -->
- **Be Watchful:** Live in expectation of His return.

<!-- p23 -->
- **Remain Faithful:** Carry out the Great Commission (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matthew 28:19-20</span>) with purpose and diligence.

<!-- p24 -->

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="28-29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

>
> Do not marvel at this; for the hour is coming in which all who are in the graves will hear His voice
>
> and come forth—those who have done good, to the resurrection of life; and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of condemnation.
>

<!-- p28 -->
</span>

<!-- p29 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p30 -->
> **<u>1 Thessalonians 4:16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first.
>

<!-- p31 -->
</span>

<!-- p32 -->
**Why It Matters:** The resurrection guarantees justice and eternal life for believers while warning unbelievers of judgment. It points to God’s supremacy over life and death.

<!-- p33 -->
**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p34 -->
- **Be Ready:** Live a life of repentance and faithfulness to God.

<!-- p35 -->
- **Share the Gospel:** Help others prepare for the resurrection of life by leading them to Christ.

<!-- p36 -->
### **3. The Judgment of All People**

<!-- p37 -->
**What Is It:** All people-believers and unbelievers-will face judgment before God. Believers will stand before the Judgment Seat of Christ to be rewarded, while unbelievers will face the Great White Throne Judgment.

<!-- p38 -->
#### The Great White Throne Judgment

<!-- p39 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p40 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:11-12</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away, and there was found no place for them.
>
> And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books.
>

<!-- p41 -->
</span>

<!-- p42 -->
As explained in the chapter about sin, there will be a great and terrifying final judgment for all who are not believers. The punishment will be eternal separation from God! **This judgment is not for the believer.**

<!-- p43 -->
#### The Judgment Seat of Christ

<!-- p44 -->
As believers, our names are written in the ***Book of Life* of the Lamb** *(*See the book of *Revelation*)**.** Therefore we will stand* *at the **Judgment Seat of Christ***. This judgment is not for condemnation, but is a reward! Again**,** it is not a time of **shame or fear** for believers, but is a **celebration** of what God has accomplished in us. It reminds us that our lives have value - not only today but also **forever**.

<!-- p45 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p46 -->
> **<u>2 Corinthians 5:10</u>** (NKJV)
>
> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive the things done in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad.
>

<!-- p47 -->
</span>

<!-- p48 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="12-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p49 -->
> **<u>1 Corinthians 3:12-15</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,
>
> each one’s work will become clear; for the Day will declare it, because it will be revealed by fire; and the fire will test each one’s work, of what sort it is.
>
> If anyone’s work which he has built on it endures, he will receive a reward.
>
> If anyone’s work is burned, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.
>

<!-- p50 -->
</span>

<!-- p51 -->
At this judgment we will receive rewards for the good works that we have done! Everyone who will be at this judgment lived and died as a believer, therefore their eternal salvation is guaranteed. However, not all will receive additional reward. The New Testament mentions rewards as crowns that stand for victory, honor, and faithfulness. These laurel crowns signify God’s recognition of the faithfulness and service of believers in His Kingdom.

<!-- p52 -->
The rewards:

<!-- p53 -->
1.  **The Crown of Righteousness** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:8</span>)* - Those who long for the coming of Christ and live a God-fearing life.

<!-- p54 -->
2.  **The Incorruptible Crown** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-25" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Corinthians 9:24-25</span>)* - Believers who practice self-discipline and faithfulness.

<!-- p55 -->
3.  **The Crown of Life** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JAS" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">James 1:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Revelation 2:10</span>)* - Those who endure trials, suffering, and persecution.

<!-- p56 -->
4.  **The Crown of Glory** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Peter 5:2-4</span>)* - Leaders and shepherds who faithfully guide the people of God.

<!-- p57 -->
5.  **The Crown of Joy** *(<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Thessalonians 2:19-20</span>)* - Those who lead people to Christ and make disciples.

<!-- p58 -->
**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p59 -->
- **Live with Purpose:** Work diligently, knowing that our efforts for the Kingdom have eternal value.

<!-- p60 -->
- **Self-examination:** Ensure that we live a life of faith and obedience.

<!-- p61 -->
### **4. Eternal Destiny**

<!-- p62 -->
**What It Is:** All people will spend eternity in one of two places—eternal life with God or eternal separation from Him.

<!-- p63 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="46" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p64 -->
> **<u>Matthew 25:46</u>** (NKJV)
>
> And these will go away into everlasting punishment, but the righteous into eternal life."
>

<!-- p65 -->
</span>

<!-- p66 -->
**Why It Matters:** Our eternal destiny is determined by whether we have accepted or rejected Christ. It underscores the urgency of the Gospel message and discipleship.

<!-- p67 -->
**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p68 -->
- **Proclaim salvation:** Share the Gospel boldly to lead others to eternal life.

<!-- p69 -->
- **Live with eternity in mind:** Focus on what matters forever—faith, obedience, and love.

<!-- p70 -->
### **5. A Defeated Enemy**

<!-- p71 -->
**What It Is:** Satan, the deceiver, will be thrown into the **lake of fire**—and will forever remain defeated by Christ.

<!-- p72 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p73 -->
> **<u>Revelation 20:10</u>** (NKJV)
>
> The devil, who deceived them, was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone where the beast and the false prophet are. And they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.
>

<!-- p74 -->
</span>

<!-- p75 -->
**Why It Matters:** This final act demonstrates God's supremacy over evil and assures believers that Satan's reign of deception and destruction has ended. It gives us confidence in God's ultimate victory.

<!-- p76 -->
**What Should We Do?**

<!-- p77 -->
- **Stand firm:** Resist the enemy's lies, knowing he has already been defeated.

<!-- p78 -->
- **Walk in power and authority:** Live with courage and faith, knowing that God has already secured the victory.
