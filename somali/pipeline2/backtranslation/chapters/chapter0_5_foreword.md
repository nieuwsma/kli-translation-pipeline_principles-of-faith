# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
For things to work effectively, order and solid structure are essential. Without them, disorder and chaos can easily arise. That is why building a strong foundation of doctrine is paramount to living fully as a healthy Christian.

<!-- p2 -->
That is why "*Principles of Faith*" will serve as a powerful resource for believers. From the very first chapter, which discusses the importance of sound doctrine, through the remainder of the more than 300 pages filled with lessons and activities on the church and our roles within it, as well as understanding and discerning the voice of God, this book is invaluable. It will be an excellent tool in the classroom for teaching and preparing leaders with a solid doctrinal foundation, and also immensely beneficial for the individual reader.
