# Introduction

<!-- p1 -->
## Building an Enduring Foundation

<!-- p2 -->
I have always had a special interest in questions. What makes *something* true? Why is it so? How do we know? How are we sure? If *something* is true, what else is true? What is false? This curiosity goes beyond a desire to be right on the surface; it is a desire to know deeply and accurately, and to be able to clearly express the new understanding that has been gained.

<!-- p3 -->
Although communication is commonplace, effective and accurate communication is a difficult task. To convey something effectively, the writer and originator must hold an idea. However, the idea alone—like water on a hard surface—has its boundaries and edges poorly defined, it is fluid, and it can easily be spread in different directions.

<!-- p4 -->
We use words, particular terms, and phrases to transform the raw idea into one generally formed and turn it into one precisely formed. Words give the idea boundaries; they help us distinguish and differentiate what the idea is, and what it is not. Now the shaped idea must be transmitted and received through a particular channel. Whether a long written form, like this book, or a short written form, like a social media post, or an oral form, like a conversation or a video recording, the channel delivers to the recipient the now well-formed idea.

<!-- p5 -->
Up to now, we have spoken only of the originator and author of the idea. For an idea to be useful, the recipient must accurately receive, unpack, and understand the message. The message transmitted and the one received should closely resemble each other.

<!-- p6 -->
All the great works accomplished by humanity are the result of effective communication. The work of a single man can accomplish only a limited amount. To truly build or accomplish something great, many people (here I mean humanity in general) must work together, sharing purpose and idea. Apart from natural disasters (such as a volcanic eruption), we also know that most of the greatest disasters that have ever happened have largely come from absent, broken, or distorted communication. Truly, life and death are in the power of the tongue (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 18:21</span>).

<!-- p7 -->
What we believe and how we communicate have the power to build a person, a family, a community, a nation, or likewise they have the power to destroy. What we believe is the foundation of our actions, and it is the blueprint for what we will do, what we will be, and how we will exist. Since belief is expressed in action and communication, it defines the role we play in existence. Our belief not only shapes our own lives, but to some extent it shapes the lives of all those around us.

<!-- p8 -->
We must correctly build our faith and learn to share it effectively. Jesus said as much when He said to His disciples:

<!-- p12 -->
Many Christians today want a stronger faith but lack a clear foundation on which to build. Without a solid doctrine, we lack the firm foundation we need to stand steadfastly; we are at risk of being misled by false teaching, discouraged by trials, or unprepared for the work God has called us to. This book is intended to equip you with the foundational teachings of Holy Scripture—truths that strengthen your faith, deepen your walk with Christ, and empower you to live a life filled with the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p13 -->
The Apostle Paul urged believers to be “*rooted and built up in Him, and established in the faith*” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>). In order for us to grow and become mature, Spirit-led believers, we must first put down deep roots in the truth of God. That is why doctrine matters. It is more than information; it is the framework that shapes how we live and how we respond to God.

<!-- p14 -->
## Why This Book, Who Is It For?

<!-- p15 -->
The teaching of this book is intended to instruct and inspire you, the reader, concerning the foundational doctrine of the New Testament Church. My hope is that this faith will convince you and encourage your walk of faith so that you are deeply rooted in Christ and come to know Him personally.

<!-- p16 -->
This teaching has been shaped and reshaped by my experience in the church, particularly the expressions of the Charismatic or Pentecostal. The blend of teaching in this book is built on my personal study of Scripture, formal and informal education, and my nearly twenty years of engagement in ministry, most of that time as an ordained minister. My background includes youth and children's ministry, outreach preaching and revival ministry, discipleship, pastoral counseling, and church and ministry administration.

<!-- p17 -->
This book will serve believers who want to strengthen their faith or disciple others in their spiritual growth. Specifically:

<!-- p18 -->
- **New Believers** - Those who need a clear foundation to begin their Christian journey.

<!-- p19 -->
- **Growing Christians** - Those who want to deepen their understanding of God and Scripture as they walk in increasing obedience and faith.

<!-- p20 -->
- **Teachers, Mentors, and Small Group Leaders** - Those preparing to disciple others, whether one-on-one, in small groups, or from the pulpit.

<!-- p21 -->
Each chapter is designed to equip believers both **personally** and **practically**, making it suitable for personal growth as well as group teaching. Whether you are using this book to strengthen your walk with God or to train others, the content is structured to be easily applicable and for growth led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p22 -->
## How to Use This Book

<!-- p23 -->
The chapters follow a logical progression, beginning with foundational truths such as **God**, **sin**, **repentance**, and **faith**, and moving into the deeper work of the **Holy Spirit**, **discipleship**, and **spiritual power**. Each topic can stand on its own but also builds on previous chapters, making it easy to go step-by-step or to focus on specific areas as needed.

<!-- p24 -->
For **individuals**, this book serves as a guide to understanding and practicing Scripture. For **teachers**, it provides resources to prepare lessons, lead discussions, and disciple others effectively. Personal reflection questions, group discussion prompts for facilitation, group activities, and word studies are included to deepen understanding and encourage practical steps of obedience.

<!-- p25 -->
## How will we learn?

<!-- p26 -->
We have been given a foundational source for sound doctrine, the teaching of the Holy Spirit, inspired and recorded in Scripture, and handed down through the teaching and practice of the church. This book will take essential teachings, presenting them in a simple, understandable way, and will conclude each chapter with personal reflection, group facilitation questions, and optional group activities.

The teaching I present in this book is not new or innovative. We should be suspicious of ‘new’ teachings about our Christian faith that have never been seen or heard before. Rather, this book is a collection and explanation of teachings that have existed since the beginning.

<!-- p68 -->
This book is not a study of church history, nor is it a comparison of the various branches of Christian churches. If you take two theology books written by different authors with different denominational backgrounds, although you will see great agreement in much of their work, you will still see obvious divisions. I do not claim infallibility, but I do claim that this work is well-studied, reviewed by faithful peers, men and women (see the Acknowledgments section), and well supported by the Holy Scriptures and a well-accepted Pentecostal tradition.
