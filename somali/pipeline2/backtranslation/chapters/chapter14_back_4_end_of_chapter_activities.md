# End-of-Chapter Activities

<!-- p1 -->
These end-of-chapter activities are designed to reinforce the core learning objectives of each chapter. Each activity is intended to take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete and may require additional resources not included in this book. Alongside personal reflection and group discussion questions, these activities are best used in a group learning format to deepen understanding and engagement with the book’s key lessons. Use these activities as a guide. Be creative, encourage participation, and modify the activities as you see fit.

<!-- p2 -->
## Chapter 1 - Sound Doctrine Is Important

<!-- p3 -->
### Activity 1: Aligning Scripture

<!-- p4 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p5 -->
To reinforce key biblical concepts about doctrine by exploring Scriptures that emphasize its importance.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **Divide participants** **into pairs or small groups** of 3-4 people.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **Assign Scripture Passages** - Give each group one of the following passages:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Colossians 2:7</span>** - *Rooted and built up in Christ.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timothy 4:1-4</span>** - *Preaching the Word and guarding against false doctrine.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-16</span>** - *Spiritual maturity and unity through sound teaching.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Group Discussion** - Instruct each group to:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Read the assigned Scripture passage** aloud.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Summarize the main teaching** about doctrine in 1-2 sentences.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Discuss a real-life example** that shows how this teaching helps prevent falsehood or helps others grow in faith.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Share Insights** - Have each group take **2 minutes** to present to the whole group their Scripture passage, summary, and application.

<!-- p17 -->
### Activity 2: Doctrine in Action

<!-- p18 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p19 -->
Apply the importance of sound doctrine to real-life situations and reinforce practical responses.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **Divide into Groups** - Form groups of **4-5 participants**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **Assign Scenarios** - Assign each group one of the following:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Scenario 1**: *A friend says to you, “It doesn’t matter what we believe, as long as we are sincere.” How would you respond?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Scenario 2**: *You are guiding a new believer who hears conflicting teachings about salvation by faith versus salvation by works. Which Scripture(s) could you share to clarify?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Scenario 3**: *A Christian friend stops attending church because they see it as unnecessary as long as they believe in God. How would you encourage them using Scripture?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Discussion Tasks** - The groups should:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Identify the doctrinal issue** at the heart of the scenario.

<!-- p28 -->
- **Write 1-2 verses** that address the issue.

<!-- p29 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** on how they would advise the person in the scenario.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **Share the Responses** - The groups should take turns presenting their scenarios and responses to the group, followed by **brief feedback or additions** from others.

<!-- p31 -->
### Task 3: Statement or Scripture?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p33 -->
Train participants to **distinguish truth from error** using Scripture as the foundation.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Set Up the Activity** - Read the following **statements** aloud, one at a time. Some of the statements are **biblical truths**, while others are **errors** or **misinterpretations**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Group Responses** - Ask participants to:

<!-- p37 -->
- Determine whether the statement is **true** or **false**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Provide **a Scripture verse** to support their answer.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Guide the Discussion** - Allow time for the groups to discuss differences or explore verses before confirming the correct answers.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Statements:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Faith alone saves us.* *(True - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *All paths lead us to God if we are sincere.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Right doctrine is not more important than love for others.* *(False - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timothy 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->

## Chapter 2 - God - The Eternal Creator and Loving Father

<!-- p47 -->
### Activity 1: Name That Attribute (Interactive Group Activity)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p49 -->
Help participants connect the names and attributes of God to practical applications in real life.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Prepare Index Cards** - Write **one name or attribute of God** (for example, Yahweh-Jireh, Faithful, Omnipresent) on each card.

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Distribute the Cards** - Give one card to each participant or each group.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Group Task:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Read the **name or attribute** and the **Scripture reference** that accompanies it in this chapter.

<!-- p55 -->
- Discuss: *How does this name or attribute apply to a personal situation or one found in the Bible?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Prepare a **1-minute summary** to share with the group.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Conclusion** - Ask each participant or each group to **present their reflections**, emphasizing practical ways God's character applies to real-life struggles or victories.

<!-- p58 -->
### Activity 2: The Names of God in Prayer (Reflection and Application)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p60 -->
Teach participants how they can incorporate the names and attributes of God into **personal prayer**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Set Reflection Time** - Provide a **quiet space** for participants to reflect on one of the names of God from the chapter.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Exercise:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Step 1: Choose a **name or attribute** related to a current need (for example, **Jehovah-Rapha** for healing).

<!-- p65 -->
- Step 2: Write a short **prayer** using that name, praising God and asking Him to act in accordance with His character.

<!-- p66 -->
- Step 3: Share the prayers (optional) in small groups or pairs to **encourage and build faith**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Conclude with Group Prayer** - Thank God for revealing Himself through His names and attributes.

<!-- p68 -->
### Activity 3: Encountering the Attributes of God (15-20 Minutes)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p70 -->
Help participants to **reflect on and apply** God's attributes in their lives through **personal stories** and **group prayer**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **Divide into groups of 3-4 people.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Focus on Attributes:**

<!-- p74 -->
- Provide each group a **list of 3-4 attributes** of God (for example, **Faithful, Merciful, Sovereign, Provider**) drawn from the chapter.

<!-- p75 -->
- Ask each group to **choose one attribute** that they consider most fitting for their current season of life.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Story Sharing (10 Minutes):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Each participant briefly **shares a story** (a personal experience or insight) connected to the chosen attribute.

<!-- p78 -->
- Guiding Examples:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“When did you encounter God as your Provider?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“How has God's mercy shaped your life?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Group Application (5-10 Minutes):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Ask the group: *“How can we encourage one another to rely on this aspect of God in daily life?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Have the group **write a short prayer** that reflects the attribute, and pray it together, focusing on applying God's character to their situations.

## Chapter 3 - Sin and Redemption

<!-- p85 -->
### Activity 1: The Ripple Effect of Sin (Discussion and Application)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p87 -->
Show how the **ripple effects of sin** touch **relationships, communities, and spiritual life**, while reinforcing God's **plan of redemption**.

<!-- p88 -->
### Instructions:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Opening Illustration (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Drop a **stone into a bowl of water** (or describe the image if materials are not available).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ask participants to **observe the ripples** spreading outward and discuss:  
      *“How does this image illustrate the impact of sin on our lives and relationships?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Small-Group Discussion (10 Minutes):**

<!-- p93 -->
- Divide into groups and assign each one a **biblical example of sin**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Adam and Eve** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:6-13</span> (Shame and separation).

<!-- p95 -->
- **Cain and Abel** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:3-12</span> (Jealousy, murder, and exile).

<!-- p96 -->
- **David and Bathsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuel 11:2-27</span> (Adultery and consequences).

<!-- p97 -->
- Discuss:

<!-- p98 -->
- *What were the immediate and long-term effects of this sin?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *How do we see God's grace and the opportunity for restoration in each story?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Reflection (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Bring the groups back together and ask:

<!-- p102 -->
- *What are some of the “rippling effects” of sin that you have personally experienced?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *How has God’s grace worked to stop those effects and bring healing?*

<!-- p104 -->

<!-- p105 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p106 -->
Trace God’s plan of redemption from Genesis to Christ, focusing on His grace in the midst of judgment.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p108 -->

<!-- p109 -->

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - God warned Cain before his sin.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - The Passover lamb protects Israel.

- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">LEV 16:21-22</span>** - The scapegoat carries sin away.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Group Activity:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Discuss the sin or judgment in the passage.

<!-- p116 -->
- Highlight the grace or redemption provided.

<!-- p117 -->
- Summarize the lesson learned about God’s character.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Presentation:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Encourage each group to share their insights, linking the passages together into a timeline of God’s plan of redemption.

<!-- p120 -->
### Activity 3: Walking in Forgiveness (Using Prayer)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p122 -->
Lead participants to **personally examine forgiveness**-both **receiving it** and **extending it** to others.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Ask participants to **silently reflect on** areas where they might:

<!-- p126 -->
- Need to **confess sin** and seek God’s forgiveness.

<!-- p127 -->
- Need to **forgive someone else** who has wronged them.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Guided Prayer Time (10 Minutes):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 John 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Lead participants in an individual prayer. The leader does not need to hear the conversation between the person and God.

<!-- p131 -->
- Confess sins before God.

<!-- p132 -->
- Pray for strength to forgive others.

<!-- p133 -->
- Encourage writing down any **commitments** (e.g., a person to forgive, reconciliation to seek).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Closing Reflection (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Read **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Psalm 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Invite participants to share (optional) how forgiveness brings freedom and restoration.

## Chapter 5 - Repentance from Dead Works

<!-- p180 -->
### Activity 1: Identifying Dead Works (Reflection and Group Discussion)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Objective:**

<!-- p182 -->
Help participants identify and address “dead works” in their lives, replacing them with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Reflection (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Give participants a **list of possible dead works** (see below).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ask them to reflect silently and identify **one or two areas** where they see evidence of dead works present in their lives.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **List of Possible Dead Works:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Religious Rituals Without Faith:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Attending church services out of habit, without engaging the heart.

<!-- p190 -->
- Performing religious acts to earn approval rather than out of love for God.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Self-Reliance:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Trying to achieve righteousness through personal effort instead of relying on the grace of God.

<!-- p193 -->
- Focusing on good works to gain approval while ignoring the role of the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Sinful Patterns or Attitudes:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Harboring resentment or refusing to forgive.

<!-- p196 -->
- Allowing pride, envy, or bitterness to rule relationships.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Complacency:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Neglecting spiritual disciplines such as prayer, Bible study, or worship.

<!-- p199 -->
- Settling into spiritual apathy, losing zeal for Christ.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Unfruitful habits:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Spending too much time on distractions or hobbies that do not glorify God.

<!-- p202 -->
- Pursuing self-centered ambitions while neglecting service to others.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Actions driven by fear or guilt:**

<!-- p204 -->
- Serving others out of obligation rather than love.

<!-- p205 -->
- Giving financially or volunteering merely to meet a requirement rather than as joyful obedience.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Misplaced priorities:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Focusing on material success instead of prioritizing spiritual growth.

<!-- p208 -->
- Investing time and energy in worldly activities that lack lasting value.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Group Discussion (10 Minutes):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Divide participants into small groups to share (if they are comfortable) one point they have identified as a dead work.

<!-- p211 -->
- Discuss practical steps to replace dead works with actions led by the Holy Spirit.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Closing Reflection (5 Minutes):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Invite participants to share key takeaways or steps they will take to address dead works.

<!-- p214 -->
- Conclude with a prayer for God's guidance to lead a fruitful life.

<!-- p215 -->

#### Objective:

<!-- p217 -->

<!-- p226 -->
- Have each group share their insights on one step of the path.

<!-- p243 -->
#### Objective:

<!-- p244 -->
Help participants understand how **faith comes from hearing** and recognize times when the word of God becomes personal and living.

<!-- p245 -->
#### Instructions:

<!-- p246 -->
