# Maqlidda Codka Ilaah

<!-- p1 -->
Lifaaqan martida ah ee uu qoray Rev. Larry Low ([<u>www.LarryLow.com</u>](http://www.larrylow.com)) waxa uu kaabaya sahamintayada doorka Ruuxa Quduuska ah, isaga oo bixiya aragtiyo wax-ku-ool ah oo ku saabsan maqalka codka Ilaah. Qoraalkan martida ah waxaa loo adeegsaday oggolaansho.

<!-- p2 -->
Intii aan salaad ku jiray subax, waxaan waydiiyey **Ilaah** su’aashan: “Waa maxay waxa ugu muhiimsan ee aan barin karo dadkaaga waayaha nagu soo socda?” Wuxuuna si dhaqso leh iigu jawaabay, waxaanan dareemay in arrintu aad u degdeg tahay markuu yidhi: “**Bar iyaga inay maqlaan Codkayga.**”

<!-- p3 -->
Marka Rabbigu ii siiyo jihayn sidan oo kale ah, mar walba waxaan qaataa warqad iyo qalin oo waxaan bilaabaa inaan qoro waxa yar ee aan ogahay, ama waxa aan u malaynayo inaan ogahay, iyo Qorniinka taageeraya. Waxaan jeclahay inaan sameeyo daraasado ku saabsan Ereyga mowduucyada Rabbigu ii hoggaaminayo inaan baro. Waxaan jeclahay awoodda waacdiinta, laakiin run ahaantii aad ayaan u jeclahay inaan baro Ereyga. Waa tacab-barasho in la helo waxyaabo mar walba halkaas yaallay balse la iska indho-tiray.

<!-- p4 -->
Daauud waa inuu u hanqal taagay in uu helo Xaqiiqooyinka Ilaah, waayo wuxuu ku baryay aayadda 18 ee Sabuur 119: “Indhahayga fur si aan u arko waxyaalo layaab leh oo ka imanaya sharcigaaga.”

<!-- p5 -->
Taasi waa waxa aan doonayo inaan arko-Waxyaalo layaab leh oo ka imanaya Ereygiisa.

<!-- p6 -->
Anigoo wax baranaya oo isu diyaarinaya in aan baro farriinta labaad ee ku saabsan maqalka iyo garashada codka Ilaah, wuxuu qalbigayga ku yidhi sidan:

<!-- p7 -->
“*Bar iyaga inay maqlaan Codkayga,  
Bar iyaga inay maamuusaan oo ay addeecaan.*

<!-- p8 -->
*Haa, Bar iyaga inay maqlaan Codkayga oo mar walba waan ka hagi doonaa:  
Ka fog dhibaatooyinka iyaga halligi lahaa,*

<!-- p9 -->
*Ka fog imtixaannada ay ku jiraan,  
Ku aaddan meel nabad, jacayl, iyo farxad ah oo ay aad u yar yihiin kuwii weligood gaadhay.*

<!-- p10 -->
*Ku aaddan meel soo-kabasho ah,  
meel farxad Rabbaani ah.*

<!-- p11 -->
*Haa haa, bar iyaga inay maqlaan Codkayga wiilkaygow  
oo dadkani weligood way xornaan doonaan*.”

<!-- p12 -->
05/27/00 9:10 A.M.

<!-- p13 -->
Kow-iyo-tobankan qodob ee soo socda waxay kaa caawin doonaan inaad fahamto sida Rabbigu kula xidhiidhi doono:

<!-- p14 -->
1.  <u>Maqlidda codka Ilaah MA aha cod la maqli karo ee waa OGAAN ku jira qalbigaaga.</u>

<!-- p15 -->
Mar ayaa Eraygiisu u yimid si aad u cad oo geesinimo leh oo ay u ekaan karto in la maqlayo. Waxaan isaga ugu jawaabay anigoo cod ku hadlaya, way ahayd mid aad u dhab ah. Si kastaba ha ahaatee, halkaas kama jirin qof aan ka ahayn Rabbiga iyo aniga, sidaa darteed si dhab ah ma oran karo inuu ila hadlay sida aynu isugu hadalno.

<!-- p16 -->
Waxaa jiray waqti Ciise lagu baabtiisay webiga Urdun oo Aabbuhu hadlay: “Kanu waa Wiilkayga, kan aan aad uga raalli ahay,” hase ahaatee xataa qaar ka mid ah kuwii halkaas joogay waxay yidhaahdeen way u ekaatay onkod, sidaas darteed waxa suuragal ah inuu si la maqli karo ila hadlay; balse hadda, waxaan rabaa inaad fahamto in marka aan iraahdo Ilaah wuu ila hadlay, ay tahay OGAAN ku jira qalbigayga in ka badan cod ku jira dhegtayda.

<!-- p17 -->
2.  <u>OGAANKA MA aha mid ku dhisan mantiq gudaha maskaxdaada; hase yeeshee, waa fikir ama faham dhammaystiran.</u>

<!-- p18 -->
Marnaba Rabbigu ima uusan la hadlin wax igu riday jahawareer. Marnaba ima uusan odhan wax igaga tagay inaan is weydiiyo ‘sabab?’ Had iyo jeer waa OGAAN leh FAHAM dhammaystiran. Ilaah ma aha abuuraha jahawareerka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:33</span>).

<!-- p19 -->
3.  <u>Markuu hadlo, wuxuu ku hadlaa fikrado dhammaystiran.</u>

<!-- p20 -->
Waligiis nagama tago annaga oo buuxinayna meelaha madhan. Il-bidhiqsiyo gudaheed, Ilaah wuu qalbigaaga ku buuxin karaa xog ku filan oo kaa dhigaysa inaad cimrigaaga oo dhan wacdiyeyso. Hal taabasho oo Ilaah ah ayaa kugu shidi karta dab kaa sii guban doona weligiis. Wuxuu yahay isgaarsiiyahan dhamaystiran.

<!-- p21 -->
4.  <u>Marka Ilaah inala hadlayo, WALIGIIS MA KAXEEYO. Wuu hoggaamiyaa.</u>

<!-- p22 -->
Ma aha sayid shaqo adag; waa Ra'iye. Kuwa adhiga kaxeeyaa way kaxeeyaan, laakiin ra'iyehu wuu hoggaamiyaa. Ciise waa Ra'iiga Weyn ee idaha, annagana waxaan nahay idihii daaqiisa.

<!-- p23 -->
5.  <u>Markuu carruurtiisa la hadlo, WALIGIIS MA CAMBAAREEYO; WUU QANCIYAA.</u>

<!-- p24 -->
Carruurtiisu waa kuwii mar kale dhashay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:3</span>, 5) iyagoo qirashadooda iimaanka ee dhiiggii uu **Ciise** daadshay iyo shaqadiisii Kalfaari. Yaa raba inuu dhageysto qof had iyo jeer nagu cambaareeya khaladkeenna? Waxaan aaminsanahay in intiisa badan carruurta **Ilaah** ay ka baqaan inay maqlaan **Ilaah** oo la hadlaya, maxaa yeelay waxaa loo sheegay in **Ilaah** uu ku cambaareyn doono tan ama ta. Eeg <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Yooxanaa 3:19-20</span>. Yaa ayuu leeyahay in uu nagu cambaareeyo ama nagu xukumo? Waa RUUXEENNA ama qalbigayaga kaas oo canaanta ilmaha **Ilaah**, laakiin waa **Ilaah Ruuxa Quduuska ah** kan wax walba yaqaanna oo WAY KA WEYN qalbiyadeenna. Wuxuu halkaas u jooga inuu noqdo Raalli-geliyahayaga. Akhri Aayadaha soo socda, waxayna ku caawin doonaan inaad fahamto in kuwa kaliya ee Ruuxa Quduuska ahi cambaareeyo ay yihiin dembiilayaasha. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:33-34</span> waxay ina baraysaa in WAXBA aysan iman karin innaga iyo jacaylka **Ilaah** dhexdooda. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:16</span>, 26 waxay ina baraysaa in Ruuxa Quduuska ahi yahay Raalli-geliyahayaga, ee uusan ahayn cambaareeyahayaga, La-taliyahayaga, ee uusan ahayn ciqaabeheenna, Dhexdhexaadiyahayaga iyo Gargaareheenna, ee uusan ahayn carqaladeeyahayaga. Sidoo kale akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 9:31</span> iyo 15:28 tusaale ahaan sida Ruuxa Quduuska ahi (codka **Ilaah**) inoo gargaaro, inoogu raalli-geliyo, oo ina xoojiyo.

<!-- p25 -->
6.  <u>Codka Ilaah waa la tijaabin karaa si aad U OGAATO inuu Ilaah yahay ee aanu ahayn fikradahaaga ama codka ruuxyo la yaqaan.</u>

<!-- p26 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Yooxanaa 4:1-3</span> waxay ina faraysaa inaan “tijaabino ruux kasta” si aan uga hubanno inuu ka yimid Ilaah. Marka aad u malaynayso inaad ka maqleyso Ilaah, waxaad leedahay xaq iyo masuuliyad inaad codkaas su'aal weydiiso (Taasi waa tijaabinta ama caddaynta ruuxa.) Su’aasha la weydiinayo waa: “Miyaan Ciise Masiix jidh ahaan u yimid?” Hal shay oo ruuxyada la yaqaan aanay weligood qiranayn waa in Ciise dhulka u yimid jidh ahaan. Wuxuu ahaa si buuxda Bani-aadam, iyo si buuxdana Ilaah. Ruux been ah ama diin been ah midna ma qiran doono in Ciise dhulkan ku yimid jidh ahaan oo uu ka soo furtay dadka dhammaan dembiyadooda weligeed. Tijaabi codka aad maqleyso. Waa inaad kaloo ogaataa in Ilaah uusan weligii kugu hoggaamin doonin wixii ka baxsan Ereygiisa qoran, Kitaabka Quduuska ah. Haddii aad maqleyso wax aan lagu taageeri karin Kitaabka Quduuska ah, Ilaah kama lihid. Ka tag! Tijaabi codka aad maqleyso si aad u aragto inuu ka yimid Ilaah.

<!-- p27 -->
7.  <u>Codka Ilaah had iyo jeer wuxuu u keenaa Ammaanta Ciise Masiix.</u>

<!-- p28 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ahi waa Codka Jannada. Isagaa halkan dhulka jooga si uu nala u shaqeeyo, Ciisena wuxuu yidhi had iyo jeer wuxuu ka hadli doonaa oo keliya waxa uu maqli doono, wuxuuna HAD IYO JEER u keeni doonaa ammaanta Ciise (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:13-14</span>). Halkan uma joogo inuu adeeggaaga, ama camalkaaga, ama barnaamijyada kaniisadda u keeno ammaanta. Dhammaan waxyaalahaas waa wanaagsan yihiin, inta badanna waa lagama maarmaan si waxa aynu samayno loo qabto, laakiin Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuu hadli doonaa oo wuxuu ammaanta u keeni doonaa Qofka Ciise. Haddii codka aad maqleysaa adiga lagu ammaanayo, Ilaah ma tihid maqashay. Farqi baa u dhexeeya in lagu ammaano iyo in lagu dhiirrigeliyo lana xoojiyo. Ruuxa Quduuska ahi had iyo jeer wuu ku dhiirrigelin doonaa oo wuu ku xoojin doonaa mar kasta oo uu kula hadlo, xataa markuu ku saxayo (hanuuninaya) erayga. Laakiin ammaan yahay marka aad rumaysato in ADIGA aad qof tahay. Adigu waxba ma tihid la’aanta Ciise—ISAGA AYAANA KAN, annagu ma nihin.

<!-- p29 -->
8.  <u>Codka Ilaah had iyo jeer wuu inoo MUUJIN doonaa wax.</u>

<!-- p30 -->
Eeg <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:14</span>. Wuxuu had iyo jeer ka qaataa waxa **Ciise** iska leh oo wuu inoo muujinayaa. Amplified-ku wuxuu ku darayaa erayada: ku dhawaaq, shaaca ka qaad, iyo gudbi marka laga hadlayo erayga “muujin.” **Ilaah** had iyo jeer wax buu innoo shaaca ka qaadaa kaddib markuu innoo ku dhawaaqo, dabadeedna iimaankaaga ayuu inoogu gudbiyaa. Sidoo kale baro <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 2:10</span> iyo 12.

<!-- p31 -->
9.  <u>Codka Ilaah had iyo jeer wuu keenaa nabad.</u>

<!-- p32 -->
Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:26</span> iyo 27, iyo <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 3:14</span> iyo 15. Codkiisu weligiis inagama walbahaar geliyo. Waa cod keena nabad qumman. Nabadda gudaha waxay sabab u tahay in ay jirto OGAANSHO keenta kalsooni Ilaah hortiisa, taas oo isu beddesha nabad qumman. Haddii codka aad dhageysanaysaa kugu khaldo, Ilaah kama lihid—xir. Ciise wuxuu inoo ballanqaaday nabad, aan ahayn sida dunidu bixiso ee sida uu inoogu siiyaa erayada raalli-gelinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah iyo joogitaankiisa joogtada ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:27</span>).

<!-- p33 -->
10. <u>Codka Ilaah HAD IYO JEER wuu keenaa RUNTA.</u>

<!-- p34 -->
Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:17</span>, 26; 16:13. Runtu waxay tahay, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:13</span> wuxuu Ruuxa Quduuska ah ugu yeedhaa: “Ruuxa Runta.” Ilaah been ma sheegi karo! Isagu waa RUN, sidaas darteedna wuxuu hadli doonaa oo keliya RUN. Dad ayaa iigu yidhi sidan oo kale: “Ma rumaysan karo in waxa aan maqlayo ay ka yimaadeen Ilaah. Aad buu u wanaagsan yahay in uu run noqdo.” Waxaa laga yaabaa in uu aad u wanaagsan yahay in uu run noqdo; taa awgeed waa inaad Tijaabisaa ruuxa adigoo la waafajinaya Ereyga Ilaah si aad u hubiso in ay Run tahay. Dad qaarkood si fudud uma rumaysan karaan maxaa yeelay waxa ay maqlayaan had iyo jeer waa togan, waxaana la baray in Ilaah uu khaladka inaga cambaareeyo. Ilaah wuxuu sheegi doonaa oo keliya RUNTA, waana had iyo jeer WAR WANAAGSAN. Runta murugada leh waa in inta badan dadka ay ka maqlaan oo keliya war xun codka ay dhageysanayaan oo aanay marnaba filayn in ay maqlaan war wanaagsan, marka war wanaagsan yimaaddo, waxay su'aal geliyaan sidii wax laga yaabo in uusan run ahayn. **Runta mar walba waa war wanaagsan!** Waa **runta taas** ina xoreysa, ee sidee **run** uga noqon kartaa wax aan ahayn war wanaagsan?

<!-- p35 -->
11. <u>Codka **Ilaah** had iyo jeer NAGA HOGGAAMIYAA HORE IYO KOR.</u>

<!-- p36 -->
Akhri Sharciga Kunoqoshadiisa 28:1-2, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ishacyaah 30:21</span>, iyo <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:13</span>

<!-- p37 -->
Ilaah waligiis hoos kuguma wado! Waligiis ku ma liido; kaliya wuu ku dhisaa. Marka Ilaah kula hadlayo, wuxuu ka hadlayaa aragti tixraaceed oo ah in wax kasta oo Ereygu kaa yiraahdo hore loo fuliyey oo ADIGA aad AQBASHAY. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="33" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 33:3</span> waxay tidhaahdaa: “Ila iigu yeedha, anna waan kuugu jawaabi doonaa, waxaanan ku tusayaa waxyaabo **WAAWEYN IYO XOOG BADAN** oo aadan garanayn.” Taasi ma u egtahay in lagu liidayo ama musiibo iyo murugo? Ilaah gudaheeda WAXBA kama jiraan musiibo iyo murugo, sidaa awgeed wax doomy ama gloomy ah kuu sheegi karo ma aha. Waa inaad la xirmataa wakaaladaha wararka ee dunida si aad uga hesho musiibo iyo murugo.

<!-- p38 -->

> **<u>Yeremyaah 29:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Waayo waxaan ogahay fikirrada aan idinka ka fekero, ayuu leeyahay RABBIGU, fikirro nabad ah ee aan shar ahayn, inaan idin siiyo mustaqbal iyo rajo.

<!-- p40 -->
</span>

<!-- p41 -->
Hadda, haddii Ilaah KA FIKIRO fikrado wanaagsan oo nabad, mustaqbal, iyo rajo ah oo noo jeeda, maxuu u hadli lahaa si ka duwan? Jawaabtu waa, MARNABA UMA HADLI LAHAYN, MANA HADLO! Isagu keliya ayuu ina hoggaamiyaa KOR iyo HORE isagoo rajo weyn leh si uu noogu diyaariyo mustaqbal aan filayno.

<!-- p42 -->
Waxaan rajaynayaa in tani kugu dhiirrigeliso inaad “u baryo Isaga” oo aad barato sida loo **maqlo codkiisa**. Wuxuu kuu hayo waxyaabo waaweyn oo uu kuu sheego, balse wuxuu u baahan yahay inaad dhegaysato ka hor intaan dhammaan waxyaabihiisa wanaagsan ay wax kuu tarin.
