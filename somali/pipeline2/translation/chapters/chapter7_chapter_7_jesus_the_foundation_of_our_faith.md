# Cutubka 7 - Ciise-Aasaaska Iimaankeena

<!-- p1 -->
Anagoo ka sii dhiseyna daraasaddeenna ku saabsan Ciise sidii Masiixa, cutubkan waxaan sii eegi doonnaa nolosha, qofnimada, iyo shaqada Ciise. Waxaan arki doonnaa in Ciise yahay Dhexdhexaadiyaha axdi cusub oo ka wanaagsan-inuu si qumman u buuxiyo kaalmaha Nabi, Wadaad, iyo Boqor. Axdigan Cusub gudaashiisa ayaynu, annagoo rumaystayaal ah, Ilaah hortiisa ku leenahay istaag wanaagsan, waxaynuna ka gudubnaa axdi camal-ku-saleysan una gudubnaa axdi kalsooni-ku-saleysan (iimaan-ku-saleysan). Ugu dambayn, waxaan akhrin oo baran doonnaa *Qirashada Rasuullada* si aan u dhamaystirno daraasaddeenna ku saabsan Ciise.

<!-- p2 -->
## Dhexdhexaadiyaha Axdiga Cusub

<!-- p3 -->
Fikradda dhexdhexaadiye waxay tilmaamaysaa qof u dhexeeya laba dhinac, oo buuxiya farqiga, una sahla xiriir ama heshiis. Sheekada Baxniintii gudaheeda, Muuse wuxuu ahaa dhexdhexaadiyihii ugu horreeyay ee axdiga Ilaah la sameeyey dadkiisa. Muuse wuxuu qaatay sharciga, wuxuu bixiyay allabaryo (wadaad), wuxuu u yeedhay dadka Ilaah addeecid (nabi), wuxuuna xukumi jiray (xaakim/boqor). Inta kalena ee Axdigii Hore ka dambaysay Muuse, doorka dhexdhexaadintu waxa lagu fulin jiray doorka Nabiyada, Wadaadnimada Laawi, iyo Xaakinnada iyo dabadeed Boqorradii qaranqa.

<!-- p4 -->
Doorrada Dhexdhexaadiyayaasha ee Axdigii Hore:

<!-- p5 -->
1.  **Nabi:** Nabiyaashu waxay ahaayeen fariinwadeennada Ilaah, waxaana loo soo diray inay dadka u sheegaan qalbigiisa oo ay ugu yeedhaan inay ku soo laabtaan markay ka leexdaan. Muuse, tusaale ahaan, wuxuu u adeegay sidii nabi dhexdhexaadinayay Axdigii Hore isagoo ka helay Sharciga Ilaah oo u baray Israa'iil. Nabiyaashu sidoo kale waxay sii sheegeen imanitaanka Masiixa iyo furashada ugu dambaysa ee aadanaha. Tusaalooyin kale oo caan ah oo nabiyo ah waxaa ka mid ah: Ishacyaah, Yeremyaah, Eliiyaas, Elishaa, Samuu'eel.

<!-- p6 -->
2.  **Wadaadnimada Laawi:** Wadaaddadu waxay u shaqayn jireen sidii dhexdhexaadiyeyaal iyagoo bixin jiray allabaryo kafaaraha dembiyada iyo u shafeecayn jiray dadka hortiisa Ilaah. Wadaadka Sare, gaar ahaan, wuxuu door muhiim ah ka ciyaari jiray Maalinta Kafaaraha, isagoo geli jiray Quduuska Quduuska ah si uu dhiig ugu buufiyo kursiga naxariista, taas oo astaan u ahayd heshiisiinta Ilaah. Hawsha wadaadku waa inuu u shafeeco dadka.

<!-- p7 -->
3.  **Xaakim/Boqor:** Xaakinnadu, iyo dabadeed Boqorradu, waxaa lagu xil saaray inay xukuntaan dadka Ilaah, hirgeliyaan sharciyadiisa, dadka u kala xukumaan, ka siiyaan cadowgooda, iyo hoggaamiyaan xaqnimo. Kitaabka Xaakinnadii wuxuu sheegayaa sheekooyinka hoggaamiyeyaashan-dad sida Deboora, Samsuun, iyo Gidecoon-oo Ilaah kiciyay si ay u badbaadiyaan una hanuuniyaan Israa'iil. Israa'iil dabadeed waxay yeelatay silsilad boqorro ah, intooda badan xun, qaarna wanaagsan (eeg Boqorradii 1<sup>aad</sup> iyo 2<sup>aad</sup>, Samuu'eel 1<sup>aad</sup> iyo 2<sup>aad</sup>, iyo Taariikhdii 1<sup>aad</sup> iyo 2<sup>aad</sup>). Tusaalooyin caan ah waxaa ka mid ah Sa'uul, Daawuud, iyo Sulaymaan.

<!-- p8 -->
Waxaa mudan in la xuso in dhammaan dhexdhexaadiyeyaashii Axdigii Hore ay ahaayeen bini-aadam buuxa, oo aan ahayn rabaani. Maadaama ay yihiin dad, way dembi geli kareen, marar badanna way ku dhaceen dembi. Qaar ka mid ah dhexdhexaadiyeyaasha ayaa dembiile ahaa, waxayna dadka ka fogeeyeen Ilaah oo u horseedeen cibaado xun: nebiyo been ah, wadaaddo beenaalayaal oo musuqmaasuq ah, iyo boqorro sharka ah.

<!-- p9 -->
### Masiixu sidii Dhexdhexaadiyaha Axdiga Cusub

<!-- p10 -->
Geeridii, sarakiciddiisii iyo kor-qaadiddiisii Ciise waxay dhamaystireen Axdigii Hore, waxayna aasaaseen Axdiga Cusub.

<!-- p11 -->
Axdiga Cusub waa xidhiidh beddelaya nolol oo u dhexeeya Ilaah iyo dadkiisa, kaas oo lagu aasaasay nolosha, geerida, iyo sarakicidda Ciise Masiix. Si ka duwan Axdigii Hore oo xooga saaray sharciyo dibadeed, Axdiga Cusub wuxuu xoogga saarayaa isbeddel gudaha ah iyo naxariis. Wuxuu ku bilaabmay mar uu Ciise ku dhawaaqay Cashadii Ugu Dambaysay, halkaas oo uu sheegay in dhiiggiisu uu dhisi doono axdigan, isagoo beddelaya allabaryadii xoolaha ee soo noqnoqon jiray ee Axdigii Hore kagana beddelaya kafaaraggudkiisa mar-kaliya ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 22:20</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:10</span>). Allabarkan dhexdiisa, Axdiga Cusub wuxuu bixiyaa cafis dhamaystiran iyo nadiifin dembi, isagoo buuxinaya shuruudda heshiisiin qumman oo joogto ah la yeesho Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 31:34</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 8:12</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:12-18</span>).

<!-- p12 -->
Mid ka mid ah sifooyinka udub-dhexaadka u ah Axdiga Cusub waa dabeecaddiisa gudaha, maadaama Ilaah uu sharcigiisa ku qorayo qalbiyada dadkiisa, isaga oo kobcinaya xiriir heer qalbi ah isaga lala yeesho iyadoo loo marayo Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 31:33</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 8:10</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Korintos 3:3</span>). Isbeddelkani wuxuu awood siiyaa rumaystayaasha inay ku noolaadaan doonista Ilaah, annagoo aan ku ekaan ku-dhaqanka amarro dibadeed oo keliya ee, balse iyadoo Ruuxu xoojinayo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:1-4</span>). Axdiga Cusub wuxuu sidoo kale siinayaa helitaan toos ah Ilaah, isaga oo meesha ka saaraya baahida wadaadnimo dhuleed. Ciise wuxuu u adeegaa sidii Wadaadka Sare ee weligiis ah, rumaystayaashana waxaa lagu magacaabaa wadaadnimo boqortooyo, iyagoo ku dhawaan kara Ilaah si kalsooni leh allabariga Masiixa darteed (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24-25" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 7:24-25</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:19-20</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:9</span>).

<!-- p13 -->
Si ka duwan Axdigii Hore oo gaar u ahaa Israa'iil, Axdiga Cusub waa guud, wuxuuna u fidsan yahay dhammaan kuwa ku rumeeya Masiixa, iyadoon loo eegin qoomiyad ama asal (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:16</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 3:28</span>). Axdi kani wuxuu gebi ahaanba ku salaysan yahay naxariis, isagoo badbaado iyo cafis si xor ah ugu bixinaya iimaanka Ciise dhexdiisa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:8-9</span>). Laguma hayo dadaalka bini'aadam ee waxaa haya awoodda Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee xoojinaysa, kaas oo beddela oo xoojiya rumaystayaasha inay ku noolaadaan addeecid oo ay muujiyaan dabeecadda Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EZK" data-chapter="36" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yexesqeel 36:26-27</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:9-16</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22-23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 5:22-23</span>). Intaa waxaa dheer, Axdiga Cusub waa weligeed, wuxuuna ka sarreeyaa dabeecadda ku-meel-gaarka ah iyo shuruudaysan ee Axdigii Hore (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="32" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 32:40</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 8:13</span>). Waxay ka dhigan tahay dhamaystirka ugu dambeeya ee qorshaha furashada Ilaah, iyadoo aadanaha kula heshiisiisa isagiisa, rumaystayaashana u suurto gelisa inay weligood ugu noolaadaan sidii dadkiisa hoos jooga xukunkiisa.

<!-- p18 -->
Iyadoo geeridiisa kafaaraha ah iyo sarakiciddiisa, Masiixu wuxuu bilaabay axdigan cusub, isagoo beddelay nidaamkii hore ee allabaryo iyo sharciyo dibadeed, kuna beddelay axdi lagu qoray qalbiyada rumaystayaasha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31-34" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 31:31-34</span>). Wuxuu u dhexeeyaa Ilaah iyo aadanaha, mana aha oo keliya inuu shafeeco u sameeyo dadkiisa, balse sidoo kale wuxuu bixiyaa habka heshiisiinta iyada oo dhiiggiisa la marayo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:12</span>). Si ka duwan dhexdhexaadiyeyaashii Axdigii Hore, Ciise waa eedlaawe, weligiis ah, isla markaana si buuxda ugu qalma inuu buuxiyo shuruudaha rabbaaniga ah ee cadaaladda iyo naxariista, isagoo dammaanad qaadaya xidhiidh waara oo u dhexeeya Ilaah iyo rumaystayaasha.

<!-- p19 -->
Ciise Wuxuu Buuxiyaa oo Kaamil ka Dhigay Doorradan

<!-- p20 -->
1.  **Nabi:** Ciise waa Nabiga ugu sarreeya; ma aha oo keliya fariinwade erayga Ilaah balse waa Eraygii jidh noqday (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:14</span>). Wuxuu si buuxda oo qumman u muujinayaa dabeecadda Ilaah, doonistiisa, iyo qorshihiisa badbaadada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 1:1-2</span>). Waxbaristiisa, mucjisooyinkiisa, iyo bayaannadiisa nebiyadeed waxay ka dhabeeyaan nebiyadii Axdigii Hore waxayna dhidibada u taagaan Axdiga Cusub.

<!-- p21 -->
2.  **Wadaad:** Ciise waa Wadaadka Sare ee kaamilka ah, isaga oo naftiisa u bixiyey allabari mar-keliya-loo-dhan-yahay oo loo bixiyey dembiga (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 7:27</span>). Si ka duwan wadaadadii Axdigii Hore oo ku qasbanaa inay marar badan allabaryo u bixiyaan dembiyadooda iyo kuwa dadka, Ciise, maadaama uu ahaa dembi-la’aan, dhiiggiisa ayuu bixiyey si uu u sugo furashada weligeed ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:14</span>).

<!-- p22 -->
3.  **Boqor &amp; Garsoore:** Ciise waa Boqorka weligiis ah, isagoo ku xukuma xaqnimo iyo caddaalad. Sida Wiilka Daa’uud, wuxuu ka dhabeeyaa ballanka boqortooyo weligeed ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuu’eel 7:16</span>). Boqornimadiisu ma aha kaliya mid dunyadeed ee waa mid koonkeenna oo dhan ah, isagoo ka taliya abuurka oo dhan, nabaddana dhidibada u taaga guushiisa ka dhanka ah dembiga iyo dhimashada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PHP" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Filiboy 2:9-11</span>). Ciise wuu soo noqon doonaa isagoo boqor iyo garsoore ah, oo xukumi doona ‘kuwa nool iyo kuwa dhintay’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 10:42</span>).

<!-- p23 -->
Masiixa dhexdiisa, kaalmaha Nabi, Wadaad, iyo Boqor way isu yimaadaan oo si kaamil ah ayey u rumoobaan. Isagoo ah Dhexdhexaadiyaha Axdiga Cusub, wuxuu buundo ka dhigayaa farqiga u dhexeeya Ilaah quduus ah iyo aadanaha dembiilaha ah, isagoo rumaystayaasha u suurta gelinaya inay Ilaah ugu soo dhowaadaan kalsooni (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 4:16</span>) isla markaana xaqiijinaya dhaxalka weligeed ah ee lagu ballanqaaday isaga.

## Caqiidada Rasuullada

<!-- p25 -->
*Caqiidada Rasuullada* lagama helo Qorniinka; waxa la sameeyay oo la beddelay iyadoo lagu salaynayo waxbaristii ugu horreysay ee kiniisadda. Caqiidadu waa qirasho rasmi ah oo aaminsanaan; u fiirso sidii ballan ama dhaar oo lagu daray bayaanka aaminsanaanta. Caqiidooyinku waa muhiim sababtoo ah waxaa loo adeegsadaa in lagu mideeyo ama lagu kala saaro kuwa si gaar ah u aaminsan iyo kuwa aan aaminsanayn. Dalka Maraykanka waxa caadi ka ah in munaasabado madani ah sida shirarka golaha magaalada ama kulamada sharci-dejinta ka-qaybgalayaashu ku akhriyaan Ballanqaadka Daacadnimada. Ballanku si weyn ayaa looga adeegsadaa Maraykanka, balse waddamada kale looma adeegsado, waayo muwaadiniinta waddamadaas uma laha daacadnimo (allegiance) ay u hayaan Maraykanka. Ballanku ma aha waxa ka dhiga qof ‘Maraykan’, laakiin wuxuu metelaa aaminsanaan muhiim ah oo asaasi ah oo ay haystaan muwaadiniintu.

<!-- p26 -->
Noocyadii ugu horreeyey ee la garan karo ee caqiidada waxa la qoray qarnigii afraad S.D. Caqiidadu si yar bay isu beddeshay qarniyada, waxaana jira kala duwanaanshooyin yar yar oo ku salaysan firqada. Dhammaan firqooyinka Masiixiyiintu ma isticmaalaan caqiidada, hase yeeshee guud ahaan si ballaaran ayaa loo aqbalaa qaab uun guud ahaan Masiixiyadda oo dhan.

<!-- p27 -->
Caqiidada Rasuullada waxaa qarniyo badan loo adeegsaday qalab waxbarid iyo kateekis, iyadoo gudbinaysa runaha ugu aasaasiga ah ee iimaanka Masiixiga, aaminsanaanta salka ah ee la aqoonsan oo la hayo si loo noqdo Masiixi. Qaar ka mid ah firqooyinka waxay leeyihiin hab kateekis rasmi ah, kaas oo ah sida rumaystaha cusub ugu biiro firqadaas gaarka ah oo loogu baro caqiidadooda.

<!-- p28 -->
Qof looma baahna inuu si buuxda u fahmo aaminsanaadyadan si uu Ilaah ugu yimaado oo uu helo badbaado; badbaadadu waxay ku timaaddaa rumaysad, ma aha imtixaan caqiido! Si kastaba ha ahaatee, sii wadidda rumaysadkaas dhexdiisa, dhammaanteen waa in aynu baranno oo fahanno runahan aasaasiga ah.

<!-- p30 -->
## Sharaxaad Kooban oo ku Saabsan Caqiidada Rasuullada

<!-- p31 -->
Runaha aasaasiga ah ee ku jira Caqiidada Rasuullada waa tiro badan oo aad u qoto dheer; sharaxaad buuxda waxay hubaal ahaan qaadan lahayd dhowr mug oo buug ah. In kasta oo cutubkan uu ku saabsan yahay Ciise, haddana mowduucyo badan ayaa ku gudba xubnaha kale ee Ilaahnimada. Tani waxay noqon doontaa sharaxaad kooban oo caqiidada ah, oo diiradda saaraysa kaliya mowduucyada ugu dhexeeya. Qaar badan oo ka mid ah arrimahan ayaa si qoto dheer looga daraaseeyaa cutubyada buuggan.

<!-- p32 -->
#### *Waxaan rumaysanahay Ilaah, Aabbaha Qaadirka ah, abuuraha samada iyo dhulka.*

<!-- p33 -->
Tani waxay xaqiijinaysaa rumaysadka Ilaah sida Abuuraha iyo Aabbaha suvereenka ah ee awood kasta leh. Cinwaanka "Aabbe" wuxuu adkeeyaa xidhiidhka jacayl ee Ilaah la leeyahay abuurkiisa, gaar ahaan carruurtiisa iyada oo rumaysad ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:12</span>). Isagoo Abuuraha ah, wuxuu eray ku abuuray koonka, isagoo ka keenay nidaam fawdo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Bilowgii 1:1-3</span>). Awooddiisa Qaadirka ah waxaa lagu arkaa baaxadda abuurka, halka daryeelkiisa aabbannimo laga arko taageeriddiisa joogtada ah iyo u-habaynkiisa. Weedhan aasaasiga ahi waxay dhigaysaa Ilaah inuu yahay asalka iyo xukunkaba nolosha oo dhan, mudanna cibaado iyo kalsooni.

<!-- p34 -->
#### *Waxaan rumaysanahay Ciise Masiix, Wiilkiisa keliya, Rabbigeenna.*

<!-- p35 -->
Ciise Masiix waa Wiilka gaar ahaaneed ee Ilaah, weligiis la dhalay, aan la abuurin, oo ka qayb leh dabeecadda Ilaahnimo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:1-2</span>, 14). Ciise waa ‘Erayga’ tan iyo bilowgii, wuuna jidh noqday oo nala joogay. Isagoo ah "Masiix", waa kii la subkay, Masiixii, oo ka dhabeeya ballamihii Axdigii Hore ee ku saabsanaa Badbaadiye. Cinwaanka "Rabbiga" wuxuu qirayaa awooddiisa ka sarreynta abuurka oo dhan iyo kaalintiisa sharciga ah ee ahaanaysa taliyaha iyo Boqorka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PHP" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10-11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Filiboy 2:10-11</span>). Isagu keliya ma aha Rabbiga, ee waa Rabbiga badbaadiya (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 16:31</span>).

<!-- p36 -->
Ciise ma aha oo keliya in Ilaah diray; Isagu waa qayb ka mid ah Ilaah. Ciise wuxuu ku jiraa Aabbaha, Aabbuhuna wuxuu ku jiraa Ciise. Injiilka Yooxanaa si isdaba joog ah ayuu u caddeeyaa in Ciise iyo Aabbuhu ay hal yihiin (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="30" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 10:30</span>), in Ciise uusan falin isagoo iskiis ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 5:19</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="49-50" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 12:49-50</span>), iyo in wax kasta oo uu qabtay ay ahaayeen in uu Aabbihiis ammaano (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="17" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 17:4</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:13</span>), isla markaana lagu sameeyey amar iyo awoodda Aabbihiis (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:10</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28-29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 8:28-29</span>).

<!-- p37 -->
#### *Wuxuu uur galay xoogga Ruuxa Quduuska ah oo ka dhashay Maryan Bikradii.*

<!-- p38 -->
Tani waxay xaqiijinaysaa uuraysigii mucjisada ahaa ee Ciise iyada oo Ruuxa Quduuska ah, waxayna xoogga saartaa asalnimadiisa Ilaahnimo. Isagoo ka dhashay Maryan Bikro, Ciise wuxuu ka dhabeeyaa saadaashii <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ishacyaah 7:14</span>, taas oo hore u sii sheegtay dhalashada bikro ee Masiixa. Dhacdadan waxay calaamad u tahay jidh-qaadashada, halkaas oo Eraygii weliga ahaa ee Ilaah uu qaatay jidh bini’aadam (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:14</span>). Isagoo ah isku mar Ilaah dhammeystiran iyo bini’aadam dhammeystiran, Ciise wuxuu buundo ka dhigayaa farqiga u dhexeeya Ilaah iyo aadanaha, isaga oo u suurtagelinaya inuu noqdo dhexdhexaadiye iyo Badbaadiye kaamil ah.

<!-- p39 -->
Ciise waxa lagu uuraysiiyey abuurkii Ruuxa Quduuska ah, mana uu dhaxlin dabeecadda dembiga ee annaga, oo ka dhalanay abuurka ragga, na haysata. Ciise dhinac kasta waa la jirrabay oo waa la tijaabiyey, laakiin wuu kaamil ahaa oo dembi ma lahayn. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 4:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Korintos 5:21</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:22</span>.

<!-- p40 -->
#### *Wuxuu ku silicaay xilligii Bontiyos Bilaatos, waa la iskutallaabay, wuu dhintay, wuuna la aasay.*

<!-- p41 -->
Silicii iyo dhimashadii Ciise waxay ahaayeen dhacdooyin dhab ah oo taariikhi ah oo ka dhacay xilligii maamulihii Roomaanka ee Bontiyos Bilaatos, taasoo ku salaynaysa iimaanka Masiixiga taariikh la xaqiijin karo. Iskutallaabtiisu waxay ahayd rumoobid nebiyadii Axdigii Hore, sida <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="53" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ishacyaah 53:5</span>, oo ka hadlaysa Addoonka Silca ee qaadi doona dembiyada dad badan. Dhimashadii Ciise waxay ahayd mid allabari ah, isaga oo qaadanaya ciqaabta dembiga aawadi dadka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:24</span>). Aaskiisu wuxuu xaqiijiyaa dhabta dhimashadiisa, wuxuuna gogol-xaaraa macnaha sarakicidda.

<!-- p42 -->
#### *Wuxuu u degay xagga kuwii dhintay.*

<!-- p43 -->
Dhimashadiis ka dib, Ciise wuxuu u degay degaanka kuwii dhintay, oo Qorniinka badanaa loogu yeero *Hades* ama *Sheol*. Fal kani wuxuu muujinayaa isu-aqoonsigiisa buuxa ee aadanaha, xitaa geeri dhexdeeda, wuxuuna caddaynayaa guushiisa dembiga, geerida, iyo shaydaanka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 3:18-20</span>). Isagoo u degay ayuu ku dhawaaqay guushiisa iyo dhamaystirka shaqadiisa furashada.

<!-- p44 -->
#### *Maalintii saddexaad ayuu mar kale sarakacay.*

<!-- p45 -->
Sarakicidda Ciise waa tiirka-geeska ee iimaankeena Masiixiga, waxayna xaqiijisaa Ilaahnimadiisa iyo runnimada waxbariddiisa. Isagoo ka soo sara kacay dhintay, wuu ka adkaaday dembi iyo geeri, wuxuuna siiyay rumaystayaasha xaqiijinta nolosha weligeed ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 15:17-22</span>). Sarakiciddu sidoo kale waxay rumoobisaa Qorniinka, oo ay ku jiraan saadaashii Ciise laftiisu sheegay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 16:21</span>). Dhacdadan mucjisada ah waxay u beddeshay niyad-jabka xertiisii iimaan geesinimo leh, iyadoo dhidibada u taagtay markhaatifurnida Kaniisaddii hore.

<!-- p46 -->
#### *Wuxuu u kacay samada, wuxuuna fadhiyaa midigta Aabbaha.*

<!-- p47 -->
Sare u kaciddiisa waxay calaamad u tahay ku-laabashadiisa joogitaanka Aabbaha ee ammaanta. Isagoo ku fadhiya midigta Aabbaha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:20</span>), wuxuu qaataa xilka Wadaadkeena Sare ee weliga ah, isagoo u baryaya rumaystayaasha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="25" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 7:25</span>). Jagadiisa awoodu sidoo kale waxay muujinaysaa kor u qaadiddiisa sida Boqor, isaga oo u taliya khalqiga oo dhan, oo hagiya hawsha Kaniisadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:22</span>) si ay u qabato shaqooyin ka weyn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:12</span>) oo ay u fuliso amarka weyn ee uu na siiyay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:18-20</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 16:15-18</span>). Sare u kaciddu waxay rumaystayaasha ku xaqiijisaa joogitaankiisa iyo awooddiisa oo sii socda iyada oo loo marayo Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p48 -->
#### *Wuxuu mar kale iman doonaa si uu u xukumo kuwa nool iyo kuwa dhintay.*

<!-- p49 -->
Tani waxay tilmaamaysaa soo laabashada mustaqbalka ee Masiixa markaasoo uu keeni doono caddaalad iyo soo celin kama dambays ah. Ciise wuxuu xukumi doonaa dhammaan dadka, isagoo abaalmarinaya kuwa daacadda ah oo caddaalad ku samaynaya kuwa aan toobad keenin (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 4:1</span>). Soo laabashadiisu waxay rumoobisaa qorshaha Eebbe ee ugu dambeeya ee abuurista, iyadoo soo gelinaysa Boqortooyadiisa weligeed ah ee xaqnimada iyo nabadda. Rajo tani waxay ku dhiirrigelisaa rumaystayaasha inay si daacadnimo iyo filasho leh u noolaadaan, iyagoo og in hawshoodu Rabbiga dhexdiisa aanay waxba u noqon (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="58" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 15:58</span>).

<!-- p50 -->
#### *Waxaan rumaysanahay Ruuxa Quduuska ah.*

<!-- p51 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ah, Qofka saddexaad ee Saddexmidnimada, waa joogitaanka iyo awoodda Eebbe ee maanta dunida ka shaqaysa. Wuxuu dembiga ku baraarujinayaa, wuxuu dib u dhalinayaa qalbiyo, wuxuuna xoojiyaa rumaystayaasha inay ku noolaadaan nolol quduus ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:8-13</span>). Ruuxu wuxuu mideeyaa Masiixiyiinta sidii hal jidh, oo ah Kaniisadda, wuxuuna ku qalabeeyaa hibbooyin ruuxi ah u adeegid (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:4-11</span>). Waa Gargaare, Difaaca, iyo Hagaha, isagoo ku nool rumaystayaasha dhexdiisa sidii shaabaddii dhaxalkooda weliga ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:13-14</span>).

<!-- p52 -->
#### *Kaniisadda quduuska ah ee caalamiga ah, wadajirka quduusiinta.*

<!-- p53 -->
Erayga "kaniisadda quduuska ah ee caalamiga ah" wuxuu tixraacayaa jidhka caalamiga ah ee Masiixa, oo ka kooban dhammaan rumaystayaasha waqti iyo meel kasta. Kaniisaddu waxay u go'an tahay ujeeddooyinka Eebbe, waxaana loo yeedhay inay muujiso quduusnimadiisa oo ay la wadaagto Injiilka. Erayga "wadajirka quduusiinta" wuxuu xoogga saarayaa midnimada dhammaan Masiixiyiinta, kuwa nool iyo kuwii dhintayba, sidii xubnaha jidhka Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 4:4-6</span>). Wada-jirkan wuxuu ka sarraysiiyaa kala qaybsanaanta adduunyo, isagoo muujinaya rajada iyo hawsha wadaagga ah ee rumaystayaasha.

<!-- p54 -->
Ciise waa madaxa kaniisadda. Kaniisadda waxaa loogu yeeraa labadaba aroosadda Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="25-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 5:25-27</span>) iyo jidhka Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 1:18</span>).

<!-- p55 -->
#### *Cafitaanka dembiyada.*

<!-- p56 -->
Tani waxay iftiiminaysaa wadnaha Injiilka: dhimashadii allabari ee Ciise awgeed, Eebbe wuxuu bixiyaa cafis buuxa oo dembiyada ah kuwa oo dhan ee toobad keena oo rumeeya (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:7</span>). Cafiskan wuxuu dib u soo celinayaa xiriirka jaban ee u dhexeeya aadanaha iyo Eebbe, isagoo ka qaadaya eeda iyo ceebta. Waxay sidoo kale ku yeedhaysaa rumaystayaasha inay cafis u fidiyaan kuwa kale, iyagoo muujinaya nimcada ay heleen (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 6:14-15</span>).

<!-- p57 -->
#### *Sarakicidda jidhka.*

<!-- p58 -->
Sida Ciise oo kale, dhammaadka wakhtiga waxaan la kulmi doonnaa sarakicid jidheed. Sarakiciddan waxay beddeli doontaa jidhyada dhiman kara, halligma leh kuwa ammaansan, aan halligmin, oo ku habboon nolosha weligeed ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="42-44" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 15:42-44</span>). Waxay xoogga saartaa rajada Masiixiga ee soo celinta, aan kaliya qof qof ahayn, balse dhammaan abuurka, maadaama Eebbe wax walba cusub ka dhigayo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="21" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 21:4-5</span>).

<!-- p59 -->
#### *Iyo nolosha weligeed ah. Aamiin.*

<!-- p60 -->
Nolosha weligeed ah ee Ilaah la jirta waa ballanka ugu sarreeya ee iimaanka Masiixiga. Nololkan wuxuu ka bilaabmaa hadda iyadoo loo marayo xiriir lala yeesho Masiixa waxaana si buuxda loo xaqiijin doonaa samooyinka cusub iyo dhulka cusub, halkaas oo rumaystayaashu Ilaah la deggi doonaan weligood (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:16</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 22:1-5</span>). Qirashadan gabagabada ahi waxay ka tarjumaysaa farxadda iyo kalsoonida badbaadada, iyadoo ka dhex yeedhaysa kalsoonida iyo rajada lagu muujiyey guud ahaan Qirashada.

<!-- p61 -->
## Gunaanad

<!-- p62 -->
Xaqiiqooyinka aasaasiga ah ee lagu muujiyey Qirashada Rasuullada, waxaan ku aragnaa nolosha iyo shaqada Ciise inay udub-dhexaad u yihiin qorshaha Ilaah ee aadanaha. Qoraal kasta oo ku jira Qirashadu waxa uu qabtaa dhinacyo muhiim ah oo ku saabsan rumaysadkeenna Ilaah, iyo cidda Ciise yahay-Ilaahnimadiisa, bini'aadannimadiisa, hawshiisa, iyo guushiisa kama dambaysta ah. Xaqiiqooyinkani waxay tilmaamayaan kaalintiisa gaarka ah ee Dhexdhexaadiyaha Axdiga Cusub. Isagoo ah Dhexdhexaadiye wuu na casuumayaa in aan Ilaah u dhownaanno annagoo ku kalsoon allabarigiisa qumman iyo u-dhexgalkiisa sii socda. Waxay ina wacaysaa in aynu caabudno isaga oo Boqorka xaqa ah oo aynu raacno bariddiisa isagoo ah Nebiga ugu sarreeya. Ugu dambayntii, barashada Ciise waxay horseeddaa xidhiidh qoto dheer oo isaga lala yeesho, oo inaga beddelaysa si aynu u noqono dad ku nool iftiinka naxariistiisa iyo runtiisa.

<!-- p63 -->
Fahmidda ciddii Ciise yahay iyo waxa uu qabtay waa aasaaska iimaanka Masiixiga. Magacyada, cinwaannada, iyo kaalmooyinka badan ee Ciise waxay iftiimiyaan dhinacyo kala duwan oo dabeecaddiisa iyo hawshiisa ka mid ah, iyaga oo bixinaya sawir ka buuxa jacaylkiisa iyo awooddiisa xukun.

<!-- p64 -->
Sida daraasaddeenna Ilaah, Qorniinku waxa uu ka buuxaa magacyo kala duwan oo tixraacaya Ciise. Magac kastaa wuxuu metelaa dhinac gaar ah oo ka mid ah dabeecaddiisa guud, adeeggiisa, iyo kaalintiisa. Magacyadan waxa naloo siiyey si ay u muujiyaan dhinac ka mid ah Ciise. Daraasaddaada shakhsiyadeed awgeed jadwal tixraac ah ayaa lagu daray Lifaaqa: *Jadwalka 12 - Magacyada Ciise.* Magacyadan ka akhri oo bar tixraacyada Qorniinka.

## Su'aalaha Fekerka Shakhsiyeed

<!-- p66 -->
1.  Door kee Ciise—Nebi, Wadaad, ama Boqor—ayaa waqtigan safarkaaga iimaan kugu saamaynaya ugu badan? Sabab?

<!-- p67 -->
2.  Sidee ayay Qirashada Rasuulladu kaa caawinaysaa inaad si ka wanaagsan u fahanto xaqiiqooyinka aasaasiga ah ee hawsha iyo shaqada Ciise?

<!-- p68 -->
3.  Ka feker mid ka mid ah magacyada Ciise (tusaale, Immanuel, Wanka Ilaah, Alfa, iyo Omega). Sidee ayuu magacani u muujinayaa dabeecaddiisa oo u saameeyaa xiriirka aad isaga la leedahay?

<!-- p69 -->
4.  Ka fiirso kaalinta Ciise ee ah Dhexdhexaadiyaha Axdiga Cusub. Sidee bay runtaani u qaabaynaysaa fahamkaaga badbaadada iyo u dhowaanshahaaga Ilaah?

<!-- p70 -->
5.  Qaababkee ayaad maanta ku aragtaa Ciise oo hogaaminaya, u-dhex-galaya, ama kuu baraya? Sidee baad uga jawaabi kartaa shaqadiisa meelahaan?

<!-- p71 -->
6.  Haddii qof ku weydiiyo inaad sharaxdo sababta Ciise uu aasaas ugu yahay iimaankaaga, maxaad odhan lahayd? Sidee ayay fahamkani u xoojin kartaa markhaatigaaga shakhsiyadeed?

## Su'aalaha Doodeed ee Kooxda (20-30 Daqiiqo)

<!-- p73 -->
1.  **Su'aal Baraf-jebis:** Hal magac ama cinwaan oo Ciise ah kee ayaa kuula lahaa macne gaar ah? Sabab?

<!-- p74 -->
2.  **Baadhista Qorniinka:** Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 1:1-3</span>. Sidee ayay qormadani u sharraxaysaa kaalinta Ciise ee ah Nebiga ugu sarreeya? Sidee ayay tani maanta innoo khusaysaa?

<!-- p75 -->
3.  **Nebi, Wadaad, Boqor:** Maxay ka dhigan tahay in Ciise uu buuxiyo saddexdan door? Sidee ayay doorarkan uga jawaabaan baahiyaha ugu waaweyn ee bani-aadamka?

<!-- p76 -->
4.  **Aasaaskayaga:** Maxay yihiin siyaabo wax-ku-ool ah oo aynu nolosheenna uga dhisi karno Ciise isagoo aasaaska iimaankeenna? Sidee tani u saameyn kartaa xiriirradeenna, shaqadeenna, ama adeeggeenna?

<!-- p77 -->
5.  **Maxaa dhacaya haddii:** Maxay muhiim u tahay in Ciise uu buuxiyo saddexdan door-Nebi, Wadaad, iyo Boqor? Maxaa ahaan lahaa mid aan dhammaystirnayn haddii mid ka mid ah doorarkan uu ka maqnaado?
