# Cutubka 13 - Awoodda Maamul ee Ruuxiga ah

<!-- p1 -->
Ciise waa isha ugu sarreysa oo dhamaystiran ee awoodda maamul ee ruuxiga ah. Maadaama uu yahay Masiixa guuleystay, wuxuu leeyahay awood maamul oo kama dambays ah dhammaan waxyaalaha ku jira samada iyo dhulka.

<!-- p2 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p3 -->
> **<u>Matayos 28:18-20</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Markaasaa Ciise u yimid oo la hadlay iyaga isagoo leh, "Awood oo dhan ayaa la i siiyey samada iyo dhulka.
>
> Haddaba taga oo ka yeela xerta quruumaha oo dhan, ku baabtiisa magaca Aabbaha iyo Wiilka iyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah,
>
> oo bara inay ilaaliyaan waxyaalaha oo dhan ee aan idin amray; oo bal eeg, anigu idinla joogi doonaa mar walba, ilaa dhammaadka waqtiga." Aamiin.
>

<!-- p4 -->
</span>

<!-- p5 -->
Cutubkan waxaan baran doonaa awoodda maamul iyo awoodda Masiixa, kuwaas oo awooddo maamul iyo awooddo uu noo wakiishay (kaniisaddiisa), iyo sida loogu shaqeeyo awoodda maamul ee magaca Ciise. Waxa kale oo aan baran doonaa makhluuqaadka malaa'igaha iyo jinniyada, oo aan eegi doonaa awooddooda maamul, awooddooda, iyo dabeecadahooda.

<!-- p6 -->
Awoodda maamul waxay ka dhigan tahay lahaanshaha xaqa ficil. Awooddu waa kartida lagu fulinayo. Labadooduba waa lama huraan nolosha ruuxiga ah. Awoodda maamul way ka duwan tahay awoodda. Aan eegno jadwalkan soo socda ee isbarbar dhigaya awoodda maamul &amp; awoodda oo aan tixgelinno dhowr tusaale.

<!-- p7 -->
[See Table 7](../tables/table7.md)

<!-- p8 -->
Halkan waxaa ku yaal dhowr tusaale si loo fahmo farqiga u dhexeeya awoodda iyo awoodda maamul.

<!-- p9 -->
Tusaale 1: Shaqada, shaqaale waxa laga yaabaa inuu leeyahay hibada iyo xirfadaha uu ku fulin karo iibinta hantida shirkadda (awood). Awooddan waa sharci haddii ay sidoo kale hayaan oggolaanshaha la xidhiidha (awoodda maamul) ee milkiilayaasha shirkadda. Iyadoo aan awooddan maamul jirin, iibinta hantida shirkadda waa xatooyo.

<!-- p10 -->
Tusaale 2: Sarkaal boolis waxaa la siiyaa tababar gaar ah oo hubka iyo dagaalka gacan-ku-gacanta ah si uu u barto sida dembiile loo qabto loona adeegsado xoog jireed (awood). Booliisyadu waxaa la siiyay masuuliyad shaqo oo ay shuruucdu taageerayaan (awoodda maamul) si ay u fuliyaan xeerarka ciqaabta.

<!-- p11 -->
## Waa maxay Awoodda Maamul ee Ciise?

<!-- p12 -->
Sida Ciise ku yidhi Matayos, ‘Awood oo dhan’ waa la siiyey. Aan eegno si sax ah waxa ay awooddaas maamul ka koobantahay:

<!-- p13 -->
- Ka sarreynta Samada iyo Dhulka

<!-- p14 -->
- Ka sarreynta nolosha iyo dhimashada

<!-- p15 -->
- Ka sarreynta dembiga iyo dembidhaafka

<!-- p16 -->
- Ka sarreynta jinniyada iyo xoogagga ruuxiga ah

<!-- p17 -->
- Ka sarreynta jirrada iyo cudurka

<!-- p18 -->
- Inuu aadanaha xukumo

<!-- p19 -->
- Ka sarreynta Kaniisadda

<!-- p20 -->
- Ka sarreeya abuurka iyo dabeecadda

<!-- p21 -->
- Ka sarreeya dhimashada, Jahannamada, iyo qabriga

<!-- p22 -->
- Si loo awood-siiyo rumaystayaasha

<!-- p23 -->
### Awood Ka Sarreysa Samada iyo Dhulka

<!-- p24 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p25 -->
> **<u>Matayos 28:18</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Oo Ciise ayaa u yimid oo la hadlay iyaga, isagoo leh, "Awood oo dhan waa la i siiyey samada iyo dhulka.

</span>

<!-- p27 -->
Awoodda Masiixu waxay ku fidsan tahay wax kasta oo ka mid ah abuurka, oo ay ku jiraan adduunka jireed iyo kan ruuxiga ah. Awooddan waxay dhidib u taagtaa xukunkiisa madax-bannaan ee ah Boqorka boqorrada iyo Rabbiga rabbiyada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 19:16</span>). Waxay noqotaa aasaaska Amarka Weyn, iyadoo rumaystayaasha awood-siineysa inay faafiyaan Injiilka.

## Isha Awoodda Ciise

<!-- p74 -->
Hadda markii aan si sax ah u eegnay waxa ay tahay awooddu, waxaan u baahanahay inaan ogaano ciddii isha u ah awooddaas. Awoodda Ciise waa rabaani isla markaana loo wakiishay. Waxay ka timaaddaa Ilaaha Aabbaha ah, waxaa lagu xaqiijiyey dhimashadiisii iyo sara-kiciddiisii, waxayna u shaqaysaa si qumman oo waafaqsan doonista Aabbaha. In kasta oo ay tahay mid mutlaq ah, haddana awooddiisu waxay ixtiraamtaa doorashada xorta ah ee dadka, taasoo muujinaysa jacaylka Ilaah halkii ay ka ahaan lahayd xukun xoog ku ah.

<!-- p75 -->
Awoodda Ciise waxay ka xididaysan tahay aqoonsigiisa rabaaniga ah ee ah Wiilka Ilaah, waxaana Aabbuhu siiyey. Aabbuhu wuxuu Masiixa ku aaminay awood kasta, oo ay ku jirto xukunka iyo taliska samada iyo dhulka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 11:27</span>). Awoodda Ciise waxay ka tarjumaysaa midnimada iyo jacaylka ku dhex jira Saddexmidnimada, halkaas oo Wiilku si qumman u fuliyo qorshaha Aabbaha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="35" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:35</span>).

<!-- p76 -->
In kasta oo Ciise lahaa awood ku jirta sida Wiilka Ilaah, guushiisa ka dhanka ah dembiga, geerida, iyo cadaabta si buuxda ayaa loo suuliyey oo loo xaqiijiyey dhimashadiisa iyo sara-kiciddiisa. Adeecidda Masiixa ee allabarkiisa ayaa horseedday sarreyntiisa (fadhiisiga dhinaca midig ee Aabbaha), taasoo sugtay awooddiisa abuurista oo dhan korkeed. Isagoo ka adkaaday geerida, Masiixu wuxuu helay maamul mutlaq ah nolosha iyo weliga.

>

<!-- p79 -->
</span>

In kasta oo Ciise leeyahay awoodda oo dhan, haddana waxa uu u adeegsadaa iyadoo si buuxda ula jaanqaadaysa qalbiga iyo ujeeddada Aabbaha:

<!-- p81 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p82 -->
> **<u>Yooxanaa 5:19</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Markaasaa Ciise u jawaabay oo wuxuu ku yidhi iyaga, "Run runtii, waxaan idinku leeyahay, Wiilku iskiis waxba ma samayn karo, laakiin wuxuu sameeyaa waxa uu arko Aabbaha samaynayo; waayo, wixii uu sameeyo, Wiilkuna si la mid ah buu u sameeyaa."
>

<!-- p83 -->
</span>

<!-- p84 -->
Ciise si madaxbannaan uma dhaqmo; taa beddelkeeda, waxa uu ka tarjumaa falalka Aabbaha, isagoo hubinaya in awooddiisu had iyo jeer caddaalad iyo xaqnimo tahay. Xataa erayadiisa iyo waxbaristiisuba waxay ka tarjumayaan ujeeddada Aabbaha, iyagoo muujinaya midnimadooda ujeeddo ahaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="49" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 12:49</span>).

Ciise waligiis ma qasbo qof inuu addeeco. Wuxuu ixtiraamaa xorriyaddeenna, si naxariis lehna wuu noogu yeeraa inaan raacno.

Masiixu wuu yeedhaa, laakiin ma qasbo addeecid, isagoo muujinaya jacayl halkii uu ka muujin lahaa cabburin. In kasta oo Ciise aanu burin doonista xorta ah, haddana wuu u oggolaanayaa dadka inay la kulmaan cawaaqibta doorashooyinkooda:

## Ciise wuxuu u wakiishay awoodo gaar ah raacayaashiisa

<!-- p94 -->
Ciise wuxuu mu'miniinta u wakiishay qaybo gaar ah oo awood ah. Awooddan waa mid ku dhisan xiriir. Awooddan waxa aynu ku leenahay keliya xiriirka aynu la leenahay Ciise. Ma lihin wax awood ah annagoo ka baxsan xiriirkeenna Ciise. Sidoo kale, xoogga uu Ruuxa Quduusku ina siiyey waxa uu ku yimaaddaa oo keliya xiriirka aynu la leenahay Isaga. Isaga mooyee, waxba ma qaban karno.

<!-- p95 -->

- **Wacdinta Injiilka &amp; Baabtiisidda Rumaystayaasha** - Tani waa awoodda Masiixu ugu dhisayo kiniisaddiisa oo uu xertiisa uga samaynayo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:19-20</span>). Awooddan looma siin faa’iido shakhsiyadeed ee waa qalab lagu hormariyo Boqortooyada Ilaah, lagu keeno bogsiin, laguna sii daayo dadku inay xoroobaan. Waxay u baahan tahay is-hoosaysiin, ku tiirsanaan Ruuxa Quduuska ah, iyo la jaanqaadid doonista Ilaah. Markaan ku tallaabsanno awooddan, waxaannu ku samaynaa iimaan, annagoo og in dhammaan awooddu ugu dambayntii u taallo Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:18</span>).

## Saarka Gacmaha

<!-- p116 -->
Saarka gacmaha waa dhaqan muhiim ah oo Baibleed oo laga isticmaalo labadaba Axdiga Hore iyo Axdiga Cusub si uu u astaaro barako, magacaabin/diris, bogsiin, iyo gudbinta awood ama hibbo ruuxi ah. Waxay ka badan tahay fal jireed oo keliya—it waxay meteshaa xidhiidh iyo gudbin ruuxi ah oo hoos imanaya awoodda Ilaah.

<!-- p117 -->
Axdiga Hore, saarka gacmaha badanaa waxaa lala xiriiriyey barako iyo uhibayn/dedicaad. Tusaale ahaan, Yacquub wuxuu ku barakeeyey awowgiis wiilashood, Afra’iim iyo Manaseh, isagoo gacmihiisa saaray (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="48" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Bilowgii 48:14</span>). Sidoo kale, Muuse wuxuu gacmihiisa saaray Yashuuca si uu u magacaabo beddelkiisa, isagoo gudbinaya awood hoggaamineed iyo xigmad (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="NUM" data-chapter="27" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tirada 27:18-23</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="DEU" data-chapter="34" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sharciga Kunoqoshada 34:9</span>). Falkaasi si cad ayuu u muujiyey doorashada iyo xoojinta Ilaah ee hawsha hore.

<!-- p118 -->
Axdiga Cusub, saarka gacmaha si dhow ayuu ugu xidhan yahay adeegga Ciise iyo kiniisaddii hore. Ciise si joogto ah ayuu gacmihiisa ugu saari jiray kuwa buka si uu u bogsiiyo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 6:5</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 4:40</span>), isagoo muujinaya naxariis iyo awood uu ka sarreeyo cudur iyo xanuun. Rasuulladu waxay sii wadeen dhaqankan, iyagoo u adeegsanaya gudbinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 8:17</span>), hibbooyinka ruuxiga ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 4:14</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 1:6</span>), iyo barakooyin loo jeediyey rumaystayaasha. Waxa kale oo ay door ku lahayd magacaabista hoggaamiyeyaasha iyo wacdiyeyaasha hawsha adeegga (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 13:3</span>).

<!-- p119 -->
Saarka gacmaha wuxuu astaan u yahay fal iimaan iyo addeecid Ereyga Ilaah. Ma aha caado farsamaysan, ee waa dhaqan ruuxi ah oo ku xidhan ku tiirsanaanta Ruuxa Quduuska ah. Tusaale ahaan, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 6:6</span>, rasuulladu waxay gacmaha saareen kuwa loo xulay inay u adeegaan sidii diikonno, iyagoo u hibaynaya adeegga Ilaah oo si cad u aqoonsanaya wicitaankooda. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 19:6</span>, Bawlos wuxuu gacmihiisa saaray rumaystayaal, wayna heleen Ruuxa Quduuska ah, taasoo muujinaysa kaalintiisa xoojinta ruuxiga ah.

Tani waxay muujinaysaa muhiimadda garasho-saaridda iyo bislaanshaha ruuxiga ah marka la magacaabayo shakhsiyaad xilal hoggaamineed. Sida aan ku barannay cutubkii kaniisadda, hoggaamiyeyaasha waa in la tijaabiyo lagana xaqiijiyo si looga fogaado kor u qaadidda rumaystayaal aan u qalmin ama weli aan qaan-gaarin kuwaas oo waxyeello u geysan kara Jirka Masiixa.

<!-- p125 -->
Tilmaamo dheeraad ah oo ku saabsan gacmo-saaridda:

<!-- p126 -->
1.  Konteeks ahaan ku habboon - qofka lagu saarayo gacmaha waa inuu yahay qof si ikhtiyaari ah u oggol.

<!-- p127 -->
2.  Jir ahaan ku habboon - gacmo-saariddu ma ku lug leedahay taabasho galmo ama kacsan. Dhaqan ahaan, in gacanta lagu saaro garabka, gacanta, ama foodda waxay guud ahaan ka mid yihiin meelaha la aqbalo (ogolaansho la haysto) dunida reer galbeedka.

<!-- p128 -->
3.  Dhaqan ahaan ku habboon - qaar ka mid ah dhaqamada, ma habboona in rag iyo dumar is taabtaan haddii aysan qaraabo dhiig ah ahayn ama is qabin.

<!-- p129 -->
Gacmo-saariddu weli waxay ka mid tahay muujiye awood leh oo iimaan iyo addeecid ah Kaniisadda maanta. Waxay u adeegtaa sidii hab barako, bogsiin, magacaabin, iyo awood-siinta, mar walbana waxay tilmaantaa awoodda Masiixa iyo shaqada Ruuxa Quduuska ah. Haddiiba lagu tukayo kuwa buka, la dirayo mishinariyiin, ama la magacaabayo hoggaamiyeyaal, waxay xusuusin u tahay in rumaystayaashu aanay ku shaqayn awooddooda ee ay hoos yimaadaan awoodda Ciise Masiix oo ay la jaanqaadaan ujeeddooyinkiisa.

Hadda oo aan barannay awoodda Ciise iyo awoodda rumaystaha, waxaannu ka hadli doonnaa makhluuqaadka kale ee dunida ruuxiga ah, malaa'igaha iyo jinnooyinka.

## Malaa'igaha iyo Jinnooyinka

<!-- p132 -->
### Malaa'igaha

<!-- p133 -->
Malaa'iguhu waa qayb muhiim ah oo ka mid ah abuurka Ilaah, iyagoo u adeegaya fariin-wadayaal iyo wakiillo fulinaya ujeeddooyinkiisa rabbaaniga ah. Kitaabka Quduuska ahi wuxuu muujinayaa meel ruuxi ah oo ay deggan yihiin makhluuqaadkan, kuwaas oo awood leh, caqli badan, oo u hoggaansan amarrada Ilaah. In kasta oo malaa'igo badan aan la arkin, haddana joogitaankooda iyo ficilladoodu waxay saameeyaan taariikhda aadanaha iyo dagaalka ruuxiga ah. Fahamka kaalintooda, kala-sarrayntooda, iyo hawlahoodu wuxuu inaga siinayaa aragti qoto dheer oo ku saabsan qorshaha Ilaah iyo xaqiiqooyinka aan la arki karin ee na hareeraysan.

<!-- p134 -->
Ereyga "malaa'ig" laftiisu waxa uu ka dhigan yahay "fariin-wade," taas oo muujinaysa hawshooda ugu weyn. Malaa'igtu waxay ku shaqeeyaan addeecidda doonista Ilaah, hadday yihiin gudbinta fariimo, bixinta ilaalin, fulinta xukun, ama ka qaybgalka dagaallo ruuxi ah. Looma caabudo iyaga, balse waxay u adeegaan tusaale addeecid iyo ixtiraam, mar walbana waxay tilmaamaan ammaanta Ilaah. Cutubkan waxa uu baadhayaa dabeecadda, kala-sarraynta, iyo doorkooda malaa'igaha, isagoo iftiiminaya muhiimmadooda Qorniinka iyo nolosheena maanta.

<!-- p135 -->
Qorniinnada gudaheeda malaa'igo waxaa lagu arkaa sida **fariin-wadayaal**, **adeegayaal**, iyo **dagaalyahanno**. Malaa'igtu marar badan waxay u dhaqmaan fariin-wadayaal, iyagoo gudbinaya ogeysiisyo muhiim ah, digniino, iyo dhiirrigelin. Waxay muujinayaan qorshayaasha Ilaah waxayna bixiyaan hanuun marka loo baahdo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26-38" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 1:26-38</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 2:13</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 1:1</span>). Malaa'iguhu si firfircoon ayay uga qaybqaataan ilaalinta, xoojinta, iyo samatabbixinta rumaystayaasha. Waxay u adeegaan dadkii Ilaah xilliyada culayska, waxayna bixiyaan gargaar ka baxsan dabiiciga (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="91" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuurradii 91:11</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7-11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 12:7-11</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 4:11</span>). Malaa'iguhu waxay ku jiraan dagaal ruuxi ah, iyagoo la dagaallamaya xoogagga sharka ee jinnada si ay hore ugu wadaan boqortooyada Ilaah oo ay u difaacaan rumaystayaasha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="DAN" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Daaniyeel 10:13</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 12:7-9</span>).

<!-- p136 -->
#### Dabeecadda Malaa'igaha

<!-- p137 -->
Malaa'igtu waa makhluuqaad la abuuray, kuwaas oo Ilaah u keenay jiritaan ka hor aasaaska dunida. Waxay ka duwan yihiin dadka, iyagoo leh sifooyin gaar ah oo u qalabeeya doorarkooda samada. Kitaabka Quduuska ahi wuxuu barayaa in malaa'igtu yihiin makhluuqaad ruuxi ah oo aan ku xiranayn xayndaabka jidheed, hase yeeshee mararka qaarkood qaadan kara muuqaal la arki karo marka ay la falgalayaan dadka.

<!-- p138 -->
Malaa'igaha waxaa lagu sifeeyaa kuwo aan dhimanayn, taas oo micnaheedu yahay inayna dhiman ama gabowin. Waa tiro aan la koobi karin, ciidammo waaweynna waxay buuxiyeen meelaha samada. Inkastoo badanaa loogu yeero magacyo lab ah, Qorniinku si cad ayuu u qeexaa in malaa'igtu yihiin kuwo aan jinsi lahayn oo aan guursan ama tarmin. Sifoyinkani waxay xoogga saaraan ujeeddadooda ah inay yihiin adeegayaal Ilaah, si buuxda ugu heellan doonistiisa iyo amarradiisa. Malaa'iguhu waxay leeyihiin dabeecado layaab leh oo ka tarjumaya ujeeddadooda rabbaaniga ah. Waa adeec badan yihiin, ixtiraam leh, xigmad leh, iyo kuwo awood leh. Sifoyinkan ayaa u qalabeeya inay si wax ku ool ah oo daacadnimo leh u fuliyaan doonista Ilaah.

(*<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 5:21</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="41" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 25:41</span>*)

#### Is-dhexgalka Bani’aadamka iyo Malaa'igaha

<!-- p148 -->
Bani’aadamka badanaa waxay kula kulmaan malaa'igaha Qorniinka, balse marnaba looma farin in aan ku amarno ama aan u tukanno. Halkii taas, malaa'iguhu waxay u dhaqmaan sida uu Ilaah u hago. Waxay u adeegaan mu’miniinta sidii **adeegayaal Ilaah** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 1:14</span>). Is-dhexgaladoodu waxa ku jira ilaalin, hanuunin, iyo badbaadin; mar walbana waxay innoo tilmaamaan awoodda iyo sarreynta Ilaah.

<!-- p149 -->
- **Digniino ka dhan ah caabudidda malaa'igaha**: Qorniinku wuxuu reebayaa caabudidda malaa'igaha ama raadinta la-kulankooda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 2:18</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="22" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 22:8-9</span>).

<!-- p150 -->
- **In Ilaah loo tukado, ee aan malaa'igaha loo tukannin**: Mu’miniintu waa inay u tukadaan Ilaah, kaas oo dira malaa'igaha sida uu doono (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="91" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuurradii 91:11</span>).

<!-- p151 -->
- **Dhiirigelin iyo Kalsooni**: Malaa'iguhu waxay inna xusuusinayaan daryeelka Ilaah, laakiin iimaankeennu waa inuu si buuxda ugu nastaa Ilaah, ma aha malaa'igaha.

<!-- p152 -->
Malaa'iguhu waa makhluuqyo yaab-gelin leh oo loo abuuray in ay u adeegaan Ilaah oo ay u adeegaan dadkiisa. In kasta oo shaqadoodu badanaa aan la arkin, Qorniinku wuxuu ina tusaa milicsiyo hawlahooda ah, isagoo muujinaya daacadnimadooda, xooggooda, iyo ixtiraamkooda. Malaa'iguhu waxay inna xusuusinayaan daryeelka iyo ilaalinta Ilaah, laakiin mar walba waxay ina ku celiyaan ammaantiisa. Annaga oo ah mu’miniin, waxaa naloo yeedhay in aan ku kalsoonaano daryeelka Ilaah, annagoo og in malaa'iguhu u shaqeeyaan sidii adeegayaashiisa si ay u fuliyaan ujeeddooyinkiisa Rabbaaniga ah.
