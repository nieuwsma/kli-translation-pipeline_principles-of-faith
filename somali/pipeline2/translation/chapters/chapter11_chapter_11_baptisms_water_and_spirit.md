# Cutubka 11 - Baabtiisyada-Biyo iyo Ruux

<!-- p1 -->
Baabtiiska xilligan casriga ah, oo laga soocay xididdada Yuhuudda, badanaa waxaa kaliya lagu fikiraa baabtiiska biyaha, iyo baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah (Pentekost).

<!-- p2 -->
Kitaabka Cibraaniyada waxaa loo qoray dhageystayaal Yuhuud ah. Tirada aadka u badan ee Masiixiyiinta xilligan casriga ah ma laha xididdo ama dhaxal Yuhuudi ah. La’aanta xididdada iyo dhaqanka Yuhuudda, in badan oo innaga mid ah ma hayno macnaha dhaqan ee waxyaabaha ay wadaagi lahaayeen Yuhuudda Masiixiga ah ee qarnigii 1aad (kuwa Yuhuudda ah ee qiraya Ciise). Maadaama aanan ku soo barbaarin dhaqanka Yuhuudda, caadiyan wax badan kama naqaan ciidaha, soonka, tukashada, maydhashooyinka, xeerarka, iyo caadooyinka ay yaqaannaan mu’miniinta Yuhuudda.

<!-- p3 -->
Ereyga Giriigga ee baabtiisyada, ama maydhashooyinka, waa: **Baptismos** (bap-tis-mos’ - **G909**) - Falka dhexgelinta ama maydhashada. Waxa uu tixraacaa maydhashooyinka xafladeed ama nadiifinta caadada. Waxa uu muujin karaa quusin, caadooyinka nadiifinta, ama maydhashooyin lagu dhaqmo duruufaha Yuhuudda iyo Masiixiyiinta.

<!-- p4 -->
Baabtiisyadu waa mid ka mid ah waxyaabaha aasaasiga ah ee lagu sheegay Cibraaniyada:

<!-- p5 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p6 -->
> **<u>Cibraaniyada 6:1-2</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Sidaas darteed, innagoo ka tegayna doodda mabaadi’da aasaasiga ah ee Masiixa, aynu u soconno sida ugu qummansan, annagoo aan mar kale dhigin aasaaska toobadda ee camalka dhintay iyo iimaanka Ilaah loo hayo,
>
> iyo waxbaridda baabtiisyada, iyo saarka gacmaha, iyo sarakicidda kuwii dhintay, iyo xukunka weligiis ah.
>

<!-- p7 -->
</span>

<!-- p8 -->
## Maydhashooyinkii Axdigii Hore - Macnaha Taariikhiga ah

<!-- p9 -->
Axdigii Hore, maydhashooyinka xafladeed waxay door dhexe ku lahaayeen ilaalinta daahirnimada caadada iyo astaynta quduusnimada hortiisa Ilaah. Maydhashooyinkan waxaa lagu faray Sharciga Muuse, waxaana lagu dabaqi jiray wadaaddadii u adeegayay Khaamka Kulanka iyo dadka reer binu Israa’iil labadaba. Tusaale ahaan, wadaaddadu waxaa looga baahnaa inay gacmaha iyo cagaha ku maydhaan haanta naxaasta ka hor intaysan gelin Khaamka ama u dhowaanin allabariga, taasoo muujinaysa diyaarintooda inay ka adeegaan joogitaanka Ilaah quduus ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="30" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Baxniintii 30:18-21</span>). Sidoo kale, caadooyinka nadiifinta qofka ka wasakhowday, sida taabashada meyd ama cudurrada maqaarka, waxay ku lug lahaayeen maydhasho biyo ah oo calaamad u ah soo celinta daahirnimada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Laawiyiintii 14:8</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="NUM" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17-19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tirintii 19:17-19</span>). Falalkan waxay ahaayeen muujiyaal dibadda ah oo ka tarjumaya rabitaan gudaha ah oo quduusnimo, iyagoo adkaynaya muhiimadda in Ilaah loo go’o.

<!-- p10 -->
Maydhashooyinka xafladeed ee Axdiga Hore waxay u baahnaayeen soo noqnoqosho joogto ah si loo ilaaliyo daahirnimada caadada. Si ka duwan xalalka joogtada ah, caadooyinkani waxay bixiyeen oo keliya nadiifin ku-meelgaar ah oo dibadda ah, iyagoo ka tagaya arrinta qoto dheer ee dembiga oo aan la xallin. Qoraha Cibraaniyada wuxuu iftiimiyaa xaddidaaddan, isaga oo sharxaya in waxyaalahan ay ahaayeen “tilmaan uun waqtigan,” oon awoodin inay nadiifiyaan damiirka caabudaha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:9-10</span>). Maydhashooyinkani waxay ahaayeen xusuusin joogto ah oo na tusaysa kaamil-la’aanta bini’aadamka iyo baahida wax ka weyn—nadiifin kama dambays ah oo nadiifin karta kaliya jidhka ma aha ee sidoo kale qalbiga iyo naftaba.

<!-- p11 -->
Maydhashooyinka xafladeed ee Axdigii Hore waxay dhammaadkoodii ka heleen Masiixa, kaas oo bixiya nadiifin buuxda oo weligeed ah iyada oo loo marayo allabariigiisa. Si ka duwan caadooyinka ku-meelgaarka ah ee sharciga, dhiigga Masiixu wuxuu si buuxda u daahiriyay, isagoo nadiifinaya kaliya jidhka ma aha ee sidoo kale damiirka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:13-14</span>). Geeridiisa iyo sarakiciddiisa dhexdiida, Ciise wuu fuliyey waxa ay caadooyinka Axdiga Hore oo keliya ka sii tuuri kareen—heshiisiin run ah oo Ilaah lala yeesho. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:22</span> waxay ku boorrisaa mu’miniinta inay “u soo dhowaadaan qalbi run ah iyo kalsooni buuxda oo iimaan, innagoo qalbiyadeenna lagu rusheeyey nadiifnimo ka timid damiir xun iyo jidhyadeenna lagu maydhay biyo nadiif ah.” Hadalkani wuxuu ku xidhaa shaqada Masiixa sawirradii maydhashooyinka Axdiga Hore, isaga oo muujinaya waxtarkeeda aad uga sarreeya. Allabarigiisu dembi ma daboolo oo kaliya, ee wuu ka qaadaa gebi ahaanba (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Yooxanaa 1:7</span>). Sidan, maydhashooyinkii xafladeed waxay u adeegayeen sidii hoos, iyagoo faraya nadiifinta qoto dheer ee keliya Masiixu bixin karo, oo buuxinaysa baahida aadanaha ee quduusnimo iyada oo faragelin Eebbe ahi jirto.

<!-- p12 -->
Si arrintan mar kale loo adkeeyo, Masiixiyiintu wax waajib ah ma saarna inay qabtaan wax maydhasho xafladeed ah. Maydhashooyinku badbaado ma siin jirin Axdigii hore, mana siiyaan Axdiga cusub. Maydhashada xafladeed ma kordhinayso meeqaamkeenna Ilaah hortiisa, mana ina kasbanayso raalli ahaansho. Bawlos si xooggan ayuu u canaantaa Yuhuudiyeyntii Galatiya (akhriso Galatiya) kuwaas oo isku dayay inay ku daraan shuruudo ka yimid duruufaha Yuhuudda oo loo arko inay lagama maarmaan u yihiin badbaadada (tusaale, gudniinka). Badbaadadu waa iimaan keliya oo Ciise ku ah!

<!-- p13 -->
## Baabtiiskii Yooxanaa

<!-- p14 -->
Qoraallada Axdiga Cusub waxaa lagu xusay dhowr baabtiis. Hore ayaan uga hadalnay baabtiisyada iyo maydhashooyinka dhaqanka Yuhuudda.

<!-- p15 -->
Hawsha Yooxanaa Baabtiisaha waxay ku wareegaysay diyaarinta jidka imaanshaha Ciise Masiix. Baabtiiskiisu, oo lagu sifeeyey baabtiis toobad keen ah oo dembiyada cafis u ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 1:4</span>), wuxuu dadka ugu yeeray inay qirtaan dembiyadooda, qalbiyadoodana u jeediyaan Ilaah, kuna diyaargaroobaan imaanshaha Masiixa. Waxay u adeegtay calaamad dibadda ah oo ka tarjumaysa toobad gudaha ah, oo astaynaysa rabitaan nadiifin ruuxi ah iyo diyaar garow boqortooyada Ilaah. Si kastaba, baabtiiska Yooxanaa wuxuu ahaa ku-meelgaar iyo diyaarin—wuxuu farta ku fiiqayay shaqada ka weyn ee Masiixu fulin doono. In kasta oo baabtiiska Yooxanaa uu diiradda saaray toobadda, ma uu bixin cafis dhamaystiran ama dib-u-dhalasho ruuxi ah, maadaama uusan lahayn deggenaanshaha iyo xoojidda Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 3:11-12</span>). Halkii taas, Yooxanaa si cad buu u sheegay in kan ka weyn isaga uu imanayo, kaas oo aan ku baabtiisin doonin biyo oo keliya balse Ruuxa Quduuska ah iyo dab (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 3:16</span>).

<!-- p16 -->
Baabtiiska Yooxanaa waxaa la dhammeeyey lana dhaafay iyada oo loo marayo geerida, aaska, iyo sarakicidda Ciise Masiix. Baabtiiska Masiixiga ah, oo uu aas-aasay Ciise oo ay ku dhaqmeen rasuulladu, wuxuu astaan u yahay xaqiiqo qoto dheer—ku midoobidda Masiixa, dhimashada dembiga, iyo sarakicidda nolol cusub (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span>). Si ka duwan baabtiiska Yooxanaa, baabtiiska Masiixiga ah si toos ah ayuu ugu xidhan yahay iimaanka allabariga kafaaraggudka ee Ciise iyo deggenaanshaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="38" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:38</span>). Imaanshaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee Pentekost wuxuu calaamadeeyey Axdiga cusub iyo bilowga nolol Ruux-ku-xoojin ah, taas oo baabtiiska Yooxanaa uusan bixin karin. Sababtaas awgeed, baabtiiska Yooxanaa mar dambe lama dhaqmo, waayo waxaa beddelay baabtiiska Masiixu amray (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:19</span>), kaas oo ku daraya toobad, iimaan, iyo shaabadeynta mu’miniinta ee Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:13-14</span>).

<!-- p17 -->
## Baabtiiska Masiixa

<!-- p18 -->
Baabtiiska Masiixu wuxuu dhigay tusaale xoog leh oo ku aaddan raacayaashiisa, wuxuuna dhisay aasaaska dhaqanka Masiixiga ee baabtiiska biyaha. Inkasta oo Ciise dembi-la’aan ahaa, haddana wuxuu isu hoos dhigay in Yooxanaa ku baabtiiso si uu “u wada buuxiyo xaqnimada oo dhan” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 3:15</span>). Sidaas isagoo samaynaya, wuxuu isu aqoonsaday baahida aadanaha ee toobad, wuxuuna farta ku fiiqay geeridiisa, aaskiisa, iyo sarakiciddiisa. Baabtiiskiisa markuu dhacay, Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuxuu u soo degay sida qoolley, Aabbuhuna wuxuu ku dhawaaqay, “Kanu waa Wiilkaygii aan jeclahay, kaasaan aad uga raalli ahay” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 3:16-17</span>). Xilligan ayaa calaamadeeyey bilowga adeegga dadweyne ee Ciise, wuxuuna muujiyey midnimada Aabbaha, Wiilka, iyo Ruuxa, isagoo hore u saadaaliyey isbeddelka ruuxiga ah ee ay mu’miniintu ku waayo-aragsan doonaan iimaanka Masiixa dhexdiisa.

<!-- p19 -->
Ciise dabadeed wuxuu amray raacayaashiisa in la baabtiiso, isaga oo ku diraya inay “tagaan oo quruumaha oo dhan ka yeeshaan xerti, iyaga oo ku baabtiisaya magaca Aabbaha, iyo Kan Wiilka ah, iyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:19</span>). Baabtiiska biyaha, sida uu Masiixu u aas-aasay, wuxuu noqday **fal adeecid** iyo **caddayn dadweyne oo iimaan** oo loogu talagalay mu’miniinta. Waxay meteshaa in ay isku aqoonsadaan geeridii, aaskii, iyo sarakiciddii Ciise (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span>). Biyaha hoostooda loo galo waxay astaan u tahay in dembiga loo dhinto, kasoo bixitaankuna wuxuu sawirayaa ku kicidda nolol cusub oo ku jirta Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 2:12</span>). Si kastaba ha ahaatee, waxaa lama huraan ah in la caddeeyo in ficilkan dibadda ahi uusan ahayn isha badbaadada ee uu yahay **calaamad muuqata** oo ka mid ah **shaqada nimco ee gudaha** oo horeyba dhiigga Ciise u fuliyey.

<!-- p20 -->
Baabtiiska biyaha waa **calaamad** muujinaysa xoogga nadiifinta ee dhiigga Masiixa iyo cusboonaysiinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 5:26</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tiitos 3:5</span>). Waxay ka dhigan tahay toobad, iimaan, iyo ballanqaad in la raaco Ciise. Waa marag-fur dibedda ah oo ku saabsan isbeddelka gudaha- hab ay rumaystayaashu si cad ugu dhawaaqaan daacadnimadooda Masiixa iyo ka-qaybgalka jidhka Masiixa, Kaniisadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="41" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:41</span>). In kasta oo uu Ciise amray oo lagu dayday Kaniisaddii hore, haddana ma **badbaadiyo** qof. Badbaadadu waa nimco oo keliya oo iimaan lagu maro (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:8-9</span>), nadiifinta dhabtana waxay ruux ahaan ku dhacdaa dhiigga Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 1:5</span>). Baabtiiska biyuhu waa jawaab ka soo baxda badbaadadaas, waa fal adeecid ah ee ma aha shardi u ah.

<!-- p21 -->
Axdiga Cusub, baabtiiska biyuhu si joogto ah ayuu ugu xiran yahay **toobad iyo iimaan** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="38" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:38</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 16:16</span>), kuwaas oo labaduba u baahan go’aan shakhsi ah in la raaco Masiix—wax aanu dhallaanku awoodin inay sameeyaan. Baabtiisku wuxuu astaan ka dhigayaa aqoonsiga ula kac ah ee rumaystaha la leeyahay dhimashadii, aaskiisii, iyo sarakicidda Masiixa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span>), iyo sidoo kale ballanqaadkooda in ay ku noolaadaan addeeciddiisa. Qaar ka mid ah kooxaha (tusaale, Katooligga, Lutheran) waxay dhaqmaan baabtiiska dhallaanka iyagoo u arka calaamad axdi oo la mid ah gudniinka, hase yeeshee maadaama dhallaanku aanu muujin karin iimaan shakhsi ah ama toobad, dhaqanka **baabtiiska dhallaanka** kuma waafaqsana qaabka Baybalka ee **baabtiiska rumaystaha**. Halkii taas, kaniisado badan waxay sameeyaan **xaflado u-hibayn** carruurta, iyaga oo raacaya tusaalaha Xana oo u hibeeyay Samuu’eel Rabbiga (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1SA" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27-28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Samuu’eel 1:27-28</span>) iyo Maryan iyo Yuusuf oo soo bandhigay Ciise macbudka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 2:22</span>). U-hibayntu ma aha beddelka baabtiiska ee waa ballanqaad ay waalidku ku koriyaan ilmahooda iimaanka, iyaga oo baraya Erayga Ilaah, una tusaalaya nolol Eebbe ka cabsasho leh ilaa ilmuhu ka weynaado si uu go’aan isaga u gaar ah uga gaadho raacista Masiix iyo in la baabtiiso.

<!-- p22 -->
Si ka duwan **dhaqiddooyinka xafladeed** ee Axdigii Hore, kuwaas oo u baahnaa caadooyin soo noqnoqda si loo nadiifiyo, baabtiiska Masiixigu waa dhacdo mar-kaliya ah oo ka tarjumaysa shaqo nimco oo dhammaatay. Dhaqiddooyinkii Axdigii Hore waxay astaan u ahaayeen nadiifin dibedda ah waxayna hormood u ahaayeen nadiifinta ruuxiga ah ee qoto dheer ee ka timaadda Masiix. Dhinaca kale, baabtiiska Axdiga Cusub wuxuu astaan ka dhigayaa cusboonaysiinta gudaha iyo dhalashada labaad oo uu fuliyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tiitos 3:5</span>). Waxay tilmaamaysaa ka-qaybgalka rumaystaha ee axdiga cusub, halkaas oo nadiifinta dembigu ay joogto tahay oo ku dhisan shaqada Masiix ee dhammaystiran.

<!-- p23 -->
Baabtiiska biyaha si dhow ayuu ugu xiran yahay **ballanka Ruuxa Quduuska ah**. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="38" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:38</span>, Butros wuxuu dadka ugu yeedhay inay toobad keenaan, la baabtiisaan, oo helaan hadiyadda Ruuxa Quduuska ah. In kasta oo baabtiiska biyuhu si dadweyne u muujiyo toobadda iyo iimaanka rumaystaha, baabtiiska dhabta ahi wuxuu ka dhacaa gudaha markuu Ruuxa Quduuska ahi maydho, dib u dhalo, oo shaabeeyo rumaystaha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:13</span>). Maydhashadan ruuxiga ahi waxay nagu yeelataa xubno ka mid ah jidhka Masiixa waxayna nagu xoojisaa inaan ku noolaanno nolol quduus ah. **Doorka Ruuxa Quduuska ah** ee baabtiiska wuxuu innoo xusuusinayaa in badbaadadu aan lagu helin caadooyin ee lagu helo awoodda beddelka leh ee Ilaah.

<!-- p24 -->
Waa muhiim in la kala saaro **falka calaamadaysan ee baabtiiska biyaha** iyo xaqiiqada ruuxiga ah ee badbaadada iyada oo loo marayo dhiigga Masiixa. Baybalku si cad ayuu u barayaa in badbaadadu ku salaysan tahay iimaanka Ciise (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 10:9-10</span>) ee aan ku shuqul, oo ay ku jirto baabtiiska (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:8-9</span>). Si kastaba ha ahaatee, baabtiiska biyuhu waa **fal adeecid** oo si dadweyne ugu aqoonsada rumaystaha Masiix iyo Kaniisadda. Wuxuu tusayaa ballanqaad dibedda ah in lagu noolaado sidii xertiisa Ciise, laakiin kuma darao mana dhammaystiro shaqada badbaadada.

<!-- p25 -->
Baabtiiska biyaha ee Masiixu wuxuu tusaale u dejiyey rumaystayaasha inay raacaan, isagoo muujiyey is-hoosaysiin, adeecid, iyo u-diyaargarow adeeg. Masiixiyiinta maanta, baabtiiska biyuhu wuxuu u adeegsadaa ku dhawaaqid dadweyne ee iimaanka, isagoo astaan u ah dhimasho dembiga iyo sarakicid nolol cusub. In kasta oo uu yahay amarka Ciise iyo tallaabo muhiim ah oo adeecid ah, haddana shardi uma aha badbaadada. Nadiifinta dhabta ahi waxay ku timaaddaa iimaanka dhiigga Ciise, xoogga lagu noolaado nolol Masiixi ahna wuxuu ka yimaadaa shaqada Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee rumaystaha. Baabtiisku wuxuu nagu mideeyaa Masiix iyo Kaniisaddiisa, isagoo innoo xusuusinaya isbeddelka gudaha iyo cusboonaysiinta uu Ilaah hore u qabtay.

<!-- p26 -->
Maaddaama aan u caddeynay baabtiiska biyaha inuu yahay calaamad dibedda ah oo iimaan, hadda waxaynu u leexanaynaa baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah, kaas oo xoojiya rumaystayaasha adeeg iyo u-khidmad.

<!-- p27 -->
## Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah

<!-- p28 -->
Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa waayo-aragnimo qoto dheer oo beddelaysa oo uu Ilaah ballan qaaday si uu u xoojiyo rumaystayaasha adeeg, u-khidmad, iyo nolol quduus ah. Rumayste kastaa wuxuu helaa Ruuxa Quduuska ah marka uu mar labaad dhasho—taasi waa sida aan ruux ahaan u noolaano. Laakiin dabadeed, waa in aynu sidoo kale helnaa baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah, kaas oo ina xoojiya geesinimo, hadiyado ruuxi ah, iyo adeeg wax ku ool ah.

<!-- p29 -->
Baabtiiska biyuhu waa ku dhawaaqid dadweyne ee iimaanka oo astaan ka dhigiya nadiifinta dembiga iyada oo loo marayo dhiigga Masiixa, baabtiiska Ruuxuna wuxuu matalaa joogitaanka xoojinaya ee Ilaah ee nolosha rumaystaha. Wada jir, labada hab ee baabtiisku waxay sameeyaan dhinacyo is-dhammeeya oo socodka Masiixiga- mid calaamad dibedda ah oo adeecid ah iyo kan kale isha gudaha ee awood iyo cusboonaysiin. Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ahi waa Ruuxu inuu inaga KORKA ahaado halkii uu naga dhex ahaan lahaa. Markii la badbaado rumaystayaal oo dhammu waxay helaan Ruuxa gudaha kaga jira, laakiin marka Ruuxa Ilaah korkooda yimaado, waxay helaan xoog iyo geesinimo adeeg.

<!-- p30 -->
Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah waxaa sii sheegay Yooxanaa Baabtiisaha, kaas oo yidhi,

<!-- p31 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p32 -->
> **<u>Matayos 3:11</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Anigu runtii waxaan idinku baabtiisaa biyo toobadkeen, laakiin Kan iga dambeeya waa iga ka xoog badan, kabihiisana uma qalmo inaan qaado. Isagu idin baabtiisin doona Ruuxa Quduuska ah iyo dab.
>

<!-- p33 -->
</span>

<!-- p34 -->
Ballankan wuxuu tilmaamay shaqo ka weyn oo Ciise samayn doono ka dib sarakiciddiisa- dirista Ruuxa Quduuska ah si uu ugu noolaado gudaha oo u xoojiyo raacayaashiisa. Ciise tan buu xaqiijiyey markuu xertiisa ku amray inay sugaan hadiyadda Ruuxa, isagoo sharraxaya inay heli doonaan awood ay markhaatiyadiisa u noqdaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span>). Rumaystayaashu waxay heleen rumoobidda ballankan maalintii Bentekost, markay ka buuxsamaan Ruuxa Quduuska ah, taas oo lagu caddeeyey ku hadalka afaf, wax sii sheegid, iyo geesinimo ay ku ku dhawaaqaan injiilka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:1-4</span>).

<!-- p35 -->
Daadintani waxay calaamad u noqotay bilowgii **Xilligii Kaniisadda** waxayna muujisay in awoodda Ruuxu hadda u furan tahay rumaystayaasha oo dhan. Ballankani kuma koobna rasuullada oo keliya ee wuxuu ku fidsan yahay dhammaan kuwa rumaysta, sida Butros ku dhawaaqay, *“Ballanku waa idinka iyo carruurtiinna iyo dhammaan kuwa fog-fog-dhammaan kuwa Rabbigeenna Ilaah uu waco”* (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="39" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:39</span>).

### Ruuxa Gudaha vs Ruuxa Korka

<!-- p37 -->
Marka qofu iimaankiisa ku aaddiyo Ciise, Ruuxa Quduuska ah ayaa siinaya nolol cusub gudaha. Taasi waa waxa loola jeedo mar labaad in la dhasho (**dib u dhalasho**). Xilligaas iimaan, Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuxuu degaa **GUDAHA** rumaystaha, isaga oo ka dhiga ruux ahaan nool oo ku korsada qoyska Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:5-6</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tiitos 3:5</span>). Tani waa dhalashada cusub ama in mar labaad la dhasho, sida Ciise ku sharraxay Yooxanaa 3.

<!-- p38 -->
Aasaaska Qorniinka:

<!-- p39 -->
- *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tiitos 3:5</span>* - “Wuxuu ina badbaadiyey iyada oo maydhashada dhalashada cusub iyo cusboonaysiinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah.”

<!-- p40 -->
- *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:13-14</span>* - Ruuxa Quduuska ah wuxuu ku shaabeeyaa rumaystayaasha sida **dammaanad** dhaxalkooda.

<!-- p41 -->
- *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:9</span>* - “Qof aan lahayn Ruuxa Masiixa isaga ma leh.”

<!-- p42 -->
Shaqadan dhalashada cusub waa mid guud u ah rumaystayaasha oo dhan marka la badbaado, waxayna aasaastaa ku-dhex-degitaan joogto ah oo Ruuxa ah. Kuma xirna wax ficil dibadeed ah, sida baabtiiska biyaha, waxayna dammaanad qaaddaa badbaadada nimco ahaan iyada oo iimaan.

<!-- p43 -->
Dhanka kale, baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa waayo-aragnimo gooni ah oo xigta oo ku qalabeysa rumaystayaasha awood adeeg iyo markhaatifurnaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span>). Halka dhalashada cusub ay ka hadasho nolol iyo aqoonsi cusub, baabtiiska Ruuxu wuxuu diiradda saaraa xoog-siinta **DUSHA** iyo sii-deynta hadiyadaha ruuxiga ah. Baabtiiskan badanaa waxaa la socda calaamooyin iyo muujiyaal ka mid ah hadiyadaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

## Sida Loo Helo Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah

<!-- p52 -->
Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah waxaa Qorniinka lagu sharraxaa inuu yahay hadiyad loo ballanqaaday rumaystayaasha oo lagu helo iimaan ahaan. Waxay ka gooni tahay badbaadada, waxaana ugu horrayn loogu talagalay xoog-siinta iyo markhaatifurnaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span>). Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa hadiyad xoog-siineed oo loogu talagalay rumaystayaasha, oo Ciise ballanqaaday laguna rumoobay Bentekoste. Waxa lagu helaa toobad, iimaan, iyo waydiisashada Ilaah. Inkastoo saarista gacmuhu inta badan tahay hab la adeegsado, haddana ugu dambayn Ilaah baa ah kan ku baabtiiya Ruuxa.

<!-- p53 -->
Qorniinku wuxuu ku hadalka afaf iyo waxsii sheegidda u soo bandhigaa calaamad caadi ah, laakiin ujeeddada baabtiiskan aad uga weyntahay-bixinta geesinimo, awood, iyo hadiyado ruuxi ah oo loogu adeegayo. Rumayste kasta waxaa lagu dhiirrigelinayaa inuu raadiyo waayo-aragnimadan qayb ka mid ah socdaalkiisa ruuxi ah, isagoo ku kalsoon in Ilaah uu u qalabeyn doono shaqadiisa.

<!-- p95 -->
Bawlos wuxuu adkeeyay in hadiyadaha ruuxiga ahi lagu dhaqmo si edeb iyo nidaam leh (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:40</span>). Tani waxay xaqiijisaa in isticmaalka hadiyaduhu kor u qaadaan midnimo, nabad, iyo faham, halkii ay ka abuuri lahaayeen jahawareer ama kala-qaybsanaan.

<!-- p96 -->
- Waxsii-sheegyada waa in la qiimeeyaa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:29</span>).

<!-- p97 -->
- Marka farriin carrabyo ku jirta loo gudbinayo jirka, ku tukada in la fasirro (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27-28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:27-28</span>).

<!-- p98 -->
- Hadiyaduhu mar walba waa in ay keenaan **dhisid** kaniisadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:12</span>).

<!-- p99 -->
Nidaamkani Ruuxa ma damiyo ee waxa uu hubiyaa in hadiyaduhu u shaqeeyaan siyaabo **Ilaah lagu maamuuso** oo **kuwa kale lagu anfacayo**.

<!-- p100 -->
Hawlgalka hadiyadaha ruuxiga ah waa in mar walba ku salaysnaadaa jacayl. Bawlos wuxuu u hibeeyey cutub dhan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 13</span>) si uu u sharaxo in jacayl la’aantiis, xataa hadiyadaha ruuxiga ah ee ugu waaweyn ay macno beelayaan. Jacaylku wuxuu bixiyaa dhiirrigelinta iyo qaab-dhismeedka lagu adeegsado hadiyadaha si ay dadka u dhisaan halkii ay u dumin lahaayeen.

<!-- p101 -->
Intaa waxaa dheer, hadiyaduhu waxay u shaqeeyaan **sida Ruuxu doono** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:11</span>). Lama soo saari karo ama lama maamuli karo dadaal bini’aadan- waxa si madaxbannaan loo bixiyaa loona awoodsiiyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah, badanaa xilliyo baahi ama adeeg. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 13:8</span> wuxuu leeyahay in ugu dambayn nebiyayn iyo afafku joogsadaan. Si cilmi-diineed ahaan, dadka aaminsan in hadiyadaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah ay joogsadeen waxaa loo yaqaannaa ‘Cessationists’. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 13:10</span> wuxuu leeyahay “markuu kaamilka \[Ciise\] yimaado, waxa kaas oo qayb ahaan ah waa la joojin doonaa”. Maaddaama Ciise uusan weli u soo laaban kiniisaddiisa, hadiyaduhuna weli way shaqaynayaan dhulka.

<!-- p102 -->
Aad bay muhiim u tahay in la garto in **hadiyadaha Ruuxa** aan la kala saarin **midhaha Ruuxa** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22-23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 5:22-23</span>). Inkastoo hadiyaduhu muujiyaan awood, midhahuna waxay muujinayaan dabeecad—jacayl, farxad, nabad, dulqaad, naxariis, wanaag, aaminnimo, khushuuc, iyo isxakameyn.

<!-- p103 -->
- **Hadiyado aan Midh lahayn**: Mu’min wuxuu ku shaqayn karaa hadiyado weliba uusan lahayn bisayl ama dabeecad, taas oo horseedi karta kibir, si-xun-u-adeegsad, ama waxyeello.

<!-- p104 -->
- **Midh aan Hadiyado lahayn**: Mu’min waxa laga yaabaa inuu leeyahay dabeecad Eebbe raalli ka yahay balse ka maqnaan karto awood-siinta si wax-ku-ool ah loogu adeego.

<!-- p105 -->
Labaduba waa loo baahan yahay. Hadiyaduhu waxay qalabeeyaan mu’miniinta adeegga. Midhuhu waa calaamad caafimaadkooda ruuxi ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 7:16</span>) waxayna siiyaan ‘dhadhan macaan’ kuwii ka cuna.

<!-- p106 -->
### Sharaxaadda 9-ka Hadiyadood ee Muujinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah

<!-- p107 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:8-10</span> waxa uu taxayaa 9-ka hadiyadood ee muujinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **Erayga Xikmadda** - Aragti ka baxsan dabiiciga oo ku saabsan ujeeddooyinka Ilaah ama jihada xaalad. Waxay ku dabaqdaa xikmad rabbaani ah dhibaatooyin ama go’aammo adag (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 6:10</span>).

<!-- p109 -->
2.  **Erayga Aqoonta** - Muujin ka baxsan dabiiciga oo xog ka siinaysa qof, dhacdo, ama duruufo aan si dabiici ah lagu ogaan karin (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 4:17-18</span>).

<!-- p110 -->
3.  **Iimaan** - Awood ka baxsan dabiiciga oo lagu kalsoonaado Ilaah mucjisooyin xataa xaalado aan suurto gal ahayn. Hadiyaddan waxay dhiirrigelisaa geesinimo iyo kalsooni (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 3:6-7</span>).

<!-- p111 -->
4.  **Hadiyadaha Bogsiinta** - Awood ka baxsan dabiiciga oo lagu bogsiiyo cudurro jireed, shucuur, ama ruuxi ah lana soo celiyo dhammeystir (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 3:6-8</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 9:17-18</span>).

<!-- p112 -->
5.  **Falka Mucjisooyinka** - Awoodda lagu sameeyo falal ka baxsan dabiiciga oo ka hor imanaya xeerarka dabiiciga, sida badinta cunto ama soo nooleynta kuwii dhintay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="40-41" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 9:40-41</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 2:9-11</span>).

<!-- p113 -->
6.  **Nebiyayn** - Ku dhawaaqista runta ama doonista Ilaah iyada oo Ruuxa Quduuska ahi kugu dhiirrigeliyo. Waa in ay noqotaa dhiirrigelin, dhisid, iyo tasalli (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:3</span>).

<!-- p114 -->
7.  **Kala-soocidda (Garta) Ruuxyada** - Awoodda lagu garto in saameyn ruuxi ah ay ka timid Ilaah, cadowga, ama asalka bini’aadam (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 16:16-18</span>).

<!-- p115 -->
8.  **Noocyo Kala Duwan oo Afaf ah** - Ku hadlid luqado aan hadlaha la aqoon, badanaa sidii cibaado, salaad, ama calaamad loogu talagalay kuwa aan rumaysnayn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:4</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:22</span>).

<!-- p116 -->
9.  **Fasirka Afafka** - Awood ka baxsan dabiiciga oo lagu fasiro farriinta afafka lagu hadlay si kiniisaddu u dhisanto (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:27</span>).

<!-- p117 -->
Bawlos si cad ayuu u muujiyey in dhammaan hadiyaduhu qiimo leeyihiin oo ay tahay inay si wadajir ah ugu shaqeeyaan danta guud ee kiniisadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:7</span>). Si kastaba ha ahaatee, sidoo kale wuxuu ku dhiirrigeliyaa mu’miniinta inay damcaan hadiyadaha ka weyn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="31" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:31</span>). Hadiyadaha waaweyn badanaa waxaa loo fasiraa kuwa si toos ah ugu dhisa kiniisadda, sida nebiyayn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:1-5</span>).

<!-- p118 -->
- Nebiyayn si gaar ah ayaa loo adkeeyey maxaa yeelay waxay xoojisaa, dhiirrigelisaa, oo tasallisaa dadka kale (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:3</span>). Bawlos wuxuu farayaa mu’miniinta inay si ardaysan u damcaan hadiyaddan maadaama ay kor u qaaddo caddayn iyo faham gudaha kiniisadda.

<!-- p119 -->
- Hadiyado sida afafka ayaa si weyn loogu qiimeeyaa dhisidda shakhsiga iyo salaadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:4</span>), laakiin Bawlos wuxuu baraa in marka fariin afaf lagu siiyo Kiniisadda la baryo fasirkeeda si loo hubiyo inay kiniisadda dhisaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27-28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:27-28</span>).

<!-- p120 -->
In kasta oo Ruuxa Quduuska ahi bixiyo hadiyadaha, annaga ayaa weli inaga doonan in aan isu dhiibno oo u hoggaansanno Ruuxa Quduuska si uu u adeegsado. Sida murqaha iyo hibada, marka aynu ku tababarto u hoggaansamidda Ruuxa Quduuska si uu u adeegsado hadiyadihiisa, waxaan noqonnaa kuwo kalsooni badan oo lagu hallayn karo adeegsigooda.

## Su’aalo Milicsi Shakhsiyeed

<!-- p122 -->
1.  Sidee buu u beddelmay fahamkaaga baabtiiska ka dib akhriska cutubkan? Maxay muhiimad ah ayuu u leeyahay safarkaaga ruuxiga ah?

<!-- p123 -->
2.  Dhaqitaannadii Axdigii Hore waxay tilmaamayeen baahida nadiifin ugu dambaysa. Sidee fahamkani u sii qoto-dheereeyaa qadarrintaada shaqada Masiixa?

<!-- p124 -->
3.  Ka milicsi waayo-aragnimadaada baabtiiska biyaha ama filashadiisa. Sidee ficilkani u astaan-qaadaa aqoonsigaaga la mid noqoshada dhimashada, aasitaankiisa, iyo sarakiciddiisa Masiixa?

<!-- p125 -->
4.  Maxay adiga shaqsi ahaan kaaga dhigan tahay baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska? Sidee baad u soo martay ama u raadisay awood-siintiisa noloshaada?

<!-- p126 -->
5.  Sidee awood-siinta Ruuxa Quduuska ahi kaaga caawin kartaa inaad ku noolaato wicitaankaaga oo aad u adeegto dadka kale si wax-ku-ool ah?

<!-- p128 -->
1.  **Su’aal Fur-furitaan:** “Waa kuwee adeeggii baabtiis ee ugu xusuusta badnaa ee aad mar aragtay ama aad la kulantay? Maxay u lahayd micne?”

<!-- p129 -->
2.  **Baadhista Dhaqidda Axdiyada Hore iyo Cusub:** Maxay dhaqidaha caadada ee Axdigii Hore ina barayaan quduusnimada iyo baahideenna nadiifin? Sidee buu Ciise u buuxiyaa dhaqammadan?

<!-- p130 -->
3.  **Baabtiiska Biyaha:** Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span>. Sidee buu baabtiiska biyuhu u astaan-qaadaa midowga rumaystaha la leh Masiixa dhimashadiisa iyo sarakiciddiisa?

<!-- p131 -->
4.  **Baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska:** Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span> iyo 2:4. Waa maxay ujeeddada baabtiiska Ruuxa Quduuska, sideese uu u awood-siiyo rumaystayaasha adeegga?

<!-- p132 -->
5.  **Ku-dhaqanka Wax-ku-oolka ah:** Sideen, annagoo koox ama shakhsiyaad ah, ugu dhiirrigelin oo u garab istaagi karnaa kuwa kale inay qaadaan tallaabooyin iimaan sida baabtiiska ama raadinta awood-siinta Ruuxa?
