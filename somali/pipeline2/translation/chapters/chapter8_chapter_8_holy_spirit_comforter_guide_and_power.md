# Cutubka 8 - Ruuxa Quduuska ah-Raalligeliye, Hage, iyo Awood

<!-- p1 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ah ma aha ruux cusub ee waa ka Ilaah ah, wuxuuna Ilaah la jiray tan iyo bilowgii. In <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Bilowgii 1:2</span> Ruuxa Ilaah ayaa dul wareegaya biyaha intii la abuurayay. In <span class="x-scripture" data-book="NEH" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Nexemyaah 9:20</span>, Qorniinku wuxuu sheegay in Ruuxu ahaa kii dadka ku baray lamadegaanka. Isla Ruuxa Quduuska ah ayay ahayd in Ilaah ku subkay boqorradii, garsoorayaashii, iyo nebiyadii Axdiga Hore. In <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JOL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28-29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yo'eel 2:28-29</span> waxaa lagu ballanqaaday in Ruuxu lagu daadin doono dadka oo dhan. Isla Ruuxa Quduuska ah ayaa Ilaah ku sarakiciyey Ciise kuwii dhintay dhexdiisa, imminkana nagu dhex deggan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:11</span>). Hal Ruux oo Quduus ah oo keliya ayaa jira, isaguna weligiis isku mid buu ahaa. Sida uu ula shaqeeyo dadka mararka qaar way kala duwanaatay, laakiin qalbigiisu marnaba isma beddelin.

<!-- p2 -->
Ciise wuxuu raggii xertiisa ahaa, iyo innaga oo dhanba, siiyey ballanqaadka xooggan ee Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p3 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="16-18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p4 -->
> **<u>Yooxanaa 14:16-18</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Anigu Aabbaha ayaan baryi doonaa, oo wuu idin siin doonaa Gargaare kale, si uu weligiin idiinla joogo—
>
> Ruuxa runta, kaas oo dunidu aanay qaadan karin, waayo, iyadu ma aragto mana taqaan; laakiin idinku waad taqaaniin, waayo, wuu idinla jooga, wuuna idinku jiri doonaa.
>
> Anigu idiin kama tegi doono agoomeyaal ahaan; idiin ayaan u imaan doonaa.
>

<!-- p5 -->
</span>

<!-- p6 -->
Intii Ciise dhulka ku socday isagoo jidh jireed ku jira, wuu ku xaddidnaa sidii annagaba, jidhkiisuna wuxuu u baahnaa isla waxyaalaha ay jidhkeennu u baahan yihiin: nasasho, biyo, cunto, hurdo, iwm. Xaddidaaddan saamayn bay ku lahayd ma aha oo keliya Ciise, balse waxay saamayn ku lahayd kuwa raacay, maadaama uu hal mar meel keliya joogi karay. Marar badan intuu Ciise safar ku jiray, dad fara badan ayaa ku soo cadaadiyi jiray, waxayna isku dayi jireen inay u qayliyaan oo ay taabtaan. Ciise badanaa wuxuu u baahnaa inuu waqti la qaato Aabbihiisa jannada si uu u nasto oo u tukado.

<!-- p7 -->
Si shaqada Aabbaha loo badiyo, Ciise wuxuu u baahnaa inuu koryeelayo xerta, oo ay iyaguna shaqadan sii wadaan. Ciise wuu ogaa in qayb aad u muhiim ah oo adeeggiisa dhulka ku saabsan ay ku tiirsanayd inuu la xiriiro oo la joogo Aabbihiis. Runtii, Ciise wuxuu yidhi inuusan waxba qaban karin haddii aan Aabbihiisu isaga dhex mariin oo u tusin inuu sameeyo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 5:19</span>). Ilaah xigmaddiisa ku jirta, Ruuxiisii Quduuska ahaa ayuu inoogu soo diray inuu nagu dhex dego, ina hoggaamiyo oo ina hago, ina awood siiyana inaan qabanno shaqada Aabbaha.

<!-- p8 -->
## Goorma ayaa Ruuxa Quduuska ah loo diray rumaystayaasha oo dhan?

<!-- p9 -->
Ciise wuxuu yidhi:

<!-- p10 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p11 -->
> **<u>Yooxanaa 16:7</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Si kastaba ha ahaatee, runta ayaan idin sheegayaa. Idiin bay idiin faa’iido badan tahay inaan tago; waayo, haddii aanan tagin, Gargaarihuna idiin iman maayo; laakiin haddii aan tago, waxaan u soo diri doonaa idiin.
>

<!-- p12 -->
</span>

<!-- p13 -->
Ka dib markii Ciise la iskutubay, dhimashadiisu waxay suurtogal ka dhigtay in Ruuxa Ilaah nagu dhex dego. Ilaa waqtigaas, joogitaankii muuqday ee Ruuxa Ilaah badanaa wuxuu ka dambeeyey daah qaro weyn oo macbudka ku jiray. Marba mar, dad gaar ah ayaa Ilaah doortay oo la subkay (lagu quduusiyey oo loo kala soocay adeeg gaar ah), waxayna heleen Ruuxa Quduuska ah korkooda. Subkidda Ilaah iyo Ruuxu korkooda, waa wax gaar ah, ku-meel-gaar ah, oo shuruudo leh. Daa’uud wuxuu tukaday ka dib dembigiisii Batsheba ‘Ha iga qaadin Ruuxaaga Quduuska ah!’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="51" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuurradii 51:11</span>), waayo Daa’uud wuxuu arkay Ruuxii oo ka tegey Boqor Sawuul falalkiisii caasinnimo darteed.

<!-- p14 -->
Annagu (Aadanaha) ma ku heli karayn Ruuxa Quduuska ah gudaha nafteena dabeecaddeenna dembiga leh awgeed, laakiin markii Ciise arrinta dembiga ku xalliyey allabariigiisa, waxay ka dhigtay jid ay rumaysadku ku suurtoobi karto inaan noqono carruur iyo dhaxal sugayaal Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:17</span>).

<!-- p15 -->
Yooxanaa 20, ka dib sarakiciddiisa, Ciise wuxuu u muuqdaa xertiisii bartamaha qol qufulan, wuuna ku neefsadaa iyagoo leh ‘Qaata Ruuxa Quduuska ah’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 20:22</span>). Cilmiga diinta waxoogaa ayaa ka doodda waxa si dhab ah xilligaas dhacay, laakiin fasiraadaha inta badan ee Bentekostigu waxay u arkaan tan in xertii ay ku heleen Ruuxa Ilaah gudaha iyaga, taasoo astaan u ah dhalasho cusub, in mar labaad la dhasho. Tani se ma ahayn waqtigii ay heleen Ruuxa Ilaah korkooda, oo ku xoojiya geesinimo adeegga. Tani waxay ahayd markii ay ku heleen Ruuxa Quduuska ah gudaha iyaga, taasoo u saamaxday inay la joogaan oo la xiriiraan Ilaah. Tan waxa si qoto dheer looga hadli doonaa goor dambe cutubkan iyo sidoo kale cutubyada ku saabsan quduusaynta iyo baabtiiska.

<!-- p16 -->
Markii dambe, dhammaadka muddadii 40-ka maalmood ee u dhexeysay sarakicidda iyo kormaridda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:3</span>), Ciise wuxuu bixiyey ballanka Ruuxa Quduuska ah:

<!-- p17 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="4-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p18 -->
> **<u>Falimaha Rasuullada 1:4-9</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Oo markay isaga la isu yimaadeen ayuu ku amray inayan ka tagin Yeruusaalem, laakiin ay sugaan Ballankii Aabbaha, "kaas oo," ayuu yidhi, "aad iga maqlayseen;
>
> waayo Yooxan run ahaantii biyaha ayuu dadka ku baabtiisay, laakiin waxaa laydiin ku baabtiisi doonaa Ruuxa Quduuska ah maalmo yar gudahood."
>
> Haddaba, markay isu yimaadeen, ayay weyddiiyeen iyagoo leh, "Rabbiyow, ma wakhtigan ayaad boqortooyada u soo celinaysaa Israa'iil?"
>
> Markaasuu iyaga ku yidhi, "Idinku ma aha inaad ogaataan waqtiyada ama xilliyada uu Aabbuhu amar ku dhigay xukunkiisa.
>
> Laakiin waxaad heli doontaan awood marka Ruuxa Quduuska ah idin kor yimaado; oo waxaad iigu noqon doontaan markhaatiyaal Yeruusaalem, iyo dhulka oo dhan ee Yahuuda iyo Samaariya, iyo ilaa dhammaadka dhulka."
>
> Oo markuu waxyaalahan yidhi, intay fiirinayeen, waa kor loo qaaday, daruur ayaana ka qaaday hortooda.
>

<!-- p19 -->
</span>

<!-- p20 -->
Xerti waxaa lagu amray inay sugaan ballankii Ruuxa ee la shubi lahaa, taas oo ahayd in ay ku awood yeeshaan inay noqdaan markhaatiyaal ilaa cidhifyada dhulka. Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa qayb muhiim ah oo ka mid ah qorshaha Ilaah ee aadanaha, waana lama huraan noo ah annaga oo rumaystayaal ah, si aan u dhammaystirno hawlgelkeenna, iyo sidoo kale adduunka.

<!-- p21 -->
## Barashada Ruuxa Quduuska ah

<!-- p22 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ah, ugu horrayn, wuxuu ka yimid Ilaah, waana Ilaah. Qorniinku wuxuu Ruuxa ugu yeeraa Ruuxa Ilaah, ama Ruuxa Rabbiga, ama Ruuxa Masiixa; in kasta oo kuwani muujinayaan dhinacyo kala duwan, haddana waa isku mid. Ruuxa Quduuska ah, isagoo Ilaah buuxa ah, sidoo kale waa weligiis ah waana quduus (eedeeb-la’aan, aan waxba galabsan, daahir, aan xumaan ahayn). Turjumaado qaarkood waxay Ruuxa Quduuska ah ugu yeeraan Holy Ghost.

<!-- p23 -->
Ereyga ruux ee Giriigga waa **Pneuma** (**NYOO-mah** - **G4151**), micnihiisuna waa neef, ama dabayl. Ereyga Cibraaniga ee ruux wuxuu la mid yahay. Neefku waa muhiim maadaama aan ugu baahanahay sahay joogto ah si aan u noolaano. Neefkii Ilaah ayuu ku afuufay Aadam si uu nolol u siiyo; sidoo kale neeftii Ciise ayuu u afuufay tii ugu dambaysay ka hor intuusan iskutallaabta ku dhiman. Neef la’aantiis nolol ma jirto. Neefku sida dabaysha waa la dareemi karaa oo la waayo-aragsan karaa laakiin si sahlan looma arko sidii ‘walxaha’ kale ee biyo, dab, dhul.

<!-- p24 -->
Waxaa jira dhowr sifo oo kale oo asaasi ah oo lagu tilmaamo Ruuxa Quduuska ah. Mid kasta oo ka mid ah sifooyinkan waxaa loo adeegsadaa iyadoo lala socda ‘Ruuxa ee’, taas oo ka dhigan in sifooyinkani yihiin kuwo lama huraan u ah qeexidda Ruuxa. Tusaale, *Ruuxa Runta* ama *Ruuxa Nolosha*.

<!-- p25 -->
Ruuxa ee:

<!-- p26 -->
- **Runta** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:17</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:13</span> Waxay tixraacaysaa doorka Ruuxa ee ku hanuuninta rumaystayaasha runta oo dhan, muujinta doonista Ilaah iyo xaqiiqada Masiix.

<!-- p27 -->
- **Nolosha** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:2</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 6:8</span> Waxay muujinaysaa doorka Ruuxa ee ku mannaylida nolol ruuxi ah iyo weligeed ah rumaystayaasha, kana xoreynta sharciga dembiga iyo dhimashada.

<!-- p28 -->
- **Naxariista/Fadliga** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:29</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ZEC" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sekaryaah 12:10</span> Waxay ka tarjumaysaa shaqada Ruuxa ee qaybinta fadliga Ilaah, dembidhaaf, iyo naxariis aan la kasban oo loo fidiyo aadanaha.

<!-- p29 -->
- **Ammaanta** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 4:14</span> Waxay tilmaamaysaa doorka Ruuxa ee ku muujinta oo ku muujiya ammaanta Ilaah, gaar ahaan xilliyada silic iyo dhibaatayn.

<!-- p30 -->
- **Kor-qaadashada (Dhalashada carruurnimo)** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:15</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 4:5</span> Waxay tixraacaysaa shaqada Ruuxa ee ka dhigaysa rumaystayaasha carruurta Ilaah, una siisa darajada wiilal iyo gabdho.

<!-- p31 -->
- **Quduusnimada** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 1:4</span> Waxay tilmaamaysaa shaqada quduusayn ee Ruuxa, oo u gooni yeelaysa rumaystayaasha ujeeddooyinka Ilaah.

<!-- p32 -->
- **Awoodda** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 1:7</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span> Waxay sharraxaysaa awood-siinta Ruuxa ee rumaystayaasha si ay u yeeshaan geesinimo, adeeg, iyo karti ay si wax ku ool ah ugu noolaadaan iimaankooda.

<!-- p33 -->
Dhammaan sifooyinkan Ruuxa Quduuska ah waxaa si sax ah loogu aqoonsadaa Gargaare ama Dhiirigeliye. Ciise wuxuu Ruuxa Quduuska ah ugu yeeraa gargaaraha iman doona, wuxuuna adeegsadaa erayga Giriigga ah ee ***Paraklētos** (*pah-RAH-klay-tos - **G3875**), oo micnihiisu yahay u-doodaha, gargaare, kan dhinac loo yeedhay.

<!-- p34 -->
U-dooduhu wuxuu ku hadlaa magaca qof kale, sida qareen difaac. Way u dhex galaan oo ka caawiyaan qofka ay xilka u hayaan.

<!-- p35 -->
Gargaaruhu wuu gacan qabtaa. Waxay ka caawiyaan in shaqada la awood siiyo, laakiin shaqadoodu ugu horrayn waa inay taageeraan hawsha. Gargaarihuna kuma aha keliya mas’uuliyadda qabashada shaqada, balse waxay dhinac la socdaan si ay uga caawiyaan in la hirgeliyo.

<!-- p36 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuxuu leeyahay dhowr ujeeddo iyo doorar oo kala soocan oo ku jira adeeggiisa. Shaqadiisu waa gudaha (qalbiyada iyo nolosha rumaystayaasha), kor (adeegga iyo aqoonsiga Ilaah), iyo dibadda (beerteenna hawlgel, adduunka).

<!-- p37 -->
## Doorarka Ruuxa Quduuska ah-Gudaha, Kor, Dibadda

<!-- p38 -->
### Gudaha-Ruuxa Quduuska ah wuxuu nagu midaynayaa Ciise

<!-- p39 -->
Kahor intaan Ciise samayn wax mucjisooyin ah ama uusan bilaabin adeeggiisa dadweyne, waxaa baabtiisay Yooxan Baabtiisaha, Ruuxii Ilaahna wuu ku soo degay, cod samada ka yimidna wuxuu ku dhawaaqay, ‘Kani waa Wiilkaygii aan jeclahay oo aan kaga raalli ahay’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 3:17</span>).

<!-- p40 -->
Markaan Ciise ku aqbalno iimaan ahaan, waxaan helnaa badbaado iyo shaabadda Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p41 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="13-14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p42 -->
> **<u>Efesos 1:13-14</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Isaga dhexdiisa ayaad kalsooni ku qabateen idinkuna, markii aad maqasheen erayga runta, Injiilka badbaadadiinna; oo isaga dhexdiisa, markaad rumaysateen, waxaa idinku la shaabadeeyey Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee ballanka,
>
> kaas oo yahay dammaanadda dhaxalkayaga ilaa furashada hantida la iibsaday, si ammaanta loogu yeelo ammaantiisa.
>

<!-- p43 -->
</span>

<!-- p44 -->
Shaabadkani ma aha mala-awaal ee waa mid dhab ah. Waxay u adeegtaa calaamad furashadeenna waxayna tahay horudhac (down payment) dhaxalka buuxa ee aan helno annagoo carruurta Ilaah ah. Rooma 8 waxa uu sharraxayaa in dhalashada cusub dhexdeeda aan ku helno isla Ruuxa Ilaah ee ka soo sara kiciyey Masiix kuwii dhintay, jidhyadeenna dhimanayana, oo aynu noqono macbudka dhabta ah ee Ruuxa Quduuska ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Korintos 6:16</span>).

<!-- p45 -->
Helitaanka Ruuxa Ilaah wuxuu na beddelaa, wuxuuna keenaa in webiyo biyo nool ahi ay naga qulqulaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="38" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 7:38</span>). Cusboonaysiintani waa degdeg oo dhammaystiran laakiin way sii socotaa nolosheenna oo dhan. Webiga Ruuxa Quduuska ah waligiis waa inuusan nolosheenna ka go'in!

<!-- p46 -->
Iyada oo badbaadada iyo deggenaanshaha gudaha ee Ruuxa Quduuska ah ayaan ku sii nagaan karnaa Masiixa isaguna wuu innagu nagaanaan karaa. Ruuxa Ilaah dibadda kuma xayirna ee wuu inala deggan karaa si qoto dheer. Waxaan ku socon karnaa wadaag iyo iswaafajin la leh Ilaah laga bilaabo hadda ilaa nolosha weligeed ah.

<!-- p47 -->
Wax badan baa laga odhan karaa hawsha gudaha ee Ruuxa Quduuska ah iyo kaalintiisa quduusintaheenna, laakiin taas waxa laga wada hadli doonaa cutubka quduusinta.

<!-- p48 -->
### Kor u-Ruuxa Quduuska ah wuxuu ka marag furaa Ciise

<!-- p49 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuxuu ka yimid Ilaah waana ka Ilaah. Sida Ciise yidhi waxba ma uu samayn waxa keliya ee Aabbuhu tusay mooyaane (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 5:19</span>), sidaas oo kale Ruuxa Quduuska ahna waxba ma sameeyo ilaa laga hago Aabbaha. Maadaama Ruuxa Quduuska ahi yahay Gargaare, isagu kaligiis uma shaqeeyo ee wuxuu u shaqeeyaa si qumman oo iswaafaqsan Aabbaha.

<!-- p50 -->
Ruuxu wuxuu ka marag furaa Ciise (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 15:26</span>), wuxuu ammaan u yeelaa Ciise, wuxuuna ku dhawaaqaa oo sheega waxa Ciise noo hayo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:14</span>).

<!-- p51 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska ahi wuxuu ka marag furi doonaa Ciise, wuxuu ammaani doonaa, wuu bari doonaa, wuxuuna xusuusin doonaa waxbaridda Ciise (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="26" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:26</span>), wuxuuna nagu hoggaamin doonaa runta oo dhan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:13</span>). Ciise wuxuu xertiisii u sheegay in uu wax badan baro lahaa, laakiin aanay weli qaadi karin, balse Ruuxa Quduuska ahi uu ku hagayo runta oo dhan. Tani waa qodob kale oo nuuxnuuxsi leh oo xagga cilmiga diinta ah. Waxyaalaha uu Ciise u baahnaa in xertiisu ogaato waxaa loo gudbiyey siyaabo dhowr ah. Ciise wuxuu si toos ah u baray intii ka horreysay iskutallaabtiisa, iyo sidoo kale sarakicidda ka dib iyo ka hor kor-qaadistiisa.

<!-- p52 -->
Ciise sidoo kale wuu ballanqaaday in Ruuxa Runta, oo lagu shubay iyaga maalintii Pentecost, uu ku hoggaamin doono runta oo dhan iyada oo muujin rabbaani ah. Ma weli jiraan muujinno rabbaani ah maanta? **Dabcan haa!** Hadiyadaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah, oo ay ku jiraan nebiyeyn iyo erayo aqoon iyo xigmad ah, weli ma ay joogsanin! Si kastaba ha ahaatee, wax nebiyeyn ah ama eray ka yimaadda ruux ma aha fasiraad gaar loo leeyahay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2PE" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Butros 1:20</span>). Tani waa mid aad muhiim u ah. Qaar baa si qalad ah u sheeganaya inay dhegeysanayaan Ruuxa Quduuska ah, laakiinse falalkooda iyo hadalkoodu kama marag furaan Ciise. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Yooxanaa 4:3</span> wuxuu ka digayaa ruux kasta oo aan ka marag furin Ciise, isagoo leh ruuxaasi waa ka keeni Masiixa. Tani waa in mar kale la celceliyo, haddii ruux kastaa ka leexdo waxbaridda Masiixa ma aha Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee waa mid wasakh ah!

## Dhisidda Xiriir la leh Ruuxa Quduuska ah

<!-- p101 -->
Xidhiidh kastaa waxa uu u baahan yahay waqti iyo waayo-aragnimo si uu u qoto dheeraado. Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa difaaceenna, raalligeliyeheenna, gargaareheenna, iyo hagahayaga. Wuu ina xoojiyaa oo wuu ina quduusinayaa.

<!-- p102 -->
Bilow inaad u fikirto Ruuxa Quduuska ah sida qof. Xidhiidhkeenna waxaan ku wada shaqayn karnaa inaan dhisno ama waxaan samayn karnaa waxyaabo daciifiya xiriirkeenna. Waa la iska indho tiri karaa, waa la demi karaa, waa lagu gafaa, ama waa la dhageysan karaa, la sharfaa, lana adeecaa.

<!-- p103 -->
Ha iska indha tirin codka Ruuxa Quduuska ah. Wuxuu mar walba nala hadlayaa annaga iyo kaniisadda. Haddii aadan weli garan sida loo kala saaro codka Ruuxa Quduuska ah, waxay u baahan tahay waqti, diyaarnaan, iyo dadaal (sida dhammaan xiriirrada caafimaad qaba ee faa’iido leh). Laakiin ha werwerin, hore ayay kugu jiraan dhammaan qaybaha lagama maarmaanka ah ee aad ku bilaabi karto horumarinta xiriir shakhsiyeed oo aad la yeesho Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p104 -->
Hababka ugu dhaqsaha badan uguna la isku halleyn karo ee lagu dhiso xiriir wax-ku-ool ah oo lala yeesho Ruuxa Quduuska ah waa in la

<!-- p105 -->
1.  U yeer isaga **shaqsiyeed** salaadda, hakad qaado oo sug inuu hadlo.

<!-- p106 -->
2.  U yeer isaga adigoo ka mid ah **koox** dad ah oo isaga yaqaan (kaniisadda), hakad qaado, oo sug inuu hadlo.

<!-- p107 -->
Ereyga ‘u yeer’ waxaan uga jeedaa si dhab ah, afkaaga, ama sida aad u hadasho (gacmahaaga haddii aadan afka si cod leh ugu hadli karin). Maxaa la hadlaa? Sababo dhowr ah:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  Waxaan nahay ruuxyo hadla oo lagu sameeyey sawirka Ilaah. Ilaah wuu hadlaa, annaguna sidaas oo kale.

<!-- p109 -->
2.  Hadal ayaan u adeegsannaa habka ugu muhiimsan ee isgaarsiinta. Waxa kale oo aad qori kartaa waxa aad rabto inaad tidhaahdo (xusuus-qor) oo aad si cod leh u akhrido.

<!-- p110 -->
3.  Hadalku waa fal ula-kac ah, wuxuuna muujinayaa dhinaca aad la hadlayso in aad rabto inaad wada hadal la gasho.

<!-- p111 -->
#### Maxaad oranaysaa?

<!-- p112 -->
Haddii aadan weligaa Ilaah la hadlin, isku day inaad isbarato. Haa, wax yar bay kuu ekaan doontaa mid qalaad, balse raaxo-darradaas waa hagaagsan tahay oo waa ku-meel-gaar. Marka aad isbarayso, u sheeg Ilaah inaad rabto inaad la hadasho, isla markaana aad rabto inuu isaguna kugu jawaabo.

<!-- p113 -->
Ka fiirso inaad su’aal weydiiso isaga. Ma aha in ay noqoto su’aal qoto dheer oo cilmi-diineed; waysi fududaan kartaa. Ma i tusi kartaa jacaylka aad ii qabto? Maxaan u leeyahay dareen gaar ah? Maxaad jeclaan lahayd inaad ii sheegto?

<!-- p114 -->
#### Markaas maxaa xiga?

<!-- p115 -->
Haye, dhammaan xiriirrada wax-ku-oolka ah waxaad hadashaa wax yar, dabadeed hakad qaadaa oo dhagaysataa. Ilaah wuu hadli doonaa.

<!-- p116 -->
Ilaah siyaabo badan buu u hadlaa, balse wuxuu doonayaa inuu sidaas u sameeyo si shakhsi ah, oo gudaha ah. Badanaa ma aha cod la maqli karo (sida haddii saaxiibkaa ugu dhow uu magacaaga si cod leh u yiraahdo) laakiin waa cod gudaha ah oo la hadla qalbigaaga iyo maskaxdaada. Eliiyaah waxa uu tan ugu yeeraa ‘cod deggan oo khafiif ah’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1KI" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Boqorradii 19:11-13</span>).

<!-- p117 -->
Hababka ugu caansan ee aan anigu shakhsi ahaan ku arkay Ilaah isagoo dadka la hadlay, waxaana nasiib u yeeshay inaan ka caawiyo tobannaan qof inay bartaan sida toos ula hadlaan Ilaah, waa isku-darka qaar ka mid ah kuwa soo socda:

<!-- p118 -->
- Garasho - qofka Ilaah la hadlayaa wuxuu helaa faham degdeg ah oo ka caawiya.

<!-- p119 -->
- Sawir - oo sidoo kale Qorniinka laga yaqaan aragti ama riyo (aragti waa markaad soo jeeddo, riyo waa markaad huruddo), Ilaah wuxuu ku siin doonaa sawir maskaxdaada. Waxaa laga yaabaa inuu yahay xusuus, ama meel aad tagtay ama aad taqaan? Sahamiso sawirka, maxaad arkaysaa? Maxaad dareemaysaa natiijadiisa? Maxaa dhacaya?

<!-- p120 -->
- Cod gudaha ah - sida cod la maqli karo, laakiin adiga keligaa aad maqasho, looguna hadlo qalbigaaga iyo maskaxdaada.

<!-- p121 -->
#### Ilaah muxuu odhan doonaa?

<!-- p122 -->
Ma hubo! Laakiin taasaa xiisaha ku jirta. Waxaan oran karaa in UUNA ahayn Ruuxa cabsida, balse uu yahay Ruuxa jacaylka qumman, isla markaana uusan marnaba ku xadgudbi doonin waxbarista Qorniinka. Tusaale ahaan, Ilaah marnaba kuma oran doono inaad gasho xiriir ka baxsan guurka.

<!-- p123 -->
Hadda maxaa xiga?

<!-- p124 -->
Wada hadalka sii wad! Runtii waa sidaas u fudud.

### Maxaa loo dhisaa xiriir?

<!-- p129 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska Ah waa Gargaaraha. Gargaaruhu wuu ina caawiyaa! Shaqadu si ka sii siman ayey u socotaa haddii aan wadaagno luqad iyo xiriir la leh Isaga. Ma ku qiyaasi kartaa inaad isku daydo inaad la shaqayso qof shaqadaada ah adigoon xiriir lahayn, aadan ku hadlin isla luqadda, oo aadan wada xiriiri karin?!

<!-- p130 -->
Waxaan u adeegnaa Ilaah doonaya inuu nala xiriiro. Ciise wuxuu yiri idihiisu waxay garanayaan codkiisa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 10:27</span>), awoodooyinka dabiiciga ah ee aan u baahanahay hore ayaa innagu dhex dhisan, balse waxaa laga yaabaa inaan ka dhimannahay xirfad iyo khibrad.

<!-- p131 -->
Anigoo ka hadlaya waayo-aragnimo shakhsi ah, xiriir shakhsi ah oo firfircoon oo Ruuxa Quduuska Ah lala yeesho waa mid ka mid ah waxyaabaha ugu wanaagsan ee ku saabsan Masiixi ahaanshaha. Jooggiisu wuxuu inoo keenaa nabad, tasalli, iyo jihayn, balse keliya haddii aad taqaan sida aad u raadiso, u dhegeysato, uguna hoggaansanto.

<!-- p132 -->
Horumari dareen xasaasi ah oo aad la yeelato Ruuxa Quduuska Ah. Sida lammaane badan oo isqaba oo waqti ka dib, waxaad lammaanahaaga ula xiriiri kartaa si gaar ah, mararka qaar xataa eegmo keliya. Eegmadaas oo keliya ayaa kuu sheegi karta wax kasta oo aad u baahan tahay inaad ka ogaato wixii dhacay iyo waxa dhici doona. Dhis noocaas dareen iyo yaqaannimo aad la leedahay Ruuxa Quduuska Ah. Ku dar Isaga dhammaan noloshaada; tani waa qayb qurux badan oo ka mid ah xiriirka aad la leedahay Isaga, taas oo siin doonta qoto-dheeri iyo hodannaan badan socodkaaga iimaan.

<!-- p133 -->
Ruuxa Quduuska Ah wuxuu u yimid inuu ina hoggaamiyo oo ina hago, inuu ina awoodsiiyo inaan noqono dadkii Ilaah, iyo in dadka dhulka lagu arko si qoto dheer loo beddelay, lagana badbaadiyey boqortooyada mugdiga loona geliyey boqortooyada iftiinka. Inaad baratid Isaga, oo aad la dhistid kalsooni, waa lama huraan nolol maalmeedka iyo sidii loo qaban lahaa shaqada Ciise inoo hayo.

<!-- p134 -->
Dooddeenna ku saabsan shaqada Ruuxa Quduuska Ah kuma dhammaan cutubkan. Shaqadiisa si dheer ayaa looga hadlayaa cutubyada kale ee buuggan, gaar ahaan cutubyada ka hadlaya quduusayn iyo baabtiis.

## Su’aalaha Milicsiga Shakhsiyeed

<!-- p136 -->
1.  Sidee baad u la kulantay Ruuxa Quduuska Ah isagoo ah Tesalliyahaaga, Hagahaaga, ama isha awoodda? Doorkee ayaa ugu dhigma xilliga noloshaada ee hadda?

<!-- p137 -->
2.  Ka milicsi farqiga u dhexeeya Ruuxa Quduuska Ah ee “gudaha” (shaqo gudaha ah) iyo “kor” (shaqo dibadda ah). Kala duwanaanshahan sidee buu u saameeyaa fahamkaaga ku saabsan shaqadiisa noloshaada?

<!-- p138 -->
3.  Tallaabooyin noocee ah ayaad qaadi kartaa si aad u kobciso xiriir qoto dheer oo aad la yeelato Ruuxa Quduuska Ah, uguna aqoonsato codkiisa si ka sii cad?

<!-- p139 -->
4.  Waxa nalaga rabaa inaan ku tiirsanaanno xoogga Ruuxa Quduuska Ah si aan geesinimo ugu tallaabsanno uguna fulinno hawsha Ilaah inoo dhiibay. Hawl cayiman iyo diirad noocee ah ayaa Ilaah adiga kuu siiyey?

<!-- p140 -->
5.  Ruuxa Quduuska ah wuxuu ina hoggaamiyaa runta iyo kala-soocidda. Ma jiraan meelo noloshaada ka mid ah oo aad u baahan tahay caddayn ama xikmad? Sidee ayaad ugu martiqaadi kartaa inuu ku hanuuniyo?
