# Cutubka 4 - Ciise-Masiixii

<!-- p1 -->
Annagoo ka sii amba-qaadeyna barashadeenna dembiga, waxaan soo gunaanadnay in dembigu leeyahay qiime culus, kuwaas oo lagu dembaabayna dib loo soo celiyo, kuwa dembaabayna magdhow bixiyaan. Qiimaha dembiga waa in la bixiyo. Axdigii Hore Ilaah wuxuu dejiyey taxane allabaryo ah oo ay qabtaan wadaaddo, shakhsiyaad, iyo bulshooyin si dembi loola tacaalo, loo xaliyo, loona xukumo.

<!-- p2 -->
Xataa iyadoo la haysto dhammaan allabaryada dembiga, weli kuma filnayn-not sababtoo ah Ilaah ku guuldaraystay, balse sababtoo ah dadka ayaa sii doortay dembiga. In kasta oo allabarta xooluhu keeni karto kafaaro-gud xaddidan, haddana wax kasta oo allabarta xoolo ahi ma beddeli karaan dabeecadda dembiyeed ee bini’aadamka.

<!-- p3 -->
Ilaah wuxuu lahaa qorshe cajiib ah, <u>Masiix</u> (Af Cibri ***Mashiach,*** laga dhawaaqo *Mah-shee-ahkh -* **H4899**) oo sidoo kale loo yaqaan <u>Masiix</u> (Af Giriigga ***Christos,*** laga dhawaaqo *Khris-tos* - **G5547**) micnaheedu waa “Midka la subkay"; qof loo quduusiyey door gaar ah (tusaale, boqor, wadaad, ama badbaadiye).

<!-- p4 -->
Si aan u fahamno Ciise wuxuu Yahay, waxay caawinaysaa in la eego waxa dadka xilligii Isagu noolaa ay ka filayeen Masiixa. Rajooyinkaas ayaa qaabeeyey sida ay u arkeen Ciise, marar badanna sababta ay ugu dhibtoodeen inay ku aqoonsadaan Isaga inuu yahay fulinta qorshaha Ilaah.

<!-- p5 -->
## Xaalad Taariikheed

<!-- p6 -->
Dadka Yuhuuddu muddo dheer ayay sugayeen Masiix imaanaya. Aaminsanaanta Yuhuudda ee ku saabsan Masiixa xilligii ka hor Ciise waxaa qaabeeyey saadaasha Axdigii Hore, oo fasiraaddeedu kala duwanaan jirtay oo aan si wadajir ah loo isku raacsanayn. Inta badan Yuhuuddu waxay filayeen in Masiixu noqdo hoggaamiye siyaasadeed iyo ciidan oo ka xorayn doona Israa’iil dulmiga shisheeye (gaar ahaan xukunka Roomaanka) isla markaana soo celin doona boqortooyadii Daa’uud. Halkan waxaa ku jira guudmar ka mid ah rajadii caanka ahayd ee Masiixiga iyo sida ay ula jaanqaadi jireen (ama aanay ula jaanqaadi jirin) saadaasha Kitaabka Quduuska ah:

### Aaminsanaanta kala duwan ee ku saabsan Masiixa

<!-- p29 -->
Sida maanta dunida Masiixiyiintu ugu kala jaban yihiin kooxo, madhhabo, ama qaab-dhismeedyo aaminsanaan, sidaas oo kale ayay Yuhuuddii xilligii Ciise u kala qaybsanaayeen. Intii lagu jiray xilligii Ciise, afar kooxood oo waaweyn oo Yuhuud ah ayaa qaabeeyay muuqaalka diimeed iyo siyaasadeed ee Israa’iil, mid kastaana wuxuu lahaa aaminsanaan iyo filashooyin gaar ah oo ku saabsan Masiixa. Fahamka asal-kooban ee kooxahan kala duwan wuxuu ka caawin karaa in la helo macne qoto dheer marka la baranayo Qorniinka Axdiga Cusub.

<!-- p30 -->
The **Farisiyiinta** were the most influential among the common people, known for their strict adherence to the Law (Torah) and oral traditions. They believed in the resurrection, the afterlife, and the existence of angels, emphasizing personal piety and obedience to God's commandments. Their messianic expectation was centered on a figure who would uphold the Law, bring justice, and restore Israel both politically and religiously.

<!-- p31 -->
The **Sadduusiinta**, in contrast, were a wealthy and politically powerful group that controlled the Temple and priesthood. They rejected oral traditions and accepted only the Torah as authoritative scripture. The Sadducees denied the resurrection, afterlife, and the existence of angels, focusing instead on maintaining their power and collaborating with Rome to ensure political stability. Consequently, they had little emphasis on a Messiah, as their primary concern was preserving the status quo.

<!-- p32 -->
The **Eseeniyiinta** were a separatist group that withdrew from mainstream Jewish society, living in isolated communities such as Qumran. They were deeply focused on ritual purity, apocalyptic expectations, and a disciplined lifestyle. The Essenes believed in two Messianic figures: a priestly Messiah who would restore the Temple's sanctity and a kingly Messiah who would lead Israel in righteousness. This dual-Messiah concept reflected their hope for a spiritual and political renewal. The Essenes are not directly referenced in the scriptures.

<!-- p33 -->
Finally, the **Zelotada** were militant nationalists who sought to overthrow Roman rule through violent rebellion. They rejected Roman authority and were deeply committed to the restoration of Israel's sovereignty. Their messianic expectation was for a warrior-king Messiah, akin to David, who would lead them in battle against their oppressors and establish a powerful earthly kingdom.

<!-- p34 -->
While these groups focused on earthly expectations, Jesus’ mission revealed a far greater plan-one that addressed not just political oppression but humanity’s spiritual bondage to sin.

## Runta ku saabsan Masiixa

<!-- p36 -->
### Masaxidda Ciise

<!-- p37 -->
The **Farisiyiinta**’ and **Zelotada**’ vision of a Messiah reflects a recurring pattern in human history-seeking salvation through earthly means while rejecting God’s greater plan. As we’ve seen throughout Scripture, people often turn away from God-even though He’s always faithful. Even when God is faithful and accepts us, He is often forgotten and rejected, leading to the cycles of disobedience and decay that plagued earthly kingdoms.

<!-- p38 -->
While many expected a political Messiah, Jesus revealed a far greater purpose for His anointing-one that fulfilled spiritual and eternal needs. If Jesus had come as a conquering military leader, as the Jews hoped and expected, it would have only been a matter of time before the kingdom fell into disrepair because of human disobedience.

<!-- p39 -->
Instead, Jesus established a kingdom that cannot be shaken. His mission was not rooted in military might or political dominance, but in divine purpose. As the anointed one, Jesus explained what He was anointed to do and how He would accomplish it:

Ciise waxaa la masaaxay-ka soocay Ilaah-oo loo diray hawl rabbaani ah, oo u buuxda ujeeddo iyo awood.

<!-- p44 -->
Ciise wuxuu ku dhawaaqay:

<!-- p45 -->
- Warka wanaagsan (Injilka) ee Boqortooyada.

<!-- p46 -->
- Samatabbixin - cafis, dembi-dhaaf, xorriyad.

<!-- p47 -->
- Sanadka (xilliga) la aqbalo ee Rabbiga.

<!-- p48 -->
Dadka uu u yimid waxay ahaayeen:

<!-- p49 -->
- Qalbi-jaban - kuwa ka jabay shucuur ahaan iyo ruux ahaanba.

<!-- p50 -->
- Indhoolayaal - ruux ahaan, mararka qaarkoodna jir ahaan.

<!-- p51 -->
- Masaakiin - baahsan, saboolnimo ku jira (ruux ahaan iyo/ama jir ahaan).

<!-- p52 -->
- Maxaabiis - micnaheedu ‘waran lagu qabsaday’ oo ka turjumaya kuwa lagu hayo addoonsi iyaga oo aan doonayn.

<!-- p53 -->
Ciise wuxuu beddelay dadkaas jabay isagoo:

<!-- p54 -->
- Soo celinta aragga

<!-- p55 -->
- Ku dejinta xaalad xorriyad

<!-- p56 -->
- Xidh-xidhid, bogsiin, soo celin

<!-- p57 -->
Ujeeddada adeegga iyo shaqada Ciise waxay ahayd mid gaar ahaaneed iyo mid caalami ahba, degdeg ah, isla markaana waara. Ujeeddadiisu ma aha hal arrin oo keliya ee waa qayb ka mid ah qorshaha rabbaaniga ah ee Ilaah si uu aadanaha ula heshiisiiyo Ilaah. Markii dembigu dunida soo galay, waxa uu sababay kala-qaadasho u dhexaysa Ilaah iyo dadka. Ilaah wuxuu khasab uga saaray aadanaha beerta iyo ka horjoogitaankiisa. Intii lagu jiray Axdigii Hore, aadanuhu wuu la jiri karay Ilaah, balse si xaddidan oo keliya. Aad bay u yaraatay in Ruuxa Ilaah la siiyo dadka; badankoodna dadka waxay ugu noolaayeen go'doon ka ah Ilaah. Wuxuu ka helay dadkii Yuhuudda, hase yeeshee wuxuu doonayay dariiq joogto ah, sugan, oo dhamaystiran si uu u soo celiyo xidhiidhkiisii uu la lahaa aadanaha.

<!-- p58 -->
Joogitaanka Ilaah ee dhulka xilligii Ciise badankiisu wuxuu ku koobnaa qaybta ugu gudaha badan ee macbudka, Quduuska Quduuska. Halkan gadaasha daah qaro weyn oo kala soocayay qaybta ugu gudaha badan iyo Quduuska, joogitaanka Ilaah wuxuu ku sugnaa sanduuqii axdiga. Aadanuhu wuxuu ka go'doonsanaa Ilaah, Ilaahna wuu ka soocnaa abuuriddiisa.

<!-- p59 -->
Ilaah ayaa Ciise u soo diray inuu dayactiro, dib u dhiso, soo celiyo, oo uu aadanaha la isu heshiisiiyo Isaga. (2 Korintos 5:17-21)

<!-- p60 -->
### Shaqadii Lama Huraanka ahayd ee Ciise

<!-- p61 -->
Si si buuxda loogu heshiisiiyo aadanaha Ilaah, hawsha Masiixu ma aha oo keliya wacdinta iyo bogsiinta, balse waxay kaloo u baahnayd falka ugu dambeeya ee kafaaraggudka. Ciise waa inuu noqdaa allabari qumman oo dhamaystiran, isagoo dhimashadiisa iskutallaabta ku maraya.

<!-- p62 -->
Kitaabka Cibraaniyada wuxuu si aad u dheer oo ku faafsan 13-kiisa cutub u sharxayaa sida Ciise uu u noqday allabarigii qumman, isla markaana uu ahaa labadaba allabariga dembiga, iyo Wadaadka Sare ee u bandhiga (Cibraaniyada 3 &amp; 4). Wadaadnimada Ciise ma aha tii reer Laawi (ka dib qabiilka Laawi), balse waxay ahayd sidii Milkisedeq, oo lagu yiraahdo inuusan lahayn bilow iyo dhammaad, taasoo la micno ah inaanay ku xayirnayn qaababka iyo caadooyinka dadka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 7:3</span>). Xaddidaadda wadaadnimada Laawiyiinta ayaa ah inay ku tiirsan tahay dhiigga dibiyaasha iyo riyaha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 9:12-13</span>) kuwaas oo allabarigoodu aanu ku filnayn cafis waara oo joogto ah oo dembiyada ah. Barashadeenna ku saabsan dembi iyo allabari, waxaan ka ognahay in cawaaqibta dembiyada dadka la dib dhigi karay hal sano (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 10:3</span>) intii lagu jiray Maalinta Kafaaraggudka, balse allabarigaas waa in si joogto ah loo cusboonaysiiyaa.

<!-- p63 -->
Ciise, isagoo ku dhimanaya iskutallaabta (sheekooyinkeeda waxay ku jiraan Matayos 27, Markos 15, Yooxanaa 19 iyo Luukos 23) wuxuu dhiiggiisa shubay, wuxuuna bixiyay magdhaw dembiyada—kuwii hore, kuwii joogga ah, iyo kuwii mustaqbalka—ee aadanaha oo dhan. Ishacyaah 53 wuxuu muujinayaa Ciise inuu yahay addoonka silicay, kaas oo culayska oo dhan ee ciqaabta iyo xanuunka isaga dusha u qaatay. Hal-mar-dhalkiisu wuxuu noqday allabarigii ugu dambeeyay, kii ugu sarreeyay. Ciise waa Wanka Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:29</span>), aan lahayn wasakh ama laalaab (dembi); maxaa yeelay wuxuu ahaa kaamil, dhamaystiran, oo quduus ah, daadinta dhiiggiisa aan waxba galabsan ayaa ka dhigtay kafaaraggud suurtagal. **Mar dambe allabaryo looma baahna mana aha kuwo la aqbalo. Kuwii hore waxay ahaayeen kuwo kumeelgaar ah—laakiin kii Ciise wuu dhammeystiran yahay oo kama dambays ah.**

<!-- p64 -->
Iyada oo loo marayo dhimasho iyo sarakicid, waxaan helnay axdi cusub oo ka wanaagsan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 8:6</span>), mid lagu sugo dhiigga Wiilka Ilaah laftiisa; Ciise waa dhexdhexaadiyaha axdiga, waa kii isu-dhex gala. Iyada oo loo marayo allabariga Masiixa, waxaan helnaa badbaado, isagaana qoraha *‘badbaado weligeed ah kuwa u hoggaansama*’ (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 5:9</span>).

<!-- p65 -->
Ereyga badbaado ee Giriigga waa **Sōtēria** (*so-tay-REE-ah - **G4991)**.* Wuxuu asal ahaan ka dhigan yahay **badbaado, samatabbixin, ama ilaalin**. Wuxuu ku koobban yahay falka la badbaadayo, labadaba macne ruuxi ah (badbaado weligeed ah oo Masiixa dhexdiisa) iyo duruufo jireed (samatabbixin khatar ama waxyeello). Ereygu wuxuu gudbiyaa fikradda ammaan, dhamaystir, iyo soo celin.

<!-- p67 -->

<!-- p68 -->

- Waxay ku jirtaa soo celin lagu gaadho xaalad dhamaystiran ama hagaagsan.

<!-- p73 -->
Allabariga Ciise wuxuu bixiyay qiimihii nalaga cafin lahaa, wuxuuna sameeyay jid aan ku yeelanno xiriir Ilaah, wuxuuna dejiyay waddo aan kula joogno isaga oo ku tiirsan oo keliya hoggaansamadeenna, ee aan ku xirnayn kaamilnimadeenna ama awoodda aan uga dheeraanno dembi.

<!-- p74 -->
Markaan Ciise ku aqbalno iimaan ahaan (waxaa laga hadlayaa goor dambe), waxaan helnaa badbaadadiisa. Badbaadadan waxay na xidhaysaa Ilaah, waxayna magacayaga gelisaa ***Buugga Nolosha ee Wanka*** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 20:11-15</span>). Injiilka Yooxanaa wuxuu leeyahay:

<!-- p75 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p76 -->
> **<u>Yooxanaa 3:16</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Waayo Ilaah sidaa ayuu dunida u jeclaaday oo wuxuu siiyey Wiilkiisa keli ah, si mid kastoo isaga rumeeya uusan halligmin, laakiinse uu helo nolosha weligeed ah.
>

<!-- p77 -->
</span>

<!-- p78 -->
In la halligmo waxay ka dhigan tahay in la burburo—sida miro xumaada ka hor intaan la raaxaysan. Waa nolol seegtay waxa ay loo abuuray. Ciise wuxuu u yimid inuu na siiyo nolol joogto ah, ama weligeed ah, oo uu naga dhowro halligmo. Nolosha weligeed ahi ma aha nolol ka bilaabata ka dib dhimashadeenna, waa nolol ka bilaabata isla markiiba aan Ciise u aqbalno badbaadadeenna, markaan dib-u-dhalanno.

<!-- p79 -->
### Dib-u-Dhalasho

<!-- p80 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p81 -->
> **<u>2 Korintos 5:17</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Sidaa darteed, haddii qofku ku jiro Masiixa, waa abuur cusub; waxyihii hore way dhaafeen; bal eeg, wax walba waxay noqdeen kuwo cusub.
>

<!-- p82 -->
</span>

<!-- p83 -->
Markaan aqbalno Ciise, waxaan noqonnaa ‘mar labaad la dhalay’. Weedhan waxaa adeegsaday Ciise si uu u qeexo barta kala-guurka, halka aan ka noqono kuwo ka dhashay Ruuxa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:1-15</span>). Waxay ku timaaddaa iimaanka-annagoo dooranayna inaan si buuxda ugu kalsoonaanno Isaga, sida gelitaanka axdi guur, qalbi iyo ereyo labadaba.

<!-- p84 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="9-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p85 -->
> **<u>Rooma 10:9-10</u>** (NKJV)
>
> haddii aad afkaaga ku qirato Rabbiga Ciise, oo aad qalbigaaga ka rumaysato in Ilaah Isaga ka soo sara kiciyey kuwii dhintay, waad badbaadi doontaa.
>
> Waayo qalbiga ayaa lagu rumaystaa xagga xaqnimada, afkana lagu qiraa xagga badbaadada.
>

<!-- p86 -->
</span>

<!-- p87 -->
Markii aan ku dhalannay dhulka (ka dhashay jirka iyo biyaha) waxyaalo badan ayaa dhacay: neeftii noo horraysay ayaan qaadannay waxaana nalagu dhawaaqay nool, waalidkeen ayaa na soo dhaweeyey, magac ayaa naloo bixiyey, waxaan noqonnay dhaxal-suge, waxaan helnay nafaqo iyo ilaalin, waxaana sii wadnay korriin.

<!-- p88 -->
Markaan kalsoonideenna ku ridno Ciise oo aan isaga aqbalno, waxaan noqonnaa mar labaad la dhalay, ee ma aha jirka, balse Ruuxa. Waxaan noqonnaa kuwo ruux ahaan nool! Sida dhalashada jireed, dhalashada ruuxiga ahna waxa la socda waxyaalo badan:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  Markii aan Ciise aqbalnay, Ilaah wuu tirtiray dembiyadeennii wuuna nagu dhawaaqay kuwo la cafiyey (**la xaqyeelay**). Wuxuu na siiyey bilaabasho cusub. Derbigii kala soocayay innaga iyo Isaga waa la qaaday. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Titoos 3:5</span>)

<!-- p90 -->
2.  Waxaan noqonnay ruux ahaan **dib loo dhalay** waxaana helnay deggenaanshaha Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee na dhex dega. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 6:19</span>).

<!-- p91 -->
3.  Waxaa nalagu **korsaday** qoyska Ilaah waana noqonnay dhaxleyaal. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 1:5</span>)

<!-- p92 -->
4.  Waxaan helnaa isha nafaqada ruuxiga ah, iyo ilaalin weligeed ah. (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:2</span>)

<!-- p93 -->
## Su'aalaha Milicsiga Shaqsiga ah

<!-- p94 -->
1.  Maxay adiga kuu tahay in Ciise lagu subkay Nebi, Wadaad, iyo Boqor ahaan? Sidee bay tani u qaabaynaysaa fahamkaaga doorkiisa ee noloshaada?

<!-- p95 -->
2.  Intee ayuu Ciise ka buuxiyey filashadaadii Badbaadiye? Ma jiraan dhinacyo uu ka batay waxa aad filaysay?

<!-- p96 -->
3.  Sidee bay fahamka ujeeddada Ciise ee Masiixa ahi u caqabadaysaa ama u qoto dheeraynaysaa iimaankaaga?

<!-- p97 -->
4.  Ka milicsi waayo-aragnimadaada “mar kale la dhalay”. Sidee buu dhalashadan cusub ee ku jirta Masiixa kuugu siinayaa ujeeddo iyo rajo?

<!-- p98 -->
5.  Ciise wuxuu u yimid inuu keeno xorriyad iyo soo celin. Meelaha noocee ah ee noloshaada ayaa weli u baahan taabashadiisa, sideese ugu dhiibi kartaa Isaga maanta?

## Su'aalaha Wada-hadalka Kooxda (20-30 Daqiiqo)

<!-- p100 -->
1.  **Su’aal Furitaan:** *“Waa maxay hal cinwaan ama door oo Ciise leeyahay oo noloshaada ugu macnaha badan?”*

<!-- p101 -->
2.  **Baadhista Qorniinka:** *Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="61" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ishacyaah 61:1-3</span> iyo <span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 4:16-21</span>. Sidee ayuu Ciise u fuliyey saadaalladan, maxaase taas inoo ka dhigan maanta?*

<!-- p102 -->
3.  **Doorka Masiixa:** *Ka dood sida ay Nabinnimadiisa, Wadaadnimadiisa, iyo Boqornimadiisu u saameeyaan hawlgalkiisa. Maxay muhiim u tahay inuu buuxiyo saddexdaas door oo dhan?*

<!-- p103 -->
4.  **Adeegsiga Shakhsiyeed:** *Waa maxay siyaabo wax-ku-ool ah oo aan uga tarjumno hawlgalka Masiixa ee xorriyadda iyo soo celinta qoysaskeenna, kiniisadheenna, iyo bulshooyinkeenna?*

<!-- p104 -->
5.  **Milicsiga Dib-u-dhalashada:** *Sidee ayay fikradda ah in la noqdo “dib u dhalasho” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 3:3-7</span>) ula xiriirtaa hawlgalka Ciise ee Masiixa? Sideese u qaabeeyaa aqoonsigeenna iyo ujeeddadeenna?*
