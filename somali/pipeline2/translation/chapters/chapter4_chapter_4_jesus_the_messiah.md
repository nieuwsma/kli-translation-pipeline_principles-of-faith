# Cutubka 4 - Ciise-Masiixii

<!-- p1 -->
Annagoo ka sii amba-qaadeyna barashadeenna dembiga, waxaan soo gunaanadnay in dembigu leeyahay qiime culus, kuwaas oo lagu dembaabayna dib loo soo celiyo, kuwa dembaabayna magdhow bixiyaan. Qiimaha dembiga waa in la bixiyo. Axdigii Hore Ilaah wuxuu dejiyey taxane allabaryo ah oo ay qabtaan wadaaddo, shakhsiyaad, iyo bulshooyin si dembi loola tacaalo, loo xaliyo, loona xukumo.

<!-- p2 -->
Xataa iyadoo la haysto dhammaan allabaryada dembiga, weli kuma filnayn-not sababtoo ah Ilaah ku guuldaraystay, balse sababtoo ah dadka ayaa sii doortay dembiga. In kasta oo allabarta xooluhu keeni karto kafaaro-gud xaddidan, haddana wax kasta oo allabarta xoolo ahi ma beddeli karaan dabeecadda dembiyeed ee bini’aadamka.

<!-- p3 -->
Ilaah wuxuu lahaa qorshe cajiib ah, <u>Masiix</u> (Af Cibri ***Mashiach,*** laga dhawaaqo *Mah-shee-ahkh -* **H4899**) oo sidoo kale loo yaqaan <u>Masiix</u> (Af Giriigga ***Christos,*** laga dhawaaqo *Khris-tos* - **G5547**) micnaheedu waa “Midka la subkay"; qof loo quduusiyey door gaar ah (tusaale, boqor, wadaad, ama badbaadiye).

<!-- p4 -->
Si aan u fahamno Ciise wuxuu Yahay, waxay caawinaysaa in la eego waxa dadka xilligii Isagu noolaa ay ka filayeen Masiixa. Rajooyinkaas ayaa qaabeeyey sida ay u arkeen Ciise, marar badanna sababta ay ugu dhibtoodeen inay ku aqoonsadaan Isaga inuu yahay fulinta qorshaha Ilaah.

<!-- p5 -->
## Xaalad Taariikheed

<!-- p6 -->
Dadka Yuhuuddu muddo dheer ayay sugayeen Masiix imaanaya. Aaminsanaanta Yuhuudda ee ku saabsan Masiixa xilligii ka hor Ciise waxaa qaabeeyey saadaasha Axdigii Hore, oo fasiraaddeedu kala duwanaan jirtay oo aan si wadajir ah loo isku raacsanayn. Inta badan Yuhuuddu waxay filayeen in Masiixu noqdo hoggaamiye siyaasadeed iyo ciidan oo ka xorayn doona Israa’iil dulmiga shisheeye (gaar ahaan xukunka Roomaanka) isla markaana soo celin doona boqortooyadii Daa’uud. Halkan waxaa ku jira guudmar ka mid ah rajadii caanka ahayd ee Masiixiga iyo sida ay ula jaanqaadi jireen (ama aanay ula jaanqaadi jirin) saadaasha Kitaabka Quduuska ah:

<!-- p7 -->
### Rajadii Caamka ahayd ee Yuhuudda ee Masiixa Kahor Ciise

<!-- p8 -->
### **1. Badbaadiye Siyaasadeed iyo Milatari**

<!-- p9 -->
- **Rumayn:** Masiixu wuxuu ahaan lahaa boqor la mid ah Daa’uud, oo hoggaamin doona Israa’iil kacdoon guul leh ka dhan ah quwadaha shisheeye kuna soo celin doona madaxbannaanida Israa’iil.

<!-- p10 -->
- **Aayado la xusay:** <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ISA" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ishacyaah 9:6-7</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="23" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yeremyaah 23:5-6</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ZEC" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sekaryaah 9:9-10</span>.

<!-- p11 -->
- **Xaalad Taariikheed:** Yuhuuddu oo hoos joogtay xukunkii Roomaanka waxay ahaayeen dulman oo si culus loo canshuuro, taas ayaana keentay rajada ah in Masiixu afgambin doono Rooma kana aasaasi doono boqortooyo xoog leh oo dunida ah.

<!-- p12 -->
- **Runtu:** Ciise waa Boqor, balse boqortooyadiisu ma aha mid adduunyo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="36" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 18:36</span>). Wuxuu dhisay boqortooyo ruuxi ah imaatinkiisii koowaad, laakiin Wuu soo noqon doonaa isagoo ah Boqor guuleysta si uu u xukumo oo u garsooro xaqnimo, sida lagu sharaxay Kitaabka Muujintii (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 19:11-16</span>).

<!-- p13 -->
### **2. Soo Celiyaha Boqortooyada Daa’uud**

<!-- p14 -->
- **Rumayn:** Masiixu wuxuu dib u dhisi lahaa boqortooyadii Daa’uud oo keeni lahaa xilli dahabi ah oo barwaaqo, caddaalad, iyo nabad.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Aayado la xusay:** <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="7" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuu'eel 7:12-16</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="AMO" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Aamos 9:11</span>.

<!-- p16 -->
- **Fasiraad:** Mararka badan diiradda waxa ay saarnayd soo celinta qaranimada halkii ay ka ahaan lahayd cusboonaysiin ruuxi ah.

<!-- p17 -->
- **Runtu:** Ciise, oo farac ka ah Daa’uud, wuxuu oofinayaa ballankan isagoo dhisaya boqortooyo weligeed ah. In kasta oo boqortooyadan ay hadda tahay mid ruuxi ah, waxay ku soo idlaan doontaa xukun jireed intii lagu jiro imaatinkiisa labaad (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="32-33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 1:32-33</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 20:4-6</span>).

<!-- p18 -->
### **3. Nabi la mid ah Muuse**

<!-- p19 -->
- **Rumayn:** Qaar ka mid ah Yuhuuddu waxay aaminsanaayeen in Masiixu noqon doono nabi, sida Muuse, oo si toos ah ula hadli lahaa Ilaah oo ku hadli lahaa magaciisa.
