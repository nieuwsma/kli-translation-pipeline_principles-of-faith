# Hawlaha Dhammaadka Cutubka

<!-- p1 -->
Hawlahan dhammaadka cutubka waxaa loo qaabeeyey in ay xoojiyaan ujeeddooyinka waxbarasho ee asaaska ah ee cutub kasta. Hawl kasta waxa lagu talagalay in ay qaadato qiyaastii 15-20 daqiiqo in la dhammaystiro, waxaana laga yaabaa in ay u baahdaan agab dheeraad ah oo aan buuggan ku jirin. Iyadoo ay weheliso is-milicsiga shakhsiyeed iyo su’aalaha dood-wadaagga kooxeed, hawlahani waxa ugu wanaagsan in lagu isticmaalo qaab barasho kooxeed si loo qoto dheereeyo fahamka iyo ka-qaybgalka casharrada muhiimka ah ee buugga. Adeegso hawlahadan sida tilmaame. Noqo hal-abuur, dhiirrigeli ka-qaybgalka, oo wax ka beddel hawlaha sida aad u aragto inay ku habboon yihiin.

<!-- p2 -->
## Cutubka 1 - Caqiido Sugan Waa Muhiim

<!-- p3 -->
### Hawl 1: Isku-aadin Qorniinka

<!-- p4 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p5 -->
In la xoojiyo fikradaha kitaabiga ah ee muhiimka ah ee ku saabsan caqiidada adigoo baadhaya Qorniin adkaynaya muhiimaddeeda.

<!-- p6 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p7 -->
1.  **U qaybi ka qaybgalayaasha** **laba-labo ama kooxo yaryar** oo ka kooban 3-4 qof.

<!-- p8 -->
2.  **U qoondee Aayado Qorniinka** - Si koox kasta mid ka mid ah qaybaha soo socda:

<!-- p9 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 2:7</span>** - *Xididaysan oo Masiixa lagu dhisan yahay.*

<!-- p10 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 4:1-4</span>** - *Wacdinta Ereyga iyo ka hortagga caqiidada beenta ah.*

<!-- p11 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 4:11-16</span>** - *Bisaylka ruuxiga ah iyo midnimada iyada oo loo marayo waxbarid sugan.*

<!-- p12 -->
3.  **Dood Kooxeed** - U sheeg in koox kastaa ay:

<!-- p13 -->
- **Akhriyaan Aayadda Qorniinka** ee loo qoondeeyay cod dheer.

<!-- p14 -->
- **Soo koobeen waxbarista ugu weyn** ee ku saabsan caqiidada 1-2 jumladood.

<!-- p15 -->
- **Ka doodaan tusaale nolol dhab ah** oo muujinaya sida waxbaristani uga caawiso ka hortagga beenta ama ka caawinta kuwa kale inay ku koraan iimaanka.

<!-- p16 -->
4.  **Wadaag Aragtiyo** - Koox kastaa ha qaadato **2 daqiiqo** si ay ugu bandhigto kooxda oo dhan Qorniinkooda, soo koobiddooda, iyo adeegsigooda.

<!-- p17 -->
### Hawl 2: Caqiido ku Jira Ficil

<!-- p18 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p19 -->
Ku dabaq muhiimadda caqiidada sugan xaalado nolol dhab ah oo xooji jawaabaha wax ku oolka ah.

<!-- p20 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p21 -->
1.  **U kala qaybsama Kooxo** - Sameeya kooxo ka kooban **4-5 ka-qaybgalayaal**.

<!-- p22 -->
2.  **U qoondeeya Xaalado** - Si kasta oo koox ah mid ka mid ah kuwa soo socda:

<!-- p23 -->
- **Xaalad 1**: *Saaxiib ayaa ku leeyahay, “Wax muhiim ah ma aha waxa aan aaminsanahay, inta aan daacad nahay.” Sidee baad uga jawaabtaa?*

<!-- p24 -->
- **Xaalad 2**: *Waxaad hagaysaa rumayste cusub oo maqla waxbaris is khilaafsan oo ku saabsan badbaadada iimaan ahaan marka la barbar dhigo badbaadada camal ahaan. Qorniin kee ayaad la wadaagi kartaa si aad u caddayso?*

<!-- p25 -->
- **Xaalad 3**: *Saaxiib Masiixi ah ayaa joojiya ka qaybgalka kaniisadda sababtoo ah waxay u arkaan inaysan daruuri ahayn inta ay rumaysan yihiin Ilaah. Sideed ugu dhiirrigelinaysaa adigoo adeegsanaya Qorniin?*

<!-- p26 -->
3.  **Hawlaha Doodda** - Kooxuhu waa inay:

<!-- p27 -->
- **Aqoonsadaan arrinta caqiidada** ee xudunta u ah xaaladda.

<!-- p28 -->
- **Qor 1-2 Aayado** oo ka hadlaya arrinta.

<!-- p29 -->
- Diyaariyaan **Soo koobid 1-daqiiqo ah** oo ku saabsan sida ay ula talin lahaayeen qofka xaaladda ku jira.

<!-- p30 -->
4.  **La wadaag Jawaabaha** - Kooxuhu si isdaba joog ah ha u soo bandhigaan xaaladahooda iyo jawaabahooda kooxda, ka dibna waxa raaca **jawaab-celin kooban ama wax-ku-daridyo** ka imanaya kuwa kale.

<!-- p31 -->
### Hawsha 3: Bayaan mise Qorniin?

<!-- p32 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p33 -->
Tababbar ka-qaybgalayaasha inay **kala saaraan runta iyo qaladka** iyagoo u adeegsanaya Qorniinka saldhig.

<!-- p34 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p35 -->
1.  **Deji Hawsha** - Akhri **bayaannada** soo socda si cod ah, hal mar mid. Qaar ka mid ah bayaannadu waa **runno Kitaabka Quduuska ah**, halka qaar kalena ay yihiin **khaladaad** ama **fasiraad qaldan**.

<!-- p36 -->
2.  **Jawaabaha Kooxda** - Weydii ka-qaybgalayaasha inay:

<!-- p37 -->
- Go'aamiyaan in bayaanku yahay **run** ama **been**.

<!-- p38 -->
- Bixiyaan **aayad Kitaabka Quduuska ah** si ay u taageeraan jawaabtooda.

<!-- p39 -->
3.  **Hag Doodda** - U oggolow waqti kooxaha ay uga doodaan kala duwanaanshaha ama ay u baaraan aayado ka hor inta aan la xaqiijin jawaabaha saxda ah.

<!-- p40 -->
#### Bayaanada:

<!-- p41 -->
1.  *Iimaanka keliya ayaa na badbaadiya.* *(Run - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:8-9</span>)*

<!-- p42 -->
2.  *Dhammaan jidadku Ilaah bay na gaarsiiyaan haddii aan daacad nahay.* *(Khalad - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 14:6</span>)*

<!-- p43 -->
3.  *Caqiidada saxda ah ma aha mid ka muhiimsan jeceylka dadka kale.* *(Khalad - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 4:16</span>)*

<!-- p44 -->
4.  *Waxaan u baahan nahay Kitaabka Quduuska ah, laakiin sidoo kale waxaan u baahan nahay muujinno casri ah si ay na hanuuniyaan.* *(Khalad - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 3:16-17</span>)*

<!-- p45 -->
5.  *Camal wanaagsan waa natiijada badbaadada, ee ma aha sababteeda.* *(Run - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:10</span>)*

## Cutubka 2 - Ilaah - Abuuraha Weligiis ah iyo Aabbe Jecel

<!-- p47 -->
### Hawl 1: Magacaabi Sifadaas (Hawl-kooxeed Isdhexgal ah)

<!-- p48 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p49 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay xiriiriyaan magacyada iyo sifooyinka Ilaah adeegsiyo wax-ku-ool ah oo nolosha dhabta ah.

<!-- p50 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p51 -->
1.  **Diyaari Kaararro Tusmo** - Ku qor **hal magac ama sifo Ilaah** (tusaale, Yahweh-Jireh, Daacad, Meel walba jooga) kaar kasta.

<!-- p52 -->
2.  **Qaybi Kaararrada** - Sii hal kaarar ka-qaybgalaha kasta ama koox kasta.

<!-- p53 -->
3.  **Hawsha Kooxda:**

<!-- p54 -->
- Akhri **magaca ama sifada** iyo **tixraaca Qorniinka** ee la socda ee cutubkan.

<!-- p55 -->
- Ka dood: *Sidee baa magaca ama sifadani ugu dabaqantaa xaalad shakhsi ah ama mid Baybalka ku jirta?*

<!-- p56 -->
- Diyaari **soo-koobid 1-daqiiqo ah** oo kooxda lala wadaago.

<!-- p57 -->
4.  **Gunaanad** - Ka codso ka-qaybgalaha kasta ama koox kasta inay **soo bandhigaan milicsigooda**, iyadoo la adkeynayo siyaabaha wax-ku-oolka ah ee dabeecadda Ilaah ugu dabaqanto halganka ama guulaha nolosha dhabta ah.

<!-- p58 -->
### Hawl 2: Magacyada Ilaah ee Salaadda (Milicsi iyo Adeegsiga)

<!-- p59 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p60 -->
Bar ka-qaybgalayaasha sida ay magacyada iyo sifooyinka Ilaah ugu dari karaan **tukashada shakhsiyeed**.

<!-- p61 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p62 -->
1.  **Deji Waqti Ka-fiirsasho** - Bixi **goob aamusan** si ka-qaybgalayaashu uga ka-fiirsadaan mid ka mid ah magacyada Ilaah ee cutubka.

<!-- p63 -->
2.  **Jimicsiga Tukashada ee La-hagayo:**

<!-- p64 -->
- Tallaabo 1: Dooro **magac ama sifo** oo la xidhiidha baahi hadda jirta (tusaale ahaan, **Jehovah-Rapha** ee bogsiinta).

<!-- p65 -->
- Tallaabo 2: Qor **tukasho** gaaban adigoo adeegsanaya magacaas, adigoo ammaana Ilaah, kuna waydiisanaya inuu u dhaqmo si waafaqsan dabeecaddiisa.

<!-- p66 -->
- Tallaabo 3: Ku wadaag tukashooyinka (ikhtiyaari ah) kooxo yaryar ama laba-labo si loo **dhiirrigeliyo oo loo dhiso iimaan**.

<!-- p67 -->
3.  **Ku soo gabagabee Tukasho Kooxeed** - U mahadnaq Ilaah inuu isu muujiyey iyada oo loo marayo magacyadiisa iyo sifooyinkiisa.

<!-- p68 -->
### Hawl 3: La kulanka Sifooyinka Ilaah (15-20 Daqiiqo)

<!-- p69 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p70 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay **ka fiirsadaan oo ay dabaqaan** sifooyinka Ilaah noloshooda iyada oo loo marayo **sheekooyin shakhsiyeed** iyo **tukasho kooxeed**.

<!-- p71 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p72 -->
1.  **U qaybi kooxo ka kooban 3-4 qof.**

<!-- p73 -->
2.  **Diiradda Sifooyinka:**

<!-- p74 -->
- U diyaari koox kasta **liis ka kooban 3-4 sifo** oo ka mid ah sifooyinka Ilaah (tusaale ahaan, **Daacad ah, Naxariis badan, Sarreeye, Bixiye**) oo laga soo qaatay cutubka.

<!-- p75 -->
- Weydii koox kasta inay **doortaan hal sifo** oo ay u arkaan inay si ugu badan ugu habboon tahay xilliga noloshooda ee hadda.

<!-- p76 -->
3.  **Wadaagista Sheekooyinka (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p77 -->
- Ka-qaybgalaha kastaa si kooban ha **wadaago sheeko** (khibrad shakhsiyeed ama aragti) oo ku xiran sifada la doortay.

<!-- p78 -->
- Tusaalooyin Hagitaan:

<!-- p79 -->
- *“Goorma ayaad la kulantay Ilaah sida Bixiyahaaga?”*

<!-- p80 -->
- *“Sidee bay naxariista Ilaah u qaabeeysay noloshaada?”*

<!-- p81 -->
4.  **Dhaqan-gelin Kooxeed (5-10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p82 -->
- Weydii kooxda: *“Sidee ayaan isu dhiirri-gelin karnaa inaan ku tiirsanno dhinacan Ilaah nolosha maalinlaha ah?”*

<!-- p83 -->
- Ka dhig kooxda inay **qoraan duco gaaban** oo ka tarjumaysa sifada, kuna tukadaan wadajir, iyagoo diiradda saaraya dhaqan-gelinta dabeecadda Ilaah xaaladahooda.

## Cutubka 3 - Dembi iyo Furasho

<!-- p85 -->
### Hawl 1: Saamaynta Dembiga ee sida hirarka u fida (Dood iyo Dhaqan-gelin)

<!-- p86 -->
#### **Ujeeddo:**

<!-- p87 -->
Muuji sida **saamaynta dembigu u fido sida hirar** ay u taabato **xiriirrada, bulshooyinka, iyo nolosha ruuxiga ah**, iyadoo la xoojinayo **qorshaha furashada** ee Ilaah.

<!-- p88 -->
### Tilmaamaha:

<!-- p89 -->
1.  **Tusaale Furitaan (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p90 -->
- Ku rid **dhagax baaquli biyo ku jira** (ama sawirka ku sharrax haddii qalabku diyaar ahayn).

<!-- p91 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay **fiiriyaan hirarka** u fidaya dibadda oo ka doodaan:  
      *“Sidee muuqaalkan u muujinayaa saameynta dembiga ee nolosheena iyo xiriirradeenna?”*

<!-- p92 -->
2.  **Dood Kooxo Yaryar (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p93 -->
- U kala qaybi kooxo oo mid kasta u qoondee **tusaale dembiyeed oo Kitaabka Quduuska ah**:

<!-- p94 -->
- **Aadan iyo Xaawo** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Bilowgii 3:6-13</span> (Ceeb iyo kala-go').

<!-- p95 -->
- **Qabiil iyo Haabiil** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Bilowgii 4:3-12</span> (Masayr, dil, iyo masaafurin).

<!-- p96 -->
- **Daa'uud iyo Batsheba** - <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2SA" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Samuu'eel 11:2-27</span> (Sino iyo cawaaqib).

<!-- p97 -->
- Ka doodda:

<!-- p98 -->
- *Waa maxay saamayntii isla markiiba soo baxday iyo tii muddada-dheer ee dembigan?*

<!-- p99 -->
- *Sidee baan uga aragnaa qis kasta nimcada Ilaah iyo fursadda dib-u-soo-celinta?*

<!-- p100 -->
3.  **Ka-fiirsasho (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p101 -->
- Isu keen kooxaha mar kale oo weydii:

<!-- p102 -->
- *Waa maxay qaar ka mid ah “raadka sii faafa” ee dembiga aad si shaqsi ahaan u la kulantay?*

<!-- p103 -->
- *Sidee ayey nimcada Ilaah uga shaqaysay joojinta raadkaas iyo keenista bogsiin?*

<!-- p104 -->
### Hawlgalka 2: Jadwalka Waqtiga Dembiga iyo Soo Celinta (Socod Qorniinka)

<!-- p105 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p106 -->
Raac qorshaha furashada ee Ilaah laga bilaabo Bilowgii ilaa Masiixa, adigoo diiradda saaraya nimcadiisa xilliga xukunka.

<!-- p107 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p108 -->
1.  **U qaybi Kooxo Yaryar** - Koox kasta u qoondee **qayb Qorniinka** oo iftiiminaysa dembi iyo soo celin:

<!-- p109 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 3:21</span>** - Ilaah wuxuu u siiyay Aadan iyo Xawwa daboolo.

<!-- p110 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GEN" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Genesis 4:6-7</span>** - Ilaah wuxuu ka digay Qaabiil ka hor dembigiisa.

<!-- p111 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Exodus 12:13</span>** - Wanka Fasaxa ayaa ilaaliya Israa’iil.

<!-- p112 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LEV" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">LEV 16:21-22</span>** - Riyaha dembiga qaada ayaa dembiga fogeeya.

<!-- p113 -->
- **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">John 1:29</span>** - Ciise, Wanka Ilaah, ayaa dembiga ka qaada.

<!-- p114 -->
2.  **Hawlgalka Kooxda:**

<!-- p115 -->
- Ka wadahadla dembiga ama xukunka ku jira qoraalka.

<!-- p116 -->
- Iftiimiya nimcada ama furashada la bixiyey.

<!-- p117 -->
- Soo koob casharka laga bartay dabeecadda Ilaah.

<!-- p118 -->
3.  **Soo-bandhigid:**

<!-- p119 -->
- Ku dhiirri geli koox kastaa inay wadaagaan aragtiyahooda, iyaga oo qoraallada isu xira una beddelaya jadwal-waqti ee qorshaha Ilaah ee furashada.

<!-- p120 -->
### Waxqabadka 3: Ku Socoshada Cafiska (Adeegsiga Salaad leh)

<!-- p121 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p122 -->
Hoggaami ka-qaybgalayaasha inay **si shakhsi ah u baaraan cafiska**-labadaba **qaadashadiisa** iyo **u fidintiisa** dadka kale.

<!-- p123 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p124 -->
1.  **Milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p125 -->
- Weydii ka-qaybgalayaasha inay **si aamusnaan ah uga milicsadaan** meelaha ay laga yaabo inay:

<!-- p126 -->
- U baahan yihiin inay **qirtaan dembi** oo ay raadiyaan cafiska Ilaah.

<!-- p127 -->
- U baahan yihiin inay **cafiyaan qof kale** oo ku gefay.

<!-- p128 -->
2.  **Waqti Salaad la Hago (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p129 -->
- Akhri **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1JN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Yooxanaa 1:9</span>**

<!-- p130 -->
- Hoggaami ka-qaybgalayaasha salaad gaar ah. Hoggaamiyuhu uma baahna inuu maqlo wada hadalka u dhexeeya qofka iyo Ilaah.

<!-- p131 -->
- Ku qir dembiyada Ilaah hortiisa.

<!-- p132 -->
- U ducee xoog aad dadka kale ku cafiso.

<!-- p133 -->
- Ku dhiirrigeli qorista wixii **ballanqaadyo** ah (tusaale, qof la cafiyo, heshiisiin la raadiyo).

<!-- p134 -->
3.  **Milicsi Gabagabo (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p135 -->
- Akhri **<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="103" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuurrada 103:12</span>**

<!-- p136 -->
- Ku casuum ka-qaybgalayaasha inay wadaagaan (ikhtiyaari) sida cafisku u keeno xorriyad iyo soo celin.

## Cutubka 4 - Ciise-Masiixa

<!-- p138 -->
### Waxqabadka 1: Magacyada iyo Cinwaannada Ciise (Milicsi iyo Daraasad Qorniinka)

<!-- p139 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p140 -->
Sahamiya **magacyada iyo cinwaannada Ciise** iyo sida ay u muujinayaan howshiisa sidii Masiix.

<!-- p141 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p142 -->
1.  **U qaybsama Kooxo Yaryar.** Koox kasta u qoondee mid ka mid ah cinwaannadan Ciise:

<!-- p143 -->
- **Cimmaanu'eel** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 1:23</span>) - *Ilaah waa inala jira.*

<!-- p144 -->
- **Wanka Ilaah** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:29</span>) - *Allabari dembiga.*

<!-- p145 -->
- **Boqorka Boqorrada** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 19:16</span>) - *Awoodda ugu sarraysa.*

<!-- p146 -->
- **Wadaadka Sare** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 4:14-16</span>) - *Dhexdhexaadiyaha Ilaah iyo dadka.*

<!-- p147 -->
- **Ereyga** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 1:1</span>) - *Muujinta Ilaah.*

<!-- p148 -->
2.  **Shaqada Kooxda:**

<!-- p149 -->
- Ka fiiriya Qorniinka la xidhiidha cinwaanka.

<!-- p150 -->
- Ka dooda waxa cinwaanku ka muujinayo howsha Ciise.

<!-- p151 -->
- La wadaag **hal qodob** sida cinwaankani ugu dabaqmo iimaankiinna shakhsiyeed.

<!-- p152 -->
3.  **Gunaanad:**

<!-- p153 -->
- Koox kasta ha wadaagto milicsigooda, iyagoo xoogga saaraya sida aqoonsiga Ciise uu u fuliyo howshiisa sidii Masiix.

<!-- p154 -->
### Hawlgalka 2: Howsha Ciise oo La Fulisay (Jadwal-waqti iyo Dood)

<!-- p155 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p156 -->
Raac waqtiyada muhiimka ah ee howsha Ciise si aad u **xiriiriso saadaasha iyo rumoobiddeeda**.

<!-- p157 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p158 -->

## Cutubka 5 - Toobad ka noqoshada camallada dhintay

<!-- p180 -->
### Hawl 1: Aqoonsiga Shuqullada Dhimatay (Is-milicsi iyo Dood Kooxeed)

<!-- p181 -->
#### **Ujeeddo:**

<!-- p182 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay aqoonsadaan oo wax ka qabtaan “shuqullada dhimatay” ee noloshooda, iyagoo ku beddelaya ficillo uu Ruuxa Quduuska ahi hoggaamiyo.

<!-- p183 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p184 -->
1.  **Milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p185 -->
- Sii ka-qaybgalayaasha **liis ku saabsan shuqullada dhimatay ee suurtagalka ah** (hoos ka eeg).

<!-- p186 -->
- Ka codso inay si aamusan u milicsadaan oo ay aqoonsadaan **hal ama laba meelood** halka ay ka arkaan caddayn shuqullada dhimatay oo noloshooda ku jira.

<!-- p187 -->
2.  **Liiska Shuqullada Dhimatay ee Suurtagalka ah:**

<!-- p188 -->
- **Caadooyin Diimeed oo Aan Iimaan Lahayn:**

<!-- p189 -->
- Ka-qaybgalka adeegyada kaniisadda caado darteed, iyada oo aan qalbiga la hawlgelin.

<!-- p190 -->
- Samaynta falal diimeed si loo kasbado oggolaansho halkii ay ka iman lahayd jacayl Ilaah dartii.

<!-- p191 -->
- **Isku-tiirsanaan:**

<!-- p192 -->
- Isku dayga in xaqnimo lagu gaaro dadaal shaqsiyeed halkii lagu tiirsanaan lahaa nimcada Ilaah.

<!-- p193 -->
- Diiradda saaridda shuqullo wanaagsan si loo helo raalli ahaansho, iyadoo la iska indha-tirayo doorka Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p194 -->
- **Qaabab ama Dabeecado Dembiyeed:**

<!-- p195 -->
- Haynta calool-xumo ama diidmada cafiska.

<!-- p196 -->
- U oggolaanshaha kibir, masayr, ama qadhaadh inay ka taliyaan xiriirrada.

<!-- p197 -->
- **Ku-qanacsanaan:**

<!-- p198 -->
- Dayacaidda edbiyadaha ruuxiga ah sida salaadda, barashada Baybalka, ama cibaadada.

<!-- p199 -->
- Ku deggenaanshaha danayn la’aan ruuxi ah, iyadoo la luminayo xamaasadda Masiixa.

<!-- p200 -->
- **Caadooyin aan midho dhalin:**

<!-- p201 -->
- Ku bixinta waqti aad u badan mashquuliyado ama hiwaayado aan Ilaah lagu ammaanin.

<!-- p202 -->
- Raadinta hamiyo is-nafsiyeed iyadoo la dayacayo u adeegidda dadka kale.

<!-- p203 -->
- **Camallo ay kaxeeyaan cabsi ama dambi-dareen:**

<!-- p204 -->
- U adeegidda dadka kale waajibnimo darteed halkii ay ka ahaan lahayd jacayl.

<!-- p205 -->
- Bixinta dhaqaale ama iskaa-wax-u-qabso si loo buuxiyo shuruud oo keliya halkii ay ka ahaan lahayd addeecid farxad leh.

<!-- p206 -->
- **Mudnaanno khaldan:**

<!-- p207 -->
- Diiradda saarista guusha maaddi ah halkii laga hormarin lahaa korriinka ruuxiga ah.

<!-- p208 -->
- Ku maalgelinta waqti iyo tamar hawlo adduunyo oo aan lahayn qiime waara.

<!-- p209 -->
3.  **Wadahadal Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p210 -->
- Ka qaybgalayaasha u kala qaybi kooxo yaryar si ay ula wadaagaan (haddii ay ku raaxaysanayaan) hal qodob oo ay aqoonsadeen inuu yahay camal dhintay.

<!-- p211 -->
- Ka wada hadla tallaabooyin wax-ku-ool ah oo lagu beddelayo camallo dhintay falal uu Ruuxa Quduuska ahi kaxeeyo.

<!-- p212 -->
4.  **Milicsi Gunaanad (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p213 -->
- Ka qaybgalayaasha ku casuun inay la wadaagaan qodobbada muhiimka ah ama tallaabooyinka ay qaadi doonaan si ay u wajahaan camallo dhintay.

<!-- p214 -->
- Ku soo gabagabee duco hanuunka Ilaah si uu u hoggaamiyo nolol midho-dhal leh.

- *Soo-jeedin: “Waa maxay xaaladdu? Sidee baad ku ogaatay inuu ahaa Ruuxa Quduuska ah oo kula hadlayay?” “Maxay kaa dareensiisay?”*

<!-- p336 -->
2.  ***Wadaagista Kooxda (10 Daqiiqo):***

<!-- p337 -->
- *U kala qaybi kooxo yaryar oo ay wadaagaan milicsiyadooda.*

## Cutubka 9 - Kaniisadda iyo Kaalinteenna Gudahaheeda

<!-- p345 -->

<!-- p346 -->

<!-- p347 -->
3.  **Wadaagista Aragtiyada:** Koox kasta ha u soo bandhigto kooxda oo dhan natiijooyinkooda.

<!-- p348 -->
### Hawl 2: Ka caawinta kuwa kale inay helaan doorkooda Kaniisadda (Baadhitaan Wax-ku-ool ah)

<!-- p349 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p351 -->

<!-- p353 -->

<!-- p354 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay is-milicsadaan: *“Hibooyin ama karti noocee ah ayuu Ilaah i siiyay? Halkee ayaan ka dareemaa inaan ugu wax-ku-oolka badan ama ugu farxad badanahay adeegidda?” “Hibooyin iyo karti noocee ah ayaan ku arkaa dadka halkan ila jooga?”*

<!-- p356 -->
- U kala baxa kooxo yaryar.

<!-- p357 -->

<!-- p358 -->
3.  **Qorshe Ficil (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p359 -->

<!-- p361 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p366 -->
- **Gudaha u jeeda:** Dhisidda jidhka Masiixa.

<!-- p367 -->

<!-- p368 -->
2.  **Fikrad-ururin Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p369 -->
- Koox kastaa ha ka dooddo siyaabo wax-ku-ool ah oo ay uga hirgeliyaan hawshooda loo qoondeeyey gudaha kaniisadda iyo bulshada.

<!-- p370 -->
- Tusaalooyin: Martigelinta habeen cibaado (kor u jeeda), abaabulidda koox yar (gudaha u jeeda), iskaa-wax-u-qabso ka noqoshada dhacdo wacyigelin bulsho (dibadda u jeeda).

<!-- p371 -->
3.  **Wadaagista Kooxaha (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p372 -->

## Cutubka 10 - Quduus la dhigay - Loo gooniyeeyey Ujeeddada Ilaah

<!-- p374 -->
### Waxqabadka 1: Ku Kalsoonow Geeddi-socodka Quduusyaynta (Dhiirrigelin iyo Duco)

<!-- p375 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p376 -->
In lagu xaqiijiyo ka-qaybgalayaasha in quduusyayntu ay tahay safar nolosha oo dhan ah, lana dhiirrigeliyo inay ku kalsoonaadaan waqtiga iyo shaqada Ilaah.

<!-- p377 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p378 -->
1.  **Akhri Qorniinka (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p379 -->
- Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="PHP" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Filiboy 1:6</span> (*“Kan idinku bilaabay shaqo wanaagsan wuxuu gaarsiin doonaa dhammaad ilaa maalinta Ciise Masiix.”*).

<!-- p380 -->
2.  **Dood Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p381 -->
- Weydii: *“Aayaddan sidee ayay inoo dhiirigelisaa marka quduusaysiintu u muuqato mid gaabis ah?”*

<!-- p382 -->
- Ka dooda: *“Maxay yihiin siyaabo wax-ku-ool ah oo aan nafteena ugu xusuusin karno in aan Ilaah ku kalsoonaano geeddi-socodkan?”*

<!-- p383 -->
3.  **Wareeg Duco (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p384 -->
- Ku soo gabagabee ku ducaynta midba midka kale, si gaar ahna uga baryaya samir, kalsooni, iyo farxad safarka quduusaysiinta.

<!-- p385 -->
### Hawl 2: Ka Saaridda Caadooyinka Xun (Milicsi iyo Wacyigelinta Kiciyeyaasha)

<!-- p386 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p387 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha in ay aqoonsadaan caadooyinka xun ee carqaladeeya quduusaysiinta, ayna bartaan in ay garan karaan kiciyeyaasha iyo tilmaamaha keena dabeecadahaas.

<!-- p388 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p389 -->
1.  **Milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p390 -->
- Sii warqad shaqo oo leh su'aalahan kicinta ah:

<!-- p391 -->
- *“Waa maxay caadada ama dabeecadda hor istaagaysa korriinkaaga ruuxiga ah?”*

<!-- p392 -->
- *“Xaalado, shucuur, ama fikirro noocee ah ayaaba badanaa horseeda caadadan?”* (aqoonsiga kiciyeyaasha/tilmaamaha).

<!-- p393 -->
- Tusaalooyin kiciyeyaasha oo la tixgeliyo: walbahaar, caajis, cadaadiska saaxiibbada, waqtiga maalinta, ama goobo gaar ah.

<!-- p394 -->
2.  **Liiska Tusaalooyinka Caadooyinka Xun:**

<!-- p395 -->
- **Cabasho ama xan** - Ka hadlidda dadka si taban halkii naxariis loo muujin lahaa.

<!-- p396 -->
- **Falcelin caro ama samir-la'aan** - Xanaaq degdeg ah ama jawaabo aan naxariis lahayn marka xaaladdu tahay mid walbahaar leh.

<!-- p397 -->
- **Isticmaal xad-dhaaf ah oo baraha bulshada ama madadaalo** - Ku bixinta waqti xad-dhaaf ah mashquullo ka hortaga korriinka ruuxiga ah ee macno leh.

<!-- p398 -->
- **Ka fogaanshaha wada sheekaysiyo adag** - In aan la wajahinin khilaafaadka xiriirrada, taas oo keenta xiisad aan la xallin.

<!-- p399 -->
- **Dib-u-dhigis ama caajisnimo** - Dib-u-dhigid waajibaadka ama dayaca hawlaha ujeeddo leh.

<!-- p400 -->
- **Ku dhegganaanshaha ciil ama nacayb** - Inaad u oggolaato cafis-la’aanta inay ka xidid samaysato qalbigaaga.

<!-- p401 -->
- **Ku xadgudbidda cunno, wax iibsasho, ama raaxooyin kale** - U jeedsashada kuwaas si aad ula tacaasho halkii aad ka raadin lahayd nabadda Ilaah.

<!-- p402 -->
- **Ku qufulnaanta fikirro taban** - U oggolaanshaha welwel, cabsi, ama masayr inay maankaaga maamulaan.

<!-- p403 -->
3.  **Wadahadal Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p404 -->
- U qaybi kooxo yaryar. Ka-qaybgal kasta ha wadaago hal caado oo uu doonayo inuu ka tirtiro iyo kiciyeyaasha keena (haddii uu raalli ka yahay).

<!-- p405 -->
- Ka dood: *“Maxaad samayn kartaa si aad u joojiso ama uga fogaato kiciyeyaashan?”* (tusaale ahaan, hakad qaado oo ducee, degaankaaga beddel, raadi isla-xisaabtan).

<!-- p406 -->
4.  **Gunaanad (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p407 -->
- Ku dhiirrigeli ka-qaybgalayaasha inay ka go’aan qaataan in caadada taban lagu beddelo mid wanaagsan (eeg Hawsha 2).

<!-- p408 -->
- U ducee hanuunka iyo xoogga Ilaah si loo garto loona jebiyo caadooyinkan.

<!-- p409 -->
### Hawsha 3: Dhisidda Caadooyin Wanaagsan (Dhisidda Qaabab iyo Abaal-marinno Cusub)

<!-- p410 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p411 -->
Hag ka-qaybgalayaasha inay abuuraan caadooyin wanaagsan oo waafaqsan safarkooda quduusaysi, iyagoo adkeynaya ficillada joogtada ah iyo abaal-marinnadooda ruuxiga ah.

<!-- p412 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p413 -->
1.  **Milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p414 -->
- Weydii ka-qaybgalayaasha inay ka fekeraan meelaha ay ruux ahaan ka kori karaan oo ay tixgeliyaan caadooyin ay hirgelin karaan.

<!-- p415 -->
- Su’aal: *“Waa maxay caado aan ku dari karo si aan u adkeeyo socodkayga Ilaah ama aan ugu muujiyo dabeecaddiisa nolol-maalmeedkayga?”*

<!-- p416 -->
2.  **Liiska Tusaalooyinka Caadooyin Wanaagsan:**

<!-- p417 -->
- **Duceynta maalinlaha ah** - Inaad waqti gooni ah u qoondeyso inaad Ilaah kula xirmato bilowga ama dhammaadka maalintaada.

<!-- p418 -->
- **Akhri ama baro Baybalka** - Inaad dhigato waqti joogto ah oo aad akhrido, ka fiirsato, oo aad ku dhaqanto Qorniinka.

<!-- p419 -->
- **Qorista mahadnaqa** - Maalin kasta qor saddex shay oo aad uga mahadcelinayso si aad u beddesho diiraddaada.

<!-- p420 -->
- **U adeegidda dadka kale** - Helidda siyaabo yaryar oo joogto ah oo lagu caawiyo ama lagu dhiirrigeliyo qof baahan.

<!-- p421 -->
- **Ku hadlidda ereyo xaqiijin ah** - Si ula kac ah u dhiirrigelinta xubin qoys, saaxiib, ama qof aad wada shaqeysaan.

<!-- p422 -->
- **Xifdinta Qorniinka** - Barashada hal aayad toddobaadkii si loogu milicsado runta Ilaah.

<!-- p423 -->
- **Ku dhaqanka aamusnaanta ama kelinimada** - Qaadashada dhowr daqiiqo maalin kasta si si aamusnaan ah loogu fariisto looguna dhagaysto codka Ilaah.

<!-- p424 -->
- **Ka-qaybgalidda wadajirka** - Si joogto ah uga soo qaybgal koox yar ama kaniisad si loo dhiso xiriirro iyo isla xisaabtan.

<!-- p425 -->
3.  **Dhisidda Caadada (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p426 -->
- Hag ka-qaybgalayaasha inay u kala jebiyaan caadada saddex qaybood:

<!-- p427 -->
- **Ficil:** Ku qeex caadada si cad (tusaale, *“Ku bixi 5 daqiiqo salaad subax kasta ka hor quraacda.”*).

<!-- p428 -->
- **Kiciyayaal:** Aqoonsada waxa xasuusin doona inay dhaqtaan caadada (tusaale, waqtiga maalinta, dhigista Baybal miiska sariirta dhinaceeda).

<!-- p429 -->
- **Abaalmarinno:** Ka milicsada abaalmarinnada ruuxiga ah ee joogtaynta (tusaale, *“Tani waxay i siin doontaa nabad waxayna i caawin doontaa inaan Ilaah si qoto dheer ugu kalsoonaado.”*).

<!-- p430 -->
4.  **Wadaagis Kooxeed (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p431 -->
- Isku-lammaane ama kooxo yaryar, ka-qaybgalayaashu waxay wadaagaan caadada ay rabaan inay aasaasaan iyo qorshahooda hirgelinta.

<!-- p432 -->
5.  **Xiritaanka:**

<!-- p433 -->
- Ku dhiirrigeli ka-qaybgalayaasha inay qoraan caadadooda cusub oo ay la wadaagaan lamaane isla xisaabtan si loo dhiirrigeliyo ku-daba-galidda.

## Cutubka 11 - Baabtiisyada-Biyo iyo Ruux

<!-- p435 -->
### Waxqabadka 1: Sahaminta Muhiimadda Baabtiiska (Milicsi iyo Wadaagis)

#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p438 -->
#### Tilmaamaha:

- Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span> iyo <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 19:1-6</span>.

<!-- p443 -->

<!-- p444 -->
- *“Baabtiisku adiga shaqsiyan muxuu kaaga dhigan yahay?”*

<!-- p446 -->
3.  **Tukasho iyo Dhiirigelin (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p449 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

- Bixi soo-jeedinno:

3.  **Dhiirigelin Gunaanad (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p460 -->

<!-- p461 -->
### Hawl 3: Astaanta Baabtiiska (Jimicsi Is-dhexgal ah oo Hal-abuur leh)

<!-- p462 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p463 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay sahamiyaan astaanta hodanka ah ee baabtiiska oo ay ku xidhmaan safarkooda iimaanka shakhsiga ah.

<!-- p464 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p465 -->
1.  **Sahaminta Qorniinka (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p466 -->
- Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 6:3-4</span> (*“Sidaa darteed, baabtiiska dhexdiisa waxaa nala aasay isaga dhimasho si, sida Masiixu ammaanta Aabbaha awgeed looga soo sara kiciyey kuwii dhintay, annaguna aynu nolol cusub ku noolaano.”*).

<!-- p467 -->
- Ka dood: *“Maxay qaybaha baabtiiska-biyo, quusin, kacitaan-u astaan u yihiin rumaystayaasha?”*

<!-- p468 -->
2.  **Jimicsiga Astaanta (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p469 -->
- Siiya ka-qaybgalayaasha liis ka kooban astaamaha baabtiiska iyo macnahooda:

<!-- p470 -->

<!-- p471 -->
2.  **Quusin** - Geeri iyo aasin; u dhiman naftii hore iyo dabeecadda dembileed.

<!-- p472 -->
3.  **Ka-soo-bixid Biyaha** - Sarakicid iyo nolol cusub; midnimo la leh sarakicidda Masiixa.

<!-- p473 -->
4.  **Dhar** - In lagu lebisto Masiixa, xaqnimo, iyo aqoonsi cusub.

<!-- p474 -->
5.  **Iftiin** - Iftiimid iyo hoggaamin; ku socodka iftiinka Masiixa.

<!-- p475 -->
6.  **Shaabad** - Lahaansho iyo ka tirsanaan; lagu calaamadeeyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah ee qoyska axdiga Ilaah.

<!-- p476 -->
7.  **Saliid** - Saliidayn iyo awood-siis; loo gooni-yeelid ujeeddooyinka Ilaah.

<!-- p477 -->
8.  **Qoolley** - Joogitaanka Ruuxa Quduuska ah; nabad, daahirnimo, iyo awood-siis loogu adeegayo.

<!-- p478 -->
9.  **Iskutallaab** - Midnimo la leh allabariga Masiixa; is-dhiibid rabitaanka Ilaah iyo ka-go’naansho maalinle ah.

<!-- p479 -->
10. **Bulsho** - Ka mid noqoshada Jidhka Masiixa; wada-jir, isla xisaabtan, iyo howlgal la wadaago.

<!-- p480 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay ka milicsadaan astaanta si ugu badan ula jaanqaadda safarkooda ruuxiga ah iyo sababta.

<!-- p481 -->
- Ikhtiyaari ah: Sii kaarar tusmo ama warqado si ay u qoraan bayaan kooban oo ku saabsan sida baabtiisku u saameeyey iimaankooda.

<!-- p482 -->
3.  **Wadaagista Kooxda (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p483 -->
- Ha ka-qaybgalayaashu ku wadaagaan milicsigooda kooxo yaryar ama laba-labo. Ku dhiirrigeli inay ka doodaan sida ay astaanta baabtiiska ugu qaadan karaan nolol-maalmeedkooda.

<!-- p484 -->
4.  **Ikhtiyaar Hal-abuur ah:**

<!-- p485 -->
- Haddii waqti jiro, ku casuum ka-qaybgalayaasha inay sameeyaan matalaad muuqaal ah (sawir, daruur erayo, ama sawir fudud) oo waxa baabtiisku uga dhigan yahay iyaga iyadoo lagu salaynayo astaamaha la falanqeeyey.

## Cutubka 12 - Xerti-nimo

<!-- p487 -->
### Hawl 1: Aqoonsashada Wareegga Saamayntaada (Milicsi iyo Wadaagid)

<!-- p488 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p489 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay gartaan dadka Ilaah ku geliyey noloshooda si ay xerti-nimo ugu barbaariyaan uguna dhiirrigeliyaan inay tallaabo qaadaan.

<!-- p490 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p491 -->
1.  **Milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p492 -->
- Qof-qof ahaan: Warqad gudaheeda, ku sawir saddex wareeg oo isku bartamaha ah oo loogu magac daray:

<!-- p493 -->

<!-- p494 -->
- **Wareegga Dhexe:** Xubnaha kaniisadda ama bulshada.

<!-- p495 -->
- **Wareegga Dibedda:** Dad aad barasho-kooban leedihiin, shaqa-wadaagayaal, ama deris.

<!-- p496 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay qoraan magacyada dadka ay si joogto ah ula falgalaan.

<!-- p497 -->
2.  **Wadaagista Kooxda (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p498 -->
- Laba-labo, ka wadaag hal magac wareeg kasta, kadibna talo urursada siyaabaha aad ugu hawlgelin kartaan dadkaas wada sheeko ruuxi ah oo macno leh.

<!-- p499 -->
3.  **Ballanqaad iyo Duco (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p500 -->
- Ka dhig ka-qaybgalayaashu inay ballanqaadaan hal tallaabo ficil oo loogu talagalay hal qof toddobaadkan (tusaale ahaan, inay ugu casuumaan qaxwo, inay bilaabaan barashada Kitaabka Quduuska ah, ama inay u soo bandhigaan inay u duceeyaan).

<!-- p501 -->
- Ku soo gabagabee duco geesinimo iyo xikmad.

<!-- p502 -->
### Hawl 2: Astaamaha Xerta-Sameeyaha (Dood-fakar Isdhexgal ah)

<!-- p503 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p504 -->
Ku dhiirrigeli ka-qaybgalayaasha inay ka fiirsadaan sifooyinka ka dhiga xerta-sameeye wax-ku-ool ah oo ay gartaan meelaha ay shakhsi ahaan ka kori karaan.

<!-- p505 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p506 -->
1.  **Dood-fakar (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p507 -->
- Ku qoro sifooyinka soo socda sabuurad cad ama warqad la qaybinayo:

<!-- p508 -->
- Samir, tawadduc, diiradda xiriirka, kas-u-yeelasho, daacadnimo, iyo geesinimo.

<!-- p509 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay ku daraan sifooyin kale oo ay rumaysan yihiin inay muhiim yihiin.

<!-- p510 -->
2.  **Is-milicsi (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p511 -->
- Ka codso ka-qaybgalayaasha inay doortaan hal ama laba sifo oo ay hadda muujiyaan iyo mid ay jeclaan lahaayeen inay ku koraan.

<!-- p512 -->
3.  **Wadaagista Kooxda iyo Dhiirrigelinta (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p513 -->
- Kooxo yaryar ahaan, ha wadaagaan doorashadooda oo ha ka doodaan sida sifooyinkan loo horumarin karo iyada oo loo marayo duco, ku-dhaqan, iyo isla-xisaabtan.

<!-- p514 -->
### Hawl 3: Abuuridda Qorshe Samaynta Xerta (Ku-dhaqan Wax-ku-ool ah)

<!-- p515 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p516 -->
Ku qalabee ka-qaybgalayaasha inay horumariyaan qorshe fudud oo la fulin karo si ay u xertiyeeyaan qof noloshooda ku jira.

<!-- p517 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p518 -->
1.  **Qaab-dhismeedka Qorshaha:** Sii warqad shaqo oo leh su'aalaha soo socda:

<!-- p519 -->
- **Yaa?** Magacaabi qofka aad dareemayso in laguu hanuuniyey inaad xertiyeeyso.

<!-- p520 -->
- **Sidee?** Aqoonso hal dariiqo oo aad toddobaadkan ula xiriiri karto iyaga (tusaale ahaan, ku martiqaad qado, ka wada hadla mawduuc ruuxi ah).

<!-- p521 -->
- **Waa maxay?** Go'aanso qalab ama mawduuc oo hagi doona waqtigiinna wada jir (tusaale ahaan, su'aal la waydiiyo, buug ka mid ah Baybalka, duco, ama ka hadlid caqabadaha nolosha). Tani looma baahna inay noqoto mid aad u hab-raac badan; ha ka soo qulqulato si dabiici ah xiriirka aad la leedahay iyaga!

<!-- p522 -->
2.  **Wadaagid Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p523 -->
- La wadaag qorshayaasha lammaane si loogu helo dhiirigelin iyo isla xisaabtan.

<!-- p524 -->
3.  **Duco loogu talagalay Ficil (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p525 -->
- U wada duceeya koox ahaan geesinimo iyo hanuun si loo hirgeliyo qorshayaashooda.

## Cutubka 13 - Awood Ruuxi

<!-- p527 -->
### Hawsha 1: Aqoonsiga Awoodda Ruuxiga ah ee Qorniinka (Jimicsi Daraasadda Baybalka)

<!-- p528 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p529 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay aqoonsadaan tusaalooyin awood ruuxi ah oo ku jira Baybalka oo ay ka fiirsadaan sida ay noloshooda ugu dabaqi karaan.

<!-- p530 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p531 -->
1.  **Daraasad Kooxeed:**

<!-- p532 -->
- U kala qaybiya kooxo yaryar oo u qoondee koox kasta qayb muujinaysa awood ruuxi ah. Adeegso liiska hoose ee la ballaariyey si kala-duwanaansho badan loo helo.

<!-- p533 -->
2.  **Liis la ballaariyey oo Qoraallo Quduus ah (Tusaalooyin Ciise ama Xertiisa oo adeegsanaya Awood):**

<!-- p534 -->
- **Ciise:**

<!-- p535 -->
- Dejinta duufaanta: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="35-41" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 4:35-41</span>.*

<!-- p536 -->
- Saaridda jinni gudaha sinagogga: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21-28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 1:21-28</span>.*

<!-- p537 -->
- Bogsiinta addoonkii taliyaha boqolka: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 8:5-13</span>.*

<!-- p538 -->
- Soo noolaynta Laasaros oo dhintay: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="38-44" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 11:38-44</span>.*

<!-- p539 -->
- Quudinta 5,000: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="13-21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 14:13-21</span>.*

<!-- p540 -->
- **Xerti:**

<!-- p541 -->
- Bogsiinta nin curyaan ah: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 3:1-10</span>.*

<!-- p544 -->
- Butros oo Tabiita (Dorcas) ka soo sara kiciyey kuwii dhintay: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="9" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="36-42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 9:36-42</span>.*

<!-- p545 -->
- Bawlos oo ka badbaaday qaniinyada abeeso: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 28:3-6</span>.*

<!-- p546 -->
3.  **Dood:**

<!-- p547 -->
- Koox kasta ka codso inay ka doodaan:

<!-- p548 -->
- *“Sheekadan maxay inaga bartaa ku saabsan awoodda ruuxiga ah?”*

<!-- p549 -->
- *“Sidee tusaalahan ugu dabaqi karnaa nolosheena maanta?”*

<!-- p550 -->
4.  **Wadaagid:**

<!-- p551 -->
- Koox kastaa ha u bandhigto natiijooyinkooda kooxda weyn.

<!-- p552 -->
### Hawl 2: Fahamka Aagga Awooddaada (Milicsi iyo Wadaagid)

<!-- p553 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p554 -->
Ka dhiirigeli ka qaybgalayaasha inay aqoonsadaan meelaha saameyn iyo awood ee Ilaah u dhiibay.

<!-- p555 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p556 -->
1.  **Milicsi:**

<!-- p557 -->
- Mid kasta si gooni ah warqad ku qoro, kuna jawaab su'aalahan:

<!-- p558 -->
- *“Maxay yihiin meelaha saameyn ee Ilaah igu aaminay (tusaale, qoys, shaqo, kaniisad, bulsho)?”*

<!-- p559 -->
- *“Sideen awoodda ruuxiga ah ugu adeegsan karaa inaan Boqortooyada Ilaah ku keeno meelahan?”*

<!-- p560 -->
2.  **Dood Kooxeed:**

<!-- p561 -->
- La wadaag aragtiyo laba-labo ama kooxo yaryar.

<!-- p562 -->
3.  **Ducada:**

<!-- p563 -->
- Ku soo gabagabee duco kooxeed oo ka qaybgalayaasha lagu xilsaaryo inay si kalsooni leh ugu socdaan meelaha ay awood ku leeyihiin.

<!-- p566 -->
**Tilmaamo:**

<!-- p567 -->
1.  **Daraasad Kitaabka Quduuska ah:**

<!-- p568 -->
- U qoondee qoraallo ku saabsan malaa’igaha. Adeegso liiska la ballaariyey ee hoose si aad u sahamiso si qoto-dheer.

<!-- p569 -->
2.  **Liiska la Ballaariyey ee Aayadaha ku Saabsan Malaa’igaha:**

<!-- p570 -->
- **Malaa’igaha oo Ilaalinaya oo Hanuuninaya:**

<!-- p571 -->
- Ilaalinta rumaystayaasha: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="91" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuur 91:11-12</span>.*

<!-- p572 -->
- Ilaalinta Elishaa iyo adeegiisii: *<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2KI" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2KI 6:15-17</span>.*

<!-- p585 -->

## Cutubka 14 - Heegan ahaaw

<!-- p588 -->
### Hawl 1: Aragti Weligeed Ah (Fikir-wadaag Is-dhexgal ah)

<!-- p589 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p590 -->
Ka caawi ka-qaybgalayaasha inay diiradda saaraan abadka oo ay noloshooda la waafajiyaan mudnaanta weligeed ah.

<!-- p591 -->
#### Tilmaamo:

<!-- p592 -->
1.  **Fikir-wadaag (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p593 -->
- Qor “Kumeelgaar” iyo “Weligeed” sabuurad cad ama warqad weyn.

<!-- p594 -->
- Weydii ka-qaybgalayaasha inay taxaan waxyaalaha nolosha ka mid ah ee ku jira qayb kasta.

<!-- p595 -->
2.  **Dood Kooxeed (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p596 -->
- Ka dooda:

<!-- p597 -->
- *“Sideen ugu maalgelin karnaa waqti, tamar, iyo kheyraad badan waxyaalaha weligeed ah?”*

<!-- p598 -->
- *“Maxay yihiin qaar ka mid ah siyaabaha wax-ku-oolka ah ee loogu noolaan karo aragti weligeed ah?”*

<!-- p599 -->
3.  **Duco iyo Ballanqaad (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p600 -->
- Ku soo gabagabee salaad kooxeed, adigoo Ilaah ka baryaya inuu ka caawiyo dhammaan inay diiradda saaraan waxa ugu muhiimsan oo ay ku noolaadaan diyaar u ahaanshaha Imaanshihiisa.

<!-- p601 -->
### Hawl 2: Masalka Tobanka Bikrood (Daraasad iyo Milicsi Kitaabka Quduuska ah)

<!-- p602 -->
#### Ujeeddo:

<!-- p603 -->
Gundhig uga sii gal masalka Tobanka Bikrood si aad u baarto diyaargarowga ruuxiga ah.

<!-- p604 -->
#### Tilmaamaha:

<!-- p605 -->
1.  **Akhri Qorniinka Quduuska ah (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p606 -->
- Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 25:1-13</span> si cod dheer koox ahaan.

<!-- p607 -->
2.  **Dood (10 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p608 -->
- Weydii su'aalahan:

<!-- p609 -->
- *“Maxay saliiddu masalkan u taagan tahay?”*

<!-- p610 -->
- *“Waa maxay micnaha ay leedahay in la noqdo xikmad leh halkii nacas laga ahaan lahaa marka la diyaargaroomayo soo laabashada Masiixa?”*

<!-- p611 -->
- *“Sideen ku xaqiijin karnaa in laambadeennu ay buuxaan?”*

<!-- p612 -->
3.  **Dhaqan-gelinta (5 Daqiiqo):**

<!-- p613 -->
- Ku dhiirrigeli ka-qaybgalayaasha inay qoraan hal dhinac oo noloshooda ruuxiga ah (tusaale, salaadda, barashada Qorniinka, u adeegidda kuwa kale) halka ay rabaan inay “dib u buuxiyaan laambaddooda.”

<!-- p614 -->
- Ku wada duceeya koox ahaan hanuunka iyo awood-siinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah.
