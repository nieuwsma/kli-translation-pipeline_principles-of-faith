# Cutubka 9 - Kaniisadda &amp; Doorkeenna Gudahdeeda

<!-- p1 -->
Kaniisaddu waa sida Eebbe isu muujiyo dhulka—iyada oo dadkiisa samaynaya shaqadiisa. Waxaa naloo yeedhay in aan noqono jidhkiisa; annagu waxaan nahay gacmihiisa iyo cagihiisa si aan u fulinno shaqadiisa iyo ujeeddadiisa. Haddii dadaalkeennu yaraado, goosashadu waxay ku sii hadhi doontaa beerta oo lama goosan doono.

<!-- p2 -->
## Kaniisaddu waa ayo?

<!-- p3 -->
Kaniisadda, ***Ekklesia** (eh-klee-SEE-ah - **G1577**),* waxay ka soo jeeddaa laba erey oo Giriig ah:

<!-- p4 -->
- **"Ek"**: "Ka soo bax."

<!-- p5 -->
- **"Kaleo"**: "In la waco."

<!-- p6 -->
Isku wada, **Ekklesia** macne ahaan waxay la micno tahay "kuwa loo yeedhay" ama "shir loo yeedhay." Magaalo-dawladdii hore ee Giriigga, waxay tixraacaysay isu-imaatin muwaadiniin oo loogu yeedhay ujeeddo gaar ah, sida kulammo sharci-dejin ama garsoor. Waxay ahayd eray madani ah oo tilmaamaya isu-imaatin dad.

<!-- p7 -->
Axdiga Cusub gudaheed, inta badan waxaa lagu isticmaalaa in lagu tilmaamo bulshada rumaystayaasha Ciise Masiix. Waxay muujin kartaa:

<!-- p8 -->
- **Kaniisadda Deegaanka**: Isu-imaatin gaar ah oo rumaystayaal meel go'an ah (tusaale, kaniisaddii Korintos, Filibi, iwm.).

<!-- p9 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p10 -->
> **<u>1 Korintos 1:2</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Ee kaniisadda Ilaah ee Korintos, kuwa lagu quduusiyey Masiix Ciise, oo loo yeedhay inay noqdaan quduusiin, iyo kuwa oo dhan ee meel kasta ku dhawaaqaya magaca Rabbigeenna Ciise Masiix, kan iyaga iyo innagaba ah:
>

<!-- p11 -->
</span>

<!-- p12 -->
- **Kaniisadda Caalamiga ah**: Jidhka wadajirka ah ee dhammaan rumaystayaasha waqti iyo meel kasta.

<!-- p13 -->
<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="fragment" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

<!-- p14 -->
> **<u>Matayos 16:18</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Aniguna waxaan kuu leeyahay in adigu tahay Butros, oo dhagaxan korkiisaan ka dhisi doonaa kaniisaddayda, oo irridaha Hadesna kama adkaan doonaan.
>

<!-- p15 -->
</span>

<!-- p16 -->
Kaniisaddu waa dadka, ma aha dhismaha ama goobta ay ku kulmaan. **Kuwa Loo Yeedhay**: Waxay ka tarjumaysaa fikradda ah in Masiixiyiintu laga soo yeedhay dunida oo loo kala saaray ujeeddooyinka Ilaah (tusaale, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:9</span>) hawl iyo ujeeddo gaar ah dartood (amarka weyne). Kaniisadda si farsamo ahaan waa laba ama ka badan oo rumaystayaal ah oo wada kulma (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="18" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 18:20</span>). Caadi ahaan, kaniisaddu waxay isu abaabushaa kulan joogto ah, oo laga yaabo meel iyo waqti la dejiyey, laakiin meel kasta oo laba ama ka badan ay ku kulmaan magacii Ciise, halkaas baa kaniisaddu ka jirtaa!

<!-- p17 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa aasaasay Ciise, waxayna sii kortay qarnigii 1aad Miilaadiga iyada oo ay shaqo ka geysteen xerta raacday. Kaniisadda aan maanta ku jirno oo aan la kulanno dhammaan jidhkeeda deegaan ee dunida dacalladeeda waa natiijo toos ah oo ka dhalatay shaqadii asalka ahayd ee Masiix iyo rasuulliintiisa. Annaga oo rumaystayaal ah waa in aan u heellannaa kaniisad deegaanka si aan u helno wada-jir, daryeel, qalabayn adeegga, iyo in aan falal wanaagsan samayno!

<!-- p18 -->
Si ay nooga caawiyaan in aan fahanno waxa kaniisaddu tahay, Qorniinku wuxuu na siiyaa sawirro awood leh oo muujinaya waxa loogu talagalay inay noqoto.

<!-- p28 -->
### Jirka

<!-- p29 -->
Kaniisadda inta badan waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Jirka Masiixa**, taas oo xoogga saareysa labadaba midnimadeeda iyo kala-duwanaanteeda. Iyadoo Masiixu yahay madaxa, kaniisaddu waxay u shaqaysaa sidii jir nool halkaas oo rumaystaha kastaa ka ciyaaro door asaasi ah oo kala duwan noqon kara. Xubin kasta si gaar ah ayaa loo hibeeyay, waxa uuna ka qayb qaataa caafimaadka iyo hawsha jirka iyadoo la eegayo shaqadiisa. Sawirkani wuxuu muujinayaa isku-tiirsanaanta rumaystayaasha, gaar ahaan in jirku uu khasab ku yahay inuu isku xirmo si uu u shaqeeyo, iyo in shaqadeennu tahay mid wadajir ah ee aan tartan ahayn. Marka qayb kasta si wadajir ah u shaqayso, jirku wuu koraa oo ku badhaadha midnimo iyo jacayl (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:12-27</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 4:15-16</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 1:18</span>).

<!-- p30 -->
### Aroosadda

<!-- p31 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Aroosadda Masiixa**, tani waxay ka tarjumaysaa xiriir dhow oo jacayl leh oo la leh Ciise. Sida aroosad loo maamuuso loona diyaariyo ninkeeda, kaniisaddu waxaa quduusiya oo nadiifiya jacaylka naf-hurnimo ee Masiixa. Istiqaalaaddani waxay qabataa u-dednaanta iyo daacadnimada laga filayo kaniisadda, iyo sidoo kale jacaylka qoto dheer ee Masiixu u qabo dadkiisa. Sawirkani waxa kale oo uu tilmaamayaa isu-biirka ugu dambeeya ee u dhexeeya Masiixa iyo kaniisadda, oo lagu dabaaldego cashada arooska Wanka, halkaas oo kaniisadda lagu soo bandhigo ammaanta, iyada oo aan wasakh ama ceeb toona lahayn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="25-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 5:25-27</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="19" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="7-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 19:7-9</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="21" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Muujintii 21:2</span>).

<!-- p32 -->
### Macbudka

<!-- p33 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Macbudka Ilaah**, degaanka Ruuxiisa. Rumaystayaasha waxaa lagu sifeeyaa dhagxaan nool oo si wadajir ah loogu dhisayo aasaaska rasuullada iyo nebiyada, Masiixuna Isaga laftiisu waa dhagaxa geeska. Dhismahan quduuska ah wuxuu astaan u yahay joogitaanka Ilaah ee dadkiisa dhexdiisa iyo doorkooda ahaan bulsho cibaado iyo duco. Sawirka macbudku wuxuu adkeeyaa muhiimada quduusnimada iyo ujeeddada wadajirka ah ee kaniisaddu ku weyneynayso Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 3:16-17</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:19-22</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 2:4-5</span>).

<!-- p34 -->
### Qoyska

<!-- p35 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Qoyska Ilaah**, taas oo adkaynaysa midnimada xiriirka iyo daryeelka ka dhexeeya xubnaheeda. Iyada oo loo marayo Ruuxa korsashada, rumaystayaal waxaa laga dhigay carruurtii Ilaah iyo xubno ka mid ah reerkiisa. Sawirkan qoyska ahi wuxuu muujinayaa jacaylka, ka mid ahaanshaha, iyo dhaxalka wadaagga ah ee rumaystayaashu ku leeyihiin Masiixa. Waxa kale oo uu ka tarjumayaa wicitaanka in aan isula dhaqanno naxariis iyo is-hoosaysi sidii walaalo ku jira qoyska Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 6:10</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:19</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 8:15-17</span>). Intaa ka sii dheer, iyada oo loo marayo sawirka qoyska waxa ay muujisaa sida xubnaha kaniisaddu ay u noqdaan bisil, in ay wax la baraan oo la nafaqeeyaan hooyooyin iyo aabbayaal ruuxi ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 5:1-2</span>), si aynu uga gudubno ilmo nuugaya caanaha nadiifka ah ee Erayga, una noqono dadka waaweyn ee qaangaadh ah ee awooda in ay cunaan hilibka adag ee Erayga (Cibraaniyada 5).

<!-- p36 -->
### Adhiga

<!-- p37 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Adhiga**, idaha hoos jooga daryeelka Ciise, Adhijirka Wanaagsan. Sawirkani wuxuu gudbiyaa tiirsanaanta kaniisadda ee Masiixa hagid, quudin, iyo ilaalin. Waxa kale oo uu adkeeyaa mas’uuliyadda hoggaamiyeyaasha kaniisadda in ay si daacad ah u adhi-jiraan adhiga Ilaah iyagoo ah adhijirro ka hooseeya, kuna daydaya tusaalaha jacaylka iyo daryeelka ee Masiixa. Sawirka adhigu wuxuu ka hadlaa kalsooni, adeecid, iyo xidhiidhka dhow ee ka dhexeeya Adhijirka Weyn iyo idihiisa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 10:14-16</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Butros 5:2</span>; <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 20:28</span>).

<!-- p38 -->
### Geedka Canabka iyo Laamihiisa

<!-- p39 -->
Kaniisadda waxaa lagu tilmaamaa **Geedka Canabka iyo Laamihiisa**, taas oo muujinaysa xidhiidhka lama-huraanka ah ee u dhexeeya rumaystayaasha iyo Masiixa. Ciise waa geedka canabka dhabta ah, rumaystayaashuna waa laamaha oo ay khasab ku tahay inay isaga ku sii jiraan si ay midho u dhalaan. Istiqaalaaddani waxay muujisaa tiirsanaanta Masiixa ee firfircoonida ruuxiga ah, korriinka, iyo midho-dhalka. Iyada oo la’aantiis, rumaystayaashu waxba ma qaban karaan, laakiin isaga ku sii jiritaankiisa ayay Ilaah ku weyneeyaan oo ka gutaan ujeeddadooda. Ciise wuu manjooyin doonaa si uu midho badan uga dhaliyo nolosheena (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 15:1-5</span>).

<!-- p40 -->
## Doorka Kaniisadda

<!-- p41 -->
Hadafka kaniisaddu, sida kaalinta Ruuxa Quduuska ah, waxaa lagu fahmi karaa saddex jiho: kor, gudaha, iyo dibadda, mid walbana wuxuu ka tarjumayaa dhinac gaar ah oo ka mid ah wicitaankeeda ee qorshaha Ilaah. Jihooyinkan ayaa hagaya kaniisadda iyada oo gudanaysa doorkeeda sidii bulsho cibaadaysata, wax-dhisaysa, oo ergaynimo leh.

<!-- p42 -->
### Kor: Cibaado iyo Ammaanidda Ilaah

<!-- p43 -->
Ujeeddada koowaad ee kaniisaddu waa in ay caabuddo oo ammaanto Ilaah. Cibaadada wadajirka ah, halkaas oo rumaystayaashu ku kulmaan si ay Ilaah si wadajir ah u weyneeyaan, waa milicsi muuqda oo ammaantiisa ah iyo jawaab u ah weynaantiisa. Sida uu gabyaaga yidhaahdo, “*Rabbiga ku caabuda quruxda quduusnimada; isaga ka ruxruxooda, dhulkoo dhan*” (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PSA" data-chapter="96" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Sabuurradii 96:9</span>). Ka sokow cibaadada wadajirka ah, kaniisadda waxaa loo yeedhay in ay ku noolaato adeecid iyo allabari, iyagoo naftooda u keenaya “allabaryo nool” fal ahaan cibaado ruuxi ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 12:1-2</span>). Diiraddan kor u jeedda ah waxay kaniisadda ku haynaysaa bartamaha maamuuska Ilaah waxayna la jaanqaadaysaa mudnaanteeda doonistiisa.

<!-- p44 -->
### Gudaha: Dhisidda Jirka

<!-- p45 -->
Amarka Weyn wuxuu na farayaa inaan ‘*wax u barno inay ilaaliyaan’*, amarkan wuxuu aad uga fogaanayaa hababka waxbarid ee rasmiga ah sida buuggan. Xerta-samayntu (si faahfaahsan looga hadlay cutubka ku saabsan xeriyada) waa qaabaynta raacaha si uu ugu ekaado edbinta, sida waalidku u koriyo ilmahooda. Waalid wanaagsan ma siiyo ilmo shan sano jir ah encyclopedia oo uma sheego inay iska fahmaan! Halkii, way tusaaleeyaan, muujiyaan, saxaan, hoggaamiyaan, koriyaan, jeclaadaan, daryeelaan, oo adhijiro u noqdaan.

<!-- p46 -->
Waalidku waxay u huraan carruurtooda maxaa yeelay way jecel yihiin, maxaa yeelay waxay ka tirsan yihiin isla jirka, maxaa yeelay dhiig bay wadaagaan. Innagoo ah xubno ka mid ah jidhka iyo qoyska Masiixa, isla jirka ayaannu wadaagnaa, isla dhiigna waan wadaagnaa. Wadaagga (communion) wuxuu ku xusuusinayaa astaan ahaan in nala isu xidhay innaga oo kale iyada oo loo marayo allabariga Masiix. Falka wadaagga waa inuu noqdaa meel midnimo, innagu xusuusiya in dhammaanteen aynu u baahanahay furasho, iyo in badbaadadu Ilaah kali ka timaaddo. Midnimadan gudaheeda waxaan aragnaa inaanay jirin xubin jirka ka mid ah oo ka weyn mid kale marka laga reebo madaxaheenna, Masiix.

<!-- p47 -->
### Dibadda: Gaadhidda Adduunka

<!-- p48 -->
Qaybta ugu caansan ee hawsha kaniisaddu waxay dibadda ugu baxdaa si ay adduunka ugu gaadhsiiso injiilka. Amarka Weyn ee Ciise wuxuu ku yeedhayaa raacayaashiisa inay wacdiyeyaan oo ay xerta ka dhigaan quruumaha oo dhan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="28" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="19-20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 28:19-20</span>). Iyadoo Ruuxa Quduuska ah lagu xoojinayo, kaniisaddu waa inay noqotaa maragtiisa ilaa dhammaadka dhulka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 1:8</span>).

<!-- p49 -->
Ciise waa iftiinka dunida (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 8:12</span>). Iftiinku wuxuu u iftiimaa jiho walba isagoo ka soo baxaya ilaha. Iftiinkiisu wuxuu ku shidmaa muujiyaashiisa: Ereyga, Ruuxiisa, iyo annaga, jidhkiisa. Annaga ayaa iftiinka dunida; nolosheennu waa inay ka iftiimiyaan iftiinkiisa adduunyadeenna madoobaaday oo ay muujisaa shuqullada wanaagsan ee Ilaah ammaanta (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14-16" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 5:14-16</span>). Dhab ahaan, tani waxay la micno tahay in kaniisadda loo yeedhay inay u dooddo caddaalad iyo naxariis, ruuxi iyo jireed labadaba. Sida Yacquub yidhaahdo, cibaadadeenna saafi ahi waa daryeelidda kuwa nugul, agoonta, carmalkana (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JAS" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yacquub 1:27</span>).

<!-- p50 -->
## Hoggaaminta Kaniisadda

<!-- p51 -->
Guud ahaan dunida Masiixiga ah waxaa jira noocyo badan oo firqooyin, kulan, isu-imaatin, iyo goleyaal ah. Aad bay u yar yihiin xeerarka caalamiga ah ee hoggaaminta kaniisadda, balse xeerarka aan hayno aad bay muhiim u yihiin:

<!-- p52 -->
1.  Ciise waa madaxa keliya ee Kaniisadda. Qof walba isaga ayuu u adeega (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="COL" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Kolosay 1:18</span>).

<!-- p53 -->
2.  Waxaa jira heerar sarreeya kuwa hoggaamiya, waxay mudan yihiin maamuus laba-laab ah, waayo laba-laab xukun bay heli doonaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 5:17</span>).

<!-- p54 -->
2.1  Waa inay ahaadaan bisil, aan isla weynayn, mas’uul ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Timoteyos 3:1-13</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="TIT" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Tiitos 1:5-9</span>).

<!-- p55 -->
2.2.  Waa inay noqdaan addoommo u ah dhammaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MRK" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="42-45" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Markos 10:42-45</span>).

<!-- p56 -->
2.3.  Waa inay isu huraan Ereyga iyo tukashada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 6:4</span>).

<!-- p57 -->
2.4.  Iyagaa mas’uul ka ah qalabeynta jidhka shaqada adeegga awgeed.

<!-- p58 -->
3.  Xubno ka weyn ama ka yar oo jirka ka mid ahi ma jiraan. Qoraallo taas taageeraya waa:

<!-- p59 -->
3.1.  Loogama eexan karo hodan iyo sabool midna (Yacquub 2).

<!-- p60 -->
3.2.  Ma jirto kala-qaybsanaan u dhexaysa Yuhuud iyo quruumo, addoon ama la xoreeyey, lab ama dhedig (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="27-28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiyada 3:27-28</span>).

<!-- p61 -->
Qaab-dhismeedka Kaniisadda, ka baxsan xeerarka hoggaanka, waa muhiim, balse badankiisu waa xaalad-ku-xiran oo arrin dhaqan ah halkii uu ka ahaan lahaa mid si guud loo faray. “<u>Sidaas ayaan mar walba u samayn jirnay!</u>” waa hadal caan ah; haddii aad dib ugu noqoto meel ku filan, badanaa waxa jiray sabab wanaagsan oo ay sidaas u sameeyeen. Tani guud ahaan ma aha mid wanaagsan ama xun, ee waa arrin dhadhan, baahi, iyo heli-karaan.

<!-- p62 -->
Tusaale faa’iido leh oo muujinaya dhaqanku inuu saldhig u yahay qaab-dhismeedku waa waqtiga cashada qoyska. Qaybta ugu muhiimsan ee waqtiga cashada qoyska waa in qof kastaa cuno oo cuntaduna nafaqo noqoto. Kala-duwanaanshuhu waa aan dhammaad lahayn: waxa ay cunaan, sida ay u cunaan, sida ay u xawaashaan cuntadooda, iwm. Isku-dhafka kala duwan guud ahaan waa la aqbali karaa, waana inta badan arrin dhadhan iyo doorbidasho.

<!-- p63 -->
Kaniisad leh 10,000 xubnood waxay leedahay baahiyo iyo awoodo ka duwan kuwa kaniisad ka kooban 50. Hannaanka la dejiyey ee ku jira kitaabka Falimaha Rasuullada iyo kanonka Axdiga Cusub wuxuu ahaa qaabka **odayaal** iyo **diikoonno**. In kasta oo qaab-dhismeedka hoggaanka uu ka duwanaan karo kaniisad ilaa kaniisad iyo madh-hab ilaa madh-hab, ugu dambayntii qaabkani wuxuu metelaa qaabka hoggaamineed ee ugu yaraan shaqayn kara ee kaniisadda. Aan eegno jagooyinkan hoggaamineed iyo sida loo doorto.

<!-- p94 -->
### Kaniisadda ku taalla Yeruusaalem

<!-- p95 -->
Kaniisadda Yeruusaalem waxa hoggaaminayey inta badan **rasuullada**, kuwaas oo diiradda saaray waxbaridda, salaadda, iyo kormeerka ruuxiga ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:42</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 6:4</span>). Markii bulshada ay kortay, baahida loo qabo qaab-dhismeed maamulka ayaa caddaatay, gaar ahaan in wax looga qabto arrimaha wax ku oolka ah sida daryeelka carmalo iyo qaybinta kheyraadka. Tani waxay keentay in la magacaabo **diikonno** (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-7" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 6:1-7</span>). Shakhsiyaadkan, oo loo doortay bisaylkooda ruuxiga ah iyo sumcad wanaagsan awgeed, waxay maareeyeen adeegyada wax ku oolka ah si ay rasuulladu ugu sii jeestaan masuuliyadooda ruuxiga ah. Waqti ka dib, **waayeello** ayaa sidoo kale u soo baxay hoggaamiyeyaal kaniisadda Yeruusaalem. Tusaale ahaan, intii lagu jiray Golaha Yeruusaalem (Falimaha Rasuullada 15), rasuullada iyo waayeelladuba waxay ka qaybqaateen go'aan-qaadashada, taasoo muujinaysa qaab hoggaamineed wadajir ah.

<!-- p96 -->
### Kaniisadda ku taalla Antiyookiya

<!-- p97 -->
Kaniisadda Antiyookiya waxay lahayd qaab-dhismeed hoggaamineed oo aan si dhexe loo koobin oo kala-duwan marka la barbar dhigo Yeruusaalem. Waxa ay hogaaminayeen koox **nabiyaal iyo macallimiin** ah, oo ay ka mid ahaayeen Barnabas, Saul (Paul), Simeon, Lucius, iyo Manaen (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 13:1</span>). Kaniisadda Antiyookiya sidoo kale aad bay ugu jirtay gaarsiinta quruumaha aan Yuhuudda ahayn, halka kaniisadda Yeruusaalem ay ka tarjumaysay xididdeheeda Yuhuudda. In kasta oo Axdiga Cusub si cad ugu xusin diikonno Antiyookiya, haddana waxaa u badan in ay jireen hawlo wax ku ool ah maadaama ay kaniisaddu sii weynaanaysay oo ay ku hawllaneyd diridda gargaar Yeruusaalem (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="11" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="29-30" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 11:29-30</span>). Kaniisadda Antiyookiya waxay noqotay tusaale loogu talagalay dirista misiinarro, iyadoo hoggaamiyayaashu tukaynaya, soomaya, oo gacmaha saaraya Paul iyo Barnabas si ay u diraan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 13:2-3</span>). Tani waxay muujinaysaa qaab-dhismeed isku dheellitira kormeerka ruuxiga ah (nabiyaal iyo macallimiin) iyo gaarsiinta wacdinta iyo taageerada wax ku oolka ah.

<!-- p98 -->
Si ka duwan sixitaannadii Bawlos ee uu u diray Korintosiyiinta iyo Galatiya, Qorniinku kama dhaleeceeyo qaab-dhismeedyada hoggaaminta ee Yeruusaalem ama Antiyookiya. Tani waxay muujinaysaa in kaniisadahani, hagidda Ruuxa Quduuska ah hoosteeda, ay xor u ahaayeen inay ku habeeyaan isu-imaatinkooda si ay u daboolaan baahidooda gaarka ah iyo duruufahooda, iyagoo ku dhex jira qaab-dhismeedka caqiidada sugan iyo waxbaridda rasuullada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="42" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 2:42</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="16-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 3:16-17</span>).

<!-- p99 -->
Inta lagu jiro qaybahan erayo gaar ah ayaa la adeegsaday oo aan weli qeexnay oo aan baran: rasuul, nabi, wacdiyeye. Hadyadahani waxay qayb ka yihiin shanta adeeg ee Ciise siiyey kaniisadda.

<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="quote" data-verses="11-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">

> **<u>Efesos 4:11-13</u>** (NKJV)
>
> Isaga qudhiisu wuxuu qaar ka dhigay rasuullo, qaarna nebiyo, qaarna injiiliistayaal, qaarna raaciyayaal (adhijirro) iyo macallimiin,
>
> si loogu qalabeeyo quduusiinta shaqada adeegga, looguna dhiso jidhka Masiixa,
>

<!-- p106 -->
1.  **Rasuullo** - Hormuudyo iyo aragti-yaqaaniin dhisaya aasaasyo, beera kaniisado, kuna sii hormariya Boqortooyada Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="20" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 2:20</span>). <!-- p107 -->
2.  **Nebiyo** - Farriinwadayaal iyo hagayaal ku dhawaaqaya runta Ilaah, keenana dhiirigelin, dhisid, iyo jihayn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="14" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="3" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 14:3</span>). Nebiyadu waxay kiciyaan iimaan, waxayna dadka ugu yeedhaan inay la jaanqaadaan Erayga Ilaah, iyaga oo muujinaya ku-tiirsanaanta maqalka Codka Ilaah.

<!-- p108 -->
3.  **Injiiliistayaal** - Ku dhawaaqayaasha Injiilka ee xooga saara gaadhista kuwa lumay, kuna kiciya kuwa kale falgelin si ay Masiix uga marag furaan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="8" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 8:5-8</span>). <!-- p109 -->
4.  **Raaciyayaal (Adhijirro)** - Daryeelayaal iyo korsheyaal ilaaliya, hago, oo bixiya korjoogteyn ruuxi ah oo loogu talagalay kaniisadda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="20" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="28" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 20:28</span>). Raaciyeyaashu waxay barayaan xerta muhiimadda bulshada, daryeelidda dadka kale, iyo xisaabtanka dhexdiisa jirka Masiixa.

5.  **Macallimiin** - Bareyaal iyo tababarayaal caddeeiya caqiidada oo ka caawiya rumaystayaasha inay ku koraan fahamka iyo adeegsigga Erayga Ilaah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2TI" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="15" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Timoteyos 2:15</span>). Macallimiintu waxay qalabeeyaan xerta inay si qoto dheer u bartaan Qorniinka, ugu dabaqaan nolol maalmeedkooda, ayna si wax ku ool ah u baraan kuwa kale.

## Hoggaaminta iyo Hibada

<!-- p112 -->
Qodob si khaldan loo fahmo oo ku saabsan hoggaaminta kaniisadda waa kala-soocidda awoodda iyo mas’uuliyadda hoggaaminta iyo hibbooyinka khaaska ah.

<!-- p113 -->
Khaladkani wuxuu sal ku leeyahay sida aan u adeegsanno ereyada sida macallin, raaciye, nabi, rasuul, injiiliiste. Ereyadani waa hibbooyin. Kama jiro **xafiis** ‘rasuul’ oo ku jira kaniisaddii Axdiga Cusub. Waxaa jira dad qabta shaqada rasuulka, oo leh hibada ama yeedhmada rasuul. Haysashada hibada rasuulnimo ma siiso awood ama mas’uuliyad.

<!-- p114 -->
Anigu waxaan ahay muusikiiste xirfad leh; saaxofoon ayaan ciyaaray in ka badan 20 sano. Dugsiga iyo jaamacaddaba waan ku ciyaaray, waxaanan xilliyo xagaaga qaarkood si nus-xirfadle ah ugu ciyaaray anigoo la shaqaynaya koox wacdiyin. In kasta oo aan haysto xirfad, hibbo, iyo karti aan ku noqdo saaxofooniste, haddana ma lihi xaq aan ku imaado kaniisaddiinna kuna istaago masraxa anigoo la jooga kooxda caabudaadda.

<!-- p115 -->
Sidoo kale, waxaa jira dad ku hibo leh ahaanshaha raaciye/adhijir, laakiin taasi kama dhigeyso ‘RAACIYAHA’ kaniisadda deegaanka ama ‘kaaga’ raaciye. Hadyadaha adeegga ee shan-laab ma hoggaamiyaan kaniisadda; hoggaamiyeyaasha (waayeellada iyo diikonadu) ayaa hoggaamiya. Aad bay ugu badan tahay in hoggaamiyeyaashu yeeshaan hibbooyin gaar ah oo ka mid ah shanta-laab, laakiin hibadaasi waxay xoojisaa hoggaamintooda, ma abuurto. Sidoo kale, waa wax caadi ah in dad weli horumaraya oo leh hibbooyin shan-laab ay u adeegaan oo ay u hanqaltaagaan hoggaamin, laakiin Ilaah marka hore wuxuu danaynayaa dabeecadda, dabadeed hibbooyinka. Dhammaanteen waa in aynu noqonnaa kuwo bisil, awood u leh kala-soocidda Erayga Ilaah, ruuxa ka buuxa, oo samaynaya shuqullada wanaagsan.

<!-- p116 -->
Hoggaamiye kasta iyo hibbo kasta waa inay marka hore isu hoggaansamaan noqoshada xer, qayb ka ahaanshaha jirka.

<!-- p117 -->
Ciise wuxuu bixiyey hibbooyin adeeg oo gaar ah si loogu qalabeeyo quduusiinta shaqada adeegga. Kuwani way ka duwan yihiin hadyadaha kicinta (lagaga hadli doono goor dambe cutubkan) ama hadyadaha muujinta (lagaga hadli doono cutubka ku saabsan baabtiisyada). Marka xertu koraan, hadyadaha adeegga ee shan-laab ayaa u qalabeeya adeeg, halka waayeellada iyo diikonadu bixiyaan korjoogteyn si loo hubiyo caqiido iyo dhaqangelin sax ah.

<!-- p118 -->
## Doorkaaga Kaniisadda

<!-- p119 -->
Ilaa hadda cutubkan waxaan ka hadalnay hoggaaminta kaniisadda iyo tusaalooyinka udub-dhexaadka u ah kaniisadda. Aan ku dhisno tusaalaha gaarka ah ee *jirka* oo aynu ka fiirsanno orodyahan maradoon.

<!-- p120 -->
### Adigu Waxaad Tahay Qayb Ka Mid Ah Guudka

<!-- p121 -->
Kaniisadda, maadaama ay tahay Jirka Masiixa, waxay ku tiirsan tahay xubin kasta oo wada shaqaynaya si loo fuliyo ujeedadeeda. Sida jirku u shaqayn karin si habboon haddii hal xubin maqnaato, sidaas oo kale kaniisaddu waxay u baahan tahay in rumaystaha kastaa si firfircoon uga qaybqaato, koro, oo u adeego. Xubin kasta oo jirka ka mid ahi waxa uu leeyahay door aad muhiim u ah. Qayb ka mid ah jirka noqoshadu waxay ka dhigan tahay in la sii ahaado ku-xirnaan. Jirku wuu sii noolaan karaa awood yaraanteeda iyadoo aan xubin jirin, laakiin xubinta lafteedu ma noolaan karto marka laga gooyo jirka. Waa in aynu noqono qayb firfircoon oo ka mid ah kaniisadda oo aynaan ka go’in. <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="10" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="24" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Hebrews 10:24</span> waxay leedahay waa in aan isu nimaadnaa oo aanan dayicin isu-imaatinka.

<!-- p122 -->
Si dhab ah meel iska kaga shaqaysaan oo u shaqaysaan waxaad u baahan tihiin walaaltinimo! Waa inaad dadka kale taqaan, jeclaato dadka kale oo sida ugu fiican aad ka heshid dadka kale! Dhiso xidhiidho adag iyo saaxiibtinimooyin, oo hagaaji marka ay adkaadaan ama dhaawacmaan. Marka kala-goys dhaawacmo ama bararo, waa inaad ka shaqaysaa in la nasto ama dib loo dejiyo; si kala-goyska siibtay uusan u jabin! Xubin jaban oo aan bogsanayn ugu dambayn waa la kala gooyaa, adiguna kaligaa kama noqon kartid qayb ka mid ah jirka.

<!-- p123 -->
Dhammaanteen shaqo isku mid ah ma lihin. Qaar naga mid ah waxay Injiilka ka wadaagi karaan masraxa, qaar kalena hareer miis casho. Laakiin DHAMMAANTEEN waxaa naloo yeedhay inaan noqono adeegayaal isu-heshiisiinta (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="2CO" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="18-19" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">2 Corinthians 5:18-19</span>). Dhammaanteen waxa nala geliyey wadaadnimada rumaystayaasha (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1PE" data-chapter="2" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="9" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Peter 2:9</span>, <span class="x-scripture" data-book="REV" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Revelation 1:6</span>). Tan macnaheedu waxa weeye in dhammaanteen aynu awoodno inaanu qabanno shaqada adeegga annagoo ku xoogaysanayna Ruuxa Quduuska ah.

<!-- p124 -->
Fara-cagtu waxay la shaqeeyaan cagaha, caguhuna canqowyada, iyo wixii la mid ah-qayb kastaa waxay wax ku darsataa himilada guud. Oradyahannada maratoonka waa inay tababartaan haddii ay doonayaan inay dhammeeyaan tartanka. Waa inaad diyaar ahaataa-ma aha oo keliya barashada, ee waa inaad si dhab ah u dhaqangelisaa waxa aad baratay. Kaniisaddu, sida orodyahan maratoon, way kobcaysaa marka xubin kastaa door-keeda si isu-duuban u guto.

<!-- p125 -->
Waxaa jira tiro badan oo dad ah oo noloshaada ku jira oo adiga keliya ay suurtagal kuu tahay inaad gaadho. Baastar-ku ma noqon karo kan keliya ee isku dayaya inuu daryeelo oo adhigo, Injiliistuhuna ma noqon karo kan keliya ee isku dayaya inuu gaadho. Ilaah wuxuu doonayaa inuu kugu adeegsado inaad gaadho kuwa aan rumaysan adigoo ku xoogaysanaya Ruuxiisa Quduuska ah. Dhammaanteen waxa aynu qabannaa shaqada adeegga; Ruuxa Quduuska ah ayaa ina kaalmaynaya.

<!-- p126 -->
### Tababar Ayaad U Baahan Tahay

<!-- p127 -->
Rumaystayaashu waa inay tababartaan oo isu qalabeeyaan kaalmooyinkooda gaarka ah ee kaniisadda, sida orodyahanku u xoojiya murqaha gaarka ah ee tartanka. Kuma guulaysan doontid haddii aad u booddo maratoon adigoon tababaran, oo dhammaan tababartu isku mid ma aha, waxaad u baahan tahay inaad si ku filan ugu diyaar garowdo tartanka. Ciise wuxuu siiyey hibbooyin adeeg oo gaar ah si uu u qalabeeyo rumaystayaasha, ee aanu ahayn inay shaqada oo dhan iyagu qabtaan, balse si uu xubin kasta u xoojiyo inuu si wax ku ool ah ugu adeego kaalintiisa gaarka ah.

<!-- p128 -->
Tababarka maratoonku wuxuu keenayaa isu-beddel. Waxaad u baahan tahay xoog, waxaadna u baahan tahay inaad isu beddesho. Masuuliyaddeennu waa in aynu is-hoosgelinno Ilaah oo aynu u oggolaanno inuu ina beddelo cusboonaysiin dhexdeeda (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1-2" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Romans 12:1-2</span>). Is-beddelku waa inuu kugu hoggaamiyaa qaan-gaar, kaa awoodsiinaya inaad sidato culayskaaga oo aad taageerto kuwa kale. Waa inaad awooddaa inaad midho BADAN dhasho. Tan waxa si dheer looga hadli doonaa cutubka ku saabsan quduusaynta.

<!-- p129 -->
Tababarku waa adag yahay, mararka qaarkoodna waa raaxo-darro. Mararka qaarkood is-beddelka aad doonayso inaad marto waxaad ka doorbidi lahayd inaad ka boodo. Laakiin haddii aad rabto inaad gaadho yoolka, waa inaad ku sii jirtaa waddada ciriiriga ah. Mararka qaarkood, waxaad u baahan kartaa digniin, tasalli, ama taageero-dhammaan lagu bixiyo dulqaad (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1TH" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Thessalonians 5:14</span>).

<!-- p130 -->
Oradyahan maratoon oo kasta oo weyn waxa uu leeyahay tababare si uu u tababaro, adiguna waxaad u baahan doontaa tababare ku caawiya. Dabcan, waxaad haysataa Ruuxa Quduuska ah, laakiin badanaa Wuxuu ku tiirsan yahay tababarayaashiisa, ragga iyo dumarka qaan-gaarka ah ee Ilaah, ee Jirka Masiixa ee aad loogu yeedhay inaad la shaqayso.

<!-- p131 -->
Annaga oo ah shakhsiyaad, waa in aan marka hore is-hoosgelinno Ilaah, marka xigta, xaaladdan, jidh rumaystayaal ah. Is-hoosaysiinta kaniisadda waxay ka dhigan tahay in la maamuuso hoggaaminta rabaaniga ah (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="13" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Hebrews 13:17</span>) oo la is-hoosaysiiyo midba midka kale iyadoo la jiro is-hoosaysiin (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="5" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="21" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 5:21</span>).

<!-- p132 -->
Is-hoosaysiintu way adkaan kartaa, gaar ahaan marka aad hore u soo martay kalsooni jabtay ama hoggaamin fashilantay. Tani macnaheedu ma aha adeecid indho la’aan ah ama xakamayn balse waxay ka tarjumaysaa ruux ixtiraam iyo iskaashi. Is-hoosaysiinta adduunku waxay ka dalbanayaan kuwa awoodda haya, waxayna noqon kartaa mid hoos u dhigta oo keenta nacayb. Is-hoosaysiinta Kitaabiga ah ee ku xidhan is-hoosaysiin iyo garasho waxay adkaysaa kaniisadda waxayna kobcisaa isla-xisaabtan. Noqo mid diyaar u ah inuu dhagaysto hanuun iyo jawaab-celin wax-dhis ah.

<!-- p133 -->
Hadda oo aynu dhignay aasaas adag - adigu waxaad tahay qayb macno leh oo jirka ka mid ah balse tababar ayaad u baahan tahay - aynu u gudubno helidda meesha aad ku habboon tahay.

<!-- p134 -->
### Helidda Meesha Aad Ku Habboon Tahay

<!-- p135 -->
Helidda doorkaaga ee kaniisadda waxa ku jira raadinta hanuunka Ilaah, fahamka hibbooyinkaaga ruuxiga ah, iyo ka-qaybgal firfircoon ee bulshadaada kaniisadda. Waa safar salaad, baadhitaan, iyo korriin ah, kaas oo aad ku ogaaneyso sida Ilaah si gaar ah kuugu qalabeeyey inaad uga qaybqaadato boqortooyadiisa. Aad bay muhiim u tahay in la ogaado in doorkaaga ee kaniisadda uu u badan yahay inuu is beddelo waqti ka dib oo aanu u badnayn inuu ahaado isku mid inta noloshaada oo dhan.

<!-- p136 -->
Halkan waxaa ku jira hab wax-ku-ool ah oo kaa caawinaya inaad hesho meeshii aad ku habboon tahay, waxa lagu soo bandhigay tillaabooyin isdaba-joog ah si caddeyn loo helo, balse waxa laga yaabaa inaad isla tillaabada ku celceliso marar badan inta aad Ilaah la socoto.

<!-- p137 -->
#### Tallaabada 1 - Raadi Hanuunka Ilaah Adigoo Salaadda iyo Ereyga Adeegsanaya

<!-- p138 -->
Ku bilow inaad raadsato hanuunka Ilaah adigoo salaad ku jira. Ilaah waa isha ugu dambaysa ee ujeeddadaada iyo yeedhmadaada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JER" data-chapter="29" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Jeremiah 29:11</span>). Si joogto ah u ducee si aad u hesho caddayn ku saabsan sida aad ugu adeegi karto kaniisadda, adigoo ka dalbanaya Inuu kuu muujiyo hibbooyinkaaga ruuxiga ah iyo xiisahaaga. Ku kalsoonow inuu hagi doono tallaabooyinkaaga marka aad u dhiibto qorshayaashaada doonistiisa (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="3" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="5-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Proverbs 3:5-6</span>).

<!-- p139 -->
Baro Ereyga Ilaah si aad u qoto-dheereyso fahamkaaga himilada kaniisadda iyo doorka aad ku leedahay dhexdeeda. Aayado muhiim ah sida 1 Korintos 12 iyo <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-12" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Ephesians 4:11-12</span> waxay iftiiminayaan hibbooyinka ruuxiga ah iyo kala-duwanaanshaha doorarka Jirka Masiixa. Ka fakar tusaalooyinka shakhsiyaadka Qorniinka ee helay oo gutay doorarkooda, sida Bawlos, Timoteyos, iyo Lidya.

<!-- p140 -->
#### Tallaabada 2 - Aqoonso Hibooyinkaaga, Xiisahaaga, iyo Xirfadahaaga

<!-- p141 -->
Aqoonsashada **hibooyinkaaga ruuxiga ah** waa tallaabo lama-huraan ah. Waxaad ogaan kartaa in qaadashada qiimeyn hibooyin ruuxi ah ay waxtar leedahay. Hiboyinkan waxaa Ilaah bixiya si loo dhiso kaniisadda. Waxa jira dhowr fasal oo hibooyin ah oo ku taxan Axdiga Cusub:

<!-- p142 -->
- **Hibooyinka Dhiirrigelinta** ee uu Ilaah bixiyo: <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6-8" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 12:6-8</span>

<!-- p143 -->
- Waxaa laga hadli doonaa ka dib liiskan.

<!-- p144 -->
- Hibooyinka Adeegga Shanta-Qaybood ee uu Ciise bixiyey: <span class="x-scripture" data-book="EPH" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="11-13" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Efesos 4:11-13</span>

<!-- p145 -->
- Waxaa horay looga hadlay cutubkan.

<!-- p146 -->
- Hibooyinka Muujiinta ee uu bixiyo Ruuxa Quduuska ah: <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="8-10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:8-10</span>

<!-- p147 -->
- Waxaa laga hadlay cutubka ku saabsan baabtiiska.

<!-- p148 -->
**Hibooyinka dhiirrigelinta** waxay ka tarjumaan sida rumaystuhu u habaysan yahay inuu u fikiro, u dareemo, uguna dhaqmo. Waxay dhiirrigeliyaan rumaystayaasha inay u adeegaan siyaabo gaar ah oo dhisa kaniisadda. Mid kasta oo hibo ah waxay kaabayaaan kuwa kale, iyagoo hubinaya in kaniisaddu u shaqeyso sidii jidh mideysan oo leh doorar kala duwan (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="ROM" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="4-5" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Rooma 12:4-5</span>). Waxay u shaqeeyaan sidii karti nimco lagu siiyay (Giriig: ***charis** - khar'-ece - **G5463 -** hadiyad aan la kasban*) taasoo micnaheedu yahay inay ka timaaddo Ilaah, lagamana kasban ama lagama barto. In kasta oo hibadu ka timaaddo Ilaah, qofka hibo leh wuu ka sii kasban karaa xirfad adeegsigeeda.

<!-- p149 -->
Hibooyinka dhiirrigelinta waa:

<!-- p150 -->
1.  **Waxsii-sheegid** - U-janjeera Ilaah-bixiyey oo si geesinimo leh loogu dhawaaqo runta, badanaa diiradda saaraya qirasho iyo xaqnimo, kuwa kalena ku hagaya inay la jaanqaadaan Ereyga Ilaah.\*

<!-- p151 -->
2.  **Adeeg (Gargaar)** - Waxa uu daboolaa baahiyaha wax-ku-oolka ah iyada oo loo marayo adeeg iyo gargaar si loo taageero kuwa kale.

<!-- p152 -->
3.  **Waxbarid** - Sharaxdaa oo caddeeyaa runta, dadka kalena ka caawisaa inay fahmaan oo ay ku dabaqaan Ereyga Ilaah.

<!-- p153 -->
4.  **Waano (Dhiirrigelin)** - Kiciya, dhiirrigeliya, oo raalli geliya kuwa kale si ay u koraan oo u adkaystaan.

<!-- p154 -->
5.  **Bixinta** - Kheyraad si deeqsinimo leh ayuu u bixiyaa si loo daboolo baahiyaha loona hormariyo shaqada Ilaah isaga oo aan dooneyn aqoonsi.

<!-- p155 -->
6.  **Hoggaamin (Maamul)** - Habeeya oo hagiya kuwa kale si loo gaaro yoolal iyadoo la adeegsanaayo aragti iyo hufnaan.

<!-- p156 -->
7.  **Naxariis** - Muujiya naxariis iyo daryeel si uu u dejiyo oo u taageero kuwa xanuunsan ama baahan.

<!-- p157 -->
\* Waxsii-sheegidda ee macnaha Rooma waxaa loo fahmaa inay ka duwan tahay waxsii-sheegidda sida hibo muujin ah oo Ruuxa Quduuska ah (1 Korintos). Ereyga Giriigga ah ee la adeegsaday waa isku mid labada qoraal, laakiin macnaha guud wuu kala duwan yahay. Si aad u hesho barasho qoto dheer oo ku saabsan farqiga, waxaan ku talinayaa akhrinta:

<!-- p158 -->
- *Hibada Waxsii-sheegidda* oo uu qoray Kenneth E. Hagin ([www.rhema.org](http://www.rhema.org))

<!-- p159 -->
<!-- -->

<!-- p160 -->
- *Hibooyinka iyo Adeegyada Ruuxa Quduuska ah* ee uu qoray Lester Sumrall

<!-- p161 -->
Markaad aqoonsanayso hibadaada, ka fiirso **xiisahaaga iyo xirfadahaaga**. Ilaah badanaa wuxuu na ugu yeeraa inaan ka adeegno meelaha aan ku xiisaynno ama aan ku xirfadnahay (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="EXO" data-chapter="31" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="2-6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Baxniintii 31:2-6</span>). Ka fiirso waxa ku xiise geliya adeegidda Ilaah iyo dadka kale, oo ka fikir sida xirfadahaaga shaqo, hiwaayadaha, iyo waayo-aragnimada nolosha ay uga qayb qaadan karaan adeegga.

<!-- p162 -->
Raadi hagitaan ka yimaadda **hoggaamiyeyaasha iyo la-taliyeyaasha** kuwaas oo bixin kara xigmadda iyo aragti (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PRO" data-chapter="15" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="22" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Maahmaahyadii 15:22</span>). La kulmo wadaad ama hoggaamiye adeeg si aad uga wada hadashaan danahaaga oo aad u weydiisato jawaab-celin daacad ah halka aad ugu habboonaan karto. Ku tukado jawaab-celinta aad hesho. Ka fiirso ku biirista koox talmidnimo si aad u hesho tababar iyo taageero joogto ah.

<!-- p163 -->
#### Tallaabada 3 - Noqo Aamin Hadda

<!-- p164 -->
Masalka talantiyada (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="MAT" data-chapter="25" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="14-30" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Matayos 25:14-30</span>) wuxuu ina xusuusinayaa in aan aammin noqono waxyaalaha yaryar si loogu aaminao waxyaalaha waaweyn (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="LUK" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="10" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Luukos 16:10</span>).

<!-- p165 -->
**Ka qayb qaado oo adeeg** adeegyo kala duwan si aad u ogaato halka Ilaah kugu yeerayo. Ka bilow yar adigoo iskaa u shaqeeya meelaha baahida ka jirto gudaha kaniisaddaada iyo adeegyada ay taageeraan. Tusaale ahaan:

<!-- p166 -->
- Ku biir kooxda martigelinta, adeegga carruurta, ama barnaamijyada gaadhsiinta. Ka fiirso inaad hoggaamiso koox diiradda saaraysa talmidnimada oo loogu talagalay mu’miniinta cusub ee ku saabsan buuggan *Principles of Faith,* adigoo adeegsanaya su’aalaha doodda kooxda iyo hawlaha dhammaadka cutubka si aad u fududeyso wadahadalka.

<!-- p167 -->
- U furanow ka-baxitaanka aaggaaga raaxada si aad u ogaato fursado cusub.

<!-- p168 -->
- La soco halka Ilaah kugu shaqeeyo iyo halka aad isku aragto inaad ugu wax-ku-oolka badan tahay.

<!-- p169 -->
Ku tababar bixinta. Waxaannu kaniisadda ugu deeqi karnaa siyaabo badan oo muhiim ah, waxaana naloo amray inaan sidaas yeelno si loo fuliyo hawsha.

<!-- p170 -->
- Waqti - wakhtigeenna ayaannu bixin karnaa oo iskaa u shaqayn karnaa. Markaan bixinno wakhtigeenna, dadaalkeenna iyo hibadeenna ayaannu adeegsanaynaa. Tani sidoo kale waxay noqon kartaa hab aad u wanaagsan oo lagu barto xirfado cusub laguna kordhiyo kuwa jira.

<!-- p171 -->
- Kheyraad - waxaan ka bixin karnaa kheyraadkeenna. Maanta tan ugu badan badanaa waa bixinta dhaqaale ee kaniisadda iyo adeegyada (ma aha oo keliya “hay’adda”, balse sidoo kale kuwa baahan ee kaniisadda ku jira). Lacag-bixintu waa waxtar, sababtoo ah kaniisaddu waxay ku iibsan kartaa kheyraadka loo baahan yahay, laakiin dadka waxay ka bixiyeen kheyraadkoodii jireed ee ka baxsan lacagta, tusaale ahaan guryo dheeraad ah, dhar, baabuur, cunto, agabka dhismaha, qalab, iwm. In <span class="x-scripture" data-book="ACT" data-chapter="4" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="32-35" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Falimaha Rasuullada 4:32-35</span> waxaan ku aragnaa tusaale mu’miniintii hore oo si xor ah u bixinaya, iyagoo wadaagaya dakhligii ka soo baxay iibinta dhulal iyo guryo dheeraad ah oo bixinaya si ay ‘cidna baahi u qabin’.

<!-- p172 -->
Inaad dadka kale la shaqayso adeegga dhexdiisa waxay muujin kartaa xooggaaga waxayna qoto-dheereyn kartaa fahamkaaga doorkaaga. Ka qaybgal kooxo yaryar ama hawlaha kaniisadda si aad u kobciso xiriirrada. Dhagayso culaysyada iyo baahiyaha dadka kugu xeeran oo ka fiirso sida aad u taageeri karto. Dhisidda xiriirrada gudaha kaniisadda waxay kaa caawinaysaa inaad dadka kale la xidhanto oo aad fahamto baahiyaha bulshada.

<!-- p173 -->
#### Tallaabada 4 - Raadi Caawimaad!

<!-- p174 -->
Ciise wuxuu yidhi in nolosha gudaheeda dhibaato ayaan la kulmi doonnaa! (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="JHN" data-chapter="16" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="33" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Yooxanaa 16:33</span>) Waxaa nalagu dhiirrigeliyey inaanaan keligeen qaadin culayska rarkeenna balse aan ku tuurno Ilaah ina daryeela! Mararka qaarkood safarkeenna dhexdiisa waan dhaawacmi karnaa. Oradyahannada maratoonka, xitaa marka ay “wax walba si sax ah” u sameynayaan waxay jidhkooda u saari karaan cadaadis sababa dhaawac, ama way ku turunturoodi karaan oo dhici karaan.

<!-- p176 -->
Ma aha dhammaan ‘dhicitaanku’ inay la xiriiraan dembi, laakiin qaar baa la xiriira, ma aha dhammaan tabardarridu inay tahay ‘khaladkeenna’, mararka qaarkoodna kuwa kale ayaa inaga dembaaba. Iyadoo aan loo eegin sababta tabardarradeenna ama dhaawaceenna, waa in nala soo celiyo oo nala bogsiiyo.

<!-- p177 -->
Waxaa jira xorriyad weyn oo ku jirta raadinta bogsiinta gudaha iyo soo celinta. Waxaa naloo yeedhay inaan daboolno qaybaha jilicsan ee jidhka (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="23" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:23</span>) oo aan soo celinno kuwa lagu qabtay xadgudubyo (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="GAL" data-chapter="6" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="1" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Galatiya 6:1</span>).

<!-- p178 -->
Wax badan baa laga odhan karaa mawduucan, waxaana si guud u sahaminnaa mowduucyada bogsiinta ee cutubka ku saabsan quduusaynta.

#### Tallaabada 5 - Noqo mid samra

<!-- p180 -->
U fiirso xaqiijinta ka timaadda Ilaah iyo kuwa kale. Badanaa, Ilaah wuxuu xaqiijiyaa wicitaankaaga iyada oo loo marayo midho-dhal, farxad, iyo xaqiijin ka timaadda kuwa kugu xeeran. Ka milicsi halka dadaalkaagu midho-dhal ka keenay oo dhagayso jawaab-celinta ku saabsan saameynta adeegga. Tani waxay kaa caawin kartaa inaad garato halka aad ugu wax-ku-oolka badan tahay u adeegidda Jirka Masiixa.

<!-- p181 -->
U samir oo ku kalsoonow wakhtiga Ilaah. Inaad ogaato doorkaaga waxay qaadan kartaa waqti waana ku lug yeelan kartaa tijaabo iyo khalad (<span class="x-scripture" data-book="PHP" data-chapter="1" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="6" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Filiboy 1:6</span>). Ku kalsoonow in Ilaah ku hagi doono inta aad daacad u tahay oo aad u furan tahay hoggaamintiisa. Inta lagu jiro waqtigan, u heellanow korriin iyo tababbar adigoo ka qaybgalaya aqoon-is-weydaarsiyo, barashooyinka Baybalka, iyo agabyo kale oo ku qalabeeya adeeg. Samee tan adiga oo la socda kaniisadda, la falgal Jirka Masiixa si aad u ogaato hibooyinkaaga.

<!-- p182 -->
Xusuusnow, kaniisaddu ma aha dhismo ama hay’ad; waa jir nool oo neefsanaya oo ka kooban dad adiga oo kale ah. Adigoo doorkaaga qaadaya, waxaad ka qayb qaadanaysaa howlgalkeeda waxaadna ammaantaa Masiix oo madax u ah.

## Su’aalaha Milicsiga Shakhsiyeed

<!-- p184 -->
1.  Maxay adiga ahaan kaaga dhigan tahay in aad noqoto “kan la yeedhay”? Sidee ayaad u noolaan kartaa aqoonsigan nolol maalmeedkaaga?

<!-- p185 -->
2.  Midkee sawirrada Kitaabiga ah ee kaniisadda (Jir, Aroosad, Macbud, iwm.) ayaa adiga kugu saamayn badan? Sabab?

<!-- p186 -->
3.  Ka milicsi doorkaaga Jirka Masiixa. Hibooyin, kartiyo, ama hammiyo noocee ah ayaa Ilaah ku siiyay si aad ugu adeegto kaniisaddiisa?

<!-- p187 -->
4.  Sidee ka mid ahaanshaha kaniisad maxalli ah u adkaynaysaa iimaankaaga oo ugu kaa caawinaysaa inaad fuliso hawsha Ilaah?

<!-- p188 -->
5.  Tallaabo noocee ah ayaad qaadi kartaa si aad u kobciso midnimo, isla-xisaabtan, iyo korriin gudaha bulshadaada kaniisadda?

## Su’aalaha Wada-hadalka Kooxda (20-30 Daqiiqo)

<!-- p190 -->
1.  **Su’aasha Baraf-jebinta:** “Waa maxay xasuustaadii ugu horraysay ee ka qaybgalka ama ka mid ahaanshaha kaniisad? Sidee ayay aragtidaada kaniisadda isu beddeshay waqti ka dib?”

<!-- p191 -->
2.  **Baadhista Kaniisadda oo ah Jirka Masiixa:** Akhri <span class="x-scripture" data-book="1CO" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-27" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">1 Korintos 12:12-27</span>. Maxay qaybtaan ina baraa ku saabsan midnimada iyo kala-duwanaanshaha kaniisadda? Sidee aynu u xusnaa oo u maamuusnaa kala duwanaanshaha midba midka kale?

<!-- p192 -->
3.  **Hawlgalka Kaniisadda:** Sidee ayey kaniisaddu u fulisaa ujeeddooyinkeeda kor u jeeda (caabudid), gudaha u jeeda (dhisid/edification), iyo dibadda u jeeda (wacdinta Injiilka)? Aaggee ayaad dareemaysaa in si gaar ah laguugu yeedhay inaad ka qaybgasho?

<!-- p193 -->
4.  **La-tacaalidda Khilaaf iyo Korriin:** <span class="x-scripture" data-book="HEB" data-chapter="12" data-lang="en" data-type="ref" data-verses="12-17" data-version="NKJV" dir="ltr" style="text-align: left;">Cibraaniyada 12:12-17</span> waxay na ugu yeedhaysaa inaan is xoojinno oo ka fogaanno qadhaadh. Sideen u maarayn karnaa kala qaybsanaan ama dhaawac ka dhex jira kaniisadda si ay u dhiirrigeliso bogsiin iyo midnimo?

<!-- p194 -->
5.  **Helista Meeshaada:** Ka feker waqti aad aad ugu xiranayd kaniisaddaada. Maxay arrimo u horseedeen waayo-aragnimadaas, sideese u abuuri karnaa waayo-aragnimooyin la mid ah kuwa kale?
